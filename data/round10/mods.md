## Completeness summary

**Complete from the open literature and the OSU Code V files:** the telescope and focus (effective focal length 123,765 mm, f/15 on an 8251 mm used aperture), the slit masks, the dichroic (cross-over 5650 A, 35 deg at z = 275 mm, red reflected, the measured MODS1 curve: 178 A 10-90% width), the red fold (AOI 38 deg), the collimators (R = 6900 mm paraboloids, f = 3450 mm, 342.65 mm off axis, colTilt 2.855 deg, 230 mm beams), the camera-collimator angle (30 deg), the collimator-to-grating (3600 mm red, 3580 mm blue) and grating-to-camera (720 mm) distances, both as-built gratings (400 and 250 /mm, blaze, ruled 408 x 306 mm), the cameras (decentered Maksutov-Schmidt, 700 mm), the e2v 3072 x 8192 15 um CCDs, the coverage, the dispersions (0.515 and 0.845 A/px) and the R at 0.6 arcsec (1850 and 2300). Every earlier MEASURED or SCHEMATIC layout number is now SOURCED or DERIVED from the .seq files.

**Gaps that matter:** (1) the as-built red grating's incidence (19.5 deg, WG) cannot be tested: the Code V files model 550 and 330 /mm design gratings, not the delivered 400 and 250 /mm; lamC 7600 A (20.64 deg) is entered for the red grating, INFERRED from the R reference wavelength and the red file's design reference 764.1 nm; (2) the blue camera's 718.7 mm is EFFECTIVE (fitted to the 0.120 arcsec/px pixel scale; the printed value is 700 mm); (3) the Pogge et al. 2010 and 2012 SPIE papers and the MODS1 Commissioning Report could not be obtained (named under NOT FOUND); (4) no average slitlet count (nObj 24 INFERRED).

**Status:** in operation (MODS1 since 2011, MODS2 since 2014-2015). No '(design)' tag.

## Conflicts between sources

- Red grating angle of incidence: 19.5 deg (WG, as built) vs the grating-equation centering. NOT SETTLED by the Code V files: they model a 330 /mm red grating (alpha 22.5 deg, 764 nm) and a 550 /mm blue one, not the delivered 250 and 400 /mm. At the fixed 30 deg camera-collimator angle (BO00, PDR), 250 /mm and 19.5 deg put 6060 A on the camera axis (and the 8192-px chip, 0.845 A/px, would then span 2900-9400 A), but WG's table gives a center of 8000 A (nominal), the reference wavelength is 7600 A, the red-only range is 5000-10000 A (MAN Table 1) and 20 deg was the design angle for the 673 nm blaze (PDR: sin 20 - sin 10 = 0.168 = 673 A/4000 A). The blue grating is self-consistent (19.76 deg centers 4000 A). RESOLVED for the preset by taking lamC = 7600 A (WG's R reference wavelength and the red file's design reference 764.1 nm; 20.64 deg); the listed angle may refer to a different zero. A Code-V sequence file (mods_red_v7glb.seq) would decide it.
- Resolving power of the channels in Rothberg et al. 2016: R16 Table 1 ('2300 (blue)', '1850 (red)') and Sec. 3.2 (garbled by pdftotext; read from the page: 'R ~ 2300, and 1850 ... blue and red, respectively') print 2300 for the BLUE channel and 1850 for the RED; WG, MAN, LG and WS give 1850 blue (400 /mm) and 2300 red (250 /mm). The latter follow the grating dispersions (0.515 and 0.845 A/px: 4000/(4.25 x 0.515) = 1830, 7600/(3.9 x 0.845) = 2306), so R16 is transposed.
- Pixels per 0.6 arcsec slit and design R: the PDR design gave 4.58-4.6 px (R 1636 blue, 1727 red at 0.6 arcsec; 'Resolving power at 4 pixels' 2100, 1990), SPIE 2006 quotes R = 1640 and 1730 (4 pixels), while WG gives 4.25 (blue) and 3.90 (red) px and R = 1850 and 2300 (as built). The model's geometry gives 4.78 px (blue) and 4.62 px (red) per 0.6 arcsec slit, matching the PDR design, not the as-built figures. The as-built R is 12-15% above the geometric value; no document says how the as-built R was measured (arc FWHM or the 4-pixel form).
- Plate scale: RESOLVED by the sequence files: EFL 123,765 mm gives 1.6666 arcsec/mm and 360.02 um for 0.6 arcsec (WS 360 um); 8.4 m x f/15 would give 1.637. The remaining difference is the pixel scale of the blue camera, 0.120 vs the red 0.123 (blue effective camera focal length 718.7 mm, not published; WO and PDR print 700 mm for both).
- Dichroic cross-over: 5500 A (design, P06, BO00), 5650 A (MODS1 as built, MAN, LD, WS 565 nm; R16 565 nm), 5630 A (MODS2, LD), 5700 A (MAN Sec. 2 'dichroic cross-over region (~5700Å)' and 'Basic Parameters: ~5700Å'). The arms.js cut uses 5650 A.
- Dichroic transition: 500 A (design: 'over 50 nm band'), 178 A (10-90%, measured on the MODS1 curve), 'a small (~50 angstrom) region of reduced signal-to-noise' (LD), R16 '~0.005 um centered' (50 A). Different definitions of the notch; the 10-90% width is used.
- Prism apex: 30 deg (WG 'Angle: 30°'), 8 deg (P06 Sec. 3.4 and R16 Sec. 3.2 'double-pass 8 deg glass prism'). Not used (prisms are not modeled).
- Wavelength range upper edge: 1000 nm (design, BO00), 1050 nm (WS, R16, MAN Table 1 for the blue/red-only), 1100 nm (WS 'Nominal Wavelength range', 'Imaging Performance'). Not scored.
- Camera anamorphic factor: 1.2 in the 2000-2001 design papers (BO00 Sec. 4.8, PDR Sec. 2.3.8; used to give f/2.5 in the dispersion direction) vs 1.04-1.045 for the delivered gratings at 30 deg (WG, PDR Table 2.2). The grating geometry gives 1.046; 1.2 is the design allowance, not a grating property.
- Blue detector: 'standard 16 micron' silicon (WD, P06) vs '15-micron thick' (LDET); the 16 um is used (15 um is the pixel pitch, likely a typo in LDET).
- Number of facility masks: 9 fixed slots (WS, MAN) vs 12 (LM, current). Not used.
- Spectral pixels: WG lists 5700 (red) and 5200 (blue) 'Spectral Pixels' for the nominal ranges 5800-10000 and 3200-5800 A: 4200 A / 0.845 = 4970 and 2600 / 0.515 = 5050, not the listed values. Not used.

## Notes

- Era and status. Both MODS are in operation; the presets represent the as-built MODS1/MODS2 dual-grating mode with the e2v CCD231-68 detectors (MODS1 was commissioned with 4096 x 4096 STA0500A CCDs, P06). The LBTO detector page records a controller upgrade (Archon, September 2025) that changed readout only. The layout numbers (positions, 30 deg, 720 mm, 3600 mm, dichroic 35 deg at 275 mm, fold 38 deg) come from the 2000-2001 Code V files.
- Two physical instruments, same optics: MODS1 (left, SX) and MODS2 (right, DX); the dichroic cross-over differs by 20 A (5650 vs 5630 A, LD). They can run binocularly (WH).
- Aperture. The explorer's D is 8.251 m, not 8.4 m: the sequence files' f/15 beam is limited by a 914.2 mm secondary at 13,713 mm from the focus (EFL 123,765 mm, f/15.000 on 8251 mm), the primary being 8408 mm ('NB used diameter is less'). With D = 8.251 and F = 15 the model's slit is 360.0 um for 0.6 arcsec (published 360 um), its beam is 230 mm (published), and the red pixel scale is 0.1232 arcsec/px (published 0.123). With 8.4 m the beam would be 234 mm and the plate scale 1.637 arcsec/mm. The page's drawn aperture and the pupil-limited R scale with D.
- What the explorer cannot represent: the double-pass prism mode and imaging mode; the tilting grating turret; the active IMCS flexure compensation; the curved slit-mask shell; the dichroic's wiggles and cylindrical rear surface; the decentered camera's astigmatic f/3.0 and f/2.5 (the f-numbers are not printed in the sequence files; WO and PDR give them).
- Beam size and gratings: the 230 mm beam at 19.8 deg incidence has a 245 mm footprint, inside the 408 mm ruled length (gLen 408): no overfill.
- Independent tests that did not use the published R: dispersion 0.5134 (blue, with the fitted 718.7 mm camera) and 0.8457 (red) A/px against 0.515 and 0.845; red pixel scale 0.1232 against 0.123; slit 360.0 um; beam 230.0 mm; all within 0.3%. The blue camera's 718.7 mm is fitted to the pixel scale 0.120 (EFFECTIVE); its agreement with the blue dispersion is the independent check. The model's geometric R (1629 blue, 1945 red at 7600 A) is 12% and 15% below the as-built 1850 and 2300 (see Conflicts); not tuned.
- Circularity: the blue grating's incidence angle (19.76 deg) and the chip center (4000 A) are one fact. The red lamC = 7600 A is the reference wavelength of the as-built R and also the design reference of the red sequence file (764.1 nm, REF 3), but the red incidence at that center (20.64 deg) is not the 19.5 deg of WG.
- Setting names: 'blue, G400L', 'red, G670L' (11 characters).
- SPIE papers: the 2000 (BO00) and 2006 (P06) papers are OSU copies; only the 2010 and 2012 SPIE papers (NOT FOUND) and the Commissioning Report are missing.
