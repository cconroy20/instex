# Round 10 (2026-11-18): KMOS, the K-band Multi-Object Spectrograph of the VLT (UT1), id `kmos` (revised with Tecza et al. 2006)

KMOS (consortium UK ATC / Durham / Oxford / MPE / USM / ESO; first light 21 Nov 2012, in community service since Oct 2013, still in service: ESO manual Issue 6.1.1 of 2024-07). Same rules as rounds 1-9 (data/presets_research.md): every number comes from a document saved unmodified under `rawdata/presets/kmos/`; each value carries the source key, location and a short verbatim quote; **DERIVED** marks arithmetic done here from sourced values (shown); **EFFECTIVE** marks a value chosen so that the explorer reproduces published performance where the prescription is unpublished (fit and inputs shown); **INFERRED** marks a reading that is not stated; **MEASURED** marks a value read from a figure; **NOT FOUND** marks a gap; **SCHEMATIC** marks a layout element with no source. Quotes were machine-checked (73 quote pieces, 0 failures) against `pdftotext -layout`, `-raw` and default-order text of each PDF page and the tag-stripped text of each HTML page, with whitespace, case, quotes, dashes, minus signs, ligatures and hyphenation normalized (punctuation other than . , / % + < > = ~ is dropped on both sides); " ... " joins non-adjacent pieces (also where pdftotext loses a multiplication sign). Every source is a text PDF or HTML page (no scans). Table quotes join the table rows in reading order. Machine-readable twin: `data/round10/kmos.json`.

**What changed in this revision.** Tecza et al. 2006 (T06) gives the spectrograph prescription: collimator f = 1000 mm and tilt 4.5 deg, input beam F/16 along the slit and F/44.7 in the spectral direction, camera f = 145 mm (F/4.6 spectral), camera-collimator angle 32 deg, grating angles 30-47 deg and 200-500 lines/mm, a scaled layout (Fig. 1). The earlier EFFECTIVE optics (f_col 1275, f_cam 185 from an unsaved web snippet, W 35, psi 20) are DROPPED; the snippet is not used anywhere. What T06 is: the 2006 preliminary-design (PDR) prescription; the as-built numbers are not published in an open paper, but every sourced T06 value is confirmed by an independent as-built number (see Published performance). The one thing still fitted is the per-band groove density, because T06 gives only the 200-500 /mm range. Tecza et al. 2004 (SPIE 5492, 1395) was not supplied and is not needed for the preset.

## Completeness summary

**Complete:** the feed (UT1 Nasmyth, 8000 mm pupil at f/15, 582 um/arcsec, 7.2 arcmin patrol field); pickoff arms; the IFUs (2.8 x 2.8 arcsec, 14 slices of 0.2 arcsec, 14 spaxels per slice, 2:1 anamorphic fore-optics); the pseudo-slit (254 mm; 112 slitlets per spectrograph; 18 px pitch, 13.6 px wide); the spectrograph input beam (F/16 and F/44.7); the fold (30 x 400 mm), the collimator (single toroidal mirror, 50 x 330 mm, f = 1000 mm, tilt 4.5 deg, OFF-AXIS), the grating type (reflective, order -2; 2nd order as built for IZ/YJ/H/K, 1st for HK), the grating size (120 x 60 mm) and the ranges of its angle (30-47 deg) and density (200-500 /mm), the camera (six lenses of CaF2, E-SF03, CaF2, E-SF03, fused silica, ZnSe; f = 145 mm; F/4.6 spectral, F/2.3 spatial; 32 deg to the collimated beam), the detector (HAWAII-2RG, 2048 x 2048, 18 um); coverage, dispersion (nm/px) and measured R per band (ESO manual Tables 3-6); and a scaled layout figure (T06 Fig. 1, with a 100 mm bar), from which the element distances are MEASURED.

**Not found:** the per-band groove density and blaze (T06 gives only the 200-500 /mm and 30-47 deg ranges, and the as-built gratings may differ from the 2006 design), the grating-to-camera and collimator distances as numbers (MEASURED from the figure only), the detector's tilt (if any). **Conflicts:** slit length 254 vs 256 mm; the wheel (5 vs 6 positions); IZ dispersion 0.143 nm/px (Table 6) vs 0.154 from the coverage; the 2006 vs as-built design status; the manual's pinhole-image statements. **Agreement:** f_cam / f_col = 0.145 equals chip / slit (36.864 / 254 = 0.1451); the 2.8-px slit image of T06 equals 0.2 arcsec x 8 m x 44.7 x 0.145 / 18 um; the camera's F/4.6 equals 145 mm / (22.37 mm x cos beta / cos alpha) for the fitted H and K gratings; the fitted gratings fall inside T06's 30-47 deg and 200-500 /mm ranges.

## Optical layout, from the published figure (T06 Fig. 1, PDF p. 2)

Scale: the figure carries a 100 mm bar, 92 px at 200 dpi of the rendered page (0.92 px/mm), checked on the slit rectangle, which measures 238 px = 259 mm against the sourced 254-256 mm (+2 percent). Positions read to about 3 percent (+-10 px). Element order, as drawn:

1. The **slit** (drawn as the long horizontal rectangle, MEASURED 259 mm, sourced 254 mm) lies below the **flat fold mirror** (SOURCED: "The input slit from the IFU is below the flat fold mirror on the XY plane"); the fold turns the beam (90 deg, INFERRED from the slit being out of the drawing plane) into the XY plane, along +X. 2. The beam runs about 730 mm in X to the **toroidal collimator** at the right of the figure (MEASURED from the fold's position to the collimator; the rest of the 1000 mm focal length is the slit-to-fold leg out of the plane, INFERRED). 3. The collimator returns the beam, deflected by **8.6 deg** (MEASURED; 2 x 4.5 deg = 9 deg expected, so the 4.5 deg tilt is the mirror's tilt and the explorer's colTilt = 4.5), toward the **grating** at the far left: **collimator-to-grating path 900 mm** (MEASURED: 829 px / 0.92). The returned beam passes above the slit rectangle (clear of it). 4. The **grating** (disk, drawn nearly edge-on) sends the beam up and to the right toward the **camera** (six lenses in a barrel: CaF2, E-SF03, CaF2, E-SF03, fused silica, ZnSe, SOURCED): the camera axis makes **33 deg** with the incoming collimated beam (MEASURED; sourced 32 deg). First lens about **230 mm** from the grating (MEASURED), the detector about 555 mm past the grating (MEASURED). 5. The bench is flat; the Nasmyth axis is normal to the page. The grating wheel and detector mount are shown in T06 Figs. 11-12 (not scaled).

**On-axis / off-axis verdict (deploy gate): OFF-AXIS.** T06 Sec. 2.1: "The collimator mirror is a single toroidal optical element ... tilted at 4.5º to the incoming beam"; MAN Sec. 2.2.3, p. 7: "Each spectrograph uses a single off-axis toroidal mirror to collimate the incoming light". The slit is below the plane and the returned beam passes it. Set colTilt 4.5 (convention below), not 0.

**colTilt convention.** The explorer's colTilt is the collimator mirror's tilt (half the deflection of the beam); presets.js header and data/round9/neid.md: a 5 deg off-axis (deflection) angle is colTilt 2.5, DEIMOS's 5.7 deg between chief rays is colTilt -2.8. T06 says the mirror is "tilted at 4.5º to the incoming beam": read as the mirror's tilt (normal to the beam), so colTilt = 4.5 and the deflection is 9 deg. The figure confirms it: the returned beam is 8.6 deg off the incoming one. If instead 4.5 deg were the deflection, colTilt would be 2.25; the figure excludes that.

## Conflicts between sources

- **Slit length:** 254 mm (M10, final design) vs 256 mm (S07, preliminary design); the figure's slit measures 259 mm (+2 percent against 254). Used 254 mm for the f_cam / f_col check.
- **Grating wheel:** 5-position wheel (MAN Sec. 2.2.3, INST, M10, S07 'turret') vs 6-position (S14 Sec. 2.4; T06 Sec. 4.1: 'any one of 6 gratings'). The wheel has six positions: five gratings and a blank (MAN) (INFERRED). T06's six gratings (I/z, J, H, K plus two combined) differ from the as-built five (IZ, YJ, H, K, HK, MAN).
- **2006 design vs as built:** T06 describes the PDR design (May 2006). Every T06 number that can be checked against the as-built instrument agrees (f_cam / f_col, the 2.8 px slit image, F/4.6, the grating angle and density ranges against the fitted gratings), but the as-built prescription is not published in an open paper, so any T06 value could have changed in the final design (FDR April 2008, M10 Table 2).
- **IZ dispersion:** Table 6 gives 0.143 nm/px; the IZ coverage of Table 4 (0.779-1.094 um) over 2048 px gives 0.154 nm/px (7 percent larger). The other four bands agree to 1 percent. With the sourced optics, the preset follows Table 6 (G 429 /mm, alpha 40.7 deg, R 3035) because the coverage value gives a lower R (2763 at G 401, alpha 39 deg) and misses the measured 3406 by 19 percent instead of 11 percent; the coverage span then misses by 8 percent. The conflict stays open.
- **Pinhole image:** MAN: FWHM '< 1 pixel (0.2 arcsec) ... and about 1.3 pixels (0.25 arcsec)' gives 0.2 arcsec and 0.19 arcsec per px, the along-slit scale (13.6 px for 2.8 arcsec = 0.206 arcsec per px). It cannot refer to the across-slice direction, where a 0.2 arcsec slice is 2 px (0.1 arcsec per px). Read as loosely worded (INFERRED).
- **Slit image and line width:** T06 convolves a 2.8-px top-hat slit (before the grating's de-magnification); MAN measures a 2-px FWHM. These are consistent once the grating factor (cos alpha / cos beta = 0.68-0.86) is applied: 2.8 x 0.71 = 2.0 at alpha 45 deg, beta 15 deg.
- **R, baseline vs as-built:** DEV baseline R~3200/3400/4000/4200/1800 and M10 3327/3387/3840/3766/2052 vs MAN as-built 3406/3582/4045/4227/1985; T06: 'a resolving power exceeding 3200'. The test uses Table 6 (measured).

## Notes

- **Era:** KMOS has been in service on UT1 (Antu) since Oct 2013 (S14 abstract) and is still offered (ESO manual Issue 6.1.1, 2024-07-04; ESO pages retrieved 2026-11-18). One configuration throughout. Not under construction: not a design preset.
- **What the explorer cannot represent:** (1) the 2:1 anamorphic IFU fore-optics: one F serves both directions, set for the spectral one (F 44.7), so the along-slit scale reads 0.072 arcsec per px against 0.2 arcsec per spaxel, and the camera's F/2.3 spatial speed is not shown; (2) the slitlet structure: 112 slitlets of 13.6 px with 4.4 px gaps along 2048 px, and the slit and spectrum curvature (about 1 mm, 60 px; T06 Table 1); (3) the three spectrographs differ slightly (coverage offsets up to 14 nm, MAN Tables 4-5), the preset uses spectrograph B; (4) coverage varies with the IFU's place on the chip (Table 4 center vs Table 5 edge); (5) second-order overlap: the order-sorting filters (filter wheel at the IFU entrance, M10 Table 3) isolate each band and are entered as `filt`; (6) the 24 pickoff arms, K-mirrors and the cold stop; (7) the detector-grating ghost (T06 Sec. 3.6: 1e-4; MAN: below 1 percent).
