## Completeness summary

**Stations (8)**: ALMA (37 x 12 m phased, 73 m effective), APEX 12 m, JCMT 15 m, LMT 50 m (32.5 m used in 2017), PV 30 m, SMA (6 x 6 m phased, 14.7 m), SMT 10 m, SPT 10 m (6 m illuminated). Geocentric X, Y, Z, latitude, longitude and GRS80 height from P2 Table 2; the XYZ convert to the tabulated geodetic values to <= 2.2 m. The SR1 uvfits AN table (as correlated) matches to 0.06 m (ALMA 2.2 m).

**SEFD at 230 GHz**: median 2017 values, P3 Table 2: ALMA 74, PV 6900, LMT 4500, APEX 4800, SMA 6400, JCMT 10500, SMT 17100, SPT 19300 Jy (P2 Table 3 alternative values: APEX 4700, PV 1900, SMA 6200 Jy).

**Baselines (DERIVED)**: longest among the stations that see M87, JCMT-PV 10908 km (24.7 uas fringe spacing at 229.1 GHz; P4 Table 1: 24.8 uas); with the SPT, PV-SPT 11389 km; shortest SMA-JCMT 163 m and ALMA-APEX 2.6 km.

**Bands**: two 2-GHz bands centered at 227.1 and 229.1 GHz (P3), 32 Gbps, 2-bit; 4 GHz per polarization. **Primary beam**: only APEX stated (27.1 arcsec at 228.1 GHz, P2; 1.20 lambda/D DERIVED); irrelevant for M87*.

**Elevation limits**: published for ALMA (20 deg, the 2017 M87 scheduling limit, P3), SMT (~25 deg toward M87, behind the LBT, P4), PV (9 deg) and LMT (0 deg) mechanical (SCHED); APEX, JCMT, SMA NOT FOUND, replaced by the floor of the lowest M87 elevation among the SR1 detections (20, 12, 16 deg; DERIVED). M87 never rises at the SPT.

**Observing pattern**: M87 on April 5, 6, 11 (target) and 10 (seven calibrator scans), scans of 3-7 min interleaved with 3C 279, each track from LMT rise until M87 sets below 20 deg at ALMA (P3); published schedule (P3 Fig. 2) and uv-coverage figures (P1 Fig. 2, P2 Fig. 11, P3 Fig. 12, P4 Fig. 1). From the released SR1 data (DERIVED): April 11 detections 0.53-7.52 h UTC; ALMA/APEX the whole track, PV until 5.0 h, SMT from 2.0 h, LMT from 2.4 h, JCMT from 4.2 h, SMA from 4.7 h.

**M87* ring model**: P1 Table 1: diameter 42 +- 3 uas, width < 20 uas, contrast > 10:1, PA 150-200 deg east of north (brighter in the south); compact flux 0.66 (+0.16/-0.10) Jy (P4). Adopted analytic image: the P4 Appendix C.1 crescent, a thin ring of radius 22 uas with I0 [1 + 2A cos(theta - eta)], A = 0.23, eta = 150 deg, I0 = 0.55 Jy, blurred by a 10-uas Gaussian, plus the 0.6-Jy three-Gaussian jet of P4 Table 10; P6 Table 3 gives per-day crescent fits (April 11: d = 42.4 uas, f_w = 0.34, phi = 198.1 deg, 0.49 Jy).
