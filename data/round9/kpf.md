## Round 9 completeness summary

**Keck Planet Finder** (W. M. Keck Observatory, Keck I; installed late 2022 and in regular use from 2023): the optical prescription is nearly complete. The as-built paper (Gibson et al. 2024, an accepted-manuscript copy on NSF PAR) gives the layout (Fig. 3), Table 1 (collimators 1600 and 1280 mm, 200 mm beam, R4 mosaic echelle 31.6 /mm with theta_B = 75.96 deg, dichroic 590 nm, cross-dispersers 810 and 450 /mm VPH, cameras 495 and 492 mm, 4080 x 4080 of 15 um), the reformatter (3 slices, f/3.7 fibers, f/8 slit), the FIU (f/15 to f/3.7) and the measured R = 95,000-97,000. The 2018 preliminary-design paper adds the 8 deg collimator off-axis angle, the two-sub-grating echelle mosaic and the 7.5-to-8 deg change. The Keck pages add the VPH angles, the detector table, the status and (through the pipeline's L1 format) the order counts 35 and 32.

**Gaps that matter.** (1) The echelle gamma is not published (about 1 deg is read by eye from Fig. 3, low confidence). (2) The science slice width is not given as a number: the sky and calibration masks are 64 um wide 'the same slit width' as the science orderlets (INFERRED, supported by the exposure meter taking the leftover outer edges of the octagon); at the f/8 slit this is 138 um and the slit-limited R is 92,500, 5 percent below the measured 95,000-97,000. (3) The dichroic's transition width and angle of incidence are not published (angle MEASURED on Fig. 3, 31 +-4 deg). (4) The grism prism apex angles ('shallow') and the VPH angle in air or in glass are not published, so the explorer's cross-dispersers are EFFECTIVE (the physical line densities give the right order spacing to 1-3 percent). (5) Fig. 3 has no length scale (calibrated here on the 840 mm echelle). (6) The SPIE papers of 2016, 2022 (FIU) and 2018 (double scrambler) have no free copy found (listed in NOT FOUND); two Keck web values are stale (science fiber 250 um, calibration fiber 50 um).

**On/off-axis verdict: OFF-AXIS.** The collimators are off-axis paraboloids ('an off-axis paraboloid'; off-axis angle 8 deg; decenter 237 mm); the reformatter exit stands beside the echelle and the returned beam goes to a fold mirror beside it, not back to the slit. colTilt 4 deg (INFERRED: half the 8 deg off-axis angle, the DEIMOS convention).

**Recommended presets: one line 'Keck I / KPF' with Settings 'green' and 'red' (and the All-arms group).** Both are the as-built instrument as in routine use (the web pages say Era 4.0 since late October 2025; the instrument has been OFFLINE since April 2026 after a vacuum pump failure, KSTAT, saved 2026-11-18, so the note should say so), single slice-channel recipe: slitW 0.326 arcsec, nObj 5.

## Optical layout (from Fig. 3, p. 7 of G24; G18's layout figure carries the same element labels, and the Keck 3D figure KIMG3 shows the same arrangement)

Fig. 3 of Gibson et al. 2024 is a plan view of the bench (it is not to scale, with no scale bar; the 3D Keck figure KIMG3 is the same layout in perspective). Scale used for every MEASURED value: **0.504 px/mm on a 300 dpi render, from the 840 mm echelle substrate = 423 px** (the two sub-gratings are 207 and 216 px, the seam at the midpoint). A check: the primary collimator's face is 806 px = 1600 mm from the seam, equal to the SOURCED 'one primary collimator focal length'. Orientation below: the figure's left, right, top and bottom.

Element order along the light (SOURCED from the text of Sec. 3.3 unless marked):

1. **Fibers** (the science, sky and calibration fibers, after the scramblers) enter at the lower left and end at the **reformatter** (lower left, below the echelle's left end; the triplet, the slicing and masking mirrors, the pupil mirrors and two relay mirrors, Fig. 11 of G24). It forms the f/8 slit-plane image: five orderlets (three science slices, sky, calibration). The reformatter output is rotated (clocked) 2.13 deg.
2. A beam diverging at f/8 goes right to the **primary collimator** (an off-axis paraboloid, f = 1600 mm; at the far right of the figure, face 456 mm tall and 302 mm wide, decenter 237 mm). The line from the reformatter exit to the collimator makes about 6 deg with the collimated axis (MEASURED; the 8 deg off-axis angle is SOURCED in G18). The collimated 200 mm beam travels left along the bench to the **echelle**.
3. The **R4 echelle mosaic** (840 x 214 x 125 mm; two sub-gratings; its long axis lies along the beam, seen edge on, centered 1600 mm from the collimator, SOURCED). It is 'rotated slightly out of plane' (gamma, unpublished).
4. The diffracted beams return to the primary collimator (second reflection) and form an **intermediate focus** beside the reformatter; a small **fold flat** just before the focus (to the left of the reformatter exit, at the left end of the lower beam) turns the beam about 166 deg back to the right (AOI about 7 deg, MEASURED).
5. The **secondary collimator** (off-axis paraboloid, f = 1280 mm, at the lower right; 149 mm decenter) re-collimates it into a 160 mm monochromatic beam that **converges to the white pupil**.
6. The **dichroic** (lower center, about 31 deg AOI, MEASURED) reflects the green channel upward (toward the top of the figure, leaving about 120 deg from the incoming direction) to the green **VPH grism** at the pupil and the green camera (top right, camera axis 9 deg from the vertical, MEASURED); it transmits the red channel straight on to the **red fold mirror** (lower left of the dichroic; a flat turning the beam about 90 deg, MEASURED) and up to the red grism and camera (top left, axis 12 deg from the vertical, MEASURED).
7. Each arm: grism (fused-silica prism bonded to a VPH grating, yawed 3.9 deg green and 4.8 deg red to the camera axis), a five-element Petzval camera (about 0.5 m, Fig. 4) and a 4080 x 4080 CCD at the top.

Tags: the element order, focal lengths, beam sizes, the 1600 mm collimator-to-echelle distance, the 8 deg off-axis angle and the yaws are SOURCED; the fold, dichroic and red-fold angles, the camera-axis tilts and the 6 deg reformatter-to-collimator angle are MEASURED (+-3 to 4 deg); the separations along the bench other than 1600 mm are not recorded (the intermediate focus to the secondary collimator measures 1340 mm, 5 percent above the sourced 1280 mm).

White-pupil mapping for the explorer: transfer collimator = the secondary collimator mirror, wpT = 1280 mm; the cross-disperser stands at the white pupil in each arm after the dichroic (as in HARPS and ESPRESSO, with the secondary collimator playing HARPS's second pass); lay3d 'wp'; fold3d for the intermediate-focus flat (a turn of about 166 deg) and, in the red arm, the red fold mirror. The asymmetric ratio 0.8 is carried in the effective camera focal length (row cam_eff).

## Published performance (validation targets)

- R = 95,000-97,000, MEASURED from the broadband laser frequency comb lines (a gaussian plus top-hat fit; central science slice SCI2; the red arm uniform, the green arm down-turned at the blue end of each order), G24 Sec. 6.1 and Fig. 22 caption; median 98,000 (R23 abstract); nominal ~98,000 (G24 Table 1). The model's slit-limited Rs = 92,500 is GEOMETRIC (DERIVED above); expect a ~4 percent miss against 96,000 (the top-hat convolved with the camera PSF), reported as an info line.
- Sampling 3.4-3.7 pixels FWHM in the dispersion direction (G24 Fig. 22 caption; Table 1 '3 to 4 pixels'). The model's slit image is 138 um x 495/1600 = 43 um = 2.9 pixels, a top-hat without the PSF, so expect npix below the measurement.
- Coverage: green 443-599 nm, red 599-871 nm (G24 Table 1); orders 35 and 32 (KDF L1 arrays); DERIVED order numbers 103-137 and 71-102 (+-1).
- Order spacing check (DERIVED, row xd_eff): about 118 px (green, 520 nm) and 125 px (red, 735 nm) between orders.
- Throughput ~10 percent peak in median seeing (G24 Sec. 7); stability < 10 cm/s over two weeks (G24 Sec. 6.3).
- Detector read noise 4.3 e- (red), ~10 e- (green) (KSTAT, 2025 October); 47 s readout (KDET).

- Simultaneous calibration fiber: 120 um octagonal masked 64 x 64 (G24) against 100 um (G18) against 50 um circular (Keck table).
- Reformatter input triplet: x8 (G24) against x10 (G18).
- Green VPH: 810 lines/mm (G24 Table 1, G18, Keck VPH table) against 800 (Keck page text).
- VPH angle of incidence: design central incidence -30.5 / -24.4 deg (Keck table) against -29.5 / -25.4 deg (Keck text, the chosen values).
- Dichroic: 590 nm (G24 Table 1) against 593 nm (Keck page text).
- Cameras: f/2.8 and 495/492 mm (G24) against f/2.9 and 495/495 mm (G18, Keck table); largest lens 235 mm (G24) against 230 mm (G18) and 210/240 mm (Keck table).
- Coverage: 443-599 / 599-871 nm (G24 Table 1) against 445-600 / 600-870 nm (G24 abstract, Keck text 445-870) against 440-590 / 590-850 nm (the Keck table).
- Primary collimator decenter: 237 mm (G24) against 240.5 mm (G18); off-axis angle 8 deg (G18) against 8.5 deg from the 237 mm decenter (DERIVED).
