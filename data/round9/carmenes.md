## Completeness summary

**Complete from the open literature:** the telescope and focal ratio (f/10), the fiber set (two 100 um octagonal-core fibers per channel, 1.5 arcsec, f/3.9 in, f/3.5 out), the image slicer (two slices, four traces), the FN system (f/3.5 to f/10), the collimator (off-axis paraboloid, f/10.274 and f/10.218, beams of 154.8 and 153.3 mm, three passes), the R4 echelle mosaic (31.6 /mm, 75.2 deg, 154 x 596 mm), both grisms (LF5 223 /mm 17.8 deg; Infrasil 81 /mm 13.85 deg), both cameras (455 mm f/2.94; 548 mm f/3.53), the detectors (CCD231-84 4096^2 of 15 um; two H2RG 2048^2 of 18 um), the dichroic's cut (0.96 um) and which channel it reflects, and the measured R (94,600 and 80,400). TP18 (the physical model, VIS only) adds the chip's orders (53 to 116), the fiber pitch (0.346 mm), the as-built Zerodur mirrors, the camera as five lenses plus a toroidal field flattener and the catalog-index treatment (fit residuals under half a pixel). Two published layout figures give the distances (MEASURED).

**Gaps that matter:** (1) no document gives the dichroic's transition width or angle of incidence (INFERRED 300 A, 45 deg); (2) the echelle's out-of-plane tilt gamma and the as-built angle are NOT published even in Tala Pinto et al. 2018 (TP18; it fits 22 parameters, including the echelle orientation, but tabulates no fitted value): gamma 0.6 deg stays INFERRED; (3) the NIR detector gap is not given; (4) no source states a temperature treatment for the cold Infrasil index (the model's choice makes no difference); (5) the NIR model R (93,300) is 16% above the measured 80,400; TP18 models the VIS channel only, gives no cause, and states the NIR R as 82,000: unexplained and not tuned.

**Status:** in regular operation since 2016-01-01 (S16); the CAHA page of 2026-11-18 lists CARMENES+ and CARMENES Legacy+. No '(design)' tag.

## Conflicts between sources

- Resolving power, VIS: 82,000 (design, S12, Q14) vs 93,400 measured (Q16 Sec. 2.3; S16 Sec. 4.2) vs 94,600 (Q18, R18, Ri23, C25, W1, W2). The three are the same quantity at three dates; 94,600 is the value in current use and is the validation target. S16 says the measurement exceeds the 82,000 requirement because 'the Echelle angle [is] slightly different from the design value'; an angle of 76 deg (R4) would raise 2 tan(delta) by only 5.6%, so that does not explain 15%; the model's geometric FWHM of the half-fiber slice gives 94,000-95,000 (VIS) with the design angle, i.e. the same value as measured.
- Resolving power, NIR: 82,000 (design) vs 81,800 (Q16) vs 80,400 (Q18, R18, Ri23, C25, W1, W2). The model gives 93,300 with the same slit as the VIS channel, 16% above the measured value. Nothing in the sources explains why the NIR channel measures near the design while the VIS channel measures 15% above it. Not tuned: the explorer will show an info line.
- Early concept (Q10, 2009): NIR R = 85,000 in 31 orders with an R2.9 echelle and two S-NPH2 prisms at -30 C; VIS R = 60,000 in 42 orders. Superseded by the 2012 final design (R4, grisms, R = 82,000).
- Mean sampling: 2.8 pixels (design, S12, Q14 VIS, Q18 VIS, C25 both channels) vs 2.5 pixels (S16 VIS measured mean, minimum 2.2 at the blue end; Q14 and Q18 give 2.5 for the NIR channel). Model: 2.88 (VIS), 2.44 (NIR).
- Number of orders: VIS 53 (design, 550-1050 nm; S12, S16) vs 55 (C25, as operated); NIR 29 (S12) vs 28 (C25). Order numbers from sourced values: 520-960 nm is orders 118-64 (55 orders); 950-1700 nm is 64-36 (29).
- Collimator substrate: fused silica (VIS) and aluminum 6061-T6 (NIR) in the 2012 design (S12 Sec. 2.2) vs Zerodur for the collimator, echelle and fold mirror (TP18 Sec. 2, as built; C25). RESOLVED as Zerodur; drawing and model are unaffected.
- Resolving power, NIR, a fourth value: TP18 Sec. 2 prints a measured R = 82,000 (and 0.91-1.7 um) for the NIR channel, against 81,800 (Q16) and 80,400 (Q18 on). The paper offers no cause for the model excess of 16% (it models the VIS channel only, and its VIS R is 93,400, the 2016 value).
- VIS orders: TP18 Table 1 prints '550 - 1050 nm, orders 53 to 116'. By m = 611,880 A / lambda those orders span 527-1155 nm, so they are the chip's orders, not the 550-1050 nm design band (m = 111-58); the model's chip at lamC 7900 holds 57-116.
- Image or pupil slicer: C25 and S12 call it an image slicer (Bowen-Walraven, FEROS type); Q14 Sec. 2.4 writes 'divides their pupils into two slices'. Resolved as an image slicer: the slice width reproduces the design R (Table above); a pupil slicer would halve the beam, not the slit.
- Calibration fibers to the front end: 400 um (Q16, St14) vs 600 um (one sentence of C25, which also says 400 um elsewhere). Not used by the explorer.
- Octagonal fiber length: about 8 m (Q16), 10 m (Q18), 2-3 m (C25). Not used.
- CCD size: 4096 x 4096 (S12, S16, C25, Q16) vs 4112 x 4096 (Q14, Q18; the extra columns are margins, INFERRED). The preset uses 4096 x 4096.
- NIR band edges: 950-1700 nm (design, S12) vs 960-1710 nm (Q16, Q18, R18). The model's chip is wider than both; coverage is the orders' business, not a preset entry.
- Echelle angle: 75.2 deg working angle in the 2012 design (S12 Sec. 2.3; 'effective blaze angle around 75.4 deg'), '75.x deg' as built (S16, digit not printed), 76 deg for the R4 label (C25 Table 2). The preset uses 75.2.
- Pick-up mirror: 'flat 90 deg pick-up mirror' (Q14) vs 'flat 45 deg pick-up mirror' (Q16): the same mirror (a 45 deg tilt turns the beam through 90 deg).
- Tank size: 1.4 m x 3.5 m (Q14 Fig. 1), 1.7 m x 2.7 m (S12 Sec. 3), 1.5 m x 3.0 m (C25). Not used.

## Notes

- Sampling the explorer's slit. The slicer cuts the fiber's IMAGE in two, so the slit the spectrograph sees is half the fiber image: 100 um at f/3.5, re-imaged by the FN system at f/10.274, is 293.5 um across; one slice is 146.8 um. The explorer's fiber convention (the slit of equal line width, (sqrt 3 / 2) x width) gives 127.1 um, i.e. slitW = S3 x 0.100 / (2 x 3.5 x 3500) x 206264.806 = 0.7291 arcsec at D = 3.5 m with F = the collimator's f/10.274 (VIS) or f/10.218 (NIR); the F cancels, so both channels get 0.7291 arcsec. nObj = 4 (2 fibers x 2 slices). On the sky the same slice is (sqrt 3 / 2) x 1.5 / 2 = 0.65 arcsec at the f/3.9 input; the ratio 0.7291 / 0.65 = 1.12 is the f/3.9 to f/3.5 FRD margin. The octagonal cross-section's line profile is approximated by the round-core factor (as for HARPS).
- Self-checks that passed with no tuning: (1) the design R = 2 tan(delta) f_col / w_slice = 82,300 (VIS) and 81,700 (NIR) reproduces the 82,000 requirement; (2) the 2.8 px sampling follows from f_cam / f_col x slice width / pixel (455 / 1590.4 x 146.8 um / 15 um = 2.8 px VIS; 548 / 1566.4 x 145.8 um / 18 um = 2.83 px NIR); (3) the grism undeviated wavelengths come out at the band centers (7842 and 13182 A); (4) the NIR coverage gaps start at 11011 A in the model against 'no gaps between orders up to 1110 nm' (B16 Table 3); (5) the model's VIS R, 94,000-95,000, equals the measured 94,600. The NIR model R, 93,300, does not equal its measured 80,400 (see Conflicts).
- Chip orders: at lamC 7900 the model's VIS chip holds orders 57-116 (60) against TP18's 53 to 116 (64); the design-band orders 64-118 (520-960 nm) span 3320 of 4096 rows. At lamC 7500 it holds 59-125 (67). The NIR chip holds orders 36-65 (30) against 29 published.
- Status. CARMENES has operated since 2016-01-01 (S16); the CAHA page of 2026-11-18 lists CARMENES+ (upgrades: NIR cooling to continuous nitrogen flow in 2023, V25 and C25; new Fabry-Perot etalon April 2024, C25) and CARMENES Legacy+. None of these changes the optics. No '(design)' tag.
- Fiber and vacuum details the explorer does not model: two spectrographs in two vacuum tanks, simultaneous Fabry-Perot calibration in the second fiber, the exposure meter, the fiber shaker (turned off), the cooled NIR bench, the NIR detector gap, octagonal-core scrambling.
- TP18 reading (settled and unsettled): the paper confirms the F/N conversion 'F/3.5 output beam of the fibers into the F/10.2 focal ratio of the collimator', the slicer at the F/N focal plane, f/2.94 and 455 mm for the camera, the Zerodur mirrors, a fiber pitch of 0.346 mm (6.6 px, DERIVED), and catalog room-temperature indices for the VIS glasses. It does not give gamma, theta, the as-built blaze angle, any fitted value, NIR parameters or a cause of the NIR R excess. The fit residuals (better than half a pixel on 1154 lines; 1/1000 px against Zemax) bear on the wavelength solution, not on R.
- lamC for VIS: 7900 puts the chip at orders 57-116 (TP18: 53 to 116, bluest order exact, red end 4 short) but the chip-center column then sits at the blue end of its order (2.10 px, 0.0399 A/px); lamC 7500 gives the mid-order sampling (2.88 px) with orders 59-125. 7900 is recommended for the Detector panel.
- Glass: LF5 is in web/physics.js GLASS. Infrasil: use 'Infrasil-105K' (nearest cryogenic entry); the sources do not state a temperature treatment.
