## Round 9 completeness summary

**HPF is sourced to the level of M14's three paragraphs; the detailed prescription (camera, VPH, relay, layout) is unpublished.** Found and quoted: the telescope and fiber feed (10 m pupil, f/3.65, 36.5 m focal length, 5.65 arcsec/mm; 299 um octagonal telescope fibers = 1.7 arcsec, 312 um circular cryostat fibers; science, sky and calibration fibers, three traces per order; ball-lens double scrambler; the 93 um nickel slit on the fiber puck), **the input optics (CaF2 and SFTM-16) that re-image the f/3.3 fiber output to the f/8 of the off-axis parabolic collimator (so f_col = 8 x 200 mm = 1600 mm, DERIVED)**, the echelle (31.6 /mm mosaic, 200 x 800 mm, **blazed at 74.6 deg as manufactured**, 125 mm Zerodur, gold), the VPH cross-disperser, the asymmetric white pupil, the 8 ft bench, the detector (H2RG 2048 x 2048, 18 um, 1.7 um cut-off, 120 K), the coverage (8080-12780 A, 28 orders; M14 Fig. 1 labels orders 48-74) and R. **Not found:** the camera focal length (EFFECTIVE 362 mm from the 9.5 px slit image), the VPH groove density (EFFECTIVE 210 /mm, from 28 orders over the published coverage), the transfer optics, gamma, the off-axis tilt, and every layout distance (M14 Figs. 2-3 show only the cryostat's exterior and the shield).

**Model check (recommended state): R = 51,300 geometric (design 50,000: +2.6%; 55,000: -7%; 53,000: -3%); 28 orders m = 75 to 48, 8077-12792 A (published 8080-12780; EFFECTIVE fit); slit image 2.71 px wide against N19's 2.5 px; order edges of ME19's orders 72-69 and 61-57 reproduced to within 0.07% with the 74.6 deg blaze (not fitted).**

**Collimator verdict: OFF-AXIS** (SOURCED, 'monolithic off-axis parabolic collimator' M14 Sec. 3, also M12 and M10; 'the f/8 that the off-axis parabola is expecting'; the tilt is not published, so colTilt stays at the automatic off-axis value).

**Varying aperture (HET).** The HET pupil changes with the track from an effective 8.5 m to 5.5 m diameter (K21 footnote 5). As for LAMOST (data/round8/lamost.md), the preset keeps a fixed D = 10 m (the pupil diameter); R does not depend on it (R is set by the 93 um slit, the f/8 collimator and the echelle), and it enters only the arcsec bookkeeping and any collecting-area estimate. The explorer is entered in the virtual-fiber convention (F = 8, the collimator's input ratio, so f_col = 1600 mm), so its F and focal length are not the HET's f/3.65 and 36.5 m: the on-sky plate scale is 5.65 arcsec/mm. A throughput or S/N estimate with D = 10 m overstates the collecting area by 2.0 against the 7 m effective aperture the design papers assume.

**Recommended preset: one line 'HET 10 m / HPF' with one Setting 'HR, 93 µm'** (the only commissioned mode; HE is the same fibers without the slit and is not offered). State: D 10 m, F 8, slit 0.5813 arcsec (93 um x 8/3.3 = 225.5 um in the collimator's focal plane), nObj 3, mirror collimator W 200 mm, f 1600 mm (OFF-AXIS, automatic tilt), lens camera 362 mm (EFFECTIVE), echelle 31.6 /mm at delta 74.6 deg, gamma 0.5 deg (2012 design), gLen 800 mm, VPH 210 /mm (EFFECTIVE) at lambda_c 10500 A, H2RG 2048 x 2048 of 18 um, layLen 1600, wpT 1600 (drawing only).

## Optical layout (from the published figures)

**No scaled layout is published.** M14's Figs. 2-3 (p. 4) are exterior CAD views of the vacuum shell, the radiation shield with its MLI, heater panels and LN2 tank: they show no optical element. The only optical drawing is Fig. 6 of M12 (arXiv page 8), a 2012 concept sketch of the earlier symmetric, grism design, without a scale; M14 supersedes it in the VPH, the asymmetric pupil, the 74.6 deg blaze and the f/8 collimator. Read from M12 Fig. 6 (200 dpi render; elements read by eye; all distances SCHEMATIC):

1. Fibers, then slit (on the puck, f/3.3), then the CaF2 and SFTM-16 input optics (M14), which re-image the fiber faces at f/8 onto the collimator's focal plane (SOURCED for the optics; the position of the slit relative to them INFERRED, see input_optics).
2. **One large off-axis parabola** (the collimator, f/8, 1600 mm DERIVED): the slit stands beside the echelle and well off the mirror's axis (M12 Fig. 6; 'off-axis parabola', M14). Light goes to the mirror and the collimated beam runs to the echelle.
3. **Echelle** (31.6 /mm, blazed 74.6 deg, 200 x 800 mm, drawn as a block split into two equal halves, SCHEMATIC): quasi-Littrow, the dispersed beam returns to the large mirror.
4. A small **spectrum mirror** at the intermediate image folds the light to the large mirror again (M12), which re-collimates it as the white-pupil beam; M14 says the adopted pupil is **asymmetric** (smaller at the VPH), so the 2012 unit-magnification relay is not as built.
5. **VPH cross-disperser** then the **fast refractive camera** at the far end, the detector last (M12 drew a grism; M14 says VPH).

Dimensions: only the 8 ft (2438 mm) bench (M14 Sec. 3) and the ME19 footprint 1.5 m x 3 m are published. Measuring distances on M12 Fig. 6 was tried and rejected (a 1122 mm slit-to-mirror distance, a 110 mm beam: inconsistent). The preset's layLen 1600 mm (the echelle at the collimator's focal distance, as Fig. 6 draws it) is INFERRED and fits the bench. A photograph of the opened spectrograph is ME19 Fig. 1C ('camera optics on the left, echelle grating on the right, and relay mirrors in front'), without a scale.

Order format: M14 Fig. 1 labels orders 48 (top, red) to 74 (bottom, blue) with the three fibers per order; MEASURED spacing 109 px (48-49), 57 px (73-74), average 74 px; the model gives 111, 48 and 73 px with m = 75 to 48 (order 75 cut by the chip edge).

## Conflicts between sources

- Echelle blaze: 74.6 deg as manufactured (M14) against R4 = 75.96 deg (K18's 'R4 echelle') and '75 degrees' (M10, M12). 74.6 deg adopted: it reproduces the published order edges (ME19) to 0.07%, against 0.6-0.7% for 75.96 deg.
- Collimator and input optics: the 2012 paper's f/3.65 fiber feed and 200 mm beam suggested a 730 mm collimator (the earlier version of this record); M14 says the input optics re-image f/3.3 to f/8, so the collimator is f/8 (1600 mm for 200 mm). 2012: 'a 200 mm beam'; 2010: 150 mm (M10); M12 Fig. 6 caption: 150-200 mm.
- Cross-disperser: 150 l/mm grism (M10, 2010), 110 g/mm grism (M12, 2012), VPH (M14 2014, NS 2019). M14 wins.
- Camera: f/2.3 (M10), f/2 (M12), 'fast' with no number (M14). EFFECTIVE 362 mm adopted (2012's f/2 with a 200 mm beam gave 400 mm; the 9.5 px slit image of N19 with the f/8 collimator needs 362 mm).
- White pupil: symmetric single large mirror in the 2012 Fig. 6; 'asymmetric white pupil ... the design adopted for HPF' (M14, NS).
- Resolving power: 50,000 (design: M14, M12, S16), 55,000 (talk NS, KA20 and most science papers), 53,000 (ME19). The explorer's geometric R (51,300) lies within 7% of all three.
- Coverage: 8080-12780 A (KA20), 8170-12780 A (S21), 810-1280 nm (K18, ME19), 0.8-1.27 um (NS), 0.84-1.3 um (C14, design).
- Operating temperature: 200 K design (M12 Sec. 3.4) against 180 K built (M14, S16, K18).

## Notes

- **Fiber convention.** The 93 um mask sits on the fiber puck in the f/3.3 space; the input optics magnify it by 8 / 3.3 = 2.424 to 225.5 um at the collimator's focal plane (INFERRED order of puck, slit and optics; the slit is on the puck, K18). The explorer takes the preset as a slit of 0.5813 arcsec with F = 8 and D = 10: the virtual-fiber convention with F_out = 8, which is M14's f/8. This virtual angle is 1.106 times the on-sky 0.5256 arcsec (93 um at 5.65 arcsec/mm), the factor being 3.65 / 3.3, the fiber's FRD speed-up. The 312 um core overfills the 93 um slit in the dispersion direction, so HR mode clips most of the fiber's light (INFERRED; the HE mode, without the slit, is not commissioned: K18 Abstract).
- **What the explorer cannot represent:** the ball-lens double scrambler and the octagonal fiber shape; the CaF2 and SFTM-16 input optics (the model sees only their effect, the f/8 slit); the HET's fixed-altitude, moving-pupil geometry; the 180 K bench and 120 K detector, the vacuum and the Zerodur aging; the three fibers' unequal spacing; the asymmetric pupil relay; the H2RG's 1.7 um cut-off and crosshatch pattern; the laser frequency comb, etalon and lamp calibration; fiber focal ratio degradation beyond f/3.3, aberrations and the real PSF.
- **What is EFFECTIVE:** the camera focal length (362 mm; fitted to N19's 9.5 px slit length) and the VPH groove density (210 /mm; fitted to 28 orders over 8080-12780 A). The 9.5 px and the order edges therefore constrain the preset; the slit-image width (2.5 px, 8% off) and R (2.6% off the design 50,000) are checks.
- **Deploy gate:** collimator OFF-AXIS (SOURCED); the preset is the HR mode since commissioning (deployed October 2017, shared-risk science May 2018, K18 Sec. 1); the instrument operates (the PSU HPF blog lists results through July 2026), so it is not marked '(design)'. The preset note should say 'camera and VPH EFFECTIVE (the optical-design paper is unpublished)'; its F = 8 is the collimator's input ratio, not the HET's f/3.65.
