## Round 9 completeness summary

**NEID** (WIYN 3.5 m, Kitt Peak; Penn State instrument; commissioned 2019-2020): the optical prescription is nearly complete, from Schwab et al. 2016 (the design paper, full text via NASA NTRS), Schwab et al. 2020 (the fiber injection paper, supplied by the user; revision of 2026-11-18), Kanodia et al. 2020 and 2023 (arXiv), the port-adapter paper (Schwab et al. 2018, Caltech copy) and the pipeline documentation. Sourced: telescope f/6.3 and fiber feed f/4; every fiber (octagonal telescope fibers, circular instrument fibers, core sizes, HR 0.92 and HE about 1.5 arcsec); the injection optics (design C3: fused silica singlet on the fibers plus two cemented doublets, f/3.6 -> f/7.95; field and fiber centers); collimator (single 590 mm parabola, f = 1550 mm, triple pass), 195 mm beam; the R4 31.6 /mm 1x2 mosaic echelle (200 x 800 mm ruled, master MR263, gamma = 0.5 deg); the single 60 deg PBM2Y prism (54 deg incidence); the four-lens camera (about 600 mm, 250 mm aperture); the e2v CCD 290-99 (9216 x 9232, 10 um); the pipeline order table (122 orders, 173-52, per-order wavelength spans); design R = 100,000 and measured R about 112,000 (HR) and 72,000 (HE).

**Gaps that matter.** (1) The injection relay's magnification is not stated as a number even in Schwab et al. 2020 (no lens prescription is tabulated): the 141.5 um HR slit image is DERIVED from the f/3.6 -> f/7.95 ratio (2.208) and cross-checked against the 5-pixel sampling; it varies by 4.4 percent across the band (SOURCED, S20 Table 1). (2) The as-built camera focal length is still not published (S20 only says 'the same focal length as the NEID camera'; design requirement 'about 600 mm'; the Littrow dispersion with 600 mm reproduces the pipeline order span to 1 percent for order 170). (3) The layout (Fig. 2) is a scaled plan view only (2.4 m x 1.0 m bars); distances are MEASURED from it. (4) No published theta or measured blaze; the method used to measure R = 112,000 and 72,000 is not stated. (5) No refereed NEID instrument paper exists on ADS (only SPIE papers, a fiber paper and a solar-feed paper).

**On/off-axis verdict: OFF-AXIS, now SOURCED** (Schwab et al. 2020 Sec. 3: 'We use an off-axis portion of this mirror as OAP (off-axis parabola) collimator. The off-axis angle is 5 degrees in cross dispersion direction'; quotes in the 'Collimator on- or off-axis' row). The off-axis angle is the 5 deg deflection of the beam, so colTilt = 2.5 deg in the explorer's convention (tau turns the beam by 2 tau; DERIVED). This replaces the earlier in-plane reading of about 4 deg from the figure. The three passes use three 195 mm sub-apertures of the 590 mm mirror.

**Recommended preset: one line 'WIYN / NEID' with Settings 'HR' (the standard RV mode: R about 112,000, 3 spectra, 0.92 arcsec) and 'HE' (R about 72,000, 2 spectra, about 1.5 arcsec).** Which is most used: no source gives usage statistics; HR is the standard science mode (INFERRED: K23 'HR ... for maximum RV precision', the pipeline's default HR traces, the solar feed 'will center the Sun onto the High Resolution (HR) science fiber', LIN) and the first Setting. Both Settings share every optic; only the fiber core differs (and one trace fewer for HE).

## Optical layout (from S16 Fig. 2, p. 3 of the PDF; same figure as K20 Fig. 1)

Plan view; the figure's scale bars give the bench envelope 2.4 m x 1.0 m (SOURCED, figure labels). Pixel positions below are MEASURED on a 130 dpi render with the 2.4 m bar (823 px, 343 px/m); the 1.0 m bar gives 362 px/m, so distances carry about +-5 percent.

1. **Fiber head** (the puck, plus the input optics tube of two doublets and a singlet) at the right end of the bench (figure label 'Fiber Input'), at the intermediate focus, f/8: 'In our design, the fiber input is between the grating and the flat fold mirror' (S16 Sec. 2.1).
2. **M1, the parabola** (Zerodur, 590 mm, f = 1550 mm; left end of the bench), pass 1: collimates the f/8 beam to 195 mm. Fiber to M1 about 1.63 m (MEASURED; the SOURCED f is 1.55 m, 5 percent difference within the measurement error). The parabola is an OAP segment: injected 5 deg off the parent axis in the cross-dispersion direction (S20), i.e. perpendicular to this plan view.
3. **R4 echelle** (1x2 mosaic, two 200 x 400 mm facets drawn as one 800 mm long rectangle, 283 px = 0.83 m MEASURED, which matches the sourced 800 mm ruled length and so validates the scale), center about 1.54 m from M1 (MEASURED), tilted gamma = 0.5 deg out of the plane (SOURCED). The incident and dispersed beams run near each other along the bench.
4. **M1 pass 2** focuses the dispersed light on the **flat fold mirror M2** next to the fiber input (K20 Sec. 2.2: 'imaged on to the fold mirror (M2) by the parabola (M1)'); M2 at about 1.63 m from M1 (MEASURED).
5. **M1 pass 3** re-collimates the beam and forms the **white pupil on the entrance face of the PBM2Y prism** (K20: 'forms a white pupil on the incident face of PBM2Y'), prism center about 1.52 m from M1 and about 0.36 m 'above' the echelle in the figure (MEASURED); apex toward the echelle side, base toward the camera; clear aperture 380 x 250 mm.
6. **Camera**: the prism sends the beam to the upper right to the front triplet L1-L3 (about 0.3 m from the prism, MEASURED), then the **camera fold mirror** (top right, about 0.3 m past the triplet), which sends the beam down (fold angle about 90 deg, MEASURED by eye: SCHEMATIC) to L4 and the **CCD** (L4 to CCD about 0.22 m, MEASURED). The explorer's camera is a lens (cam 'lens'); the fold is a drawing item only.

Tags: element order, mirror and camera sizes, tilt gamma, prism and echelle parameters SOURCED; every distance and the fold angle MEASURED (+-5 to 10 percent); the collimator off-axis angle (5 deg, cross-dispersion) is SOURCED (S20). White-pupil mapping for the explorer: lay3d 'wp' with wpT = 1550 mm (the same parabola used as transfer collimator, unity magnification; cross-disperser at the pupil after the third pass, single pass through the prism, not double pass).

## Conflicts between sources

- **R (HR)**: 112,000 (K23); greater than 110,000 (WIYN); 120,000 (K20 Sec. 2.1, LIN Sec. 1, nominal); 100,000 (S16, design). Recommended validation: 112,000 with the design 100,000 as the geometric value.
- **R (HE)**: 72,000 (K23) versus about 60,000 (WIYN, 2019 proposal-era page). The width ratio HE / HR of the instrument fibers is 1.58 (DERIVED 101.2 / 64.1), which with 112,000 gives 71,000, supporting K23.
- **HR on-sky size**: 0.92 arcsec (S16, K23), 0.9 arcsec (WIYN page), 61.4 um at f/4 = 0.905 arcsec (DERIVED from the fiber table), 62 um (S16 Fig. 1) or 62.5 um (S18).
- **Injection output ratio (RESOLVED by S20)**: f/7.95 (S20 requirement, text and summary; equals 1550/195), against f/8 (K20), f/8.1 (K23) and f/7.9 (K20, puck lens only). f/7.95 is used: S20 is the injection design document and gives it three times; the others are rounded or as-built descriptions within 2 percent.
- **Fiber output ratio**: f/3.6 (S20, includes some FRD) versus f/3.65 (K20, K23, 96 percent enclosed energy); the explorer uses f/3.6, a 1.4 percent difference.
- **Lateral color of the injection relay**: S20 Fig. 9 caption says '<3%' for C4 while its Table 1 gives 6.3-6.4 percent for C4; the selected C3 is 4.4 percent in Table 1 (used).
- **Detector rows**: 9231 (S16) versus 9232 (DRPF).
- **Echelle master**: S16 says NEID's grating is replicated from MR263, 'the same master that is used for the grating of the ESPRESSO spectrograph'; the explorer's ESPRESSO record (data/round5/espresso.md) names the UVES red master MR160 for ESPRESSO (Megevand et al. 2014). The two are not reconciled here: the master number does not change the groove density, only a measured blaze would.
- **Order count**: 120 orders (K20 Sec. 3.4, ghosts) versus 122 (DRPF, extracted index range 173-52, of which 116 have wavelength solutions).

## Notes

- **Explorer limits**: the model cannot represent the HR/HE fibers as separate traces with their true cross-dispersion offsets (nObj only counts), the 2 x 2 binning of the HE image, the double scrambler and ball lens, the 4 percent wavelength-dependent magnification of the injection relay, or the single parabola's triple pass (wpT is entered as a transfer collimator of equal focal length). The camera fold and the out-of-plane tilt gamma are drawing items; gamma = 0.5 deg is small enough that the line tilt on the chip is a fraction of a pixel (K23 Sec. 6: the puck is clocked 'after correcting for the field rotation due to the gamma angle').
- **Era**: NEID is in routine operation (commissioned 2019-2020; K23 describes the as-installed fibers HR2 and HE5). The design papers (S16, 2016) set the optics; no later change to the optics is documented in the saved sources. The preset is therefore as built, not '(design)'.
- **Environment** (not modeled): vacuum chamber and sub-milliKelvin control (ROB), chromatic exposure meter on the blank between the two echelle facets (K23 Sec. 2), laser frequency comb plus etalon calibration (DRPA); the spectrograph sits in a new stable room on the WIYN ground floor (WIYN page).
- **Bent Cassegrain**: the port adapter converts f/6.3 to f/4 and contains the ADC; the spectrograph itself starts at the fiber, so the explorer's telescope focal ratio is irrelevant except through D and the fiber angle (the virtual convention above).
