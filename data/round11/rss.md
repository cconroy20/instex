## Completeness summary

**Complete from the University of Wisconsin design documents (the instrument's builders) and the current SALT call:** the F/4.2 prime-focus feed on the 11 m SAC pupil, slits, the all-refractive collimator (150 mm beam; f = 630 mm DERIVED), the F/2.2 articulating camera (330 mm), the six VPH gratings (line densities, clear apertures, usable tilts, camera angle = 2 x grating angle, 100 deg maximum), the detector (old 3 x 2048 x 4102 mosaic of 15 um pixels at 0.1267 arcsec/px and the 2026 single-chip replacement), coverage and the published R tables. No quantity is EFFECTIVE: every prescription number is sourced or derived, and the model reproduces independent coverage figures from two papers to 0.5-0.8% and the FPRD maximum R to 1.2%.

**Gaps that matter:** (1) Burgh et al. 2003 (optical design, SPIE 4841, 1463) and Kobulnicky et al. 2003 (operational modes, SPIE 4841, 1634) could not be obtained (SPIE paywall; no arXiv version); the University of Wisconsin design documents (Nordsieck 2003 CDR) carry the same prescription and are used instead. (2) No scaled layout or distances: only the 360 mm last-element-to-pupil distance, the 350 mm collimated-beam length and the < 625 mm camera length are published; Fig. 2 of the design document is unscaled. (3) No measured R or dispersion for PG2300 or PG3000 with known grating angles (the CARRSSPipeline paper gives R for two dithers without angles); the PG2300 and PG3000 settings test only the geometry. (4) No measured throughput curve in text. (5) The 2026 detector replacement: the exact pixel count along the dispersion of the new E2V 231-C6 is not stated.

**Status:** in operation (the call describes it as the main workhorse instrument on SALT). The detector was replaced in 2026 (call for 2026-2: shared-risk time); the preset represents the 2011-2026-1 three-CCD era (every published test used it) and gives the new chip in the note. No '(design)' tag.

**Varying aperture (SALT).** The illuminated pupil moves with the tracker, so the effective collecting area is that of a 7 to 9 m telescope (call Sec. 4.4; default 46 m^2). As for HET (data/round9/hpf.md), the preset keeps the design pupil D = 11 m at F = 4.2 (the SAC specification, which fixes f_col = 630 mm for the 150 mm beam). R does not depend on D (set by the slit, f_col / f_cam and the grating angle; the diffraction limit is above 1e5), so D enters only the arcsec bookkeeping and any collecting-area estimate; a throughput or S/N estimate with D = 11 m overstates the area by 95 / 46 = 2.1. Absolute spectrophotometry is not possible on SALT. Say so in the preset note.

**Collimator verdict: a refractive lens collimator, so no on-/off-axis issue (colTilt 0, col 'lens').** No obscuration and no slit in a returning beam: the slit sits at the focal plane ahead of the field lens, and the 90 deg fold mirror is inside the collimator before the last doublet (CALL Sec. A.6; OD Fig. 2).

**Recommended presets: one line 'SALT 11 m / RSS' (inst 'RSS') with four Settings:** `PG0900 15.9°`, `PG0900 21.1°`, `PG2300 40.6°`, `PG3000 50°`, all with the 1.2 arcsec long slit, D 11 m, F 4.2, lens collimator W = 150 mm (f 630 mm), lens camera 330 mm, 3-CCD 6344 x 4096 px of 15 um, gType 'trans' (symmetric Bragg setting: the model's deviation alpha + beta is the camera-collimator angle, 2 x grating angle). See Recommended presets.

1. **Focal plane and slit mask** (SOURCED: 'Focal Plane' label; the slit masks stand at the focal plane, OD Sec. 3.6) at the bottom of the side view, with the removable waveplates just behind it.
2. **Collimator** (SOURCED, lens: 9 lenses in 5 groups, CALL Sec. A.6), then the last doublet.
3. **Collimated beam** (SOURCED 150 mm diameter, 350 mm long, OD Sec. 3.3): runs horizontally with the shutter and the **VPH grating** (or the removable etalons) in it. The grating stands at the exit pupil, 360 mm from the last collimator element (SOURCED, OD Sec. 3.5).

## Conflicts between sources

- Plate scale at the focal plane: 4.5 arcsec/mm (WOV) vs 4.465 (from F/4.2 on 11 m); camera 8.6 arcsec/mm (CALL) vs 8.52 derived vs 8.447 implied by 0.1267 arcsec/px.
- Articulation step: 0.75 deg (CALL) vs 0.5 deg (ART, 2003 specification); grating angles in papers are multiples of 0.125 deg.
- Maximum R: 5300 at 1.25 arcsec (FPRD, geometric, requirement) vs 'up to R ~ 5000 with 1 arcsec' (CALL Sec. 7.3) vs the model's 5363 at 1.25 arcsec and 6700 at 1 arcsec; the call's number includes real image quality.
- Table 7.2 R ranges are not a pure tan(alpha) law (PG1300 at 32 deg: 3200 vs 2800 for the model).
- Dispersion 0.97 A/px (PGQ) vs the 6445-9400 A coverage over the mosaic (0.466 A/px unbinned): reconciled if 0.97 is the 2x2 binned value.
- Gratings: 'PG0300' (the old ruled 300 /mm) and Fabry-Perot etalons (R 500-12500) are listed in the 2003 documents; neither is available now (CALL Sec. 7.5; SMI Sec. 1: this mode has not been available since 2019).

## Notes

- **Model limits.** The explorer's single detector has no chip gaps: Nx = 6344 counts the two gaps as columns (the wavelength span is right; gap columns carry no data). It does not model the 100 deg camera articulation limit as a limit (the PG3000 50 deg setting is the limit itself), the VPH's angle-dependent efficiency or off-axis (MOS) wavelength shifts (CALL Sec. A.7: the coverage and efficiency of an off-axis slit differ by up to 50%), the dithering over chip gaps, the 2x2 binning, the slit tilt, the order-blocking filters (PC03200-PC04600), the Fabry-Perot, polarimetry (Wollaston beamsplitter) and IFU (SMI) modes, or the design image quality (<0.4 arcsec RMS in the dispersion direction).
- **UV.** PG0700 and PG0900 reach 320 nm; the explorer cuts coverage at 3200 A (the atmosphere). PG3000 at 50 deg (lamC 5107 A) is far from the cut.
- **Binning.** Published data are usually 2x2 binned (0.2534 arcsec/px, 2 x the dispersion in A/px).
- **D.** The pupil moves (see the aperture paragraph); area 46 m^2 default.
- **The other optical-design values** (glass: fused silica, fused quartz, CaF2, NaCl; Sellmeier fits in OD Table 1) are not needed by the explorer: the disperser is a VPH, not a prism.
- **Deploy gate:** lens collimator, so colTilt 0; no '(design)' tag.
