## Layout (CU12 Fig. 30, p. 1226, PDF p. 31)

Fig. 30(a) is a photograph of the spectrograph bench; Fig. 30(b), "Layout of the low resolution spectrograph optical system", is a 2D ray layout, in the plane of the bench, labeled Collimator, SLIT, VPHG, Blue Camera and Red Camera. The embedded image (1654 x 909 px) was extracted with `pdfimages` and read on a pixel grid. **Scale:** the drawing is to scale: the slit is 515 px in front of the collimator mirror (f = 800 mm, SOURCED), the returning beam is 130 px wide (202 mm against the SOURCED 200 mm; 515/130 = 3.96 = f/4) and the slit bar is 94 px tall (146 mm against the SOURCED 144 mm). So 1.553 mm/px (MEASURED, +-3%).

Element order along the light (all MEASURED unless noted; beam-axis distances +-5%, camera distances +-15%):

1. **Fibers/slit:** the slit is a thin vertical bar on the horizontal axis, 800 mm from the collimator mirror vertex, in the middle of the returning beam. Light leaves it as an f/4 cone toward the collimator (arrows to the right).
2. **Collimator:** one concave mirror at the right (vertex x = 1612 px). The collimated 200 mm beam returns leftward along the same axis, straddling the slit bar (arrows to the left). No Schmidt corrector is drawn in front of the mirror.
3. **Dichroic beam splitter** (a tilted slab) centered 1230 mm from the collimator, 427 mm past the slit. Its AOI is 23 +- 4 deg (26 deg from the slab's tilt, 20.5 deg from the reflected ray direction). **Blue is reflected**: the blue beam turns clockwise by 139 deg and heads back up and to the right, on the collimator side; **red is transmitted** straight on.
4. **Red arm** (left): a thin vertical plate 235 px (365 mm) past the dichroic (purpose NOT FOUND), then the red VPH grating at 1750 mm from the collimator (520 mm past the dichroic), tilted by its AOI of 8.63 deg, then the Schmidt camera: a corrector slab 185 mm after the grating, and the camera mirror at the left edge, the camera axis bent 22 deg upward (Table 6: AOI + AOD = 23.09 deg). The MRS red-channel grating (43.9 deg tilt, 1680 /mm) is drawn as a second slab at 45 deg beside the LRS grating with its own camera position (green rays, pointing up): the cameras are 'articulating'.
5. **Blue arm** (upper right): a tilted plate (purpose NOT FOUND), then the blue VPH grating 551 mm past the dichroic (1781 mm from the collimator), then the blue Schmidt camera (corrector slab 185 mm after the grating, mirror at the top), the axis bent about 21 deg (Table 6: 22.17 deg).

Equal path lengths: the red and blue gratings stand at 1750 and 1781 mm from the collimator, about 2.2 f_col, a little beyond the center of curvature (2 f_col = 1600 mm) of a spherical mirror: the grating is near the pupil image, as in the Schmidt-geometry presets.

## Conflicts between sources

- **Resolving power of the 2/3-slit mode:** R = 1500 (CU12 Table 4 and Sec. 3.2.3; LSPEC) versus R = 1800 (HU20, ZH12, D12L headers at 4750 / 7350 A and 5500 A). The headers probably report the measured arc resolution; the designers' table is the nominal value. Both are kept; the preset's EFFECTIVE slit follows 1800.
- **Camera focal ratio:** f/1.3 (CU12, Hou 2010 'f/1.3' in the later papers) versus the 250 mm that the dispersions give (f/1.25): 4% apart, inside the paper's rounding.
- **Huang et al. 2020 vs Cui et al. 2012 (collimator):** HU20: "two Schmidt correcting mirrors" correct the collimator; CU12 and its Fig. 30(b) show one concave mirror with no corrector. CU12 is followed for the drawing.
- **Early design vs built (Zhu et al. 2000 abstract):** reflective gratings, 2048 x 2048 CCD, f/1 camera, 130 mm slit; built: VPH gratings, 4096 x 4136 x 12 um, f/1.3, 144 mm slit.
- **XI11 vs CU12 (Table 1 / Table 6):** CU12 Table 6 lists blue 365-590 nm with center 480.38 nm; XI11's Table 1 prints 370-590 and the misprint 2.033. CU12 resolves it (480.38). XI11's text swaps the red and blue Bragg angles (8.2 and 8.6 deg) relative to the table; CU12 Table 6 agrees with XI11's Table 1 (red AOI 8.63, blue 8.17), which also satisfies the grating equation.
- **CCD format:** 4096 x 4096 (CU12 Sec. 3.2.4 first sentence) versus 4096 horizontal by 4136 vertical (CU12, the next paragraph): the chip has 4136 rows with 40 extra; the explorer uses 4136 x 4096.
- **Medium resolution:** CU12 Table 5 (2011: 510-540 and 830-890 nm, R 5000 / 7500 with the 2/3 slit) versus Hou et al. 2018 and DR12 (2017: 495-535 or 4950-5350 and 630-680 or 6300-6800 A, R 7500): different grating sets.
- **Wavelength coverage:** 3700-9100 A (ZH12), 3700-9000 A (D12L), 365-900 nm (CU12 'actual', LU15 'designed'): differ at the ends only.
