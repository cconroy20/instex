## Completeness summary

**Complete from published documents:** the focal plane (85 um MOS fibers, 1.3 arcsec; 960 fibers; f/3.2 in, f/3.1 out), the spectrograph's collimator (f/3.1, 190 mm, off-axis catadioptric, mirror R 1224.65 mm), the dichroic (5900 A, 390 x 308 x 25 mm), all five VPH gratings (line densities and the in-air angles of incidence and output, BIA Table 1), the cameras (f/1.8, 8 lenses), the detectors (2 x e2V CCD231-C6, 15 um, 8192 x 6144 useful), the coverage and the nominal dispersions and resolving powers, and the status with its date.

**Gaps that matter:** (1) the camera focal length (342 mm is f/1.8 x 190 mm, DERIVED; it reproduces the published dispersions to 1-2 percent for three gratings and runs 5-6 percent high for the two blue HR gratings); (2) no measured R(lambda): all R are nominal, and the blue arm falls short on sky (ING, 2025); (3) the layout distances are MEASURED from the Zemax-type figure only; (4) the dichroic width; (5) the Rogers et al. 2014 design paper was not reachable (SPIE), so the optical prescription of the correctors is NOT FOUND.

**Layout verdict:** OFF-AXIS collimator (the pseudo-slit sits beside the returning beam; Jin et al. 2024: 'an off-axis catadioptric f/3.1 collimator'). Tilt about 7 deg, MEASURED from the layout. Not an on-axis (Schmidt-type) design.

## Optical layout (from the published figures)

Figures: ING_vlores.gif (low-resolution mode, 608 x 516 px, dimension lines 2088 mm wide and 1870 mm high; scale from the dimension lines: 502 px = 2088 mm, 4.16 mm/px, and 452 px = 1870 mm, 4.14 mm/px, agreeing to 0.5 percent), ING_vhires.gif (high-resolution mode, 1680 x 1672 mm), ING_vSPE_layout.png (CAD plan with labels), and the same layout as Fig. 5 of Dalton et al. 2014 (PDF p. 6; 'WEAVE optical layout, low (left) and high (right) resolution mode.') and Fig. 1 of Izazaga et al. 2018 (PDF p. 2); BIA Fig. 2 (PDF p. 3) is a sketch. The ING page says the footprint is ~ 2.1 m x 1.9 m (low) and 1.7 m x 1.7 m (high).

**Element order, as drawn (light path):** pseudo-slit (a fiber slit in the Slit Exchange Unit) -> collimator mirror (concave sphere, far end of the bench) -> back past the slit's side, through the dichroic (a plane plate at 45 deg) -> split: the blue beam is reflected 90 deg (clockwise as drawn, to the north) and the red is transmitted straight on (to the west) -> a set of collimator corrector lenses in each arm -> shutter (and Hartmann shutter) -> VPH grating (transmission, in a grating exchanger: Blue VPHG (3x), Red VPHG (2x)) -> articulated camera of 8 lenses (swung to a larger angle in HR) -> cryostat window and detector.

**On-axis / off-axis verdict (deploy gate):** OFF-AXIS. Quote (Jin et al. 2024, Sec. 2.3, PDF p. 5): "design with an off-axis catadioptric f/3.1 collimator, which delivers a 190 mm collimated beam". The layout agrees: the slit lies beside the returning beam, 50 mm outside its edge, and the mirror is tilted by about 7 deg. It is NOT a Schmidt-type design with the fibers inside the returning beam. The collimator mirror diameter (624 mm clear aperture) is much larger than the 190 mm beam because the pseudo-slit's fan covers the mirror (INFERRED).

## Conflicts between sources

- **Focal ratio at the prime focus.** f/2.75 (MIG, SAY: design), 2.8 on axis and 2.9 at 1 deg radius (INGLP, as built). The explorer needs only the f/3.1-3.2 fiber-side values.
- **Collimator described as 'reflective' (DAL 2014) and 'off-axis catadioptric' (JIN 2024) and 'a spherical collimator mirror' (IZA 2018).** These are consistent: a spherical mirror plus corrector lenses (spherical surfaces) placed after the dichroic in each arm. The explorer draws a mirror collimator.
- **Collimator mirror size and material.** IZA (INAOE, the polisher): 660 mm diameter, 624 mm clear aperture, OHARA CLEARCERAM-Z HS, radius 1224.65 mm.  INGLP: 'Size 645 mm x 260 mm, material Zerodur'. Possibly a later cut-down of the blank (not stated); the 660 mm round blank with a 624 mm clear aperture is the polisher's own statement.
- **VPH angles.** INGLP: 'Littrow mode ... equal: 20 deg in low-res mode, and 51.6 deg in high-res mode'.  BIA Table 1: blue LR 22.72 / 17.01 deg, blue HR1 54.43 / 48.98, blue HR2 54.46 / 48.98, red LR 19.82 / 19.86, red HR 51.59 / 51.65 deg (the Bragg condition is not matched: slanted fringes).  The red gratings agree with ING (51.6); the blue ones do not.  The preset follows BIA (the grating design document); psi = alpha + beta.
- **Blue HR dispersion.** ING scale 0.076 (HR1) and 0.090 (HR2) A/px; the BIA angles with f_cam = 342 mm give 0.0804 and 0.0942 (5.8 and 4.6 percent higher).  Using beta = 51.6 deg (the INGLP 'equal angles') instead would give 0.0761 and 0.0891, matching ING.  The 2025 realignment (ING page) and the 'expected' values may explain it; unresolved.
- **Dichroic cut.** 5900 A (ING, INGLP) vs 600 nm (DAL 2014); the LR bands overlap 5790-6060 A (JIN Table 3), centered 5925 A. The preset uses 5900 A.
- **Fiber output aperture.** MIG: fibers deliver f/3.1; SAY: spectrograph f/3.1 and FRD loss 11 percent outside f/3.1 (prototype); but SAY's test-bench requirement RTS 01 reads 'Output aperture F/2.7 +/- 0.2'. Read here as a test requirement for the output cone, not the spectrograph acceptance; INFERRED.
- **HR resolving power.** Nominal 20,000 (ING, JIN) vs the geometric full width of the fiber image: 16,550 (blue HR) and 17,510 (red HR), DERIVED.  The LR values agree (4919 and 5001 against 5000).
- **Status wording.** ING (13 Mar 2026): surveys expected to begin about mid-2026; SPIE 2026 abstract (Aug 2026): 'expected to be in full science operations in early 2027'.

## Notes

- **Pre-fiber optics.** The WHT focal plane is f/2.75 with 17.8 arcsec/mm; each MOS button's concave prism surface slows the beam to f/3.2 so that the fibers see 15.35 arcsec/mm (85 um = 1.30 arcsec). The explorer's virtual convention folds this into F = 3.1 on a 4.2 m pupil and a core angle of 1.3466 arcsec; the alternatives are an on-sky 1.3 arcsec with F = 3.1 (the collimator then sees 82 um) or the virtual f/3.2 input (the collimator would see the wrong f_col).
- **What the explorer cannot represent.** (1) The two sets of corrector lenses in the collimated beam (drawn as part of the beam, or not at all); (2) the mid-chip gap between the two 6k x 6k CCDs (8192 px used; the gap sits at 5491-5539 A etc.); (3) slanted VPH fringes (the Bragg angle differs from alpha; the model uses alpha and beta from BIA, which is the grating equation anyway); (4) the camera swing (LR to HR) is a change of psi in the Setting (39.7 to 103.3 deg); (5) the 3D-curved pseudo-slit; (6) the 50 mm slit offset beside the beam (set by colTilt only); (7) the tumbler/positioner and the LIFU, mIFU modes (the LIFU has twice the fiber size and half the R, 170 um at f/3.2).
- **LIFU.** If a LIFU Setting is wanted: 170 um core, 2.6 arcsec, core angle at F = 3.1: 2.693 arcsec, slit-equivalent 2.332 arcsec; R about halved (ING 2500 and 10000).
- **Why psi = alpha + beta.** For a transmission grating the camera axis stands alpha + beta from the collimated beam's direction, with alpha measured from the grating normal on the collimator side and beta on the camera side (BIA Table 1 'incidence' and 'output' angles in air). The LR value, 39.7 deg, matches the layout (about 38-39 deg measured), and HR 103.3 deg matches the 'camera pointing east / south' of the HR figure.
- **Dispersion axis and format.** 8192 px is the dispersion axis (JIN: '8192 (spectral) x 6144 (spatial)').
- **Not scope.** Throughput curves, the positioner, the 1000-fiber configuration, and the sky-fiber arrangement.
