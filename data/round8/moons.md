## Completeness summary

**Complete from the open literature:** the fiber feed (512 fibers per spectrograph, 200 mm curved slit, f/3.5 collimator focal ratio, 265 mm beam), the spherical on-axis collimator (trade-off paper), the three arms and the two dichroics, the f/0.95 Wonder-Camera (two fused-silica lenses and a mirror, 19% obscuration, 430 mm long), the detectors (4096 x 4096 of 15 um: LBNL CCD in RI, H4RG-15 in YJ and H), the bands and design R of all five configurations, and the published layout figure (O16 Fig. 1, both modes), from which the dichroic angles and the grating and camera distances are MEASURED.

**Gaps that matter:** (1) no document, including Black 2022, gives the line densities, angles, prism glasses or apex angles of the five VPH dispersers (one secondary value: HR-H 1056 /mm at AOI 53 deg); the preset gratings are EFFECTIVE, fitted with web/physics.js to the design R and the dispersion. (2) The as-built fiber core is now sourced (158 um, GU20). (3) Dichroic widths and the as-built cut wavelengths. (4) Measured R and dispersion (A/px) are not in the accessible papers. (5) Guinouard 2020 gives the as-built core (158 um), FRD throughput (89.7-90.1%), microlens prescription; Guinouard 2022 gives 32 slitlets x 16 fibers and slit radii 1110 and 900 mm; Black 2022 gives measured image quality (2.5-2.7 px). None gives line densities, prisms, dichroic widths, or the slitlet pitch and gaps. (6) The instrument is in commissioning, so every number is a design value.

**Status:** not yet in science operation: first observations in summer 2026 (announced 3 September 2026, P26); the ESO project page reads 'AIV -> Commissioning' on 2026-11-17 (E26). Name the presets '(design)'.

The gratings were FITTED with web/physics.js (read-only; script in the scratch folder): two unknowns (G, incidence) against two derived targets (design R, band width / 4096 px), so a fit, not a test; lamC is the band midpoint. The HR-H pair 1052 /mm at 53.2 deg that the fit returns agrees with the published 1056 /mm at AOI 53 deg (FJ25, 0.4%), which is therefore used; it gives R 18795 and 0.277 A/px (the derived band-edge dispersion is 0.286, 3% off). The explorer's pixel scale is 0.407 arcsec/px and its fiber image 2.4-3.0 px against the measured 2.5-2.7 px (YJ), 2.5-3 px (RI) of Black 2022. A VPH-plus-prism disperser is represented by its equivalent single transmission grating (gType trans with a fixed tAlpha); the prisms' glass and apex angles are not published and are not needed for R and dispersion in this model.

## Conflicts between sources

- Fiber on-sky size, RESOLVED as far as the documents allow (INFERRED): 1.0 arcsec (O16 design; GU20 abstract 'sub field of 1 arcsec'; GU20 requirement '>= 1 arcsec') versus 1.2 arcsec (C20 Table 1, G22 Sec. 2.1, the built instrument). Core: 158 um as built (GU20) against 150 um in the design. The built core is 5% larger than the design and 1.2/1.0 = 20% larger on the sky only if the field stop was enlarged too, so the later 1.2 arcsec is taken as the built aperture; the fiber core in the explorer now follows GU20. Older text: Fiber on-sky size: 1.0 arcsec (O16 Table 1, E26 baseline table; design) vs 1.2 arcsec (C20 Table 1; G22 Sec. 2.1; built). The core is 150 um in the design (O14, O16, F22 test fibers) and 140-170 um for 1-1.2 arcsec in phase A (C12); the as-built core is NOT FOUND.
