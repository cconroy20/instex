## Round 8 completeness summary

**Tull coude spectrograph** (McDonald Observatory 2.7 m Harlan J. Smith telescope; names TS2, 2dcoude, Tull Spectrograph; in regular operation since 1992 April): the optical prescription is nearly complete. Primary paper Tull et al. 1995 (the scan) gives the layout (Fig. 1), the white-pupil concept, the prisms, the pupil mirror (R = 6.632 m), the echelles and the performance; the current observatory documents (MacQueen 2025 orientation, with a full EPRISM-style dispersion table, and Tull's 1995 grating tables) give the numbers the paper omits: the **collimator focal length 6140 mm** (190 mm beam, f/32.315), the camera focal lengths (775 mm F3, 3316 mm F1), the echelle angles (theta = 3.0 deg, gamma = 0), the 4-pass fused-silica prism train (58.188 deg total deviation), the slit plugs, the pixel scales and the order-by-order dispersion for 66 orders.

**Gaps that matter.** (1) Fig. 1 is an unscaled one-line sketch: the layout distances are only the C1-to-echelle length (text, 10.7 m) plus values MEASURED here against the F1 focus (PM-F1 = R/2 = 3.316 m gave 113.4 px/m, which the mirror equation then reproduces to 1.4 percent). (2) The collimator is a Dall-Kirkham Cassegrain: only the effective 6140 mm is published (C1 and C2 separately are not). (3) No published TS23 line-width measurement with the current TK3 CCD; the quoted R = 60,000 is the 2-pixel design value (geometric, R x slit = 70,734 arcsec). (4) TK3 versus the EV2 4096 x 4096 CCD under construction (status unknown after January 2025). (5) The explorer's echelle model has no Cassegrain collimator, no double pass through the prism train ahead of the echelle apart from entering four identical prisms, and no anamorphic line slant (+/-3.7 deg).

**On/off-axis verdict: ON-AXIS** (Cassegrain collimator; slit housing holds the primary; quotes in the col_axis row). Set colTilt 0. This is an INFERRED reading (the paper never writes 'on-axis'), supported by the Dall-Kirkham Cassegrain form, 'C1 is mounted in the slit housing', the central occulting disk the size of the collimator secondary, and Fig. 1, which draws the slit on the axis ahead of C1.

**Recommended preset: one line 'McDonald 2.7 m / Tull coude' with Settings 'TS23 E2, 1.2″' (R ~ 60,000, 3400-10,900 A, more than 95 percent of the use) and 'TS21 E2, 0.6″' (the F1 focus, R ~ 120,000 with slit 3, 1/18 of the coverage).** Optional extras: 'TS23 E1, 1.2″', 'TS21 E1, 0.3″' (the setup of the published R = 175,000 measurement). Detector: TK3, 2048 x 2048, 24 um (the instrument as published in 1995 and as in use through 2025).

## Optical layout (from Fig. 1, p. 253, and the text)

Fig. 1 of Tull et al. 1995 is a plan view in the coude room (all spectrograph elements lie on the horizontal plane under the observing floor; the slit and original coude spectrograph lie 25 deg east of north, 2dcoude on the N-S centerline, Sec. 4.7). Element order along the light (SOURCED from the text and the figure labels; distances MEASURED at 113.4 px/m on a 300 dpi render, scale from PM-to-F1 = R/2 = 3316 mm, checked against the 10.7 m of Sec. 4.6.4 to 2 percent):

1. **M5** (fifth mirror of the coude train) sends the f/32.3 beam along the room axis to the **slit S**: a concave spherical field mirror (6 arcmin field, to the autoguider) with the slit plug in its center (Sec. 4.7). S sits 0.15 m ahead of **C1** (columns 244 and 263 of the crop: SCHEMATIC at that scale).
2. **Collimator = Dall-Kirkham Cassegrain**: light from the slit passes C1's housing, goes to the secondary **C2** (2.13 m from C1, MEASURED; on its own pedestal), returns to the primary **C1**, and leaves as a 190 mm collimated beam with the central obstruction of C2 (Sec. 4.6.3 masks and Sec. 4.2). Effective f = 6140 mm (ORI). Slit and C1 are on the axis of the collimated beam: ON-AXIS.
3. **CP1**, the off-centered aspheric corrector plate for the pupil mirror, stands in the collimated beam 5.1 m from C1 (MEASURED), tilted (Sec. 4.6.1: the tilt sends its reflection west, away from the camera).
4. **Prisms P** (two 30 deg fused-silica prisms, each a pair of contacted 15 deg wedges) at 10.0 m (MEASURED; 'echelle located at a pupil' and the prisms 'in close proximity'), then the **echelle E** at 10.5 m (MEASURED; text: slit image 10.7 m from C1 'very close to the echelle'). The light crosses the prisms, hits the echelle at alpha = 68.29 deg, and returns through the prisms (the double pass, 4 prism passes, 58.2 deg total deviation).
5. **Pupil mirror PM**, a spherical mirror (R = 6.632 m, f = 3316 mm) on the return side, at 5.9 m from the echelle (MEASURED; its center of curvature CC lies near the echelle; PM is displaced to magnify the pupil x 1.27) and about 0.6 m below the collimator axis. PM receives dispersed collimated light and focuses it at **F1** (3.3 m beyond PM, MEASURED = R/2 by construction; the R ~ 200,000 focus).
6. Beyond F1 the beam diverges through the Schmidt corrector **CP2** to the **folding flat FF** (7.5 m from PM, MEASURED; 7.59 m from the mirror equation), where the white pupil forms (the flat's square central hole fits inside the shadow of the telescope secondary, Fig. 2). FF folds the beam about 90 deg down to the **camera mirror CAM** (spherical, folded Schmidt, f = 775 mm effective, f/4.08), which returns the converging beam through the hole in FF to **F3** (R = 60,000 focus) behind a field-flattening lens and the dewar window.

Tags: the element order, the prism and echelle details, PM's R, the 1.27 magnification, the camera f and the C1-echelle length are SOURCED; the C1-C2, C1-CP1, C1-prism, echelle-PM and PM-FF distances are MEASURED; the fold angle at FF (about 90 deg) and the sideways offsets are SCHEMATIC (read by eye from Fig. 1). The unfolded path from the echelle to F3 through PM and FF is 5.9 + 7.5 = 13.4 m MEASURED.

White-pupil mapping for the explorer: transfer collimator = the pupil mirror (wpT = 3316 mm), a single pass, not a second collimator pass as in HARPS; the cross disperser stands at the pupil with the echelle (as in FEROS and UVES, but here in double pass ahead of the echelle). lay3d 'wp', fold3d 'folding flat' (the camera's fold).

## Conflicts between sources

- **E1 blaze angle**: 63.900 deg (T95 Sec. 4.1 and TAB) against 63.43 deg (ADC Table 2, the nominal arctan 2). Use 63.900 (measured effective angle; T95 notes 'both significantly greater than the nominal arctan 2').
- **Slit 4**: 1.18 x 8.0 arcsec (ORI) against 1.20 x 8.2 arcsec (SLIT table: 511 um x 3.478 mm; MAN). 1.18 arcsec is the 2-pixel width at 2.348 arcsec/mm (502 um); the plug is cut at 511 um. Preset: 1.20.
- **E2 groove density**: 52.67 (T95 text, ADC), 52.6759 (TAB 1995), 52.6746 (ORI 2025): 0.002 percent, immaterial. Preset: 52.6759.
- **Telescope for the tables**: 2.72 m at f/32.36 (TAB; EFL 88.02 m, 2.344 arcsec/mm) against 2.718 m at f/32.315 (ORI; EFL 87.83 m) and f/32.32 (ADC): 0.2 percent. Preset: 2.718 m, F = 32.315 so that F x W = 6140 mm.
- **Plate scale from the paper's slit list**: 83 um = 0.20 arcsec (2.41 arcsec/mm), 1 mm = 2.40 (2.40), 3.5 mm = 8 arcsec (2.29), 12.8 mm = 30 arcsec (2.34): rounded values scattered by up to 2.5 percent about 2.348 arcsec/mm (ORI, ADC); the slit tables of the manual (e.g. 511 um = 1.20 arcsec = 2.348 arcsec/mm) agree with 2.348.
- **Dispersion in the laser test**: T95 Sec. 5.1 says 'the known reciprocal dispersion of 2.234 A/mm' for the 6328 A line with E2 at F3; the 1995 tables give 2.109 A/mm at the blaze of order 54 (6378.7 A, the order holding 6328 A) and 2.15 at its blue edge: 6 percent, unexplained (the test used a different CCD and echelle setting). The tables are the later, internally consistent source.
- **TS21 resolving power**: T95 abstract and Conclusion 'up to 250,000' and the Fig. 1 label 'R = 250,000 focus' (OCR); MAN 'R ~ 200,000'; WEB 'up to R ~ 240,000'; ORI 4.28 x 60,000 = 257,000. The numbers depend on the slit (R x slit = 70,734 arcsec for E2): the pixel-limited R at 0.13 arcsec/px x 2 px = 0.26 arcsec is 272,000.
- **Minimum order separation**: 10.3 arcsec (ORI table, E2), 10.5 arcsec (WEB; E1 15.5), 10 arcsec 'at minimum (in the IR), increasing to 20 arcsec in the UV' (T95).
- **Camera field**: 3.7 deg square (T95) against 3.63 x 3.63 deg (ORI).
- **Replacement CCD**: EV2 4096 x 4096 (ORI, January 2025) against 'E2V 4096 x 4112' (ADC, September 2026), both 15 um pixels.
- **Slit counts**: T95 'There are 18 such slits'; the SLIT table lists 20 plugs (numbers 1-11, 13-17, 20-23). The table is the later list; the 1995 count is the original set.
- **Full-coverage limit**: 5800 A (ORI: the FSR stops fitting the chip) against 5600 A (T95 Sec. 4.4, 'completely contained within the CCD boundaries only for wavelengths shortward of 5600 A').

## Notes

- **Era.** One instrument since 1992 (first light April 1992; OCTICON was retired in 1989 and replaced by 2dcoude). The optics are unchanged; the detector changed (TI 800 x 800 at 15.24 um, Tektronix 512 x 512, borrowed Tektronix 2048 x 2048 at 21 um in 1993-94; then the TK2048 / TK3 at 24 um). In March 2023 the echelle and prism drives and encoders were replaced (ts23_disperser.pdf); the optics were not. Recommend the TK3 era, the documented standard configuration.
- **Setting names** (at most 16 characters): 'TS23 E2, 1.2″' (R ~ 60,000, default), 'TS21 E2, 0.6″' (F1, slit 3), optional 'TS23 E1, 1.2″' and 'TS21 E1, 0.3″'. The Setting changes fcam (775 or 3316 mm), the echelle (E2: 52.6759 gr/mm, 65.293 deg; E1: 79.0139 gr/mm, 63.900 deg), the slit and the chip center; the telescope, collimator, theta, gamma, prisms and TK3 detector stay.
- **State sketch for web/presets.js** (not entered; research only): `mode: "echelle", D: 2.718, F: 32.315, e: { lay3d: "wp", wpT: 3316, layLen: 10700, colTilt: 0, fold3d: "folding flat", slitW: 1.20, nObj: 1, col: "mirror", W: 190, fcam: 775, cam: "mirror", eG: 52.6759, eDelta: 65.293, gamma: 0, theta: 3.0, xd: "prism", xGlass: "silica", xA: 30, xPasses: 4 (or xPrisms: four {glass: "silica", A: 30}), lamC: 4718.6, Nx: 2048, Ny: 2048, pix: 24 }`. lamC = 4718.6 A is the center of the chip with the ORI 'On blaze' configuration (order 73 blaze at row 1024, Y = 0 in the table). TS21: fcam 3316, slitW 0.59 (plug 3, 251 um), same else.
- **Check for the model.** f_col = F x W = 32.315 x 190 = 6140 mm; slit demagnification f_col / f_cam = 7.92 (vertical) and 9.96 (horizontal, anamorphic); R x slit = 70,734 arcsec should come out of alpha = 68.29 deg, beta = 62.29 deg, D = 190 mm (the footprint 514 mm on the 408 mm echelle: the echelle is overfilled, the projected-length effect that sets the 70,734; the explorer's R model should be checked for this overfill).
- **Prisms.** The prism train is at minimum deviation for 4718.6 A (incidence 22.267 deg on every face, ORI); the first pass (before the echelle) and the second (after) both lie in the collimated beam: the paper's 'echelle sees a variation of +/-0.9 deg in gamma due to the first pass through the prisms' gives line slants up to +/-3.7 deg, which the in-plane (gamma = 0) model cannot show. The real prism tilt of +/-3 deg between the two prisms (Sec. 4.6.2) is for ghost control only.
- **Model limits.** (a) A Cassegrain collimator (1 mirror drawn); (b) a folded Schmidt camera whose focus F3 is behind the fold flat's hole (cam 'mirror' draws the corrector, the spherical mirror and the detector in the beam, a reasonable match); (c) no line slant; (d) the F1 camera (3316 mm) has no corrector change; the explorer's single 'camera' covers both foci by fcam; (e) the 7 percent vignetting loss from echelle overfill (T95 Sec. 4.3) is not in the model; (f) the optional EV2 (4096 x 4096 x 15 um) is a different detector, not a Setting here.
- **Use pattern.** E2 more than 96 percent of the time; TS23 more than 95 percent of the TS2 time (ORI); slit 4 'most of the time'; the iodine cell is used in front of the slit for radial velocities (the McDonald Planet Search, 'On blaze' = MOPS configuration, ts23_disperser.pdf).
