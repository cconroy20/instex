## Round 12 completeness summary

**VIRUS is sourced from the instrument paper (Hill et al. 2021, H21) and its companions; the one gap that matters is that no saved source states the collimator focal length.** Found and quoted: the HET feed (10 m pupil, f/3.65, 36 500 mm, 5.65 arcsec/mm), the 266 um fibers (1.5 arcsec), 224 fibers per spectrograph (448 per IFU, 78 IFUs, 156 spectrographs), the f/3.33 collimator accepting the fibers with a 125 mm beam, the spherical reverse-Schmidt collimator with the slit in a slot of the fold mirror, the 930 /mm transmission VPH (9 deg in, 15.3 deg out at 450 nm; 12.15 deg in Table 3), the f/1.25 Schmidt camera of 166 mm with the 2064 x 2064, 15 um CCD inside the beam, coverage 3500-5500 A (as built 3492 + 2003 A), R about 800 (5.6 A at 4500 A), the measured dispersion of 1.99 A per binned pixel and the 7.5 px fiber profile. Published layout: H21 Fig. 8 (p. 21) and L14 Fig. 1a.

**Model check (hand-run through web/physics.js with the recommended state, not edited into the explorer): R = 773 (published about 800: -3%); dispersion 0.950 A/px against the measured 0.995 (-4.5%); coverage 3514-5467 A against 3492 + 2003 (3492-5495): 2.5% narrow; fiber image 6.12 px across (0.866 x 7.07 px).**

**Collimator verdict: ON-AXIS** (SOURCED): 'The slits of the IFU assembly protrude into slots in the fold mirrors' (H21 Fig. 8 caption) and the fibers' 'axes pass through the center of curvature of the spherical collimator mirror and are normal to the surface of the mirror' (H21 Sec. 4.2). The collimator is a spherical MIRROR (a reverse Schmidt: 'a double-Schmidt, with the collimator reversed', L14), colTilt 0, with a flat fold mirror in the beam. **Camera verdict: SCHMIDT with the CCD in the beam** (SOURCED): 'an f /1.25 vacuum Schmidt design with 166 mm focal length' and 'an internal CCD in the vacuum' (H21 Sec. 4.1, Fig. 8): explorer cam = mirror.

**Varying aperture (HET).** The pupil changes with the track. As for HPF (data/round9/hpf.md) the preset keeps the fixed pupil D = 10 m; R does not depend on it. HETDEX reduces against an effective 50 m2 (equivalent diameter 7.98 m, DERIVED), so a collecting-area estimate with D = 10 m overstates the area by 1.57. The explorer's F = 3.33 is the collimator's input ratio (virtual convention), not the HET's f/3.65.

**Recommended preset: one line 'HET 10 m / VIRUS' with one setting (VIRUS has no moving parts and one fixed grating).** State: mode grating, D 10, F 3.33, slit 1.4269 arcsec (= sqrt3/2 x 266 um / (3.33 x 10 000 mm) x 206 265; the core angle is 1.6476 arcsec), nObj 224, collimator mirror W 125 mm, f_col 416 mm (DERIVED, see Conflicts), colTilt 0 (on-axis, SOURCED), layLen 832 (the collimator-fold-grating path, MEASURED 836 mm, = 2 f_col), a flat fold at 0.49 of the path, transmission VPH 930 /mm, m 1, psi 24.3 deg (DERIVED 9 + 15.3; = 2 x the 12.15 deg of Table 3), tAlpha null (symmetric Bragg), lamC 4500 A, gLen 138 mm (clear aperture), camera mirror (Schmidt) fcam 166 mm, camLen about 90 mm, CCD 2064 x 2064 of 15 um.

## Conflicts between sources

- **Collimator focal length / ratio**: H21 Table 3 f/3.33, Sec. 4.1 'f /3.32', C14 'f /3.33', H06 (2006 design) f/3.35 against L14's 'focal reduction factor from the collimator to the camera is 2.8x' (166 mm x 2.8 = 465 mm, i.e. f/3.7 at 125 mm). Adopted f/3.33 and 416 mm: it matches the fiber profile (7.07 px against the measured 7.5 px; 465 mm would give 6.3 px) and the model's R (773 against 800; a 465 mm collimator would put the slit image 10% narrower and R near 860).
- **Resolution**: 5.6 A, R about 800 (H21) against 4.7 A, R 750-950 (G21, citing H21 in preparation) against R about 700 (C14, H16: design) against R about 850 (H06, the prototype design). H21 adopted (it is the instrument paper and the sourcing for the 2021 survey).
- **Beam and input ratio**: H21 says the 125 mm beam includes 'the margin for FRD' (Table 3), while L14 says the design is 'optimized to accept a f /3.65 input beam (it can accept a beam as fast as f /3.4 ...)'. The explorer's F = 3.33 is the collimator's acceptance, as HPF's F = 8.
- **Grating angle**: Table 3's 12.15 deg 'to grating normal' against the text's 9 deg in and 15.3 deg out. Reconciled: 9 + 15.3 = 24.3 = 2 x 12.15 (Table 3 gives the half-angle between the beams); both give psi = 24.3 deg.
- **Fiber core angle**: 1.5 arcsec on the sky (266 um x 5.65) against the explorer's virtual 1.6476 arcsec (266 um at f/3.33): not a conflict, the convention.
- **Number of spectrographs and the field**: the 2006 design had 145 units, 246 fibers of 200 um and a 29 x 29 arcsec IFU (H06) and the 2016 text a 16 arcmin field (H16); H21's 156, 224, 266 um, 51 x 51 arcsec and 18 arcmin are the as-built values.

## Notes

- **Layout for the drawing** (H21 Fig. 8 right, p. 21; all positions MEASURED from a 300 dpi render with the scale set by slit-to-collimator = f_col = 416 mm, 0.874 mm/px; independent checks: the 131 mm grating extent against 138 mm, and the 836 mm collimator-fold-grating path against 2 f_col = 832 mm). Order along the light: the fiber slit at the focal surface (in a slot of the fold mirror) -> the spherical collimator mirror 416 mm away, on the right and slightly below the slit line -> the collimated beam returns 407 mm to the flat fold mirror (beam turned about 160 deg, mirror incidence about 10 deg) -> 429 mm down and to the right to the VPH grating at the pupil (0.49 of the path is the fold) -> the dispersed beam (24 deg deviation) runs horizontally to the corrector plate (about 90 mm) -> the spherical camera mirror about 250-340 mm farther, with the CCD and its field flattener at its focus inside the beam. Explorer values: layLen 832 (tagged MEASURED 836, with the Schmidt condition 2 f_col), foldAng for a turn of about 160 deg (the explorer's convention for the fold angle is set by the integrator from the figure: heading 177.5 deg in, -22 deg out), foldPos 0.49, camLen about 90 (SCHEMATIC at 20 percent). The L14 Fig. 1a is the same design (the collimated beam is 125 mm).
- **Two channels per unit**: each VIRUS unit holds two identical channels fed by one IFU (two slits, 224 fibers each); the preset is one channel. In the 2D layout the unit's pair shares a housing 970 x 740 x 460 mm (H21 Fig. 8 caption); the explorer draws one channel.
- **What the explorer cannot represent**: the curved, bifurcated fiber slit (a curved slit with the fibers aimed at the collimator's center of curvature) and the slit's gaps (a 3-fiber central gap, a gap at 2/3 of the extent: nObj 224 counts only the fibers); the corrector plate's aspheric correction and the camera's field flattener (the model has a thin camera of 166 mm); the central obscuration by the CCD in the Schmidt camera (f/1.25 versus f/1.33 on axis); the 1 deg fringe tilt of the VPH (it only moves a ghost); the CCD's amplifier split and binning 2 x 1 (the preset is unbinned: 15 um; the survey data are 2 A per binned pixel); the HET's moving pupil; the 3-position dither (the IFU covers 1/3 of its field per exposure).
- **Settings**: one setting only (no moving parts, one grating). Suggested name for the Preset menu 'HET 10 m / VIRUS' (the Setting menu is not needed). No second era is offered: the 2006 design (VIRUS-P, 200 um fibers on the 2.7 m telescope) is the Mitchell Spectrograph, a separate instrument.
- **Deploy gate**: collimator ON-AXIS (SOURCED, quotes above); camera Schmidt with the CCD in the beam (SOURCED); in operation (all 78 units on sky August 2021, H21 Table 2), so not marked '(design)'. The preset note should say that f_col = 416 mm is DERIVED from f/3.33 x 125 mm and that the layout distances are MEASURED from Fig. 8.
- **Model state not tuned**: nothing in the recommended state is fitted to a published performance number, so R, the dispersion and the coverage are independent tests (the 3-5% misses are the model's thin camera against a real Schmidt with a field flattener and the 1 deg tilt; reported, not tuned away).
