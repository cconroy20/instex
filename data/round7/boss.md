## Completeness summary

**Complete from one primary paper (Smee et al. 2013, SM13):** both eras' optical prescriptions (Tables 1 and 2: collimator R 1263.904 mm, f/4, 160 mm beam; 45 deg dichroic at 6000 / 6050 A; SDSS grisms 640 and 440 /mm on right-angle BK7 prisms (37 and 39.5 deg), BOSS VPH grisms 520 and 400 /mm between prisms of 14.0 and 17.8 deg; f/1.5, 240 mm eight-element cameras; SITe 2048 x 2048 x 24 um and e2v / LBNL 4k x 4k x 15 um CCDs), the fibers (180 um = 3 arcsec, 320 per spectrograph; 120 um = 2 arcsec, 500 per spectrograph; f/5 in, f/4 out), the layout figures (Figs. 7, 22, 14, 29), and measured performance (Figs. 35-38). **Collimator verdict: ON-AXIS** (the slit plate, < 3.2 mm thick, stands in the returning beam; 'one-dimensional Schmidt collimator'; BOSS masks a 3/16 inch strip down the middle of the mirror). **Original SDSS gratings: ruled-master replicas on grisms, not VPH.** **Recommended presets:** four settings on two Preset lines, 'BOSS' and 'SDSS-I/II', each Blue channel and Red channel, each with a dichroic tree for the All-arms view.

**Gaps that matter:** the native dispersion in A/px is not published (derived from R and sigma ~ 1 px, and consistent with the explorer's computation to 1-2.4% for BOSS); the measured R(lambda) exists only as plots; Table 1's distances disagree with the center-of-curvature statement by 105-112 mm (the preset follows the text); the BOSS dichroic cut is stated as 6050 A, 605 nm and 640 nm; the BOSS CCDs' exact active format and the BOSS grating-to-camera air gap are NOT FOUND.

## Conflicts between sources

- BOSS dichroic cut: 6050 A (SM13 Fig. 22 caption), 605 nm (Sec. 3.3.3), 640 nm (Sec. 3.3.1: "Light blueward of 640 nm is reflected into the blue channel"), 'roughly 6000 A' (S4B). Sec. 3.3.3 also calls 605 nm 'a 15 nm shift toward the red' from the SDSS cut, but the SDSS cut is 6000 A (Sec. 2.3.4, Fig. 7), a 5 nm shift. The record uses 6050 A (the caption and Sec. 3.3.3 agree) and treats 640 nm as a slip.
- Collimator-to-grating distance: the text puts the gratings at the mirror's center of curvature, 1263.9 mm (Sec. 2.3.3), the Fig. 7 drawing MEASURES 1257 mm (blue) and 1302 mm (red) with the scale fixed by the slit-to-mirror distance; Table 1's rows sum to 1087.831 + 64.592 = 1152.4 mm (blue) and 1087.831 + 70.663 = 1158.5 mm (red) to the grating plane (the prism row's thickness is the glass on axis; the Zemax model omits the dichroic, which the real beam crosses or reflects from before the grism). The explorer uses 1263.9 mm (text, supported by the figure); the 105-112 mm gap to Table 1 is unexplained.
- Collimator focal ratio: 'f/4' and a 160 mm beam (Sec. 2.3.1) = 640 mm, against R/2 = 631.95 mm (Tables 1-2) = f/3.95. The paper's demagnification 1.5/4 = 0.375 gives 67.5 um for the 180 um fiber; 240/631.95 gives 68.4 um (+1.3%). The preset takes the prescription (3.950).
- BOSS spectrum spacing: 'separated by 6 pixels center-to-center' (Sec. 3.3.1) against 260 um x 0.375 / 15 um = 6.5 px within a v-groove block (the 6 px follows for SDSS: 390 x 0.375/24 = 6.1).
- SDSS resolving power: ~2000 (SM13 abstract), 1800 (Y00), 1500 at 3800 to 2500 at 9000 A (S4D, one merged cell for both instruments); BOSS: 1560 at 3700 to 2650 at 9000 A (S4B, by channel) against 1300 at the blue end to 2600 at the red end (D13); all are rounded, and only SM13 Figs. 35-36 are the measurements.
- BOSS coverage: 3560-10400 A (SM13 Sec. 4.2.1), 3650-10,400 A (S4D table), 361-1014 nm (D13), blue cameras 'at least 3600-6350 A' and red 'at least 5650-10000 A' (S4B), 3600-10400 A for the pipeline's mask (B12).
- Peak efficiencies: predicted 17% and 22% (SDSS), 31% (BOSS) on sky (Secs. 2.3.8, 3.3.7); measured peak 21% (SDSS) and 29% (BOSS) (Sec. 4.2.3); '70% peak instrumental efficiency' against '45%' (Sec. 3.3.1). The last pair is the instrument alone (grating, camera, CCD, collimator, dichroic), the first two include telescope, fibers and seeing losses.
- Fiber size: the SDSS fiber is described as 180 um / 3 arcsec (SM13, S4D); the plate scale makes it 2.98 arcsec. Y00 gives 0.2 mm, the outer size of the 180-198-218 fiber.
- Telescope central obscuration: the BOSS fiber test used 'the 27% areal obscuration' (SM13 Sec. 3.2.2); data/telescopes/sdss.md has 26.0% (secondary baffle) or 28.6% (with the conical baffle) on axis. A telescope matter, not changed here.

## Notes

- ERAS AND SETTINGS. Two Preset-menu lines, each with two Settings: 'BOSS' (Blue channel, Red channel; 2009 onward, still in use in SDSS-IV and SDSS-V) and 'SDSS-I/II' (Blue channel, Red channel; 1999-2008). Each line can have an All-arms view with its own dichroic tree: BOSS cut 6050 A, SDSS 6000 A, both 45 deg in the collimated beam, one shared slit, collimator and fiber head. Recommend BOSS as the default entry, listed first.
- WHY THE SDSS BLUE/RED GRISMS ARE 'ruled': the original gratings were replicas of ruled masters (new masters from Hyperfine), cemented on the hypotenuse of right-angle prisms; the explorer's ruled-grism relation m G lam0 = (n-1) sin A returns 4909 and 7403 A against the published 4960 and 7400 A zero-deviation wavelengths. BOSS's VPH grisms use the explorer's VPH model; the 3.5 deg layer tilt (a ghost fix) and the unequal apex angles it needs (14 +- 3.5 deg) are not modeled.
- FIBER ENTRY. Use the explorer's 'virtual' convention as for APOGEE: F = 3.950 (the collimator's ratio, W = 160 mm, f_col = 631.95 mm), slit = 0.866 x core / (F x D): 3.256 arcsec (SDSS, 180 um) and 2.171 arcsec (BOSS, 120 um); on the sky the fibers subtend 2.98 and 1.99 arcsec. telF = 5, fibAp = 3.0 and 2.0 give the Telescope box the f/5 focus and the on-sky sizes.
- ON-AXIS COLLIMATOR (deploy gate). The slit plate (< 3.2 mm thick, 233 mm deep along the axis) stands edge-on in the middle of the returning beam; SM13 calls the arrangement a 'one-dimensional Schmidt collimator' without corrector, and BOSS added a 3/16 inch vertical strip down the middle of the mirror to stop reflections from the fiber tips. The obstruction is a thin vertical strip (2-3% of the beam width), not a central circle: set colTilt 0, and the explorer's returning-beam drawing applies. Hectospec is the nearest existing analogue (Schmidt-type, fiber head in the returning beam).
- SHARED PROPERTIES OF THE FOUR SETTINGS: collimator 631.95 mm, beam 160 mm, camera 240 mm f/1.5, dichroic 45 deg, layLen 1263.9 mm (center of curvature). Per arm: the red arm needs no fold (the camera sits on the collimator axis, offset 11 mm by the refracting dichroic plate in the drawing), the blue arm gets the dichroic as a 90 deg fold at foldPos 0.86 (turn clockwise as printed).
- MODEL COMPARISON (explorer physics, F = 3.950, run on 2026-11-17 in a scratch script, nothing in web/ changed). The fiber image and the spectrum pitch reproduce SM13 (diam 1-1.4%, SDSS pitch 2.8%), the zero-deviation wavelengths agree to 1.0% and 0.04%, and the dispersion agrees with the one derived from R and sigma = 1 px to 0.9-2.4% for BOSS. The explorer's R is 11-13% below the adopted BOSS design (geometric FWHM 2.6 px against a measured Gaussian fit near 2.35 px, and SM13 says its own prediction 'is on the low side'); the SDSS red R at 9000 A is 15% below (and the derived dispersion differs by 16%, so the S4D value is probably a design curve).
- WHAT THE EXPLORER CANNOT REPRESENT: the tilted VPH layer and unequal prisms of BOSS; the curved slit and fan of fibers (the one-dimensional Schmidt geometry that keeps the fiber images round over the 126-139 mm slit); the detector curvature (field flatteners, 'saddle-shaped focal plane'); the spatial variation of R across the CCD (20-25% worse at the slit ends); chip ranges cut by the dichroic and throughput (the model draws the whole chip: BOSS blue 3260-7263 A, red 5168-11079 A, SDSS blue 3797-6169 A, red 5767-9215 A, against the data ranges 3560-10400 A and 3800-9220 A); second-order light in the red; two independent spectrographs (one is drawn).
- LAYOUT SOURCE QUALITY. The only published layout is SM13 Fig. 7 (SDSS) and Fig. 22 (BOSS), Zemax-style line drawings with no scale bar; the numbers MEASURED here take the scale from the slit-to-mirror distance in Table 1 (630.123 mm), which two other dimensions of the drawing (beam, mirror height) confirm to 3-4%. Fig. 14 and Fig. 29 are mechanical sections of the same T-shaped bench.
