## Completeness summary

**Hamilton**: the prescription is nearly complete from Vogt (1987) and the Lick user's guide: Shane 3-m coude focus, f/36 and 1.89 arcsec/mm; slit widths 320-960 um (0.6-1.8 arcsec) in an aperture plate or free jaws; an off-axis parabolic collimator (f = 7.4754 m, 208.4 mm beam, 11 deg turn: OFF-AXIS); a 31.5 /mm R-2 (blaze 64.7 deg) 204 x 408 mm echelle used in plane with 2 theta = 11 deg; two UBK7 prisms (apex 43.4 deg effective) crossing the beam after the echelle; a folded Schmidt camera (508.3 mm, f/1.67, 40 deg fold, external focal plane); the detector sequence TI 800 x 800 -> 2048 x 2048 15 um (Ford, Dewar 13, 6, 8) -> Dewar 4 4096 x 4096 12 um; the planet-search resolving power (R = 62,000 nominal, 64,000 measured with Dewar 13). Recommended: the planet-search setup with the Dewar 13 CCD (1994-98) and the 1.2 arcsec slit. Gaps: no manufacturer data sheet for UBK7 (SECONDARY Sellmeier, which differs from N-BK7 by < 7e-5 in index), no collimator off-axis distance in mm, no published element distances (read from Vogt's Fig. 1, MEASURED +/- 15%), and an unresolved echelle-footprint puzzle (a 208 mm beam at alpha = 70.2 deg needs 615 mm of a 408 mm echelle; see Conflicts).

The user's guide: "an off-axis parabolic mirror (i.e., the mirror is made from an off-axis portion of a larger parabolic mirror)" that turns the beam "11-degrees off its original optical axis". The slit is not in the returned beam (Fig. 1 top view: the slit lies at the far end of the long arm, 11 deg from the collimator-to-echelle ray). Set colTilt = 5.5 deg explicitly (half the 11 deg deviation, the DEIMOS rule; INFERRED), not 0.

### Conflicts between sources

- **Groove density**: 31.6 /mm actual (V87 p. 1220, p. 1218; FT94) vs 31.5 /mm modeled (V87 Fig. 2, Table I; HQR). The preset uses 31.5 because Vogt says only 31.5 with 43.4 deg reproduces the format.
- **Prism apex**: 45 deg design (V87 p. 1219) vs 44.8 deg actual (V87 p. 1220) vs 43.4 deg effective (V87 Fig. 2, Table I) vs 43.9 deg (HQR). 43.4 reproduces the tabulated order separations to 0.4-1.7%; 43.9 to 2-3%; 44.8 to 5-6% (DERIVED).
- **Collimated beam**: 204 mm (V87 text, 8 inch) vs 207.0 mm (V87 Table I) vs 8.15 inch = 207 mm (FT94) vs 208.4 mm (HQR). Spread 2%; the preset uses 208.4 (f_col / W = 35.87 equals the f/36 feed).
- **Echelle size and footprint**: V87: 204 x 408 mm (8 x 16 inch); FT94: 8 x 12 inch. UNRESOLVED footprint problem: with alpha = 70.2 deg on the collimator side (needed for the published -22 and the dispersions) a 208.4 mm beam covers 208.4 / cos(70.2) = 615 mm of the echelle, more than 408 mm; the other assignment (alpha = 59.2: 407 mm) fits the 408 mm length exactly but gives magnification -9.7 and 2.1 A/mm at order 100. The sources do not discuss it; the preset follows the dispersion and magnification evidence.
- **Order separation in arcsec**: V87 Table I, order 100: 0.23 mm = 6.47 arcsec (28.1 arcsec/mm at the detector; geometry gives 27.8) vs HOR, same order: 0.231 mm = 6.898 arcsec (29.9 arcsec/mm, +7%). The arcsec column of the manual is not consistent with its own f_col, f_cam and 1.89 arcsec/mm; the scored value uses the geometry.
- **Resolving power**: R = 30,000 (V87, 1987, TI chip) / 40,000 (F14, MB92, before 1994) / 62,000 (B96, after the 1994 camera upgrade; 'intrinsic' 120,000) / 64,000 (F14, Dewar 13, 1.2 arcsec, measured) / 47,000 (Dewar 6) / 56,000 (Dewar 8) / 'about 60,000-100,000 depending on the detector' (HIN) / 50,000-115,000 (F14, by decker). The slit-limited model for 1.2 arcsec is 62,560 (DERIVED).
- **Wavelength format of the 2048 chips**: MB92: 3800-9500 A in ~100 orders on the Ford chip; F14: the Ford format spanned 4850-6700 A, Dewar 13's 4850-9362 A; HNV: the 2048 chips capture 'virtually the entire useful spectrum'. The format is a setting (dewar height and grating tilt); the sum of the order separations (28.5 mm) fits a 30.72 mm chip.
- **Dewar 6 profile**: HCC: narrow-slit FWHM ~2.2 px (Dewar 6) vs ~1.45 px (Dewar 8); F14: R = 47,000 (Dewar 6), 56,000 (Dewar 8), 64,000 (Dewar 13) with the 1.2 arcsec slit.

### Notes

- THE RECOMMENDED SETTING is the planet-search configuration with the Dewar 13 CCD (1994 Nov camera upgrade to Feb 1998): it is the era whose resolution was published as R = 62,000 (B96) and measured as 64,000 (F14), and the narrowest line profile of the program (Dewar 6 and Dewar 8 broaden it by charge diffusion, which the explorer does not model). The geometry is the same for every detector era; only the pixel format changes.
- The Hamilton's central wavelength in the iodine region is chosen (5500 A) rather than sourced: the format is a setting. The iodine cell, the collimator mask and the 600 um (1.13 arcsec) slit are not drawn.
- The 11 deg turn at the collimator and the 11 deg between the collimator and camera beams at the echelle are the same angle by design (V87: the dispersed beam runs alongside the main I-beam).
- The explorer's model cannot represent: the pre-1994 asymmetric profile; charge diffusion of the thinned and deep-depletion chips; the echelle footprint (a beam wider than the ruled length); the order curvature the manual mentions; the collimator mask; a catadioptric camera's central obstruction (the Schmidt's fold flat with its hole).
- The Hamilton is also fed by the 0.6-m CAT with the same f/36 beam but 9.45 arcsec/mm (HQR); a CAT preset is the same optics with D = 0.6 m (not recommended).
- The manual's 'Slit on Detector 330 um/pixel' is a magnification statement (22:1 in the dispersion direction), not a slit length.

## Glass dispersion formula, round 7 (cross-dispersing prisms)
