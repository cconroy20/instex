## Round 7 completeness summary

**ELODIE** (one configuration, 1993-2006): the optical prescription is nearly complete from Baranne et al. 1996 (A&AS 119, 373; the scan), supplemented by the OHP user pages (Internet Archive copies; the live pages are gone). Telescope and feed: OHP 1.93 m at the f/15 Cassegrain focus; two 300 um holes (2 arcsec) feeding two 100 um Polymicro FHP fibers (f/5 in, f/15 out, star image on the fiber, a 3x relay to a 300 um image at the collimator). Spectrograph: white-pupil Czerny-Turner. M1 (toroidal, f/15) -> R4 echelle (tan theta = 4, 408 x 102 mm, '31 g/mm', Littrow in conic mounting) -> M2 -> intermediate spectrum -> small flat -> M3 (second collimator) -> 75 mm white pupil -> 40 deg F2 prism + 8.63 deg crown 150 /mm grism at minimum deviation -> f/3 lens camera (real f = 225 mm; equivalent 300 mm for the main dispersion) -> Tk1024 1024 x 1024 x 24 um CCD. 67 orders (156 to 90) over 3906-6811 A, R = 42,000, 2.1-2.25 px per FWHM, 2 A/mm at 4800 A. Fig. 3 of the paper gives (lambda, x, y) at five positions of orders 88, 89, 123, 124, 156 and 157: the linear dispersions (2.133 A/mm at 4964 A), the order spacings (0.34-0.41 mm) and m lambda = 610,600 A come from it (DERIVED).

**Gaps that matter.** (1) No collimator focal length or distance is published (1530 mm is INFERRED from f/15 and the 102 mm pupil; the figures are unscaled perspective drawings). (2) The echelle groove density: the printed 31 /mm and the order numbers of Fig. 3 disagree by 2.5% (needs 31.64-31.78 /mm at gamma = 0, EFFECTIVE). (3) The grism glass is only 'crown'; the camera's real focal length for the cross dispersion (225 mm in the footnote) does not reproduce Fig. 3's spot positions (they need ~294 mm). (4) The explorer's echelle model takes ONE cross-disperser type, so the prism + grism pair cannot be entered (see 'Mapping to the explorer').

**On/off-axis verdict: OFF-AXIS** (quotes in the collimator_axis row): the fiber head is not in the returning beam; M1 and M2 are separate mirrors, 'off-axis illumination of M1 and M2'. Do not set colTilt 0.

### Mapping to the explorer's cross-disperser options

The explorer's echelle mode has cross-dispersers 'prism' (a train: `xPrisms`, or `xGlass`/`xA` repeated `xPasses` times), 'grism' (`xGlass`, `xG`, `xGrA`: one flat face and one grooved face), 'grating' and 'vph'. `xd` holds ONE of them: ELODIE's prism followed by a grism is not representable. A prism alone is a poor substitute (above: rms >= 0.76 mm and a red order spacing 0.22-0.27 mm against 0.41 mm measured); a grism alone gives the red-heavy spacing only (spacing growing as lambda^2). What the model needs (physics.js, the `xdStages` construction around lines 305-345): allow `xd: "prism"` (or a new `"prismgrism"`) with a trailing grism stage, i.e. append the two stages the 'grism' branch builds ({kind: "face", nrm: r} and {kind: "gface", nrm: m2, g: ...} with the apex `xGrA` = 8.63 deg, `xG` = 150, `xGlass` = a crown) after the loop over `xPrisms` = [{glass: "F2", A: 40}], with `r` the direction left by the last prism. The prism is at minimum deviation for lamC (as the loop does) and the grism's first face is normal to the beam (the 'grism' branch, equal to minimum deviation: see the check below). Crown: N-BK7 is on the list (INFERRED; the real crown is not named). With fcam = 300 mm the sourced elements then place the orders within about 0.4 mm rms of Fig. 3 (0.37 mm at the fitted 294 mm).

## Conflicts between sources

- Echelle blaze: tan theta = 4 (theta = 76 deg) in the text and OHP page vs 'Grating(theta=75 deg)' in Fig. 1 of the same paper.
- Echelle grooves: printed 31 /mm vs the order numbers of Fig. 3, which need 31.64 /mm (delta = 75 deg) or 31.78 /mm (delta = 75.96 deg) at gamma = 0; with 31 /mm gamma would have to be 12.7 deg.
- Camera focal length for the cross dispersion: footnote 225 mm vs Fig. 3 (y positions) which need ~294 mm for F2 40 deg + a BK7-type 8.63 deg / 150 /mm grism.
- Entrance aperture: '300 um (2 arcsec on the sky)' vs 300 um / (15 x 1930 mm) = 2.137 arcsec.
- Coverage edges: 3906-6811 A (B96) vs 3895-6815 A (OHP, M04) vs 3850-6800 A (OHP summary).
- Resolving power: 42000 (B96 average, nominal elsewhere) vs 45000 'measured' (OHP guide) vs 40000 'effective' geometric (B96 Sec. 2.4).
- Fiber length: 20 m (B96) vs 25 m (OHP, 1997 scrambled pair).
- Fig. 2 caption 'inter-order spacing is remarkably constant' vs the Fig. 3 coordinates (0.34 mm at center, 0.41 mm at both ends).
- Fig. 3: CCD 'size 25.25 mm2' vs 24.58 mm (1024 x 24 um); box '4937 x = -12.111 y = -0.518' printed twice (orders 123 and 124).
