# Imager presets: ESO VST / OmegaCAM and LBT / Large Binocular Camera (LBC-Blue, LBC-Red)

Compiled 2026-10-19 for the instex IMAGER mode, in the format of `data/imaging/widefield.md`. Every number comes from a document saved unmodified under `rawdata/imaging/vst/` or `rawdata/imaging/lbc/`; each value carries its source key, location and a short verbatim quote. **DERIVED** marks arithmetic done here (shown). All 208 quotes (main values, alternatives, figure captions and the quotes cited in notes) were machine-checked against the extracted text of the saved files (pdftotext -layout and raw for PDFs; tag-stripped text and raw source for HTML; .ini/.ahead as is), with glyphs normalised (Unicode NFKC, curly quotes, primes, dashes, minus, tilde operator, soft hyphen, micro sign, white-bullet degree) and whitespace collapsed (a quote also passes with all whitespace removed); ' ... ' joins non-adjacent pieces. Numbers that exist only in figure images (LBTO LBC drawings, the raster Fig. 1 of Bellini & Bedin 2010) are marked FIGURE: the annotation as read, the machine-checked caption sentence, and the tesseract OCR evidence. Rows are listed top to bottom; x = along a row, y = across rows; gaps are between light-sensitive areas.

## Completeness summary

**VST / OmegaCAM**: complete except the central obscuration, which is only bounded below (eps >= 0.36 from the 0.938 m convex M2; the M2 and "chimney" baffles are not dimensioned). D = 2.61 m, Cassegrain focus of a modified Ritchey-Chretien (concave hyperbolic M1, convex hyperbolic M2, no tertiary), published f/5.5; f_eff = 14,526 mm DERIVED from 0.213"/px (F = 5.57); M1 f/1.82 DERIVED from R = 9509 mm; corrected field 1.47 deg. Direct imager behind a two-lens corrector + filter + dewar-window lens. 32 science e2v CCD44-82, 2048 x 4100 used, 15 um, rows [8, 8, 8, 8], long axis along the columns; gaps 1.5 mm in a row, 5.64 / 0.82 / 5.64 mm between rows (manual "average gap sizes"; they reproduce the 91.4% fill factor). 4 auxiliary 2k x 4k CCDs (2 guide, 2 at +/-2 mm). Validation: 0.213"/px (0.214 ESO page), 1 x 1 deg, 268 Mpix.

**LBT / LBC-Blue**: complete except the central obscuration (NOT FOUND; eps >= 0.106 from the 0.889 m M1 hole). D = 8.4 m, prime focus of the parabolic F/1.142 primary, F/1.45 with f_eff = 12,180 mm (G08), 6-lens fused-silica Wynne-type corrector (incl. cryostat window), filters between L5 and L6; corrected field 110 mm = 0.52 deg. 4 science e2v CCD42-90, 2048 x 4608, 13.5 um, rows [1, 3] (chip #4 rotated on top), gaps 1.0 mm in the row and 1.03 mm to chip #4 (G08; on-sky 0.84-0.88 / 0.70 mm, BB10). **LBT / LBC-Red**: as LBC-Blue but with fewer red-specific numbers: f_eff = 12,205 mm DERIVED from the LBTO 16.9"/mm stated for both cameras (F = 1.453; LBTO also says f/1.47), 6-lens BK7 corrector, corrected field 118.5 mm = 0.56 deg (LBTO drawing; R16 says 108.2 mm), deep-depletion CCD42-90 in the same [1, 3] layout, gaps 70 px = 0.945 mm (shared R16/LBTO statement). Validation: 0.2255"/px average; ~23' x 25'; 37.7 Mpix DERIVED. Central obscuration NOT FOUND (>= 0.106).

**Recommended preset:** D = 2.61 m (M1 aperture; 2650 mm blank), eps = 0.36 (0.938 m M2 / 2.61 m DERIVED; a lower bound -- the M2 baffle and the "chimney" baffle are not dimensioned in any source found), F = 5.57 = f_eff/D DERIVED (published "f/5.5 modified Ritchey-Chretien"), f_eff = 14,526 mm DERIVED from the manual's 0.213"/px at 15 um for the two-lens corrector (the 0.214"/px of the ESO page gives 14,458 mm; the manual's "1mm=14.3 arcsec" gives 14,424 mm). Telescope: Cassegrain focus of a modified Ritchey-Chretien (concave hyperbolic meniscus M1, convex hyperbolic M2, no tertiary; the two-lens corrector is mounted on the back side of the M1 cell, so the focus lies behind the primary). Primary f/1.82 DERIVED from R = 9509 mm (drawing only; ESO's "(f/3.6)" is R/D). Corrected field 1.47 deg diameter (A10; 1.5 deg in the 1998 design, 1.4 deg in K02 and the manual's "All CCDs fit inside a circle of diameter 1.4 degrees"); 1 deg x 1 deg science array. Optics: direct imager; two-lens corrector (two spherical lenses, L1 and L2) + planar filter + spherical dewar entrance window acting as the third lens, all in the converging beam; an ADC + single-lens corrector exists but has not been offered since P99. Detector: 32 science e2v CCD44-82, 2048 x 4100 used (2048 x 4102 active), 15 um, in 4 rows of 8 (N up, E right at PA 0), 4100-px axis along the columns. Gaps (manual "average gap sizes"): 1.5 mm between the long sides (within a row, 7 per row); between rows 5.64, 0.82, 5.64 mm (top to bottom; the wide gaps carry the readout registers / bond wires). Not science: 4 auxiliary 2k x 4k CCDs (2 guide G1/G2, 2 image-analysis at +/-2 mm from focus) 15.9 mm outside the array. Validate: 0.213"/px (two-lens; 0.214 on the ESO page; 0.215 with ADC), 1 deg x 1 deg, 268 Mpix science (16k x 16k), fill factor 91.4%.

### Not found

- The diameter of the central obscuration as built (M2 baffle / M1 "chimney" baffle): only the 0.938 m M2 diameter (EPV, EPM) and the 600 mm M1 hole (EPM) are published; the 2014 baffle upgrade (MAN Sec. 2.9.1) is described without dimensions.

### Conflicts

- Pixel scale (two-lens corrector): 0.213"/px (MAN, P109) vs 0.214"/px (EOI, R14, THELI ini) vs 0.21343 (THELI astrometric solution, DERIVED) vs the manual's own "1mm=14.3 arcsec" = 0.2145"/px; ADC: 0.215"/px (all).

- Mirror "focal ratios": EPM lists M1 "(f/3.6)" and M2 "(f/4.7)", which are R/D; the M1 focal ratio is f/1.82 (DERIVED, R/2D).
- Total pixels: 268 Mpix science (MAN) vs "300-megapixel" / "almost 300 million" (K11, includes the 4 auxiliary CCDs: 302 Mpix DERIVED).
