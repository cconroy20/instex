# Imager presets: reimaged imagers (FORS2, IMACS f/2, LRIS, MOSFIRE, NIRC2) -- sourced optical parameters

Compiled 2026-10-19 for the IMAGER mode of the instex Instrument Explorer, in the format of `data/round4/mosfire.md`. Model: telescope focus (D, F_tel) -> ideal collimator f_col (= F_tel x W) -> collimated beam / pupil of diameter W -> ideal camera f_cam -> detector. Effective focal ratio = f_cam / W; pixel scale = 206265 x p / (D x F_tel x f_cam / f_col). Every number carries a source key, location and a short verbatim quote from a document saved unmodified under `rawdata/imaging/<inst>/` or an existing `rawdata/presets/` file (existing keys from the spectrograph rounds are reused, not re-sourced). **DERIVED** marks arithmetic done here (shown). Quotes (266, including every ALT quote; 0 failures) were machine-checked against the extracted text of the saved files: pdftotext (layout and raw) for PDFs, an HTML-to-text pass for web pages, an astropy dump of the FITS header (FD300V), and tesseract OCR for the two scans without a usable text layer (AR92, KNRM). Glyphs were normalised (Symbol-font private-use mu/degree/double-prime glyphs, curly quotes, primes, dashes, minus, tilde and multiplication signs; '|' table separators dropped); a piece may match after joining hyphenated line breaks or with spaces removed (pdftotext renders 6.8' as "6.′ 8"); ' ... ' joins non-adjacent pieces. Machine-readable twin: `data/imaging/reimagers.json` (adds `preset_inputs` per instrument).

**VLT/FORS2 (imaging, SR collimator; HR noted)**: complete. Telescope from the VLT interface spec (entrance pupil 8115.0 mm set by M2, f/13.4106, 108827 mm, 528 um/"; M2 1116 mm -> eps = 0.1375 DERIVED; M1 f/1.8). MIT mosaic: 2 chips of 4096 x 2048 x 15 um stacked in y, one gap of 480 um (32 px DERIVED) with 30 um x-offset and 0.08 deg rotation. Field: SR limited by the MOS unit in the focal plane to ~6.8' x 6.8' (7.1' x 7.1' nominal; mosaic covers 8.6' DERIVED), HR limited by the CCDs (4.2-4.3') with camera-lens vignetting in the corners. Validation: 0.12591"/px measured (SR, unbinned; DERIVED 0.12519), HR 0.06323 (DERIVED 0.06255). Gaps: no source states the gap in pixels or the pupil position relative to the filter wheels; W published (90 mm) and W = f_col/F (91.9 mm) differ by 2%.

**Magellan/IMACS f/2 (imaging)**: complete. Baade 6.5 m at f/11 (71526.4 mm, 1998 design), primary f/1.25, central obscuration 29% (measured for the twin Clay telescope; secondary baffle). Field: the 8192-px mosaic spans 28' with gaps; light is unvignetted to R = 12', ~10% vignetted at R = 15', and baffled beyond (corners dark) -> 670 sq arcmin (DERIVED 676 for a R = 15' circle on the mosaic). Validation: 0.200"/px (DERIVED 0.2005), 27.4' field. Weak points: collimator and telescope scale are 1998 design values; the obscuration is the Clay value; which f/2 mosaic edge is the long side is inferred from the 4 x 2 layout.

## VLT (UT1 Antu) / FORS2 imaging (IMG) -- all-dioptric focal reducer at the Cassegrain focus; standard-resolution (SR) collimator, high-resolution (HR) collimator as the alternative

**Recommended preset:** SR collimator, MIT mosaic, unbinned 15 um pixels (the default imaging readout is 2x2 binned = 0.25"/px). D = 8.115 m (entrance pupil, set by M2; 8.2 m = M1), eps = 1116/8115 = 0.1375 DERIVED (secondary alone, sky baffle retracted), F_tel = 13.4106 (Cassegrain), f_col = 1233 mm, W = 1233/13.4106 = 91.9 mm DERIVED (ESO quotes a 90 mm pupil), f_cam = 280 mm (effective f/3.05 DERIVED; manual f/3.13), filters in the collimated beam (broadband) or converging beam (interference), detector = 2 rows of 1 chip (4096 x 2048 each, 15 um) with a 480 um (32 px) gap between the rows, field = 6.8' x 6.8' square (MOS-unit limit). ### Notes

- Reuse: telescope, collimator, camera and detector values are the round-5 FORS2 records (FUM117, AR92, FD300V, VLTSPE); new here are the imaging field (FUM117 Sec. 2.3.1, FOVW, FINST, FMIT), the filter locations, the pupil diameters stated by ESO (FINST), the chip-gap geometry (FUM117 Table 2.7 + FD300V header), M1 f/1.8 and the M2 size (EM1, EM2).
- Imaging mode puts no disperser in the beam: the grism wheel is empty and the camera sits on the collimator axis (same straight-through geometry as the grism presets).
- Two filter planes: broadband filters sit in the collimated beam (every field point uses the whole filter; filter size >= 138 mm for the full field), interference filters in the converging beam just before the CCD (AR92: to avoid position-dependent bandpasses in the parallel beam).
- Default imaging readout is 2x2 binned, 200 kHz, low gain (FUM117 Sec. 2.7.1: "200kHz,2x2,low — direct imaging IMG, OCC IPOL IPOL"); the validation scale is quoted per unbinned pixel.
- The two chips are offset 33" from the optical axis so that the field centre falls on chip 1 (unbinned y ~ 238 of chip 1 in SR, FUM117) and the gap falls between two MOS slits (FMIT).

### Notes

- Reuse: telescope (CS98N9, LCOT), collimator and pupil (CS98N11, BD03), f/2 camera focal length (BD03, CS98N15) are the round-4 IMACS records; new here are the f/2 imaging field, filter location, the Mosaic3 layout and gaps, and the obscuration (M18).
- The f/2 camera is in line with the collimator: imaging uses the open position of the disperser wheel (no fold), unlike f/4 imaging, which uses a silvered mirror in the disperser wheel.

- The Durham IFU, MMTF and the guiders are not part of the imaging preset; the two outboard guiders can shadow 1-2' arcs at the top and bottom of the f/2 field (LCOUM).

### Conflicts between sources

- Narrow-camera scale: 0.009942 +/- 0.00005" (KNG) vs 9.952 +/- 0.002 mas (Yelda et al. 2010, pre-2015, quoted in S16) vs 9.971 +/- 0.004 mas (S16, after the 2015-04-13 realignment) vs nominal 0.01 (KNOM, KTG02).
- AO output f-ratio: 'f/15' (KNG, KTG02) is the nominal label; with D = 10.95 m the f/15 focal length 149.6 m gives f/13.66 (DERIVED).
- Central obscuration: 0.24 of 10.93 m = 2.62 m (H17) vs 2.6 m on a 10 m primary (SE17, 0.26).
- Aperture-mask hole sizes vs the old Keck masks: 9-hole 2.5 mm -> 1.086 m DERIVED (Keck mask '110cm holes'); 18-hole 1.15 mm -> 0.499 m DERIVED (Keck mask '57cm holes'), i.e. the NIRC2 18-hole holes are undersized by 12% while the hole positions match to 0.3%.

### Notes

- Read this preset as two tiers: tier 1 (sourced or robust DERIVED): D, eps, F_tel, f_eff per camera, f_cam/f_col magnifications, effective focal ratios, detector, fields, scales. Tier 2 (inferred DERIVED, flagged): W = 25.2 mm and f_col = 344 mm from the mask drawings, hence the absolute f_cam values. Changing W leaves the pixel scale, field and effective f-ratio unchanged.
- Mask-drawing fit (KNRM, DERIVED here): circle centres were measured on 200-dpi renderings of pp. 2 and 4 (ring-filter peak finding; radii 86 and 42 px), NIRC2 hole centres were read off pp. 1 and 3 (OCR confirms several: '(2.52110,7.85996)', '(-9.58012,6.11331)', '5.92876,7.20237)', '-9.20181,1.53327)', '6.10974,-4.08020)'); the best similarity transform is a mirror image with ~0 deg rotation, residual 0.27% of the pattern radius. Segment grid: horizontal hexagon edges every 116.14 px (p. 2) and 116.21 px (p. 4) = half the 1.5588-m flat-to-flat of a 1.8-m (corner-to-corner) segment.
- AO relay: K-mirror rotator, OAP collimator imaging the primary on the 349-actuator DM (7 mm pitch = 56.25 cm on the primary, KTG02), second OAP restoring f/15 and the pupil position; NIRC2 therefore sees the telescope's f/15 beam and pupil geometry (eps, spiders).
- Coronagraph spots and the vortex masks sit in the focal-plane slit slide; Lyot stops are the pupil masks (fixedhex since 2019, KNOM). Not part of the imaging preset.
