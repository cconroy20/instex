### Conflicts

- Field: 25' x 25' (M15, M06, MCM table, F04) vs 24' x 24' (MCM introduction, LCO pages) vs 0.4 deg on a side (S12C). The 24' value matches the active pixels (24.54' DERIVED), the 25' value the gap-inclusive envelope (25.7' x 25.3' DERIVED).
- Gap units: M15 Sec. 1.1 and Table 1 print 6′ / 31′ (arcmin) -- a misprint; M15 Fig. 3 (caption and image) and MCM give 6" / 31" (arcsec).

- Central obscuration: 0.277 (1.8 m secondary) vs 0.262 (1.7 m) vs 0.355 (DERIVED from the 29 m^2 throughput area, M15 Sec. 8.1).

- Single-CCD field: 160" x 360" published (M15, MCM) vs 163.6" x 368.1" from 2048 x 4608 px at 0.0799"/px (DERIVED).

### Notes

- Explorer mapping: Cassegrain (no Nasmyth flat), direct imager. 9 rows of 4 chips (regular grid, rows aligned), each chip 4608 px along the row and 2048 px across; gaps along every row = 5.24, 1.01, 5.24 mm (31", 6", 31"), between rows 1.01 mm (6"). If the explorer takes one gap_x per preset, it cannot reproduce the 31/6/31 pattern; a uniform 6" gap underestimates the row length by 2 x 25" (50").
- With D = 6.5 m and F = 5.363 (f_eff = 34,859 mm) the explorer reproduces 0.0799"/px at 13.5 um; with F = 5 it would give 0.0857"/px.
- Megacam was recommissioned on Clay in October 2009 and used in routine operation since 2010 (M15); LCO's legacy instrument list now says 'The instrument is no longer offered' (LCOMI).
- Megacam is normally read out binned 2 x 2 (0.16"/px; M15 Sec. 3.1). Two guide CCDs (same type) sit above and below the science array (M15 Fig. 3; MCM: 'centered about 20 arcmin off axis').

### Not found

- Collimator and camera focal lengths as such: FourStar has no collimated space (field-lens doublet + camera); published are the 130 mm pupil image, f/11 -> f/3.6 and 'magnification = 0.33'. f_col = 1430 mm and f_cam = 468 mm are DERIVED equivalents for the explorer's ideal model.
- Cold-stop dimensions: the Ks iris opening and the central-disk diameter (P13 Sec. 7.3 gives neither).
- A Baade-specific central obscuration (only the Clay f/11 value, 29%, M18/L26).
- Separate x and y gaps, and which source is right among 2 mm / 18" (FSMAN), 19" (FSQF, FSH, FSOBS) and 150 px = 24" (P13 Table 1).
- Whether any telescope corrector/ADC sits in the FourStar beam: none is mentioned in the FourStar documents (P13 Sec. 8.1 describes the bare f/11 field as curved and aberrated, corrected by FourStar's own optics).

### Conflicts

- Gap between arrays: ~2 mm / ~18" (FSMAN) vs ~19" (FSQF, FSH, FSOBS) vs 150 px = 24" (P13 Table 1) vs ~1.8-1.9 mm implied by the ZEMAX footprint radii (P13 Fig. 4, DERIVED).
- Field: 10.8' x 10.8' (P13, FSQF, FSMAN, FSH, FSOPT) vs 10.9' x 10.9' (P08, FSHOME) vs 11' x 11' (FSCDR).
- Telescope scale: 71,526.4 mm / 346.77 um per arcsec (CS98N9) vs 2.88"/mm = 71,620 mm and 347 um/" = 71,574 mm (FSCDR, rounded).
- Effective focal ratio: f/3.6 published vs f/3.59 from 0.159"/px (DERIVED); pixel scale 0.159 (most) vs 0.16 (FSOPT, FSCDR slides).

### Notes

- Explorer mapping: Gregorian telescope with a Nasmyth flat (Baade east platform), REIMAGED imager with W = 130 mm, f_col = 1430 mm (= 11 x 130), f_cam = 468 mm (= 3.6 x 130), effective f/3.6; cold stop at the pupil; filters near the detector (converging beam). 2 x 2 square arrays of 2048 px at 18 um with 2.0 mm gaps; the telescope pointing centre falls in the central cross of the gaps (FSOBS).
- With D = 6.5 m, F_tel = 11.004 (71,526.4 mm), f_cam/f_col = 468/1430 the explorer gives 0.1586"/px (published 0.159; 0.3% low). f_cam = 467 mm would reproduce 0.159 exactly.
- The real optics are a field-lens reimager (PANIC-like; FSCDR): L1/L2 near the telescope focus image the primary mirror onto the cold stop (P13 Fig. 18: 'The Ks image of the primary mirror is at the axial position of the stop'), and L3-L6 + L7 form the f/3.6 image. The ideal collimator/camera pair is an equivalent with the same pupil size and magnification, not the as-built layout.
- FourStar was shipped to LCO in August 2010, first light December 2010 (P13 Sec. 1).
