# Imager presets: wide-field survey cameras -- Rubin LSSTCam, Subaru HSC, Blanco DECam

Compiled 2026-10-19 for the instex IMAGER mode, in the format of `data/round4/mosfire.md`. Every number comes from a document saved unmodified under `rawdata/imaging/<inst>/` (`lsstcam/`, `hsc/`, `decam/`); each value carries its source key, location and a short verbatim quote. **DERIVED** marks arithmetic done here (shown). Quotes were machine-checked against the extracted text of the saved files (pdftotext -layout and raw for PDFs; tag-stripped text and raw source for HTML; .py/.yaml as is), with glyphs normalised (Unicode NFKC, curly quotes, primes, dashes, minus, division slash, tilde and degree-ring glyphs) and whitespace collapsed (a quote also passes with all whitespace removed, which covers PDF line breaks and HTML `<sup>` splits such as "deg 2"); ' ... ' joins non-adjacent pieces. Rows are listed top to bottom in the orientation of the cited layout figure; x = along a row, y = across rows; gaps are between active pixel areas unless stated.

## Completeness summary

**LSSTCam**: complete. D = 8.36 m with eps = 0.612 (M1 inner clear edge), f/1.233, f_eff = 10,310 mm (from the pipeline plate scale 20.0059"/mm), 3.5 deg / 9.6 deg^2, 189 science CCDs of two formats (117 e2v 4096 x 4004, 72 ITL 4072 x 4000; 10 um) in 21 rafts on a 127 mm grid, rows 9, 9, 9, 15 x 9, 9, 9, 9 (centred), gaps from the nominal pipeline geometry (1.29-1.78 mm along rows, 2.21-2.50 mm across). Gaps are nominal (as-built layout document LCA-13381 not public). Validation: 0.2"/px, 3.2 Gpix nominal (3.09 Gpix actual formats), etendue 319 m^2 deg^2.

**HSC**: complete except the central obscuration (NOT FOUND: the HSC instrument papers Miyazaki+18 / Komiyama+18 are not on arXiv and the publisher blocked download). D = 8.2 m, F/2.2 published (2.25 from the 11.2"/mm pipeline plate scale; f_eff = 18,416 mm DERIVED), 1.5 deg / 1.77 deg^2, 104 science CCDs 2048 x 4176 (sold as 2k x 4k), 15 um, rows 7, 13, 15, 15, 15, 15, 13, 7 with the long axis along the columns plus 4 CCDs rotated 90 deg beside the top and bottom rows; gaps 1.12 mm in a row and 4.51 mm between rows (1.13 mm where the two mirror-image halves meet), DERIVED from the pipeline geometry; published "approximately 12 arcsec and 53 arcsec". Validation: 0.168"/px, 870 Mpix.

**DECam**: complete; the central obscuration is the Blanco table value 1651 mm / 3934 mm = 0.42, listed for the pre-DECam f/8 configuration (no DECam-era value found). D = 3.934 m used clear aperture (4 m nominal), f/3.0 effective (2.98 DERIVED from 17.575"/mm, f_eff = 11,736 mm), 2.2 deg / 3 deg^2, 62 science CCDs 2048 x 4096, 15 um, rows 3, 4, 5, 6, 6, 7, 7, 6, 6, 5, 4, 3 with the long axis along the rows, gaps 2.3 mm (153 px) in a row and 3.0 mm (201 px) between rows (NOAO handbook, CTIO page; confirmed by the pipeline geometry). Validation: 0.2637"/px centre (0.2626" edge), 520 Mpix science.

### Notes

- Two CCD formats: a single-format explorer preset can use the e2v format (4096 x 4004, 117 of 189 CCDs) and the e2v gaps; the ITL rafts (R01, R02, R03, R10, R20, R41, R42, R43) are 4072 x 4000 with slightly larger gaps.
- Coordinates: OBSL and CTN-001 Fig. 1 are in the focal-plane frame ("The view is looking down from above the focal plane, i.e., through the LSSTCam lenses", CTN-001); the layout is symmetric under 90 deg rotation at the raft level, so rows vs columns is a convention.
- The field centre falls on the centre of CCD R22_S11 (raft R22 at (0, 0), sensor S11 at offset (0, 0); OBSL), not in a gap.
