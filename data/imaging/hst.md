# Imager presets: HST ACS/WFC, WFC3/UVIS and WFC3/IR -- sourced parameters

Compiled 2026-10-19 for the instex IMAGER mode, in the format of `data/round4/mosfire.md`. Every number comes from a document saved unmodified under `rawdata/imaging/acs/`, `rawdata/imaging/wfc3/` or `rawdata/imaging/hst/` (shared OTA documents); each value carries its source key, location and a short verbatim quote. **DERIVED** marks arithmetic done here (shown). Quotes were machine-checked against the extracted text of the saved files (pdftotext layout and raw, per page; HTML with tags stripped; the Tiny Tim plain-text tables), with glyphs normalised (curly quotes, primes, dashes, minus, x for the multiplication sign, u for micro); ' ... ' joins non-adjacent pieces; table rows are quoted as whitespace-separated cells. Locations give the printed page; [PDF p. N] is the page of the saved PDF (STScI handbooks: printed = PDF - 1). Figure labels with no text layer were checked against tesseract OCR of the 300-dpi page render ("ocr check"); one figure dimension is visual only ("figure check"). Quote checks: 143 against text, 4 against OCR, 1 visual.

## HST / ACS Wide Field Channel (two butted 4096 x 2048 SITe CCDs, 15 um pixels, three silver mirrors IM1-IM3 correcting the OTA spherical aberration; filters in the converging beam)

**Recommended preset:** Model as DIRECT. D = 2.4 m, eps = 0.330, 4-vane spider 25.7 mm (OTA block). Effective focal ratio: published f/25 (C02, S05; consistent with the beam at the 22-deg tilted focal surface, DERIVED 24.9-25.1) and f/26 (Tiny Tim); the explorer's square-pixel direct model reproduces the published mean 0.050"/px only with F = 25.78 (f_eff = 61.88 m, DERIVED) -- recommended. Optics: IM1 (spherical) images the HST pupil onto IM2 (anamorphic asphere = corrector), IM3 (Schmidt-like plate) sends the beam through the two filter wheels onto the CCDs: the pupil image is on a mirror, the filters are in the converging beam, and no pupil diameter or mirror focal lengths are published in the sources found. Detector: rows (top to bottom) [1 chip] [1 chip] = WFC1 over WFC2, each Nx = 4096 x Ny = 2048, 15 um, gap 50 px = 0.750 mm = 2.5" between the long edges. Validate: 0.05"/px (chip centres 0.0493 x 0.0486 WFC1, 0.0498 x 0.0503 WFC2; rhombic pixels, 8% diagonal stretch), FOV 202" x 202", 16,777,216 active pixels (DERIVED). Status: "WFC may be offered as shared risk in Cycle 34" (ACSIHB).

### Conflicts

- Effective focal ratio f/11 (WFC3IHB Table 2.1; W3CDR) vs the CDR's own 29,000 mm (= f/12.08 DERIVED) vs F = 11.46 (x scale 0.135) / 12.79 (y scale 0.121) / 12.10 (geometric mean) DERIVED from the published scales.
- Pixel scale 0.135 x 0.121 (Table 2.1, Sec. 7.4.1, App. B.3) vs 0.13 nominal (Table 5.1, Primer, Tiny Tim).
- Cold mask: central obscuration 0.354 (design; ISR0814, Tiny Tim) vs 0.370 +/- 0.007 (on-orbit fit, ISR1410); spider width 0.061 (= 0.432 mm, Telfer) vs 0.077 +/- 0.001 (fit) vs 0.526 mm (Ball memo, = 0.0738 DERIVED); mask offset 0.02, -0.02 (fit). ISR1410 attributes part of the difference to on-orbit focus moves changing the pupil scale at the mask.
- FOV 136 x 123 (as built) vs 123 x 139 (CDR design).
