Compiled 2026-10-19 for the instex IMAGER mode, in the format of `data/round4/mosfire.md`. Every number comes from a document saved unmodified under `rawdata/imaging/megacam/`, `rawdata/imaging/ztf/` or `rawdata/imaging/small80/`; each value carries its source key, location and a short verbatim quote. **DERIVED** marks arithmetic done here (shown). Quotes were machine-checked by script against the pdftotext (layout and raw) / HTML text of the saved files, after removing whitespace and table separators and normalising Unicode (NFKC), quotes, dashes, mu and degree glyphs and line-end hyphens; ' ... In the arXiv text layers the arcsec double prime appears as '00' ("1.0100" = 1.01", "0.18500" = 0.185"); such quotes are given as the text layer has them. Coordinates: x = E-W (along a row of chips), y = N-S (between rows); rows are listed top (North) to bottom (South).

## Palomar 48-inch Samuel Oschin Schmidt / ZTF camera (prime-focus Schmidt: aspheric trim plate + achromatic doublet corrector at the stop, spherical primary; 16-CCD mosaic inside the tube)

**Recommended preset:** Schmidt prime focus. D = 1.2446 m (entrance pupil = aperture stop at the corrector; D20 Table 1 "Entrance pupil diameter 1244.6 mm"), spherical primary 1828 mm diameter, radius of curvature 6124 mm (stop at its centre of curvature, focal surface mid-way, R = 3062 mm). F = 2.46 (D20 "Working focal ratio 2.46"), f_eff = 2.46 x 1244.6 = 3062 mm DERIVED (plate-scale check: 15 um / 1.01" = 3063 mm). eps = 0.473 DERIVED = sqrt(0.224), the area-equivalent of the published on-axis obscuration "22.4% including spiders" (cryostat + window frame + guide/focus CCDs at prime focus inside the tube, plus three spider vanes); without the 2.3% spider share eps = sqrt(0.202) = 0.449. Detector: 16 science CCDs e2v CCD231-C6, 6144 x 6160 px of 15 um, in 4 centred rows of 4 (rows top to bottom: 4, 4, 4, 4); 6144-px axis (x) E-W, 6160-px axis (y) N-S. Gaps (DERIVED from the published on-sky inter-detector gaps 0.140 deg E-W and 0.205 deg N-S at 1.01"/15 um): x 7.49 mm, y 10.96 mm (these reproduce the published field 7.315 x 7.528 deg vs 7.323 x 7.504 deg measured; the camera papers' physical mosaic size 386 x 395 mm implies 5.79 / 8.47 mm instead -- see conflicts). Plus 4 guide/focus CCDs (STA3600A, 2048 x 2048 x 15 um) near the corners, outside the science rows. Validate: 1.01"/px; field 7.32 deg E-W x 7.50 deg N-S = 55.0 deg2; light-sensitive 47.7 deg2 (DERIVED 47.66); fill factor 86.7%; 605,552,640 px ("600 megapixel").

### Notes

- Schmidt geometry for the drawing: corrector/stop at the mirror's centre of curvature, 6124 mm from the 1828 mm spherical mirror; curved focal surface (R = 3062 mm) mid-way, where the cryostat sits facing the mirror. The window reduces the focal-surface curvature from 3062 mm to 7000 mm (D20 Sec. 3.2.3); the CCDs sit on chords of that residual sphere.
- D = 1.2446 m is the stop; the mirror (1.828 m) is oversized so that off-axis beams walk across it -- the walk-off is what vignettes the corners.
- The obstruction (cryostat at prime focus) is large and roughly rectangular; the area-equivalent eps = 0.473 (with spiders) or 0.449 (without) is the closest circular stand-in.
- Pixel axes from the IPAC supplement Fig. 1 (image): CCD +x along the E-W (RA) direction, +y along N-S; CCDs 01-04 bottom row ... 13-16 top row.

### Conflicts between sources

- Pixel scale: 0.187"/px at the field centre (CGI, CIX) vs 0.185"/px (CID, FITS PIXSCAL, R09). f_eff 14.89 vs 15.05 m (DERIVED).
- Focal ratio: F/4.1 (R09) vs 4.16 (DERIVED from 0.187"/px on 3.58 m) vs 4.20 (from 0.185"/px). F/4.1 implies 0.190"/px.

### Notes

- Layout read from CRD's header COMMENT block (North on top, East to the left): row 1 = CCDs 00-08; row 2 = 36, 09-17, 37; row 3 = 38, 18-26, 39; row 4 = 27-35. The two upper rows are flipped 180 deg in readout (irrelevant to geometry).
