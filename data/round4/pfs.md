## PFS completeness summary

**PFS**: all four arms. Telescope, fibre and microlens from Sugai+15 (S15) and the Subaru/IPMU parameter tables; spectrograph optics from the 2012-2014 design papers: Schmidt collimator f/2.5 with a 275 mm beam (B14), slit-to-collimator distance 693.5 mm (S15), Mangin-Schmidt cameras f/1.09 with 300 mm focal length (B14), and the VPH design table (B14 Table 1: 711.4 / 556.7 / 569.5 l/mm with incidence and diffraction angles). The medium-resolution grism is 1007 l/mm between two 13 deg S-LAH53 prisms (P14). Detector formats from the PFS datamodel (4176 rows along dispersion x 4096 columns for the CCD pair; 4088 x 4088 for the H4RG); slit layout (651 holes per spectrograph: 597/600 science + 16 engineering) from the pfs_utils fibre map. Validation targets: the Subaru/IPMU table (A/pix, FWHM, R per arm, July 2024 lab values). The grating equation with the B14 angles and f_cam = 300 mm reproduces the published A/pix to within 5% and the arm edges to within 2% (DERIVED). NOT FOUND: as-built (production) grating parameters, the MR grism incidence geometry, and the 2022-2024 SpS papers (Smee+22, Madec+24, Dohlen+24; no open copies).

## Subaru / PFS (Prime Focus Spectrograph: ~2400 fibres into four identical Schmidt spectrograph modules; VPH arms b, r (low-res), m (red medium-res grism), n)

**Recommended preset (blue, b):** Blue arm: D = 8.2 m; fibre output f/2.8 (microlens F/2.2 -> F/2.8; collimator accepts f/2.5); 127 um core = 1.12" at field centre (slit-equivalent (sqrt3/2) x 1.12 = 0.970", DERIVED); 600 science (597 in SM1/SM2) + 16 engineering fibres on a 651-hole slit (pitch 213.93 um); Schmidt collimator MIRROR, f_col = 693.5 mm (slit-to-collimator distance, S15), beam W = 275 mm; Mangin-Schmidt camera f = 300 mm, f/1.09; VPH 711.4 l/mm, order 1, alpha = 7.2 deg, beta = 14.2 deg (camera angle alpha+beta = 21.4 deg, DERIVED), lambda_c 519 nm, range 380-650 nm; two Hamamatsu 2k x 4k CCDs, 15 um, 4176 px along dispersion x 4096 px spatial. Validate: ~0.7 A/px, ~2.1 A FWHM (3.0 px), R ~2500 at 500 nm (2300 in T16). DERIVED: 0.681 A/px, edges 377.6-660.6 nm.

**Recommended preset (red low-res, r):** Red low-res arm: same telescope, fibre, slit, collimator (693.5 mm, W 275 mm) and camera (300 mm, f/1.09) as b; VPH 556.7 l/mm, order 1, alpha = 9.5 deg, beta = 16.5 deg (camera angle 26.0 deg, DERIVED), lambda_c 805 nm, range 630-970 nm; two Hamamatsu 2k x 4k CCDs (standard thickness), 15 um, 4176 x 4096. Validate: ~0.9 A/px, ~2.7 A FWHM (3.0 px), R ~3000 at 800 nm. DERIVED: 0.861 A/px, edges 625.1-982.7 nm.

**Recommended preset (red medium-res, m):** Red medium-res mode (same red camera and CCDs; grism swapped in for the red VPH): VPH 1007 l/mm (P14 prints 'lines/um') between two 13 deg OHARA S-LAH53 prisms (nd = 1.80610), centred on 7992 A, range 710-885 nm; the prisms keep the camera where it is for the low-res grating. A plane VPH can only stand in for the grism: Littrow equivalent alpha = beta = 23.73 deg (camera angle 47.46 deg, DERIVED). Validate: ~0.4 A/px, ~1.6 A FWHM (4.0 px), R ~5500 at 800 nm (5000 in T16/S15). The Littrow equivalent gives 0.455 A/px and edges 702.6-891.4 nm, so A/px comes out 14% high (DERIVED); the grism incidence geometry is NOT FOUND.

**Recommended preset (NIR, n):** NIR arm: same telescope, fibre, slit, collimator and camera focal length (300 mm, f/1.09) as b; VPH 569.5 l/mm, order 1, alpha = 14.9 deg, beta = 21.9 deg (camera angle 36.8 deg, DERIVED), lambda_c 1107 nm, range 940-1260 nm; Teledyne H4RG-15, 4k x 4k, 15 um, 1.7 um cutoff, 4088 x 4088 active (4-px reference border). Validate: ~0.8 A/px, ~2.4 A FWHM (3.0 px), R ~4500 at 1100 nm (4300 in T16). DERIVED: 0.815 A/px, edges 937.4-1268.7 nm.

### Notes

- All four spectrograph modules are identical; each holds one slit of 651 fibre holes feeding all three cameras at once (FMR, GFM). Sky and flux-standard fibres are ordinary science fibres assigned to calibration targets (GSP: ~400 sky and 100-200 FLUXSTD per visit across the four modules).
- Explorer mapping: Collimator = Mirror (Schmidt). Use F = 2.8 into the collimator with W = 275 mm (the f/2.5-2.55 acceptance pupil); f_col = 693.5 mm gives f/2.52 with W = 275 mm. D = 8.2 m with F = 2.8 makes 1.12" = 124.7 um, 2% below the 127 um core (F = 2.85, the microlens-corrected focal ratio, gives 127 um).
- Detector axes: Nx (along dispersion) = 4176 rows for b, r, m; Ny = 4096 columns (two 2048-column CCDs, gap parallel to the spectra). NIR: 4088 x 4088 after removing the 4-px reference border (4096 x 4096 physical).
- VPH sign convention: B14 tabulates alpha and beta on opposite sides of the grating normal so that sin(alpha) + sin(beta) = G lambda in first order; camera angle = alpha + beta (21.4 / 26.0 / 36.8 deg).
- Medium-res grism: the explorer can only model it as a plane VPH; the Littrow stand-in (1007 l/mm, 23.73 deg) overshoots the published dispersion by 14% and spans 703-891 nm. A real grism keeps the red camera at the low-res angle (P14).
- The 2023-2024 VPHG re-orientation (T24: 'wrong orientation of the VPHG', fixed by rolling the assemblies 180 deg) restored throughput; it does not change the dispersion geometry.
- Status: PFS has been offered to the Subaru community since March 2025 (TN26 abstract).
