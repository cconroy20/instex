## Round 4 completeness summary (IMACS)

**IMACS**: complete for the f/4 grating mode. Baade 6.5 m at f/11 (71526.4 mm, 346.77 um/"; 1998 design values), refractive all-spherical collimator 1648.46 mm (1998 design value; no as-built number published, but consistent with the as-built 9.0 px/" scale), 150 mm beam, f/4.3 all-spherical camera 641.8 mm, fixed 45 deg spectrograph angle and the tilt convention (alpha = tilt + 22.5 deg) taken from the JavaScript of LCO's grating-tilt calculator, seven reflection gratings (150-1200 l/mm) with blaze angles, the LCO user-manual tilt/coverage/dispersion table (reproduced by the grating equation to <= 6 A), Mosaic2 8192 x 8192 x 15 um (dispersion across 4 CCDs, 3 gaps of 92 px), and a published validation setup (1200 l/mm, tilt 32.4 deg, 0.7" slit: R ~ 11,000, 0.19 A/px; S17, L18, S20) whose papers also tabulate slits per mask (DERIVED mean 51 over 25 f/4 masks). Weak points: collimator focal length and telescope scale are 1998 design numbers; D11's tilt range (19.5-34.5 deg) does not match the user-manual tilt convention; D11 and the user manual quote FWHM without the anamorphic factor. The f/2 grism mode (355.6 mm camera, five grisms) is documented, not modeled.

## Magellan I (Baade) / IMACS (wide-field imaging spectrograph; f/4 long camera with tiltable plane reflection gratings, f/2 short camera with grisms)

**Recommended preset:** f/4 ("long") camera, 1200 l/mm grating blazed at 32.7 deg (Littrow ~9000 A; the CaT grating of S17, L18, S20), first order, grating tilt 32.4 deg (tilt is measured from zero order: alpha = 54.9 deg, beta = 9.9 deg; fixed 45 deg camera-collimator angle), lambda_c = 8250 A (DERIVED), 0.7" slit, ~51 slits per mask (DERIVED mean of 25 published f/4 masks), Baade 6.5 m at f/11 (346.77 um/arcsec), refractive collimator 1648.46 mm (1998 design value) with a 150 mm beam, f/4.3 camera 641.8 mm, Mosaic2 8192 x 8192 x 15 um with 8192 px (4 CCDs) along the dispersion. Validate against: R ~ 11,000 for the 0.7" slit (L18, S17, S20; DERIVED 11,690 including the anamorphic factor 0.584), 0.19 A/px (S17, L18; DERIVED 0.192), at least 7550-8750 A for every slit (L18; S17 7400-9000 A; DERIVED 7462-9026 A for a central slit), 0.111"/px (DERIVED 0.1111). The LCO user-manual table (incl. its row for the same ruling, 1200/32.2 at tilt 32.18 -> 8200 A, 7400-8980 A, 0.191 A/px) is reproduced by the grating equation to <= 6 A (Notes). Low-resolution alternative: 600 l/mm / 8.6 deg (5000 A blaze) at tilt 9.68 deg -> 5180 A, 3640-6750 A, 0.378 A/px (LCOUM); D11's 0.8" example (FWHM 2.75 A, R = 2000 at 5500 A) omits the anamorphic factor. The f/2 grism mode is not modeled (Notes give what it would need).

### Conflicts between sources

- Collimated beam: 150 mm (BD03; CS98N11 "150 mm (5.9 inches)"; D11 Sec. 2.2 "150-mm (6.0-inch)") vs "6.5-inch collimated beam" (D11 Sec. 7.5.4, disperser server) vs "6-inch-beam collimator" (DSB).
- f/2 camera focal ratio: f/2.38 imaging (BD03) vs f/2.3 (D11) vs f/2.5 (LCOUM) vs f/2.4 (DSB); the f/4 camera is f/4.3 everywhere (BD03 also lists "f/2.66 (anamorphic)").
- f/4 camera focal length 641.8 mm (BD03, LCOTC) vs 641.79 mm (CS98N12, design) -- negligible.
- Resolution claims: R = 600-5000 for 1.0" on blaze (D11) vs "up to R ~ 10,000 (1.0 arcsec)" (DSB) vs R ~ 11,000 for 0.7" at 1200 l/mm, tilt 32.4 deg (L18, S17, S20) vs BD03 R/0.5" up to 20,693 (design). D11 and the LCOUM recipe compute FWHM = slit/0.111 x dispersion, i.e. WITHOUT the anamorphic factor cos(alpha)/cos(beta) (0.58-0.95 in IMACS); LCOTC and BD03 include it. The UFD R ~ 11,000 matches the anamorphic-corrected value (11,690 DERIVED), not the uncorrected one (6,820).
- LCOUM grating table: 1200/26.7 first order at tilt 27.88 deg lists 0.188 A/px, but the grating equation gives 0.194 A/px (as for the other 1200 rows); 0.188 is BD03's value for the 1200/26.7 grating at 0.846-1.000 um. The 150 l/mm row lists 1.453 vs 1.472 DERIVED.
- CaT grating blaze: 32.2 deg (LCOUM table) vs 32.7 deg (S17) vs "blazed at 9000 A" (S20; Littrow of 32.7 deg = 9007 A, of 32.2 deg = 8881 A, DERIVED). Smaller rounding differences: 17.5 vs 17.45 deg (1200 blue) and 8.6 vs 8.63 deg (600 blue) between LCOUM and BD03.
- Grating tilt range: D11 "19.5 through 34.5" deg (one GTM to 35.5 deg) vs LCOUM table tilts 3.4-32.18 deg (zero order = 0 deg), and the 1998 design minimum ranges +/-4 deg (300 l/mm) and +/-12 deg (1200 l/mm) (CS98N37). D11's zero point is not stated.
- Number of f/4 gratings: "presently six" (D11) and "a present complement of 6 gratings" (LCOUM text) vs seven distinct gratings in the LCOUM table vs "8 available gratings" (LCOUM throughput caption).
- Slit width at the mask: 0.5" = 173 um at 346.77 um/" (DERIVED) vs "0.5 arc-sec (150 microns ...)" (CS98N29) vs "0.5 arcsec wide slits (170 microns)" (BD03).

### Notes

- Geometry for the app (DERIVED from LCOTC): fixed camera-collimator angle psi = 45 deg; incidence alpha = T + 22.5 deg and diffraction beta = T - 22.5 deg in sin(alpha) + sin(beta) = m G lambda, so lambda_c = 2 sin(T) cos(22.5 deg)/(m G). With f_cam = 641.8 mm and 15 um pixels this reproduces all eight LCOUM table rows (lambda_c within 6 A, ranges within 10 A using the calculator detector width) and BD03's R/0.5" column to < 1%.
- Tilt for the recommended preset: T = 32.4 deg (L18) -> alpha = 54.9 deg, beta = 9.9 deg (same side of the grating normal); lambda_c = 8250.7 A (DERIVED). The blaze peak falls where T = theta_B, i.e. near 8321 A for the 32.7-deg grating (DERIVED in-instrument blaze, 0.9239 x Littrow).
- Anamorphic factor: the slit image in the dispersion direction is 0.7" x 9.0 px/" x cos(alpha)/cos(beta) = 3.68 px for the preset (DERIVED). The spatial scale is 0.1111"/px.
- f/4 detector layout: the dispersion crosses 4 CCDs and 3 long-side chip gaps (~92 px each on Mosaic2); the app's 8192-px Nx ignores the gaps (3.4% of the coverage falls in gaps). Before 2012 the f/4 detector was Mosaic1 (SITe, gaps ~62 px); 2012-2017 Mosaic3 (E2V deep depletion); since December 2017 Mosaic2 (LCOUM, S20).
- Slit-mask field: masks cover the full 30' f/11 field; for the f/4 channel only targets within the 15.4' square reach the detector, and the CaT programs restrict slits to a 15' x 8' region so that 7400-9000 A stays on the mosaic (S17).
- f/2 (short camera, grism) configuration -- what the explorer would need: (1) a grism disperser: transmission grating on a prism, first order passed at zero deviation, camera on the collimator axis ("in-line", "straight through"); prism apex = blaze angle (CS98N17 design), prism glass n(lambda) (BD03 names BSL7Y, S-LAL12, PBH53W for the 15, 17.5 and 34 deg grisms); (2) f_cam = 355.6 mm (0.200"/px), same 150 mm beam and collimator; (3) Mosaic3 on f/2 since Dec 2017 with dispersion along the long CCD axis (2 CCDs, 1 gap) in normal orientation. In ideal optics a plain transmission grating whose diffraction angle at lambda_c equals the prism angle reproduces the grism's linear dispersion (cos A/(m G f_cam); DERIVED match for 200/15.0 and 300/17.5), but not the zero-deviation pointing or the prism's n(lambda) term.
- The LCOTC script also contains unused lines (variables k, x, theta, r, w with constants 18396430, 23.1 deg, 27 x 6.43) that do not feed any displayed output; the displayed outputs use only cmfl, spa, pixel, detwid, skyslit and plate.
- Status: IMACS has been in regular use since 2004 (D11); the user manual and the 2017-2020 papers describe the current f/4 configuration.
