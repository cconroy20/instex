## VISTA / 4MOST (fibre-fed; two identical three-arm VPH low-resolution spectrographs LRS-A/B + one three-arm VPH high-resolution spectrograph HRS)

**Recommended presets (one per arm):** LRS-A and LRS-B are mirror-image copies, so one LRS preset per arm serves both. Common to all six: D = 4.10 m (VISTA M1); F = f/3.0 into the collimator (fibre output f/3; the WFC plate scale 59.4 um/arcsec implies f/2.99 at the fibre input, DERIVED); 85 um core = 1.45" on sky (published; 85/59.4 = 1.43" DERIVED), entered as a slit of (sqrt3/2) x 85 = 73.6 um = 1.24" (DERIVED); 824 spectra per detector (812 science + 12 simultaneous-calibration fibres; 822 with the LRS papers' 10 calibration fibres); collimator = Mirror; detector 6144 px along dispersion x 6160 px x 15 um (TOAD Dispersion HDU 'X = spectral', 'CCDPXC = 6144'). LRS: gratings and angles are the post-FDR manufacturing specification (L20); f_col 600 mm = mirror radius 1200/2 = f/3 x 200 mm (DERIVED); camera 345 mm f/1.73 (2016 PDR, L16), confirmed by the 2023 TOAD tables (the preset reproduces TOAD's centre A/px to <1% and its coverage to <1.5 nm in every arm, DERIVED). Camera angle = alpha + beta at lambda_c (DERIVED). HRS: CONCEPT optics only (2014-2015; the as-built HRS papers are paywalled): beam 250 mm (also on the 2019 post-FDR slides), f_col 750 mm = f/3 x 250 mm (DERIVED), camera 375 mm = '~f/1.5' x 250 mm (DERIVED from H14; matches TOAD's A/px to +3.4 / -6.8 / -1.9%), concept VPH 3586 / 2836 / 2343 l/mm, order 1 assumed, Littrow at lambda_c (DERIVED). lambda_c = wavelength at the centre pixel of the TOAD tables.

- **LRS blue**: D 4.10 m, f/3.0 into a Mirror collimator (f_col 600 mm, W 200 mm), fibre 85 um = 1.45" (slit-equivalent 73.6 um = 1.24"), 824 spectra, camera 345 mm f/1.73, VPH 1351 l/mm order 1, alpha 18.65 deg, camera angle 36.50 deg, lambda_c 463.6 nm (370-554 nm), detector 6144 (dispersion) x 6160 x 15 um.
- **LRS green**: D 4.10 m, f/3.0 into a Mirror collimator (f_col 600 mm, W 200 mm), fibre 85 um = 1.45" (slit-equivalent 73.6 um = 1.24"), 824 spectra, camera 345 mm f/1.73, VPH 1220 l/mm order 1, alpha 22.68 deg, camera angle 44.86 deg, lambda_c 625.4 nm (524-721 nm), detector 6144 (dispersion) x 6160 x 15 um.
- **LRS red**: D 4.10 m, f/3.0 into a Mirror collimator (f_col 600 mm, W 200 mm), fibre 85 um = 1.45" (slit-equivalent 73.6 um = 1.24"), 824 spectra, camera 345 mm f/1.73, VPH 925 l/mm order 1, alpha 22.65 deg, camera angle 44.82 deg, lambda_c 824.3 nm (691-950 nm), detector 6144 (dispersion) x 6160 x 15 um.
- **HRS blue**: D 4.10 m, f/3.0 into a Mirror collimator (f_col 750 mm, W 250 mm), fibre 85 um = 1.45" (slit-equivalent 73.6 um = 1.24"), 824 spectra, camera 375 mm f/1.5, VPH 3586 l/mm order 1, alpha 48.11 deg, camera angle 96.23 deg, lambda_c 415.2 nm (392.6-435.5 nm), detector 6144 (dispersion) x 6160 x 15 um [CONCEPT optics (2014-2015); order assumed; alpha = Littrow at lambda_c (DERIVED); f_cam DERIVED].
- **HRS green**: D 4.10 m, f/3.0 into a Mirror collimator (f_col 750 mm, W 250 mm), fibre 85 um = 1.45" (slit-equivalent 73.6 um = 1.24"), 824 spectra, camera 375 mm f/1.5, VPH 2836 l/mm order 1, alpha 50.73 deg, camera angle 101.47 deg, lambda_c 546.0 nm (516-573 nm), detector 6144 (dispersion) x 6160 x 15 um [CONCEPT optics (2014-2015); order assumed; alpha = Littrow at lambda_c (DERIVED); f_cam DERIVED].
- **HRS red**: D 4.10 m, f/3.0 into a Mirror collimator (f_col 750 mm, W 250 mm), fibre 85 um = 1.45" (slit-equivalent 73.6 um = 1.24"), 824 spectra, camera 375 mm f/1.5, VPH 2343 l/mm order 1, alpha 49.22 deg, camera angle 98.44 deg, lambda_c 646.4 nm (610-679 nm), detector 6144 (dispersion) x 6160 x 15 um [CONCEPT optics (2014-2015); order assumed; alpha = Littrow at lambda_c (DERIVED); f_cam DERIVED].

**Validation targets.** LRS: TOAD centre dispersion 0.308 / 0.330 / 0.434 A/px (ETC doc: 32 px per nm at 450 nm in LRS blue = 0.3125 A/px); TOAD coverage 367.1-554.9 / 522.0-722.9 / 688.4-952.4 nm; TOAD R at lambda_c 5554 / 7251 / 7766; published R 4000-7700 (<R> = 6500); sampling '~3 pixels' per resolution element (2.92 px median in the PDR analysis; TOAD medians 2.6 / 2.6 / 2.5). HRS: TOAD 0.0720 / 0.0958 / 0.1137 A/px (ETC doc: 89 px per nm at 650 nm in HRS red = 0.112 A/px); TOAD coverage 391.9-436.0 / 515.0-573.8 / 609.6-679.3 nm; published R 18,000-21,000 (<R> = 20,000; TOAD 22,400-22,600 at lambda_c); sampling >= 2.5 px ('~3 pixels'; TOAD 2.5-2.6). The app's ideal-optics R at lambda_c with the 73.6 um slit is 5388 / 6741 / 6734 (LRS) and 22,722 / 24,926 / 23,625 (HRS) (DERIVED; see Notes).

### Conflicts between sources

- Fibre on-sky diameter: 1.45" (DJ19, UM19, EUCAP) vs 1.42" (WS19 slide 7) vs 1.4295" (ETC mean radius 0.71476" x 2) vs 1.431" (DERIVED 85/59.4) vs ~1.35" (H14 concept at ~63 um/arcsec).
- Plate scale: 59.4 um/arcsec (UM19, EUSUB) vs ~63 um/arcsec at f/3.32 (H14 concept WFC) vs '~60 mm/arcsec' (WS19; unit typo).
- Simultaneous-calibration fibres per spectrograph: 12 (WS19 slide 22; 36 in total per WS19 slide 17 and EUSUB) vs 10 (L16; L20: '1624 for science and 20 for calibration') vs 'six ... attached to either end' (DJ19, UM19; 6 in total or 6 per end).
- LRS gratings: 1351/1220/925 l/mm at incidence 18.65/22.68/22.65 deg (L20 manufacturing spec, used here) vs 1351/1220/924 l/mm at Bragg 18.35/22.49/22.46 deg (L16 PDR) vs concept 1674/1298/993 l/mm (H14). The L20 and L16 angles give nearly the same camera angle at the TOAD centre wavelengths (36.50/44.86/44.82 vs 36.50/44.86/44.77 deg, DERIVED).
- LRS dichroic angles of incidence: 43.58 deg (blue) / 35.35 deg (red) (L20) vs 43.3 / 35 deg (L16).
- LRS camera: 345 mm f/1.73 (L16; TOAD-consistent) vs concept '~f/1.7 (fl=340mm)' (H14).
- LRS slit: 28 slitlets of 29 fibres (L16, ETC19) vs '30 slitlets make one slit' (ETC19, same table cell).
- LRS total coverage: 370-950 nm (L16, L20, L26, UM19, ESOI, DJ19) vs 'from 390 to 950 nm' (EUSUB) vs 372-950 nm measured (D22) vs 367.1-952.4 nm on the detector (TOAD).
- LRS R: <R> = 6500 (DJ19) vs 4000-7500 (ESOI text) vs >4000-7800 (ESOI table) vs 4000-7700 (EUCAP) vs requirement 10 x lambda/nm and 5000; TOAD model R spans 4444-9663 and exceeds the published figure (e.g. green centre 7251 vs ~6500 read from the figure at 625 nm).
- HRS R: <R> = 20,000 (DJ19) vs 18,000-21,000 (ESOI, EUCAP) vs >18,500 (ESOI table) vs >=18,000 (UM19); TOAD model 19,100-26,800 (centre ~22,500) is higher than the published figure (~18,000-21,000).
- HRS bands: 392.6-435.5 / 516-573 / 610-679 nm (EUSUB, ESOI, TOAD design interval, DJ19 in A) vs 392.6-435 nm blue (UM19 text) vs 'cover 69nm in the 606-681 nm range' (UM19 requirement) vs 392-437 / 515-572 / 605-675 nm (WS19 summary slide) vs concept 393-436 / 516-572 / 610-676 nm (H15).
- HRS camera: '~f/1.5 (fl=340mm)' (H14; internally inconsistent with a 250 mm beam, 340/250 = f/1.36) vs 'F1.8' (H15). The 2023 TOAD dispersion favours ~350-390 mm with the concept gratings; 450 mm cannot cover the published bands (DERIVED, see hrs_camera).
- HRS fibre separation: 170 um (H15) vs ~176 um (H14).
- Spectral sampling: '~3 pixels' (UM19, DJ19) vs median 2.92 px (L16) vs 2.5-3.5 px (WS19) vs >= 2.5 px/FWHM (EUCAP) vs TOAD medians 2.5-2.6 px.

### Notes

- Status: the LRS installation and technical commissioning at Paranal were completed on 18 Oct 2025 (L26 Abstract: 'This work was completed on October 18. 2025.'); science commissioning and verification follow in 2026 (L26 Sec. 8). The HRS numbers here are pre-construction concept values.
- LRS-A and LRS-B are two symmetrical (mirror-image) units with the same optics (L26 Sec. 2: 'The LRS is composed of two symmetrical unit spectrographs including the same optical components'); one LRS preset per arm covers both.
- Collimator choice for the app: both spectrographs use a spherical-mirror Schmidt collimator (LRS: off-axis mirror R = 1200 mm with one aspheric corrector plate per arm placed after the dichroics; HRS concept: reversed off-axis Schmidt) -> 'Mirror'.
- Grating sign convention for the app: transmission VPH, first order; camera angle = alpha + beta(lambda_c) measured from the collimator axis. LRS: alpha 18.65 / 22.68 / 22.65 deg, beta 17.85 / 22.18 / 22.17 deg. HRS (Littrow assumed): alpha = beta = 48.11 / 50.73 / 49.22 deg.
- DERIVED LRS check against TOAD (L20 gratings and angles, f_col 600, f_cam 345, lambda_c = TOAD centre pixel 463.56 / 625.44 / 824.27 nm): A/px 0.3063 / 0.3300 / 0.4353 (TOAD 0.3084 / 0.3300 / 0.4340); coverage 368.3-554.8 / 522.2-723.2 / 688.1-953.2 nm (TOAD 367.1-554.9 / 522.0-722.9 / 688.4-952.4). App-style R with the 73.6 um slit-equivalent width: 5388 / 6741 / 6734 (TOAD 5554 / 7251 / 7766; R with the full 85 um core: 4666 / 5838 / 5832).
- DERIVED HRS check (concept gratings, Littrow at lambda_c = 415.21 / 545.99 / 646.38 nm, f_col 750, f_cam 375): A/px 0.0745 / 0.0893 / 0.1115 (TOAD 0.0720 / 0.0958 / 0.1137); coverage 391.0-436.4 / 516.7-571.2 / 610.0-678.0 nm; app-style R 22,722 / 24,926 / 23,625 (full core 19,678 / 21,586 / 20,459; TOAD 22,565 / 22,416 / 22,447; published 18,000-21,000). Because R depends on G, f_col and alpha but not on f_cam, the HRS R prediction rests entirely on the concept gratings and the f/3 x 250 mm collimator.
- Fibre spacing on the LRS detector is not uniform: 6.5 px between fibres within a slitlet, 0.73 mm (= 28 px on the detector) extra between the 28 slitlets (DERIVED from L16 Sec. 3.1). An app that spreads 824 spectra evenly over the 153 mm slit gets ~7.1 px per spectrum.
- TOAD provenance: the FITS file is the instrument-model input of the public 4MOST ETC (qmostetc 2.6.1).
