# Round 4: Magellan / LLAMAS -- sourced optical parameters

Compiled 2026-10-17 for the instex Spectrograph Explorer, in the format of `data/presets_research.md`. Every number comes from a document saved unmodified under `rawdata/presets/llamas/`; each value carries its source key, location and a short verbatim quote. **DERIVED** marks arithmetic done here (shown). Quotes were machine-checked against the pdftotext (layout and raw) text of the PDFs, the tag-stripped text of the HTML pages and the raw text of the repository files, with glyphs (double primes rendered "00" in F20, micro signs, dashes, curly quotes) and whitespace normalised; " ... " joins non-adjacent pieces. Page numbers are the printed SPIE page numbers (114470A-n, 114476E-n) or the pocket guide's "Page n of 7".

## Round 4 completeness summary (LLAMAS)

**LLAMAS**: good for the feed, collimator, camera and detector; the dispersers are the gap. Sources: the 2020 SPIE status paper (F20, NSF PAR copy), the 2020 stray-light paper (FR20), the 2017 project pocket guide (PG17, two-arm concept), the MIT Specifications page (per-arm coverage and lab R), the public ETC spectrograph definition files (ETC: f_col 200 mm, f_cam 70 mm, f/4.0, 50 mm beam, 110 um core, 0.75" pitch) and the reduction pipeline's arc and trace look-up tables (PYJ). Found: 6.5 m Baade folded port at native f/11; MLA (0.75" hexagonal lenslets) converts f/11 to f/4.4 into 110 um fibres, f/4 accepted by the collimator; 2400 active fibres, 300 per spectrograph and per CCD; 8 spectrographs x 3 arms; spherical on-axis collimator mirror f = 200 mm, 50 mm beam; dichroics at 475 / 690 nm; VPH grisms at the camera pupil; 70 mm f/1.28 (ETC) refractive cameras; 2k x 2k 13.5 um CCD42-40; per-arm coverage and R ranges. NOT FOUND: any VPH line density or angle (the ETC's 1200 /mm entries are unused placeholders) -- EFFECTIVE values are DERIVED by fitting the explorer's own model to the published coverage (B 2233 /mm at 28.1 deg, G 1425 /mm at 25.0 deg, R 1070 /mm at 26.9 deg), and cross-checked against the post-first-light arc tables (2215 / 1442 / 1069 /mm). No source states A/px or sampling; both DERIVED. The companion optics paper (SPIE 11447-111) was apparently never published, and the 2022 and 2026 SPIE papers have no open copy.

## Magellan / LLAMAS (lenslet-fed fibre IFU: 2400 fibres into eight identical bench spectrographs, each split by dichroics into blue / green / red arms with VPH grisms)

**Recommended presets (blue, green, red):** Three presets (B, G, R), identical except for the disperser and band. Common: D = 6.5 m, F = 4.0 (collimator focal ratio; the MLA turns the native f/11 into f/4.4 at the fibre and FRD keeps 95.6% inside f/4), 110 um core (0.873" at f/4.0) entered as the slit of equal line width 0.756" (trace width the same), 300 spectra per detector, collimator = Mirror (spherical, on-axis), W = 50 mm (f_col = 200 mm), camera f = 70 mm (f/1.40 on the beam; ETC f/1.28 monochromatic; F20 effective f/1.05), transmission (VPH) grating in first order, detector 2048 x 2048 x 13.5 um (e2v CCD42-40; red deep depletion). VPH line densities and angles are NOT published: use the DERIVED effective values (symmetric Bragg setting at lamC) that put the published coverage on the 2048 px chip -- BLUE: G = 2233 /mm, alpha = beta = 28.13 deg, lamC = 4222 A (3417-4947 A); GREEN: G = 1425 /mm, 24.96 deg, lamC = 5923 A (4634-7100 A); RED: G = 1070 /mm, 26.93 deg, lamC = 8466 A (6771-10000 A). Validate against: coverage (MITSPEC, by construction); R 1500-2500 / 1400-2400 / 1750-2500 (MITSPEC lab), R ~ 2000 (F20, S26), 2200 (ETC); mean dispersion 0.747 / 1.204 / 1.577 A/px and centre dispersion 0.762 / 1.204 / 1.609 A/px from the post-first-light arc table (independent of the coverage used to set G); fibre image 2.85 px (LSF FWHM 2.47 px); trace pitch 6.46 px.

### Conflicts between sources

- Camera focal length: 70 mm (F20 production units; ETC) vs 90 mm (PG17 2017 concept; F20 prototype).
- Camera focal ratio: f/1.28 'monochromatic' (ETC) vs 'effective f/1.05, in polychromatic light and across all field positions' (F20) vs F/1.4 (PG17, 90 mm design); 70/50 = f/1.40 on the 50 mm beam (DERIVED).
- Dichroic splits: 690 and 475 nm (F20) vs band edges 480 and 690 nm (FR20; ETC) vs one dichroic at 570 nm (PG17 two-arm concept). As built the arms overlap: blue to 4947 A, green from 4634 A; green to 7100 A, red from 6771 A (MITSPEC).
- Red band end: 970 nm (FR20; F20 abstract) vs 975 nm (F20 Sec. 2.4; ETC) vs '1 micron' (MITSPEC, MITHOME) vs ~10048 A at pixel 2047 (PYJ, DERIVED).
- Blue on-chip range: 3417-4947 A (MITSPEC) vs 3240-4808 A from the post-first-light arc look-up table (PYJ, DERIVED; the fit is anchored by lines 3545.6-4657.9 A, so both ends are extrapolated). Green and red agree within 20-50 A. The derived blue G_eff is 2233 /mm from MITSPEC vs 2215 /mm from the arc fit.
- Resolving power: per-arm ranges 1500-2500 / 1400-2400 / 1750-2500 (MITSPEC, lab) vs R = 2000 (F20, FR20, S26) vs R = 2200 (ETC) vs R = 1300 (PG17 concept).
- Total passband: 350-970 nm (F20 abstract, FR20) vs 350-975 nm (F20 Sec. 1) vs 3400 A-1 um (MITHOME) vs 3500-9700 A (S26) vs 360-970 nm (PG17).
- Fibres: 2400 active of 3200 installed, 300 per slit (F20) vs 2,392 spatial elements, 4 x 300 + 4 x 298 (MITSPEC) vs 2560 total and 320 per spectrograph (PG17).
- Field of view: 34" x 35" (MITSPEC) vs 35" x 35" (MITHOME) vs 37" x 37" (F20, FR20, S26) vs 40" x 36" (PG17).
- Fibre run: 5 m total (F20) vs ~10 m from focal plane to the spectrograph bank (MITHOME) vs 4.5 m (PG17).
- Fill factor: 92% (MITSPEC) vs ~91% (MITHOME) vs >= 93% (PG17).

### Notes

- Explorer mapping (DESI convention): D = 6.5 m, F = 4.0 (the spectrograph's own focal ratio, so f_col = F x W = 200 mm comes out right); entrance = slit of equal line width (sqrt3/2) x 0.8727" = 0.756"; the trace width across the dispersion is the same fibre image, so no separate traceW is needed. The real sky sampling is the 0.75" hexagonal lenslet, which images the pupil on the fibre.
- Collimator = Mirror (spherical, on-axis; the slit sits inside the collimated beam at its focus, fibres fanned to the Schmidt condition).
- Disperser: the explorer models a bare plane VPH. LLAMAS uses VPH grisms (prism on the front plate) at the camera entrance pupil; the derived G_eff and symmetric-Bragg angle reproduce the published coverage on a 70 mm camera but do not describe the true grating, prism or camera angle.
- Sanity check of f_cam = 70 mm: with G_eff fixed by the band edges, the model's central dispersion (0.761 / 1.227 / 1.607 A/px) matches the arc-table derivative at pixel 1024 (0.762 / 1.204 / 1.609 A/px), and the arc-table dispersion falls by 21% / 13% / 14% across the blue / green / red chip (model: 19% / 17% / 18%, from cos(beta) at alpha ~ 25-28 deg). The arc data alone cannot separate f_cam from G (degenerate), so 70 mm rests on F20 and the ETC.
- Spectra per detector: all 300 (or 298) are IFU science fibres; sky comes from the field itself and calibration light enters through the same fibres from the front-end calibration unit (F20 Sec. 2.1). A 50% sub-array mode (masks in the IFU focal plane) doubles the trace spacing (MITPROP: 'In this occulted mode, the inter-fiber spacing is doubled at the slit for enhanced control of systematics').
- Two blue detectors (1A and 4A) were damaged in the 2025-2026 data (S26: 'We dithered to cover gaps left by the two damaged blue detectors (detectors 1A and 4A).').
- ETC sensor lines give read noise 2.46 e- (blue, green) and 2.29 e- (red) and dark 0.00119 / 0.00192 e-/s/px; F20 reports 2.3 e- at 75 kHz for the first production heads.
- Pipeline wavelength grid limits (PYJ arcLlamas.py): blue 3200-4947, green 4570-7100, red 6570-10038 A -- consistent with the blue chip reaching below 3417 A.
