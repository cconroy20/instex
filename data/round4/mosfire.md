## Round 4 completeness summary (MOSFIRE)

**MOSFIRE**: complete for the explorer inputs. Telescope (10.95 m; f/13.66 at the CSU, 0.72516 mm/"), refractive six-element collimator f = 1813.0 mm, 7-element refractive camera f = 250.0 mm (f/0.93 under-filled), fixed 40 deg included (camera-collimator) angle, one 110.5 l/mm gold grating blazed at 21.93 deg used in orders 6/5/4/3 for Y/J/H/K at two fixed stops (42.614 deg H/K, 41.524 deg Y/J; 42.6/41.5 as built), HAWAII-2RG 2048 x 2048 x 18 um, CSU with 46 slits of 7.01" on a 5.8 mm (8") pitch, nominal 0.7" slit, and the as-built per-band table (A/px, lambda_c, coverage at the field centre and at +/-1.5', R for 0.7" and 0.48"). The grating equation with these values reproduces the published A/px to <= 0.04%, the K coverage to 2 A, the 0.7" slit image (2.92/2.87 px) and 0.1798"/px (DERIVED). Gaps: f_col and f_cam are design values (no as-built numbers, but the as-built scale and dispersions confirm them); the beam is stated only as a "stipulated" 125.0 mm at an effective f/14.50 that conflicts with the as-built f/13.66; as-built grating angles only to 0.1 deg. Typical objects per mask come from surveys (MOSDEF 28; KBSS 28-34).

## Keck I / MOSFIRE (cryogenic near-IR multi-object slit spectrograph: CSU slit mask, refractive collimator and camera, one 110.5 l/mm reflection grating in orders 6/5/4/3 for Y/J/H/K)

**Recommended presets (Y, J, H, K):** Four presets (Y, J, H, K) that differ only in order, grating stop and central wavelength. Common: D = 10.95 m, F = 13.66 (M12: "the Keck Cassegrain f/13.66 focal plane (nominal scale is 0.72516 mm/")"), slit 0.7" (the nominal width), nObj = 28 (MOSDEF average; 46 slits maximum), collimator = Lens (six-element refractive, f_col = 1813.0 mm), W = 1813.0/13.66 = 132.7 mm DERIVED (the design paper's "stipulated" 125.0 mm beam belongs to its effective f/14.50), f_cam = 250.0 mm, reflection grating G = 110.5 l/mm, blaze 21.93 deg = Littrow first-order blaze 67,597 A DERIVED (the published "6.35 um" is the blaze in the 40 deg geometry, not Littrow), fixed camera-collimator angle psi = 40 deg, HAWAII-2RG 2048 x 2048 x 18 um (17.984 um at 77 K) with Nx = 2048 along the dispersion. Per band (order; published grating stop; as-built lambda_c at the field centre; DERIVED alpha that puts lambda_c at the detector centre): Y (m = 6; 41.524 deg; 10373 A; 41.465 deg), J (m = 5; 41.524 deg; 12450 A; 41.469 deg), H (m = 4; 42.614 deg; 16321 A; 42.572 deg), K (m = 3; 42.614 deg; 21760 A; 42.570 deg). Validate against M12 Table 1 / Keck grating page: 1.0855 / 1.3028 / 1.6269 / 2.1691 A/px (model 1.0856 / 1.3027 / 1.6273 / 2.1698 DERIVED); K 19540-23970 A (model 19541-23972), H 14680-18040 A (model 14656-17980); the Y (9716-11250 A) and J (11530-13520 A) ranges are filter-limited and narrower than the detector span (model 9263-11481, 11118-13779); 0.7" slit image 2.92 px (YJ) / 2.87 px (HK) (model 2.915 / 2.867); 0.1798"/px; R(0.7") measured 3380 / 3310 / 3660 / 3620 (M12; Keck page 3388 / 3318 / 3660 / 3610) vs aberration-free model 3278 / 3279 / 3499 / 3498 (DERIVED). Alternative using the design angles exactly: lambda_c = 10400 / 12480 / 16350 / 21800 A at 41.524 / 42.614 deg (DERIVED from the M10 design statement "2.18", "1.635", "1.248", "1.04" um).

### Notes

- Sign convention for the app (as for DEIMOS): sin(alpha) + sin(beta) = m G lambda with alpha - beta = psi = 40 deg; at band centre beta = +1.5 deg (Y/J) and +2.6 deg (H/K), so the camera axis sits a few degrees from the grating normal -- M12: "the geometry, which has the grating normal only a few degrees from the optical axis" (source of the 0th-order ghosts).
- Blaze convention: the explorer input lamB1 is the Littrow first-order blaze = 2 sin(21.93 deg)/110.5 = 67,597 A (DERIVED). The sources' "6.35 um" is the first-order blaze in MOSFIRE's 40 deg geometry (= Littrow x cos 20 deg, DERIVED); using 63,500 A as lamB1 would shift the blaze peaks ~6% to the blue.
- H and K share one grating stop, Y and J the other, because m x lambda_c is nearly equal within each pair (DERIVED 65,280-65,284 A and 62,238-62,250 A).
- Y and J coverage is set by the order-sorting filters (central-slit ranges equal the filter passbands); the 2048-px detector spans 2217 A (Y) and 2661 A (J) at the field centre (DERIVED), so the explorer's detector-limited coverage will exceed the published Y/J ranges. H and K are detector-limited at the field centre.
- Slits at X = +/-1.5' (+/-65.2635 mm at the CSU) shift lambda_c by 6.216/7.460/9.157/12.209 A per mm (Y/J/H/K; M12, KMG): ~400-800 A at the field edge, which truncates the H and K spectra (82% retained).
- Measured R (M12, KMG) exceeds the aberration-free geometric value (DERIVED 3278-3499) by 0.9-4.6%; KMG's R column equals lambda_c / (Resolution(pix) x A/px) with measured line FWHM 2.74-2.88 px, narrower than the 2.87-2.92 px geometric slit image.
- KMR asks that MOSFIRE papers cite M12 and M10 ("McLean, I.S., Steidel, C.C. et al. 2012 ... SPIE Procs, 8446 17"; "... 2010 ... SPIE Procs, 7735 47").
- Internal temperatures: optics at 120 K, detector at 77 K (M12 Sec. 2); the design was optimised with 120 K indices (M10).
