## Round 4 completeness summary

**MagE**: complete for the optics from Marshall et al. 2008 (SPIE 7014) -- f/11.0, off-axis paraboloid collimator 1094 mm, 99 mm beam, 175 gr/mm 32.3 deg echellette (102 x 128 mm) in quasi-Littrow, orders 6-20, fused-silica prisms 46 deg (double pass) + 40 deg (single pass), 138 mm f/1.4 vacuum Schmidt camera, E2V CCD42-20 2048 x 1024 x 13.5 um -- plus the LCO manual (slits, 0.3"/px, R = 4100 at 1", per-order dispersion table) and a measured R = 4770 (1") from MegaSaura sky lines. Gaps: echelle theta/gamma, prism rotation angles, order separation, the detector-centre wavelength. Conflict worth noting: the quoted '6.2 um first-order blaze' does not follow from 175 gr/mm and 32.3 deg (6.107 um). **ESI**: optics from Sheinis et al. 2002 (PASP 114, 851) and the 1996 UCO/Lick design report -- collimator 2286 mm, beam 160 mm (S02) or 6.57 in = 166.9 mm tip-to-tip (design report, f/13.7), 175 l/mm 32.3 deg echellette (175.555 in the report) 'nearly in Littrow', orders 6-15, two 50 deg Ohara BSL7Y prisms (first in double pass, near minimum deviation), 308 mm Epps camera, MIT/LL 2048 x 4096 x 15 um; Keck pages give slits, R per slit, FSR per order, per-order wavelength solutions and measured spatial scales. BSL7Y is not on the app's glass list: OHARA Sellmeier coefficients added (glass section). Gaps: numeric gamma ('a few degrees'), prism angles of incidence, a line-width measurement of R.

## Magellan II (Clay) / MagE (Magellan Echellette: prism-cross-dispersed echellette, vacuum Schmidt camera; on Magellan I Baade per the 2017 handout and 2024 manual)

**Recommended preset:** 1.0" x 10" slit (the slit every source quotes R for). D = 6.5 m, F = 11.0 (Magellan f/11; 2.88"/mm at the slit), 1 object, mirror collimator (off-axis paraboloid) f_col = 1094 mm -> W = 99 mm as stated (1094/11.0 = 99.45 mm DERIVED), camera: vacuum Schmidt f = 138 mm (f/1.4), echellette 175 gr/mm, delta = 32.3 deg, quasi-Littrow (theta and gamma NOT published: 0 here), orders 6-20; cross-disperser: two fused-silica prisms, 46 deg in double pass + 40 deg in single pass (three prism passes; the 'rotation angles ... adjusted to control the anamorphic beam distortion' are NOT published -> minimum deviation); detector E2V CCD42-20, 2048 (dispersion, DERIVED) x 1024 x 13.5 um. Validate against R = 4100 at 1" (design; M08, LCOMG) and the measured R = 4770 +/- 8 (1") / 2545 +/- 37 (2") from sky lines (R18); the per-order dispersion of M08 Table 2 (0.231 A/px in order 20 at 3125 A ... 0.788 A/px in order 6 at 9700 A = 22.2-24.4 km/s/px DERIVED); coverage 3100 A - 1 um in orders 6-20 with order centres ~6.2 um/m; 0.3"/px along the slit (3 px per arcsec). The central wavelength (which order sits at the detector centre) is NOT published.

### Conflicts between sources

- Blaze: M08 '32.3 deg (6.2 um first order Littrow blaze)' and LCOMG '6.2 micron' vs 2 sin(32.3 deg)/175 = 6.107 um (DERIVED, -1.5%); 6.2 um would need delta = 32.85 deg. The M08 Table 2 central wavelengths give m x lambda = 61,200-62,500 A for orders 9-20 (6.2 um fits these better), 58,200 for order 6.
- Resolution at 1": R = 4100 (M08, LCOMG, MGHO; design) vs 4770 +/- 8 measured from sky lines (R18); at 2": 2545 +/- 37 measured vs ~2000 geometric (DERIVED). R18 calls the measured values 'higher than the design resolution'.
- Slit count: eight widths (M08, LCOMG, MGHO) vs 'Five non-overlapping slits' (B09).
- Spatial scale: 0.3"/px (M08, LCOMG, MGHO) vs 0.33" (B09) vs 0.309" DERIVED from 1094/138 mm and f/11.
- Dispersion: 0.231-0.788 A/px (M08 Table 2) vs '0.3 A/px (blue) to 0.6 A/px' (B09); B09 '22 km/s per pixel' is the blue end of the DERIVED 22.2-24.4 km/s/px.
- Coverage: 3100 A - 1 um (M08 abstract, LCOMG) vs 3200 A - 1 um (M08 Sec. 1; MGHO 3200-10000) vs 3000-10500 A with a 15 A gap near 9500 A (B09).
- Telescope: Clay folded port (M08 2008, B09 2009; R18: 'The majority of observations were conducted with MagE mounted on the Clay telescope') vs Baade folded port (MGHO 2017) / Baade AUX3 (LCOMG 2024).
- Collimated beam: 99 mm (M08 Sec. 2) vs '~100 mm' (M08 Sec. 1) vs 99.45 mm DERIVED (1094/11.0).
- B09 Table 2 order ranges are internally inconsistent (order 12 lambda_min 4286 < order 13 lambda_min 4469; order 20 inside order 19).
- Flexure with elevation: 'at most 0.8 pixels' (M08 Sec. 5.2) vs 'about 0.1 pixels' (LCOMG) -- not used by the app.

### Notes

- M08 states the optical design 'is very similar to the ESI spectrograph (Sheinis et al. 2002)': same 175 gr/mm, 32.3 deg echellette, reflective collimator, two prisms (one double-pass). MagE is ESI scaled to a 99 mm beam on an f/11 telescope with a 138 mm f/1.4 Schmidt camera.
- Format check (DERIVED): Littrow dispersion with f_cam = 138 mm, 13.5 um pixels: 23.2 km/s/px; order 6 FSR (1617 A) spans ~2050 px at the tabulated 0.788 A/px -> the 2048-px axis is the dispersion axis and the reddest order just fills it (B09 notes a 15 A gap near 9500 A between orders 6 and 7).
- The 1094 mm collimator at f/11.0 and the 138 mm camera reproduce the published 0.3"/px (0.309 DERIVED) and the 3-px-per-arcsec slit sampling, so R(1") ~ 4000 is a slit-width (geometric) prediction; the measured 4770 (R18) is 16% higher.
- Throughput (M08 Table 2 / LCOMG): peak 21.8% (telescope + instrument) at 5590 A; 'Efficiency (instrument)' 35.5% -- not used by the app.
- Wavelength calibration: ThAr, 'Typical wavelength solutions give an RMS of 0.06 A for 500 arc lines' (M08 Sec. 3.5).
- Audit 2026-10-17: R18 (Sec. 2.2) dates the move to the Baade telescope: 'some observations were made after October 2015, when MagE was moved to the Baade telescope'. The 15-order count is not an independent check of the prisms: lambda_c was chosen to put orders 6-20 on the chip, the blue end is the explorer's 3200 A cutoff, and a weaker 44 + 44 + 40 deg train also gives 15. The M08 Table 2 order-6 row (9700 A, 0.788 A/px) is inconsistent with the other rows (m x lambda = 58,200 A against 61,200-62,500 A for orders 9-20); the model gives 0.787 A/px at the order-6 blaze centre (10,180 A) and 0.812 A/px at 9700 A.

### Conflicts between sources

- Collimated beam: 160 mm (S02, S99) vs 6.57 in = 166.9 mm for the full tip-to-tip aperture (UCR96 Sec. 3.1) vs 170 mm (UCR96 Sec. 4.1 spec list) vs 152.4 mm (2286/15, DERIVED).
- Telescope focal ratio: f/13.7 (UCR96, for the 10.95 m tip-to-tip aperture) vs f/15 (KESP, KTG02; nominal on ~10 m).
- Groove density: 175 l/mm (S02, KESP, KTG02) vs 175.555 (UCR96) vs 175.6 (S99). Grating size 204 x 204 mm (S02) vs 203 mm square / 203 x 204 mm (UCR96).
- Collimator shape: 'on-axis (nearly) parabolic' imaging an off-axis field (S02) vs off-axis paraboloid (UCR96, KTG02, KESP 'will be') vs 'off-axis segment of an on-axis ellipsoid' (S99).
- Prism glass: Ohara BSL7Y (S02, S99; as built) vs UBK7 / 'UBK7/BK7Y (blue transmitting BK7)' (UCR96, design).
- Number of echellette slits: five (S02) vs six (KOV, KECH, KESP, KTG02).
- Dispersion: average 11.4 km/s/px and range 9.6-12.8 (S02) vs 'constant 11.5' (KECH) vs 11.34-11.52 from the KQR polynomials (DERIVED) vs 15 km/s/px (KTG02 specification).
- Order 6 dispersion: 0.39 A/px (S02 Table 1, KQR, UCR96) vs 0.30 A/px (KOV).
- Coverage upper limit: 1.09 um (S02) vs 10900 A (KOV) vs 1.1 um (KESP) vs order-6 FSR to 11068 A (KECH).
- Scale along the slit per order: predicted 0.127-0.183"/px (S02 Table 1) vs measured 0.120-0.168"/px (KQR, KECH).
- R at 0.3": 13,000 (S02) vs 13,400 (KECH); 0.75": 5400 (KECH) vs FWHM ~45 km/s = R ~ 6700 (P01, DERIVED).
- Minimum order separation: 22.0" (S02) vs 23.0" (UCR96 design table).
- Detector scale: 0.1542 +/- 0.0010 (KESP) vs 0.153 (S02) vs 0.15 (KTG02) vs 0.1535 DERIVED.

### Notes

- Consistency check (DERIVED): f_col 2286 mm, f_cam 308 mm, 0.725 mm/arcsec and 15 um pixels give 0.1535"/px (published 0.1542, 0.153); the Littrow dispersion 11.55 km/s/px agrees with the KQR per-order solutions to 0.3-1.8%; the Littrow FSR lengths reproduce UCR96's 'Width (%)' of the chip to 1-2 points.
- Prism glass: the OHARA BSL7Y Sellmeier reproduces its data-sheet indices to <= 5e-6 from 0.326 to 1.129 um (DERIVED). N-BK7 (on the app list) is 4.7e-4 higher in n and within 0.3% (0.4-0.6 um) to 1.2% (1.0 um) in dn/dlambda, so an N-BK7 stand-in changes the cross-dispersion by about 1% (DERIVED).
- MagE (M08) is described as 'very similar to the ESI spectrograph': same 175 gr/mm, 32.3 deg echellette and two-prism (one double-pass) layout.
- ESI also has a low-dispersion (prism-only) mode and a direct-imaging mode (S02); only the echellette mode is relevant here.
- UCR96 is a January 1996 design document (version 1.0): where it differs from S02 / the Keck pages, the later sources describe the as-built instrument.

## Glass dispersion formulas, round 4 (ESI prisms)

### Formula forms

- **Sellmeier_OHARA**: `n^2 - 1 = A1 lambda^2/(lambda^2 - B1) + A2 lambda^2/(lambda^2 - B2) + A3 lambda^2/(lambda^2 - B3)`; um (B in um^2); 365-2325 nm to about +/-5e-6 (the page text layer prints the exponent as '×106'), limited to the tabulated range when a data sheet lists fewer indices. When calculating a respective refractive index, please bear in mind that each wavelength is expressed in µm units."
