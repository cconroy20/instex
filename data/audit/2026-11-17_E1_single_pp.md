# E1: echelle 3D view, "single" (HIRES) and "pp" (MagE, ESI, FIRE)

Scope: web/app.js build3dEchelle and build3dFolded (kind "pp"), README "Echelle 3D", and the lay3d / gamma / theta / prism
entries in web/presets.js for hires, mage, esi, fire. No project file was edited. Source text extracted to this folder
(vogt.txt, hm94.txt, marshall.txt, sheinis.txt, simcoe13.txt) with pdftotext; nothing was downloaded (all sources were
already in rawdata/presets/). Figures were rendered to PNG here and read by eye.

## Verdict in one paragraph

HIRES "single" is correct, and the "beams 10 deg apart ... clear only near the collimator" claim is sourced (below).
The "pp" arrangement is right in its broad outline for all three instruments (separate collimator and camera, prisms
ahead of the echelle and again on the way back, camera off to the side) but WRONG in the prism bookkeeping for all
three: build3dFolded draws one prism for each two passes and passes every drawn prism twice. None of the three
instruments is built that way. MagE and ESI each have one double-pass prism and one single-pass prism (the single-pass
one after the echelle only); FIRE has four prisms, two double-pass, one single-pass BEFORE the echelle and one
single-pass AFTER it. The drawing, its labels ("2 prisms, passed twice", "3 prisms, passed twice"), and the README rule
"one drawn prism for each two passes" are therefore wrong for MagE, ESI and FIRE.

## A. Confirmed errors (ranked)

### A1. MagE: second prism drawn as a second double-pass 46 deg prism; it is a single-pass 40 deg prism. (high)
- Preset: xPrisms = [{46}, {46}, {40}] (three passes in tracer order; the note says "46 deg in double pass and 40 deg
  single pass").
- Code: build3dFolded draws `Math.max(1, Math.round(train.length / 2))` prisms before the echelle = round(3/2) = 2
  (checked: Math.round(1.5) = 2), taking train[0] and train[1] = 46 deg and 46 deg, then `back()` sends the returning
  light through both in reverse. The 40 deg prism is never drawn. Label on screen: "2 prisms, passed twice", "46° + 46°".
- Source (Marshall et al. 2008 SPIE 7014, 701454, Sec. 1): "two large fused-silica prisms (one single-pass, one
  double-pass)"; Sec. 2: "The first prism, with an apex angle of 46°, is used in double pass and the second prism, with
  an apex angle of 40° deg, is used in single pass." LCO MagE manual (rawdata/presets/mage/lco_mage_user_manual.html):
  "separated into echelle orders by double-pass and single-pass prisms."
- Position of the single-pass prism: Marshall Fig. 2 (rendered, p. 2 of the arXiv PDF) shows the collimator at the right
  end of the bench, the grating at the upper left with the large prism directly in front of it, and a second, smaller
  prism between the first and the Schmidt camera (upper right). That is, the 40 deg prism sits on the return path only,
  between the double-pass prism and the camera. (Read from the figure; the text gives only "first" and "second".
  The same order is stated for ESI, whose design MagE copies, "The optical layout ... is similar to ... ESI".)
- Correct arrangement: collimator -> prism 1 (46) -> echelle -> prism 1 (46) -> prism 2 (40) -> camera.
- Fix: draw prism 1 once with both beams crossing it; draw prism 2 (A = 40) on the return side only, between prism 1 and
  the camera. Caption/label: "prism, passed twice (46°), and a second prism, passed once (40°)".

### A2. ESI: same error; second 50 deg prism drawn as double pass. (high)
- Preset: xPrisms = [{50, dI -4}, {50}, {50, dI -4}]; the note says "two 50 deg ... prisms, ... the first in double pass".
  The tracer order (P1, P2, P1) is the tracer's after-echelle equivalent, not the physical order.
- Code: draws train[0] and train[1] before the echelle, both passed twice, label "2 prisms, passed twice 50° + 50°".
- Sources: Sheinis et al. 2002 PASP 114, 851, Sec. 1 (p. 3) and Fig. 1 caption: "The collimated light from the slit is
  pre-cross-dispersed by the first prism before it reaches the echellette grating ... The grating-dispersed light is once
  again cross-dispersed by a second pass through the first prism. It then passes through the second prism before being
  imaged onto the detector by the underfilled f/1.07 camera." 1996 design report (ucolick_esi_report_1996.html, Sec. 3.2):
  "In ESI one prism is used in double-pass right in front of the grating ... Since one prism is operated in double pass,
  only two prisms are used ... The double pass prism is 13.0 inches high and 12.0 inches wide, while the single pass prism
  is 10.0 inches high and 13.0 inches wide." The report also names them "Pre-Disperser Prism" and "Post-Disperser Prism".
- Correct arrangement: collimator -> prism 1 -> echelle -> prism 1 -> prism 2 -> camera. Prism 1 is the larger
  (13 in high), sits right in front of the grating (the code's "first prism 0.45 of the way out" matches that); prism 2
  is the smaller (10 in high) and sits near the camera.
- Also: the preset's dI = -4 deg on prism 1 (an INFERRED rotation, set to match PypeIt order positions) is read by the
  tracer only. build3dFolded ignores `dI` (grep "dI" in app.js finds no use in the 3D code), so the drawn prisms are at
  exact minimum deviation. The caption does not say so. Minor, but the drawing then disagrees with the Detector panel's
  prism setting.

### A3. FIRE: P1 drawn as double pass, P4 not drawn, label wrong. (high)
- Preset: six passes [Infrasil 42.6, Infrasil 28.1, ZnSe 21.81, ZnSe 21.81, Infrasil 28.1, ZnSe 21.81]. This order IS the
  physical order of the six passes (P1, P2, P3, grating, P3, P2, P4), so the preset data are right.
- Code: draws round(6/2) = 3 prisms (42.6, 28.1, 21.81), each passed twice, label "3 prisms, passed twice
  42.6° + 28.1° + 21.81°". The return path goes P3 -> P2 -> P1.
- Source, Simcoe et al. 2013 PASP 125, 270, Sec. 3.2.3: "four prisms, two each of Infrasil 301 and ZnSe ... One prism of
  each material is used in double-pass, for a total of six passes." Table 2 (rows 35-66): "P1 SINGLE PASS" (Infrasil,
  tan(A/2) = 0.39, i.e. A = 42.6 deg DERIVED), "P2 FIRST PASS" (Infrasil, 0.25), "P3 FIRST PASS" (ZnSe, 0.19), grating
  (rows 46-56), "P3 SECOND PASS", "P2 SECOND PASS", "P4 SINGLE PASS" (ZnSe, 0.19). Sec. 3.2.4: "owing to the effects of three
  upstream prism passes (2 Infrasil and one ZnSe)" confirms P1, P2, P3 stand ahead of the grating.
- Correct arrangement: collimator -> P1 (42.6, once) -> P2 (28.1) -> P3 (21.81) -> echelle -> P3 -> P2 -> P4 (21.81,
  once) -> camera. The drawn entering train (42.6, 28.1, 21.81) happens to be right; the returning train is wrong
  (P1 instead of P4) and the P4 prism is absent.
- Fix: four prisms; P1 crossed by the arriving beam only, P4 by the returning beam only, P2 and P3 by both.

### A4. README rule and lSub/label text for "pp" (consequence of A1-A3). (medium)
- README "Echelle 3D", "pp" bullet: "prisms passed on the way to the echelle and back, one drawn prism for each two passes,
  tall enough for both beams" is wrong as a rule; the number of prisms is not passes/2 for any of the three.
  The line "In the three folded arrangements each returning bundle is traced back ... (Snell's law in the prisms)" is
  then also drawn through the wrong prisms in the pp case.
- Same code path serves kind "4c" (X-Shooter: three prisms, six passes, all double) where passes/2 happens to hold; the
  rule is fine there but must not be generalized.
- Fix: carry a per-prism list in the preset, for example `e.prisms3d: [{A, glass, pre: true, ret: true}, ...]`
  (MagE: [46 both, 40 ret]; ESI: [50 both, 50 ret]; FIRE: [42.6 pre, 28.1 both, 21.81 both, 21.81 ret]) and build the
  drawing from it instead of from train.length / 2.

### A5. "the instrument's angle is not published" caption for pp (low-medium)
- Preset gamma = 0 for MagE, ESI, FIRE; the pp caption prints "(the instrument's angle is not published)" when the
  published angle is 0. For ESI this is inexact: the 1996 report gives "The grating is operated only a few degrees off
  Littrow" and "quasi-Littrow, meaning that the incoming light is at an angle to the plane of dispersion"; Sheinis also
  says "nearly in Littrow". For FIRE, Table 2 rows 46-56 list grating tilts ("X tilt grating -14.95", grating "Tilt -11.00",
  "Z rotate -0.60", "Decenter grating -17"), so an out-of-plane angle may be derivable from the Zemax prescription; the preset
  and the S7 audit say "theta and gamma not published". I did not attempt the derivation (the coordinate-break conventions
  need the Zemax frame); it is a candidate to settle. MagE: nothing published (S5 audit, "NOT FOUND: theta, gamma").
- Fix (caption wording): "the instrument's angle is a few degrees (ESI), or not found" rather than "not published".

## B. Values and claims confirmed

HIRES ("single"; no lay3d key, so build3dEchelle is used; correct default):
- echelle used in-plane, theta = 5 deg (2 theta = 10): Vogt et al. 1994 SPIE 2198, Sec. 3 (p. 365): "The echelle is used
  in-plane with a collimator-to-camera angle of 10°" (the PDF text layer prints "100"; the degree sign is lost, cf. "400"
  for the 40 deg turn). HIRES manual 1994 (keck_hires_manual1.pdf, p. 17, Echelle): "The collimator-to-camera angle
  (2 theta) is 10.0 deg". gamma = 0 consistent with "in-plane".
- cross-disperser after the echelle: Vogt Sec. 3: "The diffracted beam is then sent to a cross-disperser grating located
  below and slightly ahead of the collimators. The cross-disperser turns the beam 40° and into a large fast prime-focus
  catadioptric camera." xd 250 /mm, 40 deg: Vogt Sec. 3.3 ("250 gr mm^-1") and manual ("40 turn", "collimator-to-camera
  angle is 40"). Preset xG 250, xPsi 40: correct.
- "beams 10 deg apart in the echelle's plane, clear only near the collimator": SOURCED.
  (i) The 10 deg is the collimator-to-camera angle quoted above. (ii) Vogt Fig. 2 (the xhires schematic, p. 364) draws the
  cross-disperser at the collimator end of the bench, next to the collimators, with the echelle at the far end; Vogt Fig. 1
  side view labels the cross-disperser beside the collimators. The text says it sits "below and slightly ahead of the
  collimators". The manual says the 1.75 deg collimator tilt sends the beam "up towards the echelle at an angle of 3.5 deg",
  i.e. the echelle's plane is the vertical plane of the side view, and "below" is in that plane. (iii) DERIVED: two beams
  10 deg apart have axes L sin(10 deg) apart at distance L from the echelle; for the 12 inch (305 mm) beam they separate
  only for L > 305 / 0.1736 = 1.76 m; with the preset's 2.94 m collimator-to-echelle path the clearance is 0.51 m at the
  collimator end and zero at the echelle. The sentence is correct, but Vogt does not state this reasoning; cite Fig. 2 and
  the "below and slightly ahead of the collimators" sentence, and mark the distance as derived. The "0.9 of the path" is a
  drawing choice (README says so).
- layLen 2940 mm: preset comment (presets.js line 61) says it is measured from the side view of Vogt Fig. 1, scaled by the
  4155 mm slit-to-collimator distance. Not a published number; OK as disclosed. (Unsourced beyond that.)
- f_col 4154.7 mm, W = 4154.7/13.7 = 303.3 mm (12 inch beam, Vogt Sec. 3: "collimated into a 12" diameter beam"),
  camera 762.7 mm (Vogt Sec. 3.4: "a focal length of 30.0"" = 762 mm), colTilt 1.75 deg (Vogt Sec. 3 and manual): correct.
  cam "mirror" (catadioptric prime focus, mirror + corrector lenses): consistent with Vogt Sec. 3.4.
- Caption (build3dEchelle): "(the beams X deg apart out of the echelle's plane)" appears only when gamma > 0, so for HIRES it
  is absent, correctly. It does not say that the catadioptric camera is drawn as a plain lens; harmless.

MagE, ESI, FIRE ("pp"):
- lay3d "pp" is the right family for all three: reflective off-axis paraboloid collimator separate from a camera of its
  own, echelle used near Littrow with the prisms spreading the arriving and returning beams, camera beside the prisms
  (MagE Fig. 2; Sheinis Sec. 1; Simcoe Sec. 3.2 and Fig. 3). No third use of the collimator, no white pupil, no cross-
  disperser after the camera. Correct: pp, not 4c/wp.
- Collimator and camera separate optics: MagE (OAP 1094 mm, Schmidt 138 mm, Marshall Sec. 2), ESI (2286 mm OAP, f/1.07 308 mm
  camera, Sheinis Sec. 1 and 1996 report Sec. 4.1), FIRE (OAP 555 mm, 50 mm beam, camera 176.8 mm, Simcoe Secs. 3.2.2,
  3.2.5). All match the presets (W and fcam).
- Prism glasses and apex angles in the presets: MagE 46/46/40 fused silica (Marshall Sec. 2); ESI 50 deg BSL7Y x2 (Sheinis
  Sec. 2.2: "two large (25 kg), 50.0 deg-angle prisms ... Ohara BSL7Y"); FIRE Infrasil 42.6 / 28.1, ZnSe 21.81 x2 (Simcoe
  Table 2 tan(A/2) = 0.39, 0.25, 0.19; Fig. 4 caption: "Both have apex angles of 21.81 deg"). Correct. (The 0.25 and 0.19
  give 28.1 and 21.5 deg when computed from the rounded table entries; 21.81 is the stated value, so 0.19 is rounded.)
- Camera sits clear of the arriving beam, near the prisms: ESI 1996 report: "in this case the camera will block the incoming
  light. In ESI one prism is used in double-pass right in front of the grating, which bends the incoming and outgoing beams
  from the grating such that the camera no longer blocks the incoming beam." This is the reason for the double pass and
  agrees with the drawing's "prisms tall enough for both beams".
- No folds in the dispersive path of MagE (Fig. 2) or ESI echellette mode (the two translating fold flats belong to the
  low-dispersion and imaging modes: Sheinis Secs. 1, 2.3; the first is inserted "in front of the echellette grating", the
  second "in front of the first prism"; both out of the beam in echellette mode, INFERRED from that wording).

## C. Unsupported or incomplete (still open)

1. FIRE fore-optics folds not mentioned. Simcoe Table 2 rows 6-24: three folding flats and a three-mirror Offner relay
   precede and follow the slit (flat 3 lies between slit and collimator). The drawing, and the README line "unfolded but for
   the flat", cover none of this for pp. Acceptable for a pedagogical view, but the caption could say "the fore-optics and
   fold mirrors are not drawn" for FIRE.
2. Prism incidence angles (not minimum deviation) for ESI and FIRE are not in the sources; the 3D view draws minimum
   deviation at lamC with no dI (see A2). FIRE's prisms are deliberately off minimum deviation (presets.js note).
3. MagE, ESI, FIRE gamma and theta: see A5.
4. MagE single-pass-prism position is read from a figure plus the "similar to ESI" statement, not from a sentence that says
   "after the grating". ESI states it in words (Sheinis Sec. 1).
5. The HIRES layLen 2940 mm is a figure measurement (disclosed).

## D. Suggested checks after fixing A1-A3
- On MagE, ESI, FIRE verify the on-screen prism labels read: MagE "46 deg prism, passed twice; 40 deg prism, passed once";
  ESI "50 deg prism passed twice; 50 deg prism passed once"; FIRE four prisms, "42.6 deg once, 28.1 deg twice, 21.81 deg twice,
  21.81 deg once".
- The Detector panel and the 3D tracer must still agree: the tracer applies the passes after the echelle in the order
  stored in xPrisms, which is fine; only the drawing needs the per-prism pre/return flags.
