## Most serious

### 1. ESI: a MEASURED collimator tilt where a source prints a value (WRONG or at least unreconciled)
- Preset text: `colTilt: 4.4`, and "the returned beam turns 9° from the arriving light (MEASURED from Sheinis et al. 2002, Fig. 1)".
- What the source prints: the 1996 design report, §3.1 "Collimator" (`rawdata/presets/esi/ucolick_esi_report_1996.html`), says "the collimator is off axis by 5.0 degrees. This collimator tilt does not affect the on-axis image…".
- Why it matters: the layout audit (`data/audit/2026-11-17_layout_L1_keck.md`, A4) read the 9.2° turn from the Fig. 1 pixels and never cites this sentence.
  - If 5.0° is the tilt, the preset's 4.4° is 12% low.
  - If 5.0° is the beam turn, the preset is off by a factor of about 2.
  - The report's word "tilt" suggests the first reading, but it needs a decision.
- Fix: cite the printed 5.0° beside the measured value, or switch to 5.0.
- The ESI path length (2930 mm) has no printed value. The Sheinis text only says "the exit pupil of the collimator is near the prisms". It remains MEASURED, which is acceptable.

### 2. HIRES: a printed value for the gap line is not listed (UNSUPPORTED omission)
- The scored line is "orders outgrow the detector above ≈ 6360 Å", `gap` 6360, labeled DERIVED from manual App. E.
- The current Keck HIRES page `rawdata/presets/hires/keck_hires_index.html` prints: "Spectral coverage is complete shortward of 6200 Angstroms. Longward of this, two different echelle settings are necessary to achieve complete coverage."

- Neither the 6200 sentence nor the DERIVED/printed relationship is mentioned in the preset.
- The manual's App. E rows are correct: order 57 (6252.4 Å) is 60.41 mm long and order 56 (6364.0 Å) is 61.49 mm long.

### 3. DEIMOS: citation to the wrong item, and "(measured)" without support
- Pub line: "R ≈ 6000 at 0.75″ (measured; FWHM 4 px = 1.3 Å)", src "Faber et al. 2003, Fig. 1".
  - The Fig. 1 caption reads only "The FWHM of the spectral lines is 4 px, or 1.3 Å." It gives no slit width and no R.
  - R ≈ 6000 and 0.75″ are in the paper's Introduction text: "The highest working spectral resolution in the red is R ~ 6000 using a 1200-line grating, a dispersion of 0.33 Å per pixel, and a slit width of 0.75″."
  - That sentence is a design or working statement, not a measurement.
  - Fix: cite the Introduction for R and 0.75″, and Fig. 1 only for the 4 px / 1.3 Å. The slit width of Fig. 1's mask is not stated anywhere I could find.
- Pub line "0.33 Å/px (measured)", src "Newman et al. 2013 Table 2".
  - Table 2 (PDF p. 53) reads "Dispersion 0.33 Å px⁻¹" with no "measured" and no wavelength.
  - The same value appears in Faber's design sentence above.
  - The other Table 2 entries the preset cites are verified: "1200 l mm⁻¹, gold-coated, 7500 Å blaze", "Blue-red gap 7 px", "0.1185″ px⁻¹", "R = 5900 at 7800 Å", and "FWHM of sky line 1.29–1.39 Å".

### 6. IMACS: scored R ≈ 11,000 compared with the slit-limited Rs
- Source: Simon 2017 Intro: "R ≈ 11,000 (for a 0.7″ slit) with a dispersion of 0.19 Å pix⁻¹". Li 2018 tabulates 11,000 and 0.19.
- No method is stated.
- 11,000 equals λc/(4.0 px × 0.19 Å/px) = 8250/0.76 = 10,855. That reads like a nominal four-pixel convention, not a slit-limited geometric R.
- The preset note already says "a rounded statement with no method given" and still scores it at tol 0.08.
- Treat it as an assumed convention. The 0.19 Å/px line is fine, though the wavelength is not stated.
- FINE: 0.111″/px (LCO manual "(central) scale of 0.111 arcsec per pixel"), collimator 1648.46 mm with a 150 mm beam, and camera 641.79 mm (Carnegie spec nodes 11 and 12).
