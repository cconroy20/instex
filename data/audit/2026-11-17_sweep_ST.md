## A. OLD DESIGN NOT SAID (findings, most consequential first)

1. **HARPS (preset `harps`).**
   - Current text: "two 2k × 4k CCDs of 15 µm with the 1215 µm gap between them counted in the height (4177 px; the gap itself is not drawn)." The note also describes the fibers (octagonal since June 2015) and the central wavelength "in the gap at the center of the mosaic". It says nothing about an upgrade.
   - What changed: ESO's page "HARPS upgrade 2026" says "since May 18th until June 10th 2026 we had a major upgrade of the HARPS instrument". The changes were:
     - "a new, monolithic detector (same number of pixels and same size as before)";
     - new control electronics;
     - a Stirling cooler instead of liquid nitrogen;
     - a new pipeline in the ESPRESSO/NIRPS family;
     - a new fiber link for the HAM mode (exchanged 4 June, after a throughput drop);
     - a note that the EGGS fibers are being monitored.
   - The page also expects a constant RV offset after 4 June. It was last updated 23 Oct 2026.
   - Consequence for the preset: the two-chip mosaic with a 1215 µm gap, and the mid-mosaic gap in order 115, describe the pre-June-2026 detector. The new chip is monolithic with the same pixel count and size. ESO does not say whether a gap or a mid-chip seam remains. The saved ESO HARPS description (rawdata/presets/harps/eso_harps_inst_description.html) still shows the EEV 44-82 pair, so it is also out of date.
   - Suggested note: say the preset is the 2003–2026 two-chip configuration and give the new one-line difference.
   - Source (saved): rawdata/presets/harps/eso_harps_upgrade_2026.html.

2. **DEIMOS (preset `deimos`).**
   - Current text: "8192 × 8192 mosaic of 15 µm pixels (CCD gaps not modeled; the blue–red gap is 7 px, Newman et al. 2013 Table 2)". It gives no status.
   - What is changing: Keck's instrument-availability page (fetched 2026-11-17) says DEIMOS is "unavailable February through April 2027 for commissioning of a new science detector and returns to science in May".
   - The 2024 Keck Science Meeting talk (Kirby, Roberts et al.) says "The current DEIMOS detector system is in trouble!", "The new detectors will be in a new cryostat" and "A hexapod will provide focus and flexure compensation". It scheduled commissioning for April 2025 and science for June 2025. That date has slipped to 2027.
   - Keck's saved DEIMOS news page lists current problems: science CCD5 inoperative (targets on CCD1 lose the red part of the spectrum) and FCS CCD2 inoperative.
   - I could not find the new chips' format, vendor or gaps, so I do not know whether the 8 × (2048 × 4096) layout survives. The preset should say it models the original MIT/LL 8-chip mosaic, which is to be replaced in 2027.
   - Saved: rawdata/presets/deimos/KSM2024_DEIMOS_Throughput_Upgrade_neill.pdf (source https://sites.astro.caltech.edu/~neill/COO/DEIMOS/KSM2024_DEIMOS_Throughput_Upgrade.pdf).

3. **ESI (preset `esi`).** The preset is the 2002 design and the CCD is correct (MIT/LL CCID20 4096 × 2048). The note gives no status. The Keck availability page says ESI/ESIi were hibernated (the page gives July 2026 in one place and "after 26A" in another) and ESI is "expected to be decommissioned at the end of 27A unless a compelling need emerges". Suggest adding "hibernated 2026, decommissioning planned" to the note. Hibernation for NIRES is the same pattern but NIRES is not a preset.

4. **HIRES (preset `hires`).** The three-chip MIT-LL mosaic is the current detector (installed 18 Aug 2004), so the preset is current. Two status items are missing:
   - Keck's availability page says HIRES "will skip hibernation and go straight to decommissioning, with the timeline depending on Zshooter's readiness and KPF's stability". It is available in 27A and "hibernation is not planned before the end of 27B".
   - The saved Keck HIRES news (rawdata/presets/hires/keck_hires_news.html, 2026 Sept. 15) says the dewar warmed up in August 2026 after 21 years. The red CCD is not functional on its default amplifier B. With amplifier A the red and blue chips read but the green does not, and vice versa. No recovery date is given.

5. **LLAMAS (presets `llamas*`).** The note describes the design and fits (Furesz et al. 2020). The LCO status for 2026B (rawdata/presets/m2fs/cfa_tac_2026B_MagInstr.txt) says blue channels are "to be repaired" and that the repairs "are expected to be complete by the 2026B semester". The note says nothing about this. It is a minor status point.

6. **FORS2 spectroscopy and imaging (`fors2*`, imager `fors2sr`/`fors2hr`).** The presets are current: the MIT mosaic in the spectroscopy note, two 4096 × 2048 CCDs in the imager note.
   - ESO's FORS Upgrade page says a refurbished twin (FORS1, to be called FORS3) goes to the telescope in 2027 with a new 4k × 4k Teledyne e2v CCD231-84 (with fringe suppression), a new calibration unit, high-resolution grisms near Na (580 nm) and K (770 nm), and Sloan and LSST filters. ESO says the upgrade aims to keep FORS operating "for at least 15 more years". It does not say whether FORS2 will be retired.
   - No change is needed now. A "FORS3 will differ" sentence is optional.
   - Saved: rawdata/presets/fors2/eso_fors_inst_upgrade.html is already there.

## B. OLD DESIGN SAID (fine)

- **Hectospec, Hectochelle:** both notes end "Decommissioned in 2026". This is confirmed. The MMT Observatory (MMTO) notice says the last observing night is no later than 30 April 2026 and decommissioning is 1 May 2026, "to prepare MMTO for the arrival of ViaSpec".
- **G-CLEF:** the note says it is the 2016 GMT design and gives the Magellan (G@M) configuration with MR 1.12″ at R 100,000 and HR 0.8″ at R 140,000, "commissioning planned for 2027 as of 2024". The newest source I found (a 2024 arXiv paper) says the same. I found no 2025–2026 update, so the status sentence is dated but not wrong.
- **MIKE red and blue:** both say "reproduces the 2002 design; the as-built instrument measures R 22,000 / 28,000" (Bernstein et al. 2015 Table 1).
- **MagE:** it records the move from Clay to Baade in October 2015. The E2V 42-20 chip appears unchanged.
- **ZShooter:** "Not built yet", with the June 2026 Zemax design. Its own website is internally inconsistent about channels, as the preset already notes.

## C. CURRENT (fine)

- **Spectrographs:** LRIS, KCWI blue, KCRM, MOSFIRE, Binospec, MUSE, UVES blue and red, X-shooter, FEROS, 4MOST, PFS, APOGEE-N, DESI, M2FS, IFUM, LDSS-3, FIRE.
  - **LRIS:** "4k × 4k 15 µm" matches the 2021 monolithic "Mark IV" red CCD. First light 27 Apr 2021 (Keck note).
  - **ESPRESSO:** the 2022 intervention changed the spectral format. The preset's orders come from the P117 manual, which is saved and post-2022. The preset is current.
  - **UVES:** the blue CCD "Pavarotti" (2004) and red MIT mosaic (2009) are what the preset describes.
  - **X-shooter:** the UT3 move, the Oct 2025 contamination and the Dec 2025 recommissioning change no preset parameter.
  - **FEROS:** its ESO news page lists no detector change (the EEV is still the detector; the ADC was added in 2006).
  - **4MOST:** it uses the 2023 and 2026 papers. First light was 18 Oct 2025 (LRS and HRS).
  - **PFS:** science operations began in 2025. The note gives no status.
  - **Via:** see next.
- **Via (`via`, `boombox*`):** the newest public design is the Via Project overview (arXiv:2606.18332, June 2026). It matches the Viaspec and Boombox presets:
  - Viaspec: 254 mm collimated beam, 2000 l/mm binary grating, e2v CCD290-99, 120 µm mask giving R ≈ 15,000, fold mirror.
  - Boombox: 50 mm beam, f/1.5 cameras, 2048 × 256 CCD of 15 µm, 3600–5900 Å and 5700–10100 Å.
  - Two small differences:
    - The paper's table gives "Number of Fibers (incl. calibration) 600" for Viaspec, where the preset says 660 (540 + 60 + 60 argon). The preset already notes the Science Book table's 600.
    - The paper says the 800 mm spherical mirror is a diameter. This does not conflict with the preset's focal length of 1375 mm.
  - The paper puts first on-sky and survey start in 2027. The preset states no date, so nothing is stale.
