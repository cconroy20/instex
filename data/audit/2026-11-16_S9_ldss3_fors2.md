# Audit S9: LDSS-3 (ldss3all, ldss3blue, ldss3red) and FORS2 (fors2300v, fors2300i, fors2600b)

Auditor scope: web/presets.js lines 196-222 (LDSS-3) and 403-422 (FORS2), research record data/round5/grisms.md and grisms.json, sources under rawdata/presets/ldss3/ and rawdata/presets/fors2/, VLT-SPE-ESO-10000-2723 under rawdata/presets/uves/. Model runs: scratch scripts in /private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-cconroy-memos/2c3f4bba-5bc0-400a-b287-224dc611d7a1/scratchpad/audit/s9/ (run.js, run2.js, var.js, fitarc.js, fitarc2.js, edge2.js, arcs.py, dist.py). No project file was edited.

Overall verdict. Neither instrument has an error of the 35% class. The geometry of both grism families is sourced (LDSS-3: the Carnegie maskgen instrument file; FORS2: the 1995 grism design table plus an in-plane 7.5 deg entrance-face tilt that ESO's calibrations require), and the model reproduces the measured center wavelengths and dispersions to better than 1%. The findings are of the kind the brief names: one EFFECTIVE groove density used where the documented ruling is known (LDSS-3 VPH-Red), scored resolution lines typed as measured although the sources computed the values, comparisons made at a different wavelength from the source's, an unsupported word in an info line, and page text that contradicts the tilted-grism geometry the FORS2 presets use.

## 2. LDSS-3 findings

### 2.1 VPH-Red uses an EFFECTIVE groove density (665 /mm) where the documented ruling is 660 /mm [error, low numerical effect]

Sources for 660: LUM Table 3 "VPH-Red** | 660 | 1810 | 8000 | 6000 – 10000 | 1.175 | 38"; LH22 grism table "VPH-Red 660 1810 8000 6000-10000 1.175 37"; LUG06 Table 1 "VPH Red | 660 | 1.175 (@8500A) | 1810".

Source for 665: maskgen opticdef.dat, LDSS-3 section, "EGRISM LDSS3_VPHred -24.00 S-LAL7 0.00 665.0 S-LAL7 24.00 / ALIGNROT 180.8 ... # The alignment rotation values above are from Oemler, # e-mail of 2005/10-18; used for fitting actual data. # Also, lpmm for VPHred has been changed from 660 to 665." No reason is given for the change; it sits with alignment values fitted to data.

New evidence (COSMOS 2.21, Carnegie's LDSS-3/IMACS reduction pipeline, saved this round):
- Its sdata/opticdef.dat keeps a commented-out pre-EGRISM emulation of the same grism: "!GRISM LDSS3_VPHred ZNSE -20.200 629.9 / ! 8350 is undeviated wavelength, dispersion is 1.155 A/pix at that". The model gives an undeviated wavelength of 8355 A with 660 /mm and 8294 A with 665 /mm (maskgen prisms). The 8350 A statement matches 660.
- The pipeline does not use maskgen's camera as is. source/fp2ccd.c reads each dewar-offset file as `sscanf(line,"%f %f %f %f",&dcth,&dscale,&dcx,&dcy);` and divides model focal-plane positions by dscale (`xfp/=dscl; yfp/=dscl;`). The LDSS-3 templates hold dscale = 1.00461 (VPH-Red), 1.00315 (VPH-All), 1.00272 (VPH-Blue): examples/dewoff/L3VPH_R.dewoff " -90.762 1.00461 0.640 -1.095 1", L3VPH_all.dewoff " -89.394 1.00315 0.162 -1.645 1", L3VPH_B.dewoff "-90.535 1.00272 -0.222 -1.566 -1". So COSMOS pairs 665 /mm with an effective camera of 173.076/1.00461 = 172.28 mm (DERIVED).

My fits of the model to the 2007 arc lines (fitarc.js, fitarc2.js; positions traced through physics.js, detector offset free):
- With the preset's camera (173.076 mm) fixed, the 28 VPH-Red center-slit lines fit with rms 1.48 px at 660 /mm and 5.64 px at 665 /mm; the 22 lines of the 2-arcmin "blue" slit fit with rms 1.18 px at 660 (best-fit scale 1.00001) and 6.32 px at 665.
- With the camera free, 665 needs 171.95-172.3 mm (rms 0.8-1.0 px) and 660 needs 172.95 mm. A joint fit of all eight LS10 databases (three grisms, one camera) gives rms 1.90 px for 660 at 172.6 mm and 1.87 px for 665 at 172.3-172.4 mm: the arcs cannot separate the two rulings once the camera is free.
- The measured local dispersion at 8321 A is 1.1814 A/px; the preset (665, 173.076 mm) gives 1.1762 (-0.44%), 660 gives 1.1838 (+0.20%).

Conclusion: 665 /mm is a fitted value that only works with a camera 0.5% shorter than the one the preset uses; the preset pairs maskgen's fitted ruling with maskgen's unscaled camera, the combination that fits the measured arcs worst. Fix: G = 660 (the documented ruling, consistent with the 8350 A undeviated wavelength), keeping f_cam = 173.076 mm; or, if the camera is changed for all three grisms (Sec. 2.2), keep 660 and note maskgen's 665 as an alternative. Remove "(maskgen's value, changed there from 660 ...)" from the note or reverse the statement.

Effect of 660 on the model (var.js): lambda0 8294 -> 8355 A; center dispersion 1.1755 -> 1.1847 A/px (+0.8%); coverage 6002-10719 -> 6045-10799 A; Rs at lambda0 unchanged (1756); Rs at 8500 A 1797 -> 1784. The scored lam0 line still passes (8355 vs 8321, +0.4%, tol 0.5%), and the dlpix line passes (1.1847 vs 1.1818, +0.2%).

### 2.2 Camera focal length: maskgen 173.076 mm vs 172.3-172.6 mm used by Carnegie's pipeline [open/silent assumption, <0.5%]

COSMOS's per-grism dscale values imply effective cameras of 172.53 (All), 172.61 (Blue) and 172.28 mm (Red, with 665), DERIVED from the dewoff files quoted above. My unscaled arc fits give 172.36 (All), 172.46 (Blue), 171.95 (Red 665) or 172.95 mm (Red 660). The note states 173.1 mm without qualification. Effect of 172.5 mm: dispersion +0.35%, pixel scale 0.1867 -> 0.1873"/px (closer to LCO's 0.189), R unchanged (slit image and dispersion scale together). Recommendation: keep 173.076 (the instrument file value) but say in the note that Carnegie's pipeline applies a 0.3-0.5% scale, or adopt 172.5 mm for all three presets with a sourced comment.

### 2.3 The VPH-All R info line misstates the source and uses the wrong q type [error in an info line]

The line reads "R = 860 at 0.75″ (λ over 4 px × the mean 1.89 Å/px, at 6500 Å)", q: "R", model 782 vs 860 (9.1% miss).
- "the mean" is unsupported. LUM's column header is "Linear dispersion at nom.wav. range (A/pixel)"; Stevenson et al. 2016 (S16, saved this round) Table 1 gives "Central wavelength ... 650 nm" and "Dispersion ... 1.890 Å/pixel" for VPH-All, with footnote g, "For a 4 pixel (0.76′′) wide slit". The 6500 A reference wavelength, previously an inference, is now sourced (S16).
- The figure is a calculated slit-width value (S16 footnote g; 6500/(4 x 1.890) = 860), so the comparison is with Rs, as the Blue and Red lines already do. Model Rs at 6500 A = 796 (-7.4%); Reff at lambda0 (782) mixes in the pixel term.
- The remaining 7% is LCO's dispersion figure, which the 2007 arcs contradict: the stored spline solution gives 2.035 A/px at 6500 A, and no part of the identified-line range from 4540 to 9660 A has a local dispersion as low as 1.89 except near 4500 A (arcs2.py). The model gives 2.031 A/px at 6500 A. Fix: q "Rs", what "R = 860 at 0.75″ (6500 Å over 4 px × 1.890 Å/px; S16 Table 1)", src noting that the arcs measure 2.035 A/px at 6500 A, so the published R is 7% too high. Keep the line as info.

### 2.4 Scored VPH-Blue and VPH-Red R lines are evaluated at lambda0, not at the source's reference wavelength [comparison error, small]

LUG06 Table 1 quotes the dispersions "0.682 (@5200A)" and "1.175 (@8500A)" for the "(4 pixel slit)" R column, and S16 Table 1 lists "Central wavelength 850 nm 520 nm" for VPH-Red and VPH-Blue with the same R values. The pub lines compare Rs at lambda0 (5166, 8294 A). At the sourced wavelengths the model gives Rs = 1864 (Blue, -1.9% vs 1900) and 1797 (Red, -0.7% vs 1810); at lambda0 the misses are -2.4% and -3.0%. There is no "RsAt" q type; either add one or state in the what/src that the comparison is at lambda0. Also note that LCO's dispersions are 1-2% below the measured local values (arcs: 0.697 A/px at 5200 A, 1.186 A/px at 8500 A), so the published R values themselves are 1-2% high.

### 2.5 The "measured" pub lines come from the pre-2014 detector; the lam0 line equates two different quantities [unsupported framing]

- The arcs are from 2007, when LDSS-3 had "a STA0500A 4064 x 4064 device" (LUG06 Sec. 1.4); the preset models the 2014 Fermilab chip. S16 Sec. 2 confirms the per-pixel numbers transfer: "The new CCD, having the same pixel size (15 µm) as the old device but offering only 2048×4096 unbinned pixels". The note says only "measured arc solutions" and should name the 2007 STA0500A chip.
- q: "lam0" compares the undeviated wavelength with the wavelength at the old chip's center. My fits place the undeviated ray 2 px (All), 24 px (Blue) and 17 px (Red, 665) or 30 px (Red, 660) from that center, so the two agree only to 5-60 A; the research's statement "the undeviated ray lands on the detector centre" (grisms.json camera_collimator_angle) is approximately true for VPH-All only.
- Where the LDSS-3C chip sits along the dispersion is not documented (open question, Sec. 7).

### 2.6 Smaller LDSS-3 items

- "refractive collimator": the research record marks the collimator type NOT stated. LUM Sec. 4 supports a refractive collimator indirectly ("the collimator leaves in a residual spherical aberration and the position of the pupil is wavelength dependent"). The note should say "refractive (inferred)". Drawing only; no number changes.
- 17 slits per mask comes from 2005-2006 masks on the old chip, which covered the full 8.3 arcmin field; the LDSS-3C chip is "trimmed to 6.4 arcmin in spatial direction" (LUM Sec. 2), and the LDSS-3C survey K19 Sec. 2.2.2 reports "Approximately 20-25 targets were chosen for each spectroscopic mask." The value is plausible for either era; the note should name the era. Drawing only.
- Coverage: the page shows 3200-10,779 A for VPH-All; the blue end is the app's atmospheric limit. LUM Sec. 2 gives "Wavelength Coverage: 3600-10000 Å", and S16 Table 1 gives the field-center coverage "VPH blue grism: 400 – 640 nm / VPH red grism: 640 – 1040 nm / VPH all grism: 370 – 1070 nm", narrower than the model's detector spans (3846-6616, 6003-10720, 2879-10780 A). The band info lines could add S16 as a second reference.
- Camera distortion is not modeled (maskgen "FOCUSER LDSS3_CAM ... RMOD 2 0.0 1.755637190879E-01 2.135446893793E+00"); I estimate at most 0.3-0.8% in position at the chip ends, 20-50 A of VPH-All coverage (INFERRED; maskgen's base mapping is not documented).
- state.psi 35 and lamB1 5000 are unused placeholders for a grism (physics.js sets S.psi = 0; the blaze control is hidden for grisms). Harmless.

## 3. FORS2 findings

### 3.1 Scored R lines are typed as measured effective R; ESO's values are calculated [error in pub typing]

The pub lines use q "R" (slit, diffraction and pixel in quadrature), the convention for a MEASURED lambda/FWHM. The sources say the values are estimates: FUM117 Table 2.4 caption, "Tests done in early 2017 show that estimated spectral resolutions are worse for MOS observations than for LSS, with a median difference of 7%. The resolution λ/∆λ listed above should be considered conservative"; N13 Table 1 note, "The listed values of resolution λ/∆λ are computed at the central wavelength and for a 1′′ slit." The 1" values equal lambda_c/(7.99 px x tabulated A/px) to 1% (DERIVED: 436, 662, 772). Fix: q "Rs", what "R = 440 at 1″ (calculated)". Model Rs: 447.6 / 676.3 / 794.8 vs 440 / 660 / 780 (+1.7 / +2.5 / +1.9%; Reff +0.9 / +1.6 / +1.1%). All pass either way. The residual comes from ESO's nominal dispersions (1.68 vs the measured 1.655 A/px for 300V) and from the grism's anamorphic factor, which ESO's calculation omits.

### 3.2 Unbinned detector, while ESO reads all spectroscopy 2x2 [silent assumption]

FUM117 Sec. 2.7.1: "The default readout modes for both mosaics are 200kHz,2x2,low for imaging ... and 100kHz,2x2,high for spectroscopy", and "100kHz,2x2,high — spectroscopy LSS, MOS, PMOS and MXU". The preset shows 15 um pixels, so the page reads 1.66 A/px, 0.125"/px and 7.87 x 7.99 px sampling, while a standard FORS2 MXU frame has 3.33 A/px, 0.25"/px and approximately 3.9 px. With pix 30, Nx = Ny = 2048 the model gives Reff 434 / 655 / 770 (-2.3%) and the same Rs. Either switch to 2x2 (and rescale the dlpix and pixScale pub lines) or state "unbinned 15 µm pixels (ESO reads spectra 2 × 2)" in the note.

### 3.3 Page text contradicts the tilted grism of the FORS2 presets [page error, triggered only by these presets]

web/app.js line 473, for a ruled grism: "m G λ0 = (n − 1) sin A. A grism has no tilt, so λ0 is fixed by the grism itself." The FORS2 presets set gTilt 7.5 and physics.js solves m G lambda0 = n sin(A - eps') - sin(A - eps). Line 474: "the light leaves the ruled hypotenuse at A = 21.0° from its normal"; with the tilt the angle is A - eps = 13.5 deg (300V), 19.2 deg (300I), 26.5 deg (600B). The tilt itself appears nowhere on the page outside the References note (no control or readout shows gTilt). The beam-footprint and "Angle at the detector" tooltips also give the reflection-grating formulas (W / cos alpha; theta_g cos alpha / cos beta), although the displayed numbers come from the grism trace. Fix: branch the two tooltips on gTilt and show the tilt as a read-only row.

### 3.4 Smaller FORS2 items

- F = 13.42 "so that the plate scale is the manual's 528 µm/″": VLT-SPE's own F = 13.4106 gives 527.6 µm/arcsec, which is the 528 of the same table to three figures; the adjustment (0.06%) is unnecessary. No output changes.
- Camera f-ratio on the page: f/3.00 (280 mm over the 93.4 mm diffracted beam) vs FUM117 Table 2.2 "final f-ratio 3.13". f/13.41 x 280/1233 = 3.05 (DERIVED); the design beam was 90 mm (AR92, "f of 1230 mm and a collimated beam diameter of 90 mm", 280/90 = 3.11). The 91.9 mm beam follows from VLT-SPE and affects only the diffraction term and this label.
- "measured 4629 Å ... (manual: same)": the manual gives 4627 A (FUM117 Table 2.4). Trivial.
- Glass FK5HTi stands in for FS95's FK5; N-FK5 gives identical lambda0 and dispersion (var.js).
- Groove angles (17.5, 26.7, 28 deg in FS95) are not used; the model computes no grism efficiency. state.psi 35 and lamB1 5000 are unused placeholders.
- Coverage on the page (300V: 3200-9350 A) starts at the app's atmospheric limit; FORS2 cuts at 3300 A, and the manual's 300V setting uses GG435 (4450-8700 A). The band info line already explains the difference.

## 7. Errors in the research record (data/round5/grisms.md and .json; not in the presets)

- The anamorphic factor of the tilted ruled grisms has the wrong direction. The record says "R(1") 433 incl. anamorphic factor 0.984 (440 without)" (also 646 and 752 for 300I and 600B) and "beam out/in = 0.952-0.991 at lambda0". The exit beam is wider than the entrance beam: W'/W = cos eps' cos(A - eps) / (cos eps cos(A - eps')) = 1.016, 1.023, 1.028 (DERIVED; the physics.js trace agrees), so the slit image is narrower and Rs rises to 448, 676 and 795.
- grisms.json still says "No source states the Cassegrain f-ratio"; the md Notes (audit 2026-10-17) already correct this from VLT-SPE Table 2.
- "The 2007 arc solution prefers 665 (fit rms 1.0 px vs 1.4 px)" holds only with the camera freed to 172.0 mm; with the camera the preset uses, 660 fits better (1.5 vs 5.6 px), and a joint fit of the three grisms does not separate the two (Sec. 2.1).
- "the undeviated ray lands on the detector centre": true to 2 px for VPH-All, 17-30 px for Blue and Red (Sec. 2.5).

## Summary

(a) Confirmed errors, ranked by severity

1. ldss3red: groove density 665 /mm is maskgen's undocumented, data-fitted change; the documented ruling is 660 /mm (LUM Table 3, LH22, LUG06 Table 1), and COSMOS's opticdef records "8350 is undeviated wavelength" (660 gives 8355 A, 665 gives 8294 A). With the preset's own camera the 2007 arcs fit 660 at 1.5 px rms and 665 at 5.6 px. Fix: G = 660. Effect: lambda0 8294 -> 8355 A, center dispersion +0.8%, coverage +43/+80 A, R at lambda0 unchanged.
2. fors2300v/300i/600b: the scored R lines use q "R" (measured effective R), but ESO calls the values estimated and conservative and N13 says "computed". Fix: q "Rs" and label the three values calculated. Effect on the comparison: +1.7/+2.5/+1.9% with Rs, +0.9/+1.6/+1.1% with Reff; both pass.
3. ldss3all: the R info line calls 1.89 A/px "the mean" (unsupported) and uses Reff. S16 Table 1 sources the R reference wavelength (650 nm) and the 4-px slit. Fix: q "Rs", cite S16, and note that the arcs measure 2.035 A/px at 6500 A, so the published R is 7% too high. Model Rs at 6500 A is 796.
