A. FINDINGS TO FIX

1. HST (citation): Figure 4.1 does not support the claim that Tiny Tim agrees
- The comment above the entry (lines 50-52) says the outer pads sit at "58.5 deg ... and 67.6 deg ... (Figure 4.1; the research record's own entry; Tiny Tim's pad table)". The pad angles and the Figure 4.1 citation are right. Tiny Tim does not support the second angle.
- Figure 4.1 (OTA Handbook p. 36, rendered and read): the arcs measured from -V3 are labeled "58.5°" on the +V2 (right) side and "67.6°" on the -V2 (left) side. The pad radii are 1066.8, 1071.88 and 1071.88 mm, the pads are 150.368 mm across, and the vanes are 25.654 mm wide. All of these match the preset (pads at 202.4 and 328.5 deg).
- Tiny Tim's wfc3_ir.pup, "Mirror pad positions", lists Pad 2 at (V3, V2) = (-0.4615, +0.7555) and Pad 3 at (-0.4564, -0.7606). That is 58.6° and 59.0° from -V3, so Tiny Tim has the pads symmetric at about 59°, not 67.6°. data/telescopes/hst.md row 148 also records Tiny Tim as az 121.4 and 239.0.
- Fix: cite Figure 4.1 alone. If Tiny Tim stays, say it differs on the -V2 pad (59° against 67.6°).

2. HET (printed value exists): area 55.5 m2 is labeled "no measured value"
- The src text (line 166) says the area is "DERIVED from the pyHETobs geometry with the tracker centered ... (no measured value)".
- Kanodia et al. 2021 Sec. 6.1 prints: "the peak pupil area of ~ 50 m2 is obtained when the target is at 55°" (HET's fixed altitude). Sec. 6.2 prints: "the median pupil area is about 41.7 m2 for the East track vs 48.4 m2 for the West".
- Hill et al. 2021 Sec. 6.3 (throughput) prints: "photons incident on a 50 m2 area" (the HETDEX definition).
- The model gives 55.5 m2 at the center, about 11% above the printed ~50 m2 peak. The preset's own areaTrack at 2 deg is 49.5-49.9, which matches the printed peak. het.md mentions only that HETDEX "normalises to 50 m^2", not the Kanodia numbers.
- The scored area test (tol 0.012) therefore checks the drawing against its own model while a printed ~50 m2 contradicts the centered value.
- Fix: drop "no measured value", quote the printed ~50 m2 peak and the East/West medians, and say the model's centered 55.5 m2 is about 11% above it.

3. Magellan f/5 against MMT f/5 (inconsistent obstruction and area for the same optics)
- McLeod et al. 2015 Sec. 3.1 prints: "The f/5 optics at the MMT and Magellan Clay telescopes are essentially identical". Sec. 8.1 prints: "we account for a telescope area of 29 m2".
- The Magellan f/5 focus uses eps 0.343 "DERIVED from McLeod's 29 m^2". The MMT f/5 foci use eps 0.264 (the bare 1.715 m mirror), and the MMT area src says "no official value ... baffles are not included" at 29.75 m2.
- The same 29 m2 appears for both telescopes in the same paper. So the MMT f/5 pupil is a lower bound while the identical Magellan f/5 gets the baffle-inclusive value.
- mmt.md lines 58 and 148 record that McLeod's 29 m2 is an assumed throughput area ("method not stated"), but the MMT preset text does not mention it.
- Magellan's note says McLeod "adopt" 29 m2 at f/5, which is fair. The "(DERIVED)" 0.343 rests on that assumed number.
- Fix: either give MMT f5s, f5i and f5b the same baffle-inclusive eps, or say in the Magellan pupilNote that 29 m2 is a throughput-calculation figure.

5. Keck f/40: the hole size is printed on the figure, and the "120%" text does not reconcile
- The preset says "the hole's size read from the figure". The RBC manual Figure 4 prints "0.1501 m" under the black hexagon (corner to corner) and "0.5058 m" across the primary image. 0.1501 / 0.5058 x 10.949 m = 3.249 m, so 3.25 m is correct, but it is a printed dimension, not a read-off.
- The RBC Sec. 2 text prints: "The central hole in the secondary is oversized by 120%". Neither 1.2 x 1.8 = 2.16 m nor 2.2 x 1.8 = 3.96 m matches 3.25 m (= 1.81 x the 1.8 m segment hole). keck.md lists this as open ("a definition of KRBC's 'oversized by 120%' central hole").
- Fix: reword to "printed on the manual's Figure 4"; add a note that the "120%" text is unreconciled.

6. Subaru: two values that are slightly off or unsupported

- The vane width 0.23 m is described as "measured", and pupilNote says "four 0.23 m vanes". The saved sources print 0.224 m (Lozi 2009: "224 mm thick, for a total pupil diameter of 7.92 m") and 0.237 m (Kuhn 2018); the HCI model has 0.184 m. 0.23 is a rounded mean, not a printed value.
- The comment "Iye et al. 2004 Table 2: seven focus configurations" is correct. Table 1 (optical Nasmyth secondary 1400 mm, IR 1265 mm) is correct. The Table 2 back focus values and the "image rotator 3.5′, f/12.7 / f/13.6" are correct.
