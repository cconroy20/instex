## Completeness summary

**Primary / pupil**: complete for drawing. 18 hexagons, 1.32 m flat-to-flat (1.524 m corner-to-corner DERIVED; 1.517 m STPSF), 7 mm gaps (7.6-7.7 mm between STPSF polygons), two rings (6 A; 6 B + 6 C) around an empty hexagonal centre (1.335 m flat-to-flat, DERIVED). Orientation (STPSF vertices, JDox photo): segments flat-side-up in the V2/V3 entrance pupil (edges perpendicular to V3, corners along V2); outline rotated 30 deg (corners toward +/-V3, B1 on +V3). Circumscribed 6.6035 m (STPSF) / 6.64 m (MC23) / 6.6 m (G06 label); outline flat-to-flat 6.575 m; inscribed 5.4733 m; entrance pupil 6.6 m; area-equivalent 5.7 m. Area 25.44 m^2 measured unobscured (26.3 m^2 polished). All 18 polygons are in the JSON.

**Obstructions**: complete from the STPSF model only. Secondary 0.738 m sits inside the central hole (no circular obscuration, no baffle mentioned). Three struts: +V3 (0.1189 m, bumps 0.165/0.140 m for the hinge and dampers) and two lower ones at azimuth 150/210 deg from +V3 (0.1224 m), centre to r = 3.30 m. No paper states strut widths.

**Optics and foci**: complete except plate scales (DERIVED) and the focal-surface curvature. TMA + FSM: SM convex 0.738 m at V1 = 7.169 m; Cassegrain intermediate focus at the AOS entrance (EFL 59.4 m, f/9 DERIVED, V1 ~ +1.4 m DERIVED, 2 reflections); tertiary 728 x 517 mm at V1 = -0.796 m; FSM 172.5 mm at the exit pupil, V1 = +1.048 m; telescope focus f/20, EFL 131.4 m, 1.5697"/mm DERIVED, ~18' x 9', curved focal surface ~1.96 m behind the primary vertex (2006 design), 4 reflections, shared by the instruments' pick-offs.

**Main gaps**: strut widths outside the STPSF model, a written segment-orientation statement, telescope plate scales, the Cassegrain-focus position, post-launch focal-surface position and curvature, the field-centre offset from V1.

## James Webb Space Telescope -- Optical Telescope Element (OTE)

**Recommended preset:** Three-mirror anastigmat with a flat fine steering mirror (FSM). Primary: 18 hexagonal beryllium segments, 1.32 m flat-to-flat, 7 mm gaps, two rings (6 A inside, 6 B + 6 C outside) around an empty hexagonal centre filled by the aft optics; circumscribed 6.6035 m in the STPSF pupil model (6.64 m in MC23), entrance pupil 6.6 m, inscribed 5.4733 m, area-equivalent 5.7 m; RoC 15879.7 mm, conic -0.9967 (f = 7.94 m, f/1.20 DERIVED). Segments are flat-side-up in the V2/V3 entrance pupil (edges perpendicular to V3, corners along V2); the outline is rotated 30 deg (corners toward +/-V3). Secondary: convex, 0.738 m, RoC 1778.9 mm, conic -1.6598, 7.169 m in front of the primary, on a deployable tripod whose three struts cross the pupil: one along +V3 (0.119 m wide in the model) and two at 30 deg either side of -V3 (0.122 m), all to r = 3.30 m. The secondary lies inside the central hole, so there is no circular obscuration. Collecting area 25.44 m^2 measured (25.4 m^2 JDox; 26.3 m^2 polished). Foci: an intermediate Cassegrain focus at the AOS entrance (EFL 59.4 m, f/9 DERIVED, 2 reflections), then tertiary (728 x 517 mm, behind the primary vertex) and FSM (172.5 mm, at the exit pupil) to the f/20 telescope focus (EFL 131.4 m, 1.570"/mm DERIVED, ~18' x 9' field, 4 reflections) on a curved focal surface ~1.96 m behind the primary vertex, shared by NIRCam, NIRSpec, MIRI and FGS/NIRISS through pick-off mirrors.

## Entrance pupil

Entrance pupil = the 18-segment primary (the aperture stop for most modes), seen from the sky with x = +V2, y = +V3: segments flat-side-up (edges perpendicular to V3), corners along V2; outline corners toward +/-V3; hexagonal central hole 1.335 m flat-to-flat / 1.537 m corner-to-corner (same orientation as the segments), inside which the 0.738 m secondary hides; three struts: +V3 (0.1189 m, with two bumps for the mid-boom hinge and magnetic dampers) and two lower struts at azimuth 150 and 210 deg from +V3 (0.1224 m), each from the centre to r = 3.30 m. Geometric clear area of the STPSF model 25.78 m^2 (measured 25.44 m^2). Diffraction: six bright spikes at 60 deg plus a fainter horizontal pair from the +V3 strut.
