The ELT design has evolved: the 2011 Construction Proposal (CP) and the 2012 SPIE overview (C12) describe the 39 m baseline with a 69 m primary radius and f/17.48 on 37 m; ESO's current web pages and the Nasmyth instrument ICD (ESO-253082 v3.6, through R18) give the current optics (68.685 m, f/17.75 on 38.542 m, 684.022 m). The segmentation (798 = 6 x 133 segments, 1.45 m, 4 mm gaps) is the same in all 39 m documents; ESO publishes the four radii of the segmentation for the 2011-2012 design.

## Completeness summary

**Primary**: 798 hexagonal segments (931 made), 1.45 m across the corners on the mirror, 4 mm gaps, six identical sectors of 133. The lattice has no segment at the center or in rings 1-4; rings 5-15 are complete and rings 16-17 partial, so the outline is a jagged near-circle (DERIVED: the 210 segments of the 'five inner rings' deferred in 2014 are rings 5-9, and a center-radius cut of 15.4 pitches gives exactly 798, and 588 without rings 5-9, both counts in ESO sources). ESO 2011-2012 radii: hole 4.7087 m (flats) to 5.6039 m (corners), outline 18.4525 m (inscribed) to 19.5732 m (circumscribed, 39.146 m across). Headline diameter 39.3 m; the ICD's nominal pupil 38.542 m. Radius of curvature 68.7 m (f = 34.35 m; f/0.87 quoted). Collecting area 978 m^2 (ESO).

**Pupil**: the central obscuration is the empty center (the M2 and the central tower lie in the shadow of the hole); ESO quotes 11.1 m (2011), the ICD 28.4% (10.95 m). Spider: six arms (ESO); the width comes from third-party models (AnisoCADO 0.53 m, ~0.5 m, hcipy 0.4 m); both models place the arms along the rows of segments, at 30 + 60k deg in the ESO M1 frame. The latest design makes five arms thinner than the sixth (A24; widths NOT FOUND).

**Mirrors**: M2 convex 4.25 m (4.10 m optical), R 8810 mm, 30.83 m above M1; M3 concave 4.0 m (3.78 m optical), R 21089.53 mm, at the M1 vertex; M4 flat adaptive 2.4 m, tilt 7.75 deg, 13.5 m above the M1 vertex, with the hole that limits the field; M5 flat tip-tilt 2.7 x 2.2 m, folding the beam along the elevation axis.

**Foci**: Nasmyth A and B (prefocal stations PFS-A, PFS-B, on either side of the tube): f/17.75 (on 38.542 m), 684.022 m, 0.3016 arcsec/mm (3.316 mm/arcsec), 10 arcmin unvignetted, 5 reflections to the straight-through port, a sixth flat (M6N) for lateral ports and M6C for the coude laboratory; focal surface radius 9.88 m, 27.2 m from M5. An intermediate f/4.4 focus (2012) at the M4 hole serves calibration sources. Gaps: spider width, spider angle and pupil orientation in an ESO document, the current segmentation radii, which platform faces east.

## Drawing (web/imager.js designs)

The drawing supports prime, cass, greg (each with an optional flat Nasmyth tertiary), korsch, pb, schmidt and refr. The ELT is a three-mirror anastigmat with an intermediate image: M2 sits at 30.83 m = 0.898 f1 and forms an image 13.5 m above the M1 vertex, at the hole in the flat M4; M3 at the M1 vertex sends the light back up to M4, which turns the beam down to M5, which turns the beam 90 deg along the elevation axis to a focus 27.2 m away. The existing "korsch" design is the closest: the secondary at 0.9 f1 (30.9 m for f1 = 34.35 m), an intermediate image, a concave tertiary and a fold flat with a hole for the light passing to the tertiary, then a 90 deg turn to the focus. Schematic in that drawing: the tertiary is drawn 0.35 D (13.7 m) behind the primary, while the ELT's sits at the primary's vertex; the two flats (M4 13.5 m in front of the primary, M5 6.2 m in front) are drawn as one flat 0.12 D (4.7 m) behind the primary; the focus lies 0.6 D (23.5 m) past that flat, where the ELT's lies 27.2 m past M5; the secondary's and tertiary's focal lengths are the drawing's own (chosen to give f/17.47 at that focus), not R2 = 8.81 m and R3 = 21.09 m; the fold direction is fixed (no Nasmyth side). The focal length (684.0 m) and plate scale are the ELT's. "pb" puts M3 correctly at the primary's center, but the pb secondary at 0.62 f1 (21.3 m; the ELT's M2 sits at 30.8 m), no intermediate image and a focus near the secondary misrepresent the ELT more. A faithful drawing needs a new design in imager.js and imgapp.js (M3 at the vertex, a holed flat M4 above the tertiary, a second flat M5 turning the beam to a chosen Nasmyth side).
