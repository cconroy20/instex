## Completeness summary

**Pupil (UT1-UT3, Dornier M2)**: the stop is the secondary M2 (SPEC Sec. 3.2.4 "The pupil is determined by the secondary mirror", WB Sec. 3.6.1 "The pupil is on M2"; confirmed), so the 8.2 m primary (8185.9 mm reflective) gives an entrance pupil of 8000 mm at Nasmyth and coude and 8115.0 mm at Cassegrain (M2 30.5 mm higher). Central obscuration: the M2 itself, 1116 mm (eps 0.1395; 1120 mm in WB, 1148 mm in the SPHERE model); 1550 mm (SPEC) or 1500 mm (WB) with the optional sky baffle. Spiders: 4 vanes, 40 mm (HCI; 41 mm S18, 5 cm B19), in two pairs meeting at nodes 0.524 m off centre, 101 deg apart within a pair; every vane line passes 0.4045 m from the centre, so no vane crosses the pupil (HCI geometry; offset struts also seen in K21 Fig. 1). At Cassegrain the stowed M3 adds a bump to the obscuration (measured on UT4). Collecting area: no ESO value (48.71 m^2 DERIVED at Nasmyth; 49.26 m^2 used by G26).

**UT4 (Yepun)**: deformable secondary (1120 mm, 2 mm shell, 1170 actuators) since October 2016, same spider interface; Cassegrain pupil (HCI after the ERIS document) 8.1196 m with a 1.293 m obscuration (eps 0.159) plus a 1.070 m stowed-M3 square; the UT4 Nasmyth pupil with the DSM is NOT FOUND.

**Foci (identical on UT1-UT4)**: Cassegrain f/13.4106 on 8115.0 mm (f = 108827 mm, 528 um/arcsec = 1.895 arcsec/mm, 15 arcmin total / 2.68 arcmin unvignetted, 2 reflections, focus 2500 mm below the M1 vertex, M3 flipped out). Nasmyth A and B f/15 on 8000 mm (f = 120000 mm, 582 um/arcsec = 1.719 arcsec/mm, 30 arcmin total / 7.15 unvignetted, M3 vignetting from 20 arcmin, 3 reflections, flat elliptical M3 1242 x 866 mm on the elevation axis 2500 mm above the M1 vertex, focus 6800 mm from the telescope axis on the elevation axis). Platform A is east and B west of the tube at azimuth 0 (pointing South) (WB Sec. 3.7). Coude f/47.3 (378400 mm, 1.835 mm/arcsec, 1 arcmin, 8 reflections M1-M8: M4 cylinder at the Nasmyth adapter, M5 sphere, M6 cylinder, M7 sphere, M8 flat at a pupil image), focus in the basement on the azimuth axis, feeding the VLTI; ESPRESSO's own coude trains start on the Nasmyth B platforms. Gaps: the Nasmyth side used by the VLTI coude train, the ICCF f-ratio, the spider-frame orientation.

**Instruments (EINS, May 2026)**: UT1 Cass FORS2, NasA none, NasB KMOS; UT2 Cass none (VISIR decommissioned April 2026), NasA FLAMES, NasB UVES; UT3 Cass X-shooter, NasA SPHERE, NasB CRIRES; UT4 Cass ERIS, NasA HAWK-I, NasB MUSE; ESPRESSO at the incoherent combined coude focus; GRAVITY, MATISSE at the VLTI focus. Historical: NaCo UT4 NasB 2001-2013 then UT1 NasA 2014-2019; ISAAC UT3 NasA to Dec 2013; VIMOS UT3 NasB; SINFONI UT3 Cass.
