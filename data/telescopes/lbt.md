The LBT is two telescopes on one mount: two 8.417 m f/1.142 paraboloids, centers 14.417 m apart, each with its own swing arms for the prime-focus camera (LBC), the adaptive secondary and the tertiary. The left side is SX and the right DX. The explorer draws one side; a single-side focus uses one 8.22 m (Gregorian) or 8.4 m (prime) circle, and the LBTI focus uses both mirrors in its pupil.

## Completeness summary

**Primary**: 8.417 m physical (8.4 m nominal), f/1.142, a paraboloid with a 0.889 m hole; two such mirrors, centers 14.417 m apart (14.4 m quoted). No source gives the focal length: f1 = 1.142 x 8.417 = 9.612 m (DERIVED). Collecting area 110 m^2 for both (an 11.8 m circle).

**Pupil**: at the Gregorian foci the undersized 0.911 m adaptive secondary is the stop: 8.22 m (AO and instrument papers; a real-ray trace of the quick-facts prescription gives 8.22-8.27 m, DERIVED), obstruction 11% (eps 0.1108). Struts: the secondary and tertiary swing arms, ~8% of the secondary diameter wide (0.073 m) and forming an 18 deg wedge (B20); their direction in the pupil is NOT FOUND and is INFERRED from a photograph (toward the outer edge of each mirror). At prime focus the primary is the stop (8.4 m); the LBC body's shadow is NOT FOUND (eps >= 0.106, the hole). The LBTI pupil holds both mirrors: two 8.22 m circles 14.417 m apart, 22.64 m edge to edge (22.65 m quoted).

**Secondary and tertiary**: the adaptive secondary is a concave 0.911 m Zerodur shell, 1.6 mm thick, 672 actuators, conic -0.7328; the tertiary a flat 64 x 50 cm ellipse 2.25 m above the primary's vertex. The M1-M2 spacing (10.68 m) and R2 (1.98 m) are DERIVED from a paraxial Gregorian.

**Foci**: prime focus with LBC-Blue (SX: 12.18 m, F/1.45, 16.935"/mm) and LBC-Red (DX: 16.9"/mm, F/1.453), each with a five-lens corrector plus window, 27' unvignetted. Gregorian f/15 (f/14.72 on the 8.417 m parent: 123.90 m, 1.6648"/mm; MODS headers 1.67"/mm), back focal distance 3.050 m: the direct Gregorian (MODS, 6' x 6'), the front bent Gregorian (LUCI, 4' x 4'), the center bent Gregorian (LBTI, both sides), the rear bent Gregorian (LINC-NIRVANA) and a fourth bent Gregorian (PEPSI fiber units). The LBTI combined focus: an f/15 envelope over the 22.65 m baseline (f/41 per mirror, ~340 m, 0.60-0.61"/mm DERIVED), LMIRCam 10.7 mas/pixel over ~22", NOMIC 0.018"/pixel, a phased field of order 1'. Gaps: the strut geometry, the prime-focus obstruction, a published combined-focus plate scale.

### Entering a binocular telescope: the proposal and its trade-offs

The layout drawing shows one telescope, and the pupil may hold several circles. The proposal: draw one side (SX) and let each focus say which pupil it uses. Single-side foci (LBC at prime focus, MODS at the direct Gregorian, LUCI at the front bent Gregorian) use one circle: 8.22 m at the Gregorian foci, where the undersized secondary is the stop, and 8.4 m at prime focus (focus D 8.4 with a pupil override). The LBTI focus carries a per-focus pupil override of kind "circles" with both mirrors (8.22 m each, centers +/-7.2085 m, holes 0.911 m, eps 0), so the image box shows the single-mirror envelope crossed by fringes along the baseline. The PSF computed with tpupil.js for that pupil has bright fringes at 0 and +/-12.6 px (period lambda/14.42 m) and a weak pair near +/-25 px, at the envelope's first zero (1.22 lambda/8.22 m): three bright fringes in the core, as Guyon et al. 2014 describe.

Trade-offs:

- The LBTI focus is entered at the bent Gregorian F = 15.073 (each side's focus at the beam combiner's entrance window): the drawing is the telescope's real Gregorian and the plate scale is the telescope's. The combined focus (f/15 on 22.65 m, f/41 per mirror, ~0.61"/mm) goes in the fieldNote.
- An alternative entry at the combined focus (F = 41.33 on 8.22 m, plate 0.6071"/mm) puts the beam combiner's 2.75:1 magnification into the drawn secondary, which then sits 9.98 m from the primary (the real secondary: 10.68 m). The plate check passes, but the drawing misplaces the secondary.
- The two-mirror pupil's D (22.637 m) sets the FFT grid; the strip's lambda/D and focal ratio stay those of one mirror (Dof = 8.22 m), correct for the drawing, so the lambda/D label refers to a single mirror.
- The explorer's eps obstruction is centered on (0, 0), between the mirrors in the two-mirror pupil, so the per-mirror shadows are entered as seg.hole and the LBTI focus sets eps 0; the struts use x0 = +/-7.2085 m.
- The SX pupil puts the struts on the -x side; the DX prime focus (LBC-Red) overrides the vanes to +x. The rear bent Gregorian (LINC-NIRVANA, both mirrors) and the PEPSI fiber units could be added as variants with the same optics.

## Conflicts

- Primary diameter: 8.417 m physical (LQF) vs 8.4 m (R16, GH09, G08); f1 = 9.612 m or 9.593 m depending on which diameter F/1.142 refers to.
- Center spacing: 14.417 m (LQF optics list) vs 14.4 m (LQF text, R16, S18, G14).
- Edge-to-edge baseline: 22.65 m (R16, GH09) vs 22.7 m (LTH Fizeau mode, H16) vs 22.8 m (LTH, LTM, B18, G14); DERIVED 22.637 m (Gregorian pupils) and 22.834 m (mirror rims).
- Gregorian focal length: f/14.72 on the parent (LQF) = 123.90 m if on 8.417 m vs MODS FOCSCALE 1.67"/mm = 123.51 m; nominal f/15 (LQF, LUC, MOC, LTH).
- Gregorian pupil: 8.22 m (A09, B19) vs 8.2 m (P17) vs DERIVED 8.22-8.27 m (real-ray trace).
- LBC-Red: 16.9"/mm and f/1.47 in one sentence (LFP; 16.9"/mm is f/1.453 on 8.4 m, f/1.47 would be 12.35 m); LBC-Blue F/1.45 (G08) vs F/1.46 (G08, the detector paragraph of Sec. 2.1).
- LBC corrected field: 110 mm and 108.2 mm, 'equivalent to 27 arcmin' (R16) vs 110 mm x 16.935"/mm = 31.0 arcmin (DERIVED) vs ~30 arcmin corrected and 27 arcmin unvignetted (G08).
- LBTI combined focus: f/15 envelope = 339.75 m on 22.65 m (DERIVED) vs 2.75 x 123.90 m = 340.7 m (DERIVED from the ellipsoids) vs NOMIC 0.600"/mm = 343.8 m and 17.9 mas = 345.7 m (DERIVED).
- NOMIC pixel: 0.018" (LTN) vs 17.9 mas (LTM).
- Swing-arm diffraction: MM attributes the 'bunny ears' in saturated stars to the secondary swing arm (Sec. 2.6.4) and, later, to the rigid secondary (Sec. 2.12.3).

## Notes

- Each side of the LBT is a separate telescope with an 8.417 m f/1.142 primary; the two share one mount, 14.4 m apart. The explorer draws one side (SX) at the chosen focus. The pupil at a single-side focus is one circle; at the LBTI focus a per-focus pupil override holds both mirrors (kind "circles", two 8.22 m circles centered at x = +/-7.2085 m, each with a 0.911 m central hole), so the diffraction-limited image shows the single-mirror envelope crossed by fringes along the baseline.
- At the Gregorian foci the undersized 0.911 m adaptive secondary is the stop, so the pupil (8.22 m) is smaller than the mirror; at prime focus the primary is the stop (8.4 m nominal, as G08 uses for F/1.45).
- The LBTI focus is entered at F 15.073 (the bent Gregorian focus of each side, at the LBTI entrance windows), so the drawing shows the real Gregorian geometry. The combined focus (f/15 on the 22.65 m envelope, f/41 on each mirror, 0.60"/mm) is in the fieldNote; an alternative entry with F 41.33 and plate 0.6071 draws a schematic Gregorian whose secondary sits 9.98 m from the primary (the real one: 10.68 m).
- The single-side pupil is SX's (the struts toward -x); the DX prime focus (LBC-Red) overrides the vanes to +x. LUCI, MODS and LBC differ only slightly between the sides.
- Optional further variants (same optics as the bent Gregorian front focus): the rear bent Gregorian (LINC-NIRVANA, both mirrors) and the fourth bent Gregorian (PEPSI fiber units, one pupil per side).
