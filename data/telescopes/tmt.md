## Completeness summary

**Primary**: 492 hexagonal segments (6 sectors of 82), 1.44 m across the corners on the mirror and 45 mm thick; in projection regular hexagons of side 0.716 m (pitch 1.24015 m, DERIVED) scaled radially by up to 1%; 2.5 mm gaps plus 0.5 mm bevels (3.5 mm optical gap). The center and the first lattice ring are empty; a radial cut of the lattice at 14.30-14.62 m gives exactly 492 segments and a circumscribed diameter of 30.004 m (DERIVED; the OAD requires a circumscribing circle of at least 30 m). M1: R = 60.000000 m, conic -1.00095348, f/1 (f = 30 m).

**Pupil**: the M1 is the entrance pupil. Central obstruction: the 3.6 m envelope of the secondary (drawing TMT.TEL.GTY-0001 Rev D, FIGURE READ-OFF), eps 0.11998 on 30.004 m. Spider: six legs at most 225 mm wide, from the M2 envelope to the corners of a hexagonal top-end ring, at 30 + 60k deg in the ECRS frame, along the rows of segments (FIGURE READ-OFF). The shadows of the secondary and of the supports cover at most 4% of the pupil (OAD; DERIVED 3.96%). The explorer pupil collects 633.55 m^2, within 0.01% of the 633.47 m^2 of the '28.4-meter unobstructed mirror' on the TMT web page; the Detailed Science Cases quote 664.2 m^2.

**Secondary and tertiary**: convex hyperboloid, 3.1 m across, R = -6.22768 m, conic -1.31822813, 27.0937500 m from M1, on a hexapod (the M2 system at most 3.6 m across); a flat elliptical tertiary 3.5 x 2.5 m on the M1 axis at the elevation axis, 3.5 m above the M1 vertex, articulated to reach the instruments.

**Foci**: one optical design, f/15 (450 m, 0.458366"/mm) at any of the Nasmyth foci on a 20 m circle about the elevation-axis origin; back focal distance 16.5 m; 20' unobstructed and 15' unvignetted; a curved focal surface (3.00923 m). First light: WFOS at 0 deg on the +X platform, NFIRAOS (IRIS, MODHIS) at 174.5 deg and the alignment and phasing system at 180 deg on the -X platform. Gaps: a current as-designed leg width (225 mm is a maximum), the M1 nominal radius in the scaling formula, the pupil's orientation on the sky.

## Drawing (web/imager.js designs)

The drawing supports prime, cass, greg (each with an optional flat Nasmyth tertiary), korsch, pb, schmidt and refr. TMT is a Ritchey-Chretien with a flat tertiary, so "cass" with nasmyth represents the two foci exactly: with D = 30.004 m, f1 = 30.0 m, F = 14.998 and the focus 16.5 m behind the vertex (backN x D - a3 = 20.0 - 3.5 m), the model places the secondary 27.0937 m from the primary (OAD: 27.0937500 m) with f2 = -3.11384 m (R2 = -6.22768 m; OAD: -6.22767857 m in 2018). The tertiary sits 3.5 m above the vertex (a3), on the elevation axis, and the focus 20 m from the axis (backN 0.66658), as in the OAD. Schematic: the focal surface is drawn flat (radius 3.01 m); the platforms are drawn up (-X) and down (+X), and the instruments at other angles on the 20 m circle (from -26.5 to +5.5 deg and 174.5 to 206.5 deg) are not drawn; the 3D view tiles the 492 segments on the regular lattice.

## Conflicts

- M2 vertex radius: -6.227687857 m (OAD CCR43, 2026) vs -6.22767857 m (OAD CCR34, 2018) vs -6.2276786 m (DERIVED from 450 m, f1 = 30 m, d = 27.09375 m, b = 16.5 m): the 2026 value has an extra digit in the sixth decimal place, most likely a transcription slip.
- Collecting area: 664.2 m^2 (DSC 2015, 2024) vs 633.5 m^2 (TMT web, a 28.4 m unobstructed equivalent) vs 633.55 m^2 (DERIVED, the explorer pupil with the obstructions) vs 655.3 m^2 (DERIVED, the gapless projected lattice).
- Segment size: 1.44 m across the corners on the mirror (TOP, M1O, hcipy) vs 1.432 m in projection (DERIVED, 2 x 0.716 m, OAD) and up to 1% larger near the center (the radial scaling).
- Gap: 2.5 mm nominal (TOP, M1O, hcipy) vs 3.5 mm optical gap with the bevels (OAD43).
- Central obscuration: 3.6 m M2 envelope (OAD drawing) vs 3.636 m (hcipy); the secondary itself is 3.1 m (TOP).
- Spider: at most 225 mm (OAD drawing) vs 0.22 m (hcipy, read from Jensen-Clem et al. 2021); the TMT web calls the supports ultra-thin.
- Lattice scale: hcipy places 1.44 m hexagons (across the corners) on a pitch of 1.44 sqrt(3)/2 + 0.0025 = 1.2496 m and keeps the centers within 0.98 x 30 m = 29.4 m, after removing the center and the first ring as the OAD figure and M1O do; the OAD's 0.716 m side gives a pitch of 1.24015 m, 0.76% smaller.
- Optical design wording: Ritchey-Chretien (OAD, TOP) vs 'Ritchey-Chretien 3-mirror telescope' (S13; the third mirror is the flat tertiary).

## Notes

- Design version: OAD TIO.SEN.DRD.05.002.CCR43 (2026-01-10) for the optics, segmentation and Nasmyth layout; the pupil obscuration pattern is drawing TIO.TEL.GTY-0001 Rev D (2014), printed in OAD CCR34 (2018) and still cited as AD41 by CCR43.
- The explorer's two-mirror Cassegrain with a flat Nasmyth tertiary (imager.js cass, nasmyth) reproduces the OAD prescription: with f1 = 30 m, F = 14.998, back 16.5 m, the model places the secondary 27.09375 m from the primary (OAD 27.0937500 m) with R2 = -6.22768 m. The tertiary at 3.5 m and the focus 20 m from the axis are the TMT's.
- The segment lattice is regular (pitch 1.24015 m, from the OAD's 0.716 m side); the real projected segments grow by up to 1% toward the center. The gap is the 3.5 mm optical gap.
- With the 3.5 mm gaps, the 3.6 m M2 envelope and six 225 mm legs, the explorer pupil collects 633.55 m^2, within 0.01% of the TMT web page's 28.4 m unobstructed equivalent (633.47 m^2), so the area can be validated (tol 0.005) against that value.
- Which platform is drawn up or down: WFOS (+X) is on the right and NFIRAOS (-X) on the left (DSC24 Fig. 2-2); following the tpresets convention, +X is drawn down and -X up.
