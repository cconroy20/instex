## Completeness summary

**Pupil**: complete. Unobstructed 4.0 m circle (no central obscuration, no spider: NUT, R20 Table 1, HS17), defined by a cooled aperture-stop ring above the 4.24 m M1; 12.56 m^2.

**Off-axis primary**: complete. Off-axis paraboloid, RoC 16 m (f = 8 m), conic -1, centre 4 m from the axis of a 12 m parent (aperture y = 2-6 m), f/2; chief ray at 28.07 deg to the parent axis (DERIVED; HS17 'roughly 28.1').

**Foci**: prime f/2 (8 m, 25.78"/mm, heat stop with 5' field, ~75 mm Sun, 1 reflection); Gregorian f/13 (52.2 m and 3.95"/mm DERIVED; GOS with 2.8'/5' field stops; 2 reflections); f/53 relay focus ~4 m above the coude floor (R20: f/60; 6 reflections, no access); collimated coude lab (M7-M10, ~0.2 m pupil on the DM; 10 reflections). Focal lengths and plate scales after M1 are DERIVED.

**Main gaps**: the public prescription (SPEC-0029 is used via a thesis), M1-M2 spacing and M2 decentre (positions DERIVED for a coaxial Gregorian), sizes of M3, M4, M7-M9, and a telescope coude focus (none: the coude beam is collimated).

## Daniel K. Inouye Solar Telescope (DKIST)

**Recommended preset:** Unobstructed 4 m off-axis Gregorian on an alt-az mount. M1: concave off-axis paraboloid (conic -1, RoC 16 m, f = 8 m), a 4 m clear-aperture segment of a 12 m parent whose centre is 4 m from the parent axis (4.24 m Zerodur meniscus, 75 mm thick); f/2 on the 4 m aperture; the entrance pupil is a cooled 4.0 m aperture-stop ring above M1; collecting area 12.56 m^2; no central obscuration and no spider. Prime focus f/2 (25.78"/mm, solar image ~75 mm) carries the heat stop (5' field). M2: off-axis concave ellipsoid 0.65 m (0.635 m), conic -0.54, fast tip/tilt, giving the f/13 Gregorian focus at the Gregorian Optical Station (2.8' and 5' field stops; ~3.95"/mm DERIVED). M3 (flat) and M4 (ellipsoid) put the beam over the elevation axis at f/53 (f/60 in R20); M5 (tip/tilt, pupil) and M6 send it down the azimuth axis; the f/53 image lies ~4 m above the coude floor. M7-M10 in the rotating 16.5 m coude lab: M8 collimates (~0.2 m beam) and images the pupil onto the DM (M10); the instruments (VBI, ViSP, VTF, DL-NIRSP with AO; Cryo-NIRSP via M9a) re-image the collimated beam.

## Entrance pupil

Entrance pupil = a filled 4.0 m circle (the cooled aperture-stop ring above the 4.24 m off-axis primary). Unobstructed: the off-axis secondary sits outside the incoming beam, so there is no central obscuration and no spider; collecting area 12.56 m^2. Downstream pupil images: the Lyot stop after M2, the M5 tip/tilt mirror and the deformable mirror M10 (~0.2 m).

## Not found

- A public copy of the DKIST optical prescription (Hubbard, 'DKIST Optical Prescription', SPEC-0029 Rev G, 2015): its M2, M4 and M8 radii and conics are used here through a secondary source (M20 thesis); the M1-M2 spacing and M2's off-axis distance are not published, so the M2 position, the Gregorian-focus position and the f/13.06 and f/53.9 values are DERIVED assuming a coaxial Gregorian (checked against HS17's incidence angles).
- Published focal lengths and plate scales at the Gregorian and f/53 foci (only f/13 and f/53 or f/60; the plate scales here are DERIVED). The prime-focus plate scale (25.78"/mm) is published (NUT).
- A coude focus of the telescope: the coude lab receives a collimated beam (pupil on the DM) and each instrument forms its own focus; instrument feed focal ratios were not compiled.
- Mirror sizes for M3, M4, M7-M9 (only M2 0.635-0.65 m, M5 and M6 275 mm, DM ~0.2 m), and fold angles that agree between sources (M5/M6: 30 and 60 deg in HS17 vs 22.5 deg 'inclination' each in R20).
- Distances along the beam between M2 and M3/M4, and from M6 to the coude floor (only 'about 4 m above the coude lab floor' for the f/53 image; M1 28 m above ground; facility height 41.4 m in R20).

## Conflicts

- f-number after M4: f/53 (HS17 text and Table 1) vs f/60 (R20 Sec. 4.4). The SPEC-0029 radii and conics give f/53.9 (DERIVED), and the M8 collimated beam is then ~199 mm, matching HS17's 0.2 m DM.
- M2 diameter: 0.65 m (R20 Sec. 4.1) vs 0.635 m (R20 Fig. 6 caption).
- Heat-stop field: 5' = 12.19 mm (NUT) vs 11.64 mm DERIVED from NUT's own 25.78"/mm plate scale (12.19 mm would correspond to 24.6"/mm or a 5.24' field).
- Primary size: 4.24 m blank (R20, HS17 Fig. 1) vs 4.2 m (OH16 Table 1 '4200', HS17 Sec. 1 '4.2m'); clear aperture 4.0 m in all sources.
- M5/M6 folds: HS17 'M5 folds at 30' and M6 'folding at a 60 angle' (AOI 15 and 30 deg in Table 1) vs R20 'two mirrors each at 22.5 degree inclination'.
- Heat load: 13 kW (R20) vs ~12,000 W (NUT).

## Notes

- Drawing frame used for DERIVED positions: z along the parent-paraboloid axis from the parent vertex (toward the Sun), y across it toward the M1 centre; metres. M1 aperture y = 2.0-6.0 m, z = 0.125-1.125 m (centre z = 0.50 m); prime focus (heat stop) at (0, 8.00); M2 vertex at (0, 9.20), chief-ray point on M2 at (-0.594, 9.114); Gregorian focus (GOS) at (0, 1.365). M3-M6 then fold the beam over the elevation axis and down the azimuth axis to the coude lab, where the f/53 image is ~4 m above the floor.
- Reflections: prime focus 1 (M1); Gregorian focus 2 (M1, M2); f/53 intermediate focus 6 (M1-M6; inferred position, see foci); DM pupil 10 (M1-M10); AO instruments then BS1 and one or more FIDO dichroics; Cryo-NIRSP M1-M9 + M9a = 10, no DM.
- The off-axis paraboloid's 'f/2' uses the parent focal length (8 m) over the 4 m aperture; the M1-centre-to-focus distance is 8.5 m (f/2.125 along the chief ray, DERIVED). Published plate scale 25.78"/mm = 206265/8000.
- Pupil for the explorer: a filled 4.0 m circle, eps = 0, no spiders; diffraction-limited PSF = Airy pattern; the seeing-limited image dominates except with AO.
- Sources disagree on M4's output f-number (53 vs 60); f/53 is adopted because the prescription (f/53.9) and the 0.2 m DM support it.
