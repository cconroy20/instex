## Completeness summary

**Primary**: fused quartz, 4011.6 mm across (bare clear aperture 4002.6 mm), 61 cm thick, conic -1.09763 (the Ritchey-Chretien hyperboloid), central hole 1324 mm. Masks define the pupil: 3797 mm outside, 1346 mm inside. Radius of curvature 21318.0 mm since the 2014 laser-tracker measurement (M24; 21336 mm before), so f1 = 10659.0 mm, f/2.807 on 3.797 m (f/2.8 quoted). Complete.

**Foci**: (1) prime focus with the DESI corrector (since 2019): six lenses, C1 8.69 m above the primary, f = 13.920 m on axis (f/3.68 quoted on axis, 3.86 averaged; f/3.666 on 3.797 m), 67.48 um/arcsec = 14.819 arcsec/mm, 3.2 deg on an 812 mm convex focal surface 11.116 m from the primary (DERIVED), 5020 fibers. (2) Prime focus with the Mosaic corrector (4mPFC, historical, used until 2018): four fused-silica lenses and a Risley-prism ADC, 17.43 arcsec/mm at the center (f = 11.834 m, f/3.12 on 3.797 m; f/3.14 quoted), 36 x 36 arcmin. (3) f/7.8 Ritchey-Chretien Cassegrain (historical, retired 2018): secondary R 9643.0 mm, K -5.2951, 1191.6 mm, 7492.0 mm from the primary; f = 31258.7 mm (f/8.23 on 3.797 m), 6.599 arcsec/mm, focus 1814.2 mm behind the vertex. (4) f/15 infrared Cassegrain (historical): secondary R 5000.2 mm, 742.7 mm, 8634.5 mm from the primary; f = 57160.3 mm, 3.609 arcsec/mm, focus 2260.5 mm behind the vertex. Cassegrain fields NOT FOUND. The coude focus of the original design was unused and its M3 weldment removed.

**Pupil**: DESI: obstruction 1.8 m (the cage; eps 0.474), 12 vanes in four sets of three, each 19 mm thick (desimodel: four 30 mm supports), leaving the cage 11.5 deg from the diagonals; vane geometry from a figure read-off; area 8.658 m^2 (KPNO, D22; reproduced by the desimodel formula, DERIVED). Before DESI: obstruction 1.651 m (the old prime-focus cage; eps 0.435), area 9.182 m^2 without vanes; the old spider fins (count, width, layout) NOT FOUND.

## Drawing (web/imager.js designs)

Prime foci ("prime" with a corrector): imager.js draws a corrector that changes the focal length as one thin lens a distance d before the primary's own focus. d is chosen here so that the drawn focus falls where the real focal surface lies: DESI d = 1.49 m (the lens 9.17 m from the primary, between the real C1 at 8.69 m and C2 at 9.35 m; focus 11.115 m from the primary, real 11.116 m), Mosaic d = 0.72 m (focus 10.736 m, design 10.738 m). The DESI corrector is 2.03 m long and 1.14 m across, so the one-lens drawing compresses six lenses into one plane; the real primary-to-focus distance and the focal ratio are right.

Cassegrain foci ("cass"): with F1 = 2.807, F = 8.2325 and back = 0.4778 D, imager.js places the f/7.8 secondary 7.487 m above the primary with f2 = -4.813 m; the real secondary is 7.492 m above the primary with R/2 = 4.822 m. For f/15 (back 0.5953 D) the drawing gives 8.629 m and f2 = -2.496 m (real 8.635 m and 2.500 m). Both drawings therefore reproduce the real Cassegrain geometry to better than 0.2%.

## Conflicts

- Primary radius of curvature: 21318.0 mm (KMP current, M24 as-built "updated value", measured 2014, confirmed 2018) vs 21336 mm (J98 1998, KZ8 model, KLF 10668.0 = R/2, KMP HTML comment) vs 21.3 m (FDR).
- Pupil diameter: 3797 mm used clear aperture (KMP, D22, M24, SSY) vs 3760 mm clear aperture (J98 1998 design) vs 3950 mm (KZ8 model ENPD) vs 3883.4 mm (KZ15 model, secondary as stop) vs 3.8 m (FDR, SS12) vs 4000 mm (M24 Table 4, J98 Table 1 mirror) vs 4002.6 mm bare clear aperture vs 4011.6 mm mirror (KMP).
- Inner edge: central hole 1324 mm (KMP, D22) vs inner mask 1346 mm (M24) vs 1320 mm stop annulus (KZ8).
- Central obstruction with DESI: 1800 mm cage (KMP, SSY) vs 1762 mm corrector shadow (M24); before DESI: 1651 mm (KMP, KLF) vs 1968 mm "Bottom of the Secondary (Obscuration)" (J98 Table 1).
- Primary focal ratio: f/2.8 (D22, J98, M24) vs f/2.81 (KLF, 10668 / 3797) vs f/2.807 (DERIVED from 21318 mm).
- DESI focal ratio: f/3.68 on axis (D22, M24 Table 2) vs 3.666 = 13.920 / 3.797 (DERIVED) vs 3.86 field average (M24) vs "approximately f/3.9" (D22, M24) vs f/3.85 and f/3.9 (FDR, fiber injection).
- Mosaic focal ratio: f/3.14 (J98, on 3.76 m) vs f/3.117 on 3.797 m (DERIVED) vs "f/3.1" (KMP).
- f/7.8 naming: f/7.8 (KMP, KLF; = 31258.7 / 4002.6) vs "f/7.6" (KMP link text) vs "f/8" (KZ8 name, SS12) vs f/8.23 on the 3.797 m pupil (DERIVED).
- DESI vanes: 12 vanes in four sets of three with middle (C) vanes (M24, D22, as built) vs four fins without the middle vanes (FDR 2016 design). Width 19 mm (M24) vs 0.03 m (SSY desimodel).
- Site altitude: 2123.2 m (KMP) vs "approximately 2021m" (D22).
- KMP cites J98 as "1988 SPIE, 3355, 721"; SPIE volume 3355 is the 1998 meeting (J98 cites Muller et al. in "SPIE Conference 3355, this volume").

## Notes

- Explorer conventions: F = focal length / 3.797 m; plate scale = 206264.8 / f (arcsec/mm); back = distance of the Cassegrain focus behind the primary's vertex in units of D; reflections count mirrors only.
- Pupil frame: M24 Fig. 45 end view (N up, E right, seen from the sky with the telescope at zenith); on the equatorial mount the vane pattern keeps its orientation on the sky.
- The DESI focal ratio varies across the field (f/3.68 to f/3.85-4.23 at the edge, D22, with anamorphic plate scale); the preset uses the on-axis focal length.
- Light-collecting areas: KPNO's 9.182 m^2 equals the 3.797/1.651 m annulus exactly (DERIVED), so the vanes were not subtracted; the DESI 8.658 m^2 subtracts the 1.8 m cage and four 30 mm supports (DERIVED from SSY/SSI).
- File provenance: KZ8/KZ15 (ZEMAX text), SSY/SSI (specsim) were read as text and never executed.
