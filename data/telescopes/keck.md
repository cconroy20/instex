## Completeness summary

**Pupil (both telescopes, one design)**: complete for f/15. 36 regular hexagons (side 0.900 m; 1.800 m corner-to-corner, 1.5588 m flat-to-flat DERIVED) in three rings around an empty central segment; 3 mm gaps, 7 mm non-reflecting strips with the bevels; circumscribed 10.949 m (KOTN163; 10.95 KTG02; 10.93 H17), inscribed 9.00 m (DERIVED; NIRES f/16.6 inscribed mask -> 9.01 m), equivalent 9.96 m (area of the 37-cell tiling); 76 m^2 design area (75.76 m^2 geometric; ~71.5 m^2 clear at f/15 DERIVED). Central obscuration: circular secondary + baffle, 2.6 m design (2.48 m measured with NIRC2, 2.65 m CPSF, 2.62 m H17); explorer eps 0.24. Six spiders, 25 mm wide, parallel to the segment edges and aimed at the midpoints of the outline's sides (30, 90, ... 330 deg in the L21 frame), with 0.3692 x 0.08 m nodes at the obscuration. All measurements are Keck II; no Keck I pupil measurement found.

**Keck I foci**: Cassegrain f/15 (149.583 m, 1.379"/mm, 20', 2 reflections; LRIS, LRISp, MOSFIRE); right Nasmyth f/15 (3 reflections; HIRES, Zshooter to follow); left Nasmyth f/15 feeding the Keck I AO (OSIRIS, KPF FIU; 9 reflections to the AO science window DERIVED); four bent Cassegrain f/15 stations (3 reflections, 5' design; PCS); historical forward-Cassegrain f/25 (249.7 m, 0.826"/mm, 10', 0.530 m chopping secondary; NIRC 1993-2010, LWS 1995-2005). Gaps: Keck I AO details, f/25 secondary undersizing and hole, other bent Cassegrain users.

**Keck II foci**: Cassegrain f/15 (ESI, hibernated July 2026); right Nasmyth f/15 (DEIMOS, KCWI, NIRSPEC, one at a time); left Nasmyth f/15 feeding the Keck II AO (NIRC2, NIRSPAO, SCALES; 10 reflections to NIRC2, 13 to NIRSPEC); four bent Cassegrain f/15 stations (PCS; NIRES at RBC = station 3, hibernated May 2026); historical f/40 IFSM chopping secondary (395.0 m, 0.522"/mm, 10' design / 186" at the RBC visitor port, 3 reflections; secondary undersized x 0.954 = the stop, 10.445 m DERIVED, hexagonal central hole -> 3.25 m DERIVED; MIRLIN, OSCIR). Gaps: post-HAKA reflection count, AO science field, f/40 chopping-assembly vanes.

**Design-only / proposed**: prime focus f/1.75 and an f/2 prime-focus camera (30') and an f/100 coude in the 1985 design (not among the 2002 foci); a proposed 1-deg prime-focus imager (KWFI), telescope and scale NOT FOUND.

## Notes

- Both telescopes share one optical design: KTG02 Sec. 3.1 ('The primary mirrors', 'The f/15 secondary mirrors') and KIVI describe a single primary and f/15 system; the IR secondaries differ (Keck I f/25 forward Cassegrain, Keck II f/40 IFSM).
- Nominal f-numbers use D = 10 m (f/15 = 149.6/10); instrument papers often use 10.95 m (f/13.66). Plate scale = 206264.8 / f; e.g. f/15: 1.379"/mm (0.725 mm/").
- Reflections: Cassegrain 2 (M1, M2); Nasmyth and bent Cassegrain 3 (plus the flat M3 on the elevation axis); forward Cassegrain f/25 2; AO-fed: 3 to the Nasmyth focus, 10 to the Keck II NIRC2 window (KAON501), 9 on Keck I (DERIVED, NL13).
- Pupil drawing: segment centres (segment_centres_DERIVED) are the a = 0.900 m hexagonal lattice (pitch 1.5588 m) minus the centre; segments have vertices along +/-y; spiders at 30, 90, ... 330 deg from 1.3 m to 4.5 m; obscuration a 2.6 m disc (0.24 x 10.95 m in the explorer convention). Physical segments are 0.89827 m on a side (3 mm gaps); 2 mm bevels make 7 mm non-reflecting strips.
- OCR artefacts in KOR90 quotes are kept verbatim ('l' for 1, 'O' for 0, 'Balling'/'baffies' for Baffling/baffles, '(3) 112' for 3^(1/2)).
