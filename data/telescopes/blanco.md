## Completeness summary

**Primary**: a Cer-Vit hyperboloid (conic -1.09763), radius of curvature 21311.6 mm (f = 10.656 m, f/2.7), mirror 4022.9 mm, bare clear aperture 3965.4 mm, used clear aperture 3934 mm (behind a seal, since 2000), central hole 1317.8 mm. Collecting area 10.014 m^2 (NOIRLab; the 1651 mm obstruction only).

**Pupil**: since 2012 the central shadow is the DECam prime-focus cage, 1.65 m across (eps 0.4197; NOIRLab's 1651 mm of 2000, batoid's end caps, Piff's 0.4196). The DECam filter-changer housing sticks out of that shadow on two opposite sides to 1.012 m from the center (batoid polygon; the Piff image shows the same outline). The cage hangs from the upper rings on four "fins" (F15) at 45 deg to the housing: 0.068 m wide, one 0.135 m, in the Piff pupil image (Piff's strut model: 0.0666 m, "tuned to match DECam big donut images"; batoid: 0.019 m). The pupil's orientation on the sky is NOT FOUND.

**Foci**: (1) prime focus with DECam (2012 onwards): five-lens fused-silica corrector (C1-C5, filter between C3 and C4), f/3.0 published, 17.575 "/mm (F = 2.983 on 3.934 m, DERIVED), 2.2 deg field, focal plane 10.781 m above the primary (DERIVED from the prescription). (2) the earlier prime focus, f/2.87, 18.0 "/mm, three-lens corrector with an ADC, Mosaic II (36' x 36') and the BTC before Mosaic II (historical). (3) the Ritchey-Chretien Cassegrain f/8 (COSMOS now; Hydra's fiber plate covered 40'): secondary 1295.4 mm, R 9569.6 mm, conic -5.2625, 7494.25 mm from the primary; F = 7.984 on 3.934 m and the focus 1.825 m behind the primary's vertex (DERIVED; "F/7.8" on the 4.02 m mirror, 6.56 "/mm published). (4)-(5) the retired F/14.5 and F/30 secondaries (prescriptions published; focal lengths DERIVED; sizes NOT FOUND). Gaps: a DECam-era obstruction and fin widths from CTIO or the DECam team, the pupil orientation, the pre-DECam corrector layout, the f/8 corrected field.

## Drawing (web/imager.js designs)

The two DECam-era foci map onto existing designs. **Prime focus (DECam)**: design "prime" with a corrector, the Subaru/HET treatment: one thin lens of the power that turns the primary's f/2.709 cone into f/2.983 (m = 1.1014). With d = 1.237 m the lens sits 9.419 m above the primary (between the real C1 at 8.875 m and C2 at 9.644 m) and the drawn focus falls 10.781 m above the primary, where F15 Table 1 puts the focal plane. Schematic: the single lens (the real corrector has five lenses and a filter over 1.9 m, C1 980 mm across), the camera body, hexapod and cage (only the label). **Cassegrain f/8**: design "cass", which for a pure two-mirror telescope reproduces the real geometry: with F 7.984, F1 2.7086 and back 0.4639, the model's secondary lands 7494.26 mm from the primary (published 7494.25 mm) with the focus 1.825 m behind the vertex; only the Cassegrain cage and instrument are schematic. The retired f/14.5 and f/30 foci are drawn the same way (secondary at 8621.20 and 9687.10 mm, matching the published spacings), but the size of their secondaries, and so any undersized-stop effect, is NOT FOUND. **Prime focus f/2.87** (historical): design "prime", m = 1.0754, with the lens's place and size schematic (d = 1.0 m). The pupil view draws the DECam-era shadow: cage disk, housing polygon and four fins.
