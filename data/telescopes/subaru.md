## Completeness summary

**Primary**: 8.2 m effective (8.3 m physical) thin ULE meniscus, 20 cm thick, 261 actuators; f = 15 m, f/1.83; R = -30000 mm, K = -1.008350515. Complete.

**Foci** (I04 Table 2, F = f / pupil diameter): prime focus bare f/1.83 (15 m, 13.75"/mm); with the HSC 7-element WFC f/2.2 published (f = 18.4 m, f/2.25 DERIVED, 11.2"/mm centre, 1.5 deg; in use with HSC and PFS); with the historical Suprime-Cam 3-lens Wynne corrector F/1.86 (0.202"/15 um, 34' x 27', 30' unvignetted). Cassegrain (3000 mm behind the M1 vertex, 6', 2 reflections): optical M2 (1.33 m) f = 100.0 m, f/12.195, 2.0627"/mm (FOCAS; MOIRCS); IR chopping M2 (1.265 m, silver) f = 100.0 m, f/12.373 on its 8.0819 m stop. Optical Nasmyth (M2 1.40 m + M3): with image rotator f = 104.2 m, f/12.708, 1.979"/mm, 3.5', 6 reflections; without f = 102.2 m, f/12.469, 2.017"/mm, 6', 3 reflections (HDS). IR Nasmyth (IR M2 moved toward M1 + M3): with rotator f = 108.5 m, f/13.612 on 7.9716 m, 1.901"/mm, 3.5'; without f = 110.6 m, f/13.917 on 7.9475 m, 1.865"/mm, 6', 3 reflections (AO188/AO3k: IRCS, IRD, SCExAO). Published nominal F: 2.0 / 12.2 / 12.6 / 13.6. Nasmyth platforms are named optical and infrared; no left/right naming published (NOT FOUND). Nasmyth focus distance from the axis NOT FOUND.

**Pupil**: one telescope spider for every top unit (I04): 4 thick vanes, 0.224 m (L09) / 0.237 m (K18) / 0.184 m (HCI model), at +/-51.75 deg to the pupil horizontal, non-intersecting, each pair starting 0.66 m off centre. Measured only in the IR Nasmyth pupil (SCExAO): D = 7.92-7.95 m (design 7.9475 m), central obscuration 2.37 m (K18; 2.3-2.44 m in other sources, eps ~0.30). Optical-foci and prime-focus central obscurations NOT FOUND (lower bounds: M2 1.33 / 1.40 m; prime-focus unit POpt2 inner diameter ~1.04 m). No official collecting area.

## Notes

- Explorer conventions used here: F = focal length / pupil diameter of the same configuration (I04 Table 2); plate scale = 206264.8 / f; reflections count mirrors only (correctors and AO optics excluded).
- One spider for all foci: the four thick (~0.22-0.24 m) vanes at +/-51.75 deg, offset 0.66 m from the centre, are part of the telescope (top ring + inner ring); the top units (PFU, M2 units) lock into the inner ring (I04). Only the central obscuration changes with the top unit.
- The IR secondary (1265 mm) is deliberately undersized: it is the aperture stop at the IR foci (8.08 m Cassegrain, 7.95-7.97 m Nasmyth), keeping warm structure outside the primary out of the IR beam; the published pupil diameters follow from the M2 size and spacing (DERIVED in ir_secondary_stop).
- The Nasmyth foci are fed by moving the Opt/Nas or IR M2 along the axis (I04); the paraxial check reproduces every Table 2 focal length and back focus (DERIVED in secondary_mirrors and ir_secondary_nasmyth_use).
