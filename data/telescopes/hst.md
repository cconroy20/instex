# Telescope record: Hubble Space Telescope Optical Telescope Assembly (OTA) -- sourced parameters

Compiled 2026-10-19 for the instex TELESCOPES mode, in the format of `data/imaging/hst.md`. Machine-readable twin: `data/telescopes/hst.json` (a `telescope` object with `params`, a one-entry `foci` list and a `pupil` object, plus compact `explorer_inputs`). The imaging record `data/imaging/hst.json` (block `hst_ota`) is reused by key: same source keys and files; entries that restate an imaging value name the imaging key. New documents are saved unmodified under `rawdata/telescopes/hst/`. Every number carries a source key, location and a short verbatim quote; **DERIVED** marks arithmetic done here (shown). Quotes were machine-checked against the saved files (pdftotext layout, raw and reading-order text per page; HTML with tags stripped; the Tiny Tim plain-text tables), with glyphs normalised; ' ... ' joins non-adjacent pieces; table rows are quoted as whitespace-separated cells. OCR typos in the scanned 1990 OTA Handbook are kept verbatim. Figure labels without a text layer were checked against tesseract OCR of 300-600 dpi renders ("ocr check"); one label is visual only ("figure check"). Quote checks: 114 against text, 7 against OCR, 1 visual; plus 22 short quotations inside notes. [PDF p. N] is the page of the saved PDF.

Page conventions: OTAHB printed page = PDF page - 6; FGSIHB and the STScI Cycle-34 handbooks printed page = PDF page - 1; Lallo 2012 cited by arXiv PDF page; NASA SM3B Media Guide sections cited by printed page (1-N, 5-N = PDF page N of the section file).

Coordinates: V1 = optical axis (positive toward the aperture door), V2 parallel to the solar-array drive axis, V3 toward the nominal Sun direction (OTAHB Sec. 1.3.1.3). Current STScI documents use U1 = V1, U2 = -V2, U3 = -V3 (PRIMSYS). Focal-plane aperture positions are given as projected on the sky (arcsec); 'the cassegrain telescope inverts the image of the sky onto the focal plane' (OTAHB p. 20). Pupil azimuths here are measured from +V3 toward +V2 in the plane of OTAHB Figure 4.1 (+V3 drawn up, +V2 drawn right); whether that figure (and the Tiny Tim pad table) is viewed from the sky or from the focal plane is not stated.

## Completeness summary

**Primary**: complete. 2400 mm, concave hyperboloid, R = 11040 mm, f = 5520 mm, f/2.3 (MG5 says f/2.35), design conic K1 = -1.0022985, 600 mm central hole. The figuring error, as context: as-built K = -1.0132 to -1.0144, rms wavefront 253-277 nm (~1/2 wave), edge about 2.3 um (L12) / about 2 um (MG5) too flat, caused by a 1.3 mm spacing error in the null corrector. Collecting area: 45240 cm2 full pupil, 4930 cm2 under the secondary obscuration, 1320 cm2 under pads and spiders, 38990 cm2 clear (1990 OTA Handbook); 45,239 cm2 unobstructed (WFC3 IHB).

**Secondary**: complete except its focal length (DERIVED -679 mm). Convex hyperboloid, R = -1358 mm, K2 = -1.49686, 281 mm diameter (310 mm with housing), 4906.071 mm from the primary (L12: 4906.9 mm), magnification 10.435, delivering f/24.

**Focus**: complete except pick-off sizes. One f/24 Cassegrain focus, f = 57.6 m, 3.58"/mm, 1500.129 mm behind the primary vertex; exit pupil 7003.51 mm ahead of the focus (DERIVED 292 mm across); ~28 arcmin = 0.46 m field; curved focal surface (43.5 mm sag at 14 arcmin, DERIVED radius ~650 mm). Four axial bays (ACS, COS, STIS, NICMOS; direct feed, apertures a few arcmin off axis) and four radial bays (WFC3 through a flat 45-deg pick-off at the field centre; three FGSs through pickle-shaped plane pick-offs on quarter annuli 10-14 arcmin out, ~69 arcmin2 each). Reflections ahead of an instrument: 2 axial, 3 radial (DERIVED; no source states the count).

**Pupil**: complete. Outer radius 1200 mm; central obscuration radius 396.0 mm (eps = 0.330, the secondary baffle); four 25.654 mm vanes along the V2 and V3 axes; three pads 150.368 mm across, one on the +V3 vane at 1066.8 mm and two at 1071.88 mm (58.5 deg and 67.6 deg from -V3). The Figure 4.1 dimensions reproduce the published 1320 cm2 spider-plus-pad area (1319.4 cm2 DERIVED). Tiny Tim models the same pupil with a 26.4 mm spider and 156 mm pads at slightly different places (conflict listed).

**Main gaps**: secondary focal length (DERIVED only), pick-off mirror sizes and positions, baffle dimensions, the viewing sense of the pupil drawings, and a written count of vanes and reflections.

## Hubble Space Telescope -- Optical Telescope Assembly (OTA)

**Recommended preset:** Two-mirror Ritchey-Chretien Cassegrain. Primary: 2400 mm, concave hyperboloid, R = 11040 mm, f1 = 5520 mm (f/2.3), K1 = -1.0022985 by design (as built -1.0132 to -1.0144: the famous spherical aberration, ~1/2 wave, 253-277 nm rms wavefront, surface error ~2.3 um at the edge; MG5: the edge was 'too flat' by about 2 um), 600 mm central hole. Secondary: convex hyperboloid, R = -1358 mm, K2 = -1.49686, 281 mm diameter (310 mm with housing), 4906.071 mm in front of the primary, magnification 10.435. One focus: f/24 Cassegrain, f = 57.6 m, 3.58 arcsec/mm, 1500.129 mm behind the primary vertex (6406.200 mm behind the secondary); usable field ~28 arcmin = 0.46 m diameter (focal-surface fits out to 14 arcmin radius); curved focal surface (sag 43.5 mm at 14 arcmin, DERIVED radius ~650 mm). The field is shared spatially: 4 axial bays (ACS, COS, STIS, NICMOS) take light directly a few arcmin off axis; 4 radial bays take light via pick-off mirrors -- WFC3 through a flat 45-deg pick-off at the field centre, and the 3 FGSs through pickle-shaped plane pick-offs covering quarter annuli 10-14 arcmin from the axis (~69 arcmin2 each). Reflections before an instrument's own optics: 2 for an axial bay, 3 for a radial bay (DERIVED). Pupil: 2400 mm circle, central obscuration radius 396.0 mm (eps = 0.330, the secondary baffle), 4 spider vanes 25.654 mm wide along +-V2 and +-V3, 3 primary-mirror pads of diameter 150.368 mm (Figure 4.1; Tiny Tim 156 mm) near 0.89 of the radius.

## Conflicts

- Mirror spacing: 4906.071 mm (OTAHB Table 2.1, design) vs 4906.9 mm (L12 Table 1). The on-orbit spacing is trimmed by secondary moves (L12).
- Primary focal ratio: f/2.3 (L12; DERIVED 5520/2400 = 2.30) vs f/2.35 (MG5 p. 5-18).
- Secondary diameter: 281 mm mirror, 310 mm with housing (L12 Table 1); 0.31 m (L12 Fig. 3); 12.2 in = 310 mm (MG1); 12 in (MG5). The media guides quote the housed size.
- System focal length: 57.6 m (OTAHB, L12, MG5 '189 feet (57.6 m)') vs MG1 Fig. 1-4 '189 ft (56.7 m) folded to 21 ft (6.3 m)' -- a digit transposition in MG1 (189 ft = 57.6 m; 21 ft = 6.4 m as in MG5).
- Focus behind the primary: 1500.129 mm (OTAHB Table 2.1), 'almost five feet' (OTAHB p. 13), 1.5 m (L12 Fig. 3) vs MG5 '3.3 feet (1.5 m)' (3.3 ft = 1.0 m; the metric value is the consistent one).
- Exit pupil label in OTAHB Figure 2.4: the scan's text layer reads 'O.396M', the image and tesseract read 0.596 m; Table 2.1 (7003.51 - 6406.200 = 597.3 mm) and the mirror equation (596.5 mm) support 0.596 m.
- FGS field: quarter annulus 10-14 arcmin, ~69 arcmin2, arcs 83.3/85 deg (FGSIHB Secs. 1.4.5, 2.6.1) vs inner radius 10.2 arcmin (FGSIHB glossary) vs '60 arcmin2 of field in a 90-degree sector' (MG5, 2002); PRIMSYS says '10 to 14 arcseconds' (unit typo for arcminutes).
- Spider width: 25.654 mm (OTAHB Figure 4.1; 0.0214 R) vs 0.022 R = 26.4 mm (Tiny Tim) -- as in data/imaging/hst.json.
- Pads: OTAHB Figure 4.1 (centres at 1066.8 mm on +V3 and 1071.88 mm for the other two; 58.5 deg and 67.6 deg from -V3; diameter 150.368 mm) vs Tiny Tim (0.8921/0.8853/0.8870 R = 1070.5/1062.4/1064.4 mm; 58.6 and 59.0 deg from -V3; diameter 0.13 R = 156 mm). The largest difference is the -V2-side pad azimuth (247.6 vs 239.0 deg). The Figure 4.1 numbers reproduce the published 1320 cm2 spider+pad area (1319.4 cm2 DERIVED); the Tiny Tim numbers give 1381 cm2.
- Central obscuration naming: Tiny Tim labels 0.330 'OTA Secondary Mirror Radius'; the OTA Handbook calls it the secondary baffle obscuration, and the secondary mirror itself is 0.117-0.129 of D.
- Spherical aberration size: rms 253-277 nm and edge error ~2.3 um (L12 p. 14), '256 nm' (L12 p. 10), 'about 2 microns' at the edge (MG5), 'about one-half wave' (PRIMOTA), '~1/2 wave' (WFC3IHB). Consistent at the level quoted; context only.
- Bay history (not used for values): L12 Table 2 says GHRS was 'replaced with NICMOS' and FOS 'replaced with STIS', while L12 Figure 9 draws STIS where GHRS was and NICMOS where FOS was.

## Notes

- There is one focus. Every instrument, including the guiders, sees the same f/24 beam; they are separated by position in the field, not by switching foci. Parallel observations with several instruments exploit this (L12 p. 29: 'the spatial sharing of the HST focal plane among the SIs').
- Field angle to focal-plane radius (OTAHB Table 2.7): 1 arcmin = 16.758 mm, 5 arcmin = 83.73 mm, 10 arcmin ~ 167 mm (DERIVED between 9 and 11 arcmin rows), 14 arcmin = 233.24 mm.
- Coordinate conventions: V2 = -U2, V3 = -U3 (PRIMSYS: 'V1=U1, V2= –U2 and V3= –U3').
