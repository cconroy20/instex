## Completeness summary

**Both telescopes (twin design)**: D = 6.5 m spin-cast borosilicate honeycomb paraboloid, f/1.25 (f = 8125 mm DERIVED), 1.3 m central hole. f/11 Gregorian (concave static secondary ~1.3 m) + flat rotating tertiary feeding 5 stations: Nasmyth West, Nasmyth East and three bent-Gregorian folded ports (AUX1-3) on the center section; 3 reflections to each. f/11: 71,526.4 mm design (f/11.02 in the Clay Zemax model), 2.88"/mm (346.77 um/"), focal surface convex R = -1231.74 mm, 30' corrected field (24' unvignetted) with the tertiary-turret 2-element corrector + ADC, back focus 125 mm. Pupil (measured on Clay): eps = 0.29 diameter ratio set by the secondary baffle (1.885 m), 4 vanes at 45 deg offset 0.34 m from the centre, 0.75 in wide (two widened to 1.5 in by the MagAO cable trays on Clay). No official collecting area (DERIVED 30.1-30.2 m^2). The f/15 infrared Cassegrain of the 1994 design was never implemented per the sources found.

**Baade**: f/11 only. NASW = IMACS through the turret ADC/corrector (30' field); NASE = FourStar (no corrector); folded ports = MagE (AUX3), FIRE, LLAMAS. Gaps: Baade-specific obscuration and vane widths (Clay values used; all four vanes INFERRED at 0.75 in), folded-port field and port numbers for FIRE/LLAMAS.

**Clay**: f/11 NASW = LDSS3 (permanent); NASE = rotating port (MIKE, PFS, M2FS + its own 30' corrector, IFU-M, PISCO, MagAO-X, proto-Lightspeed); folded ports: no current instrument found. f/5 Cassegrain (Clay only): convex hyperbolic 1.8 m secondary, 0.169 mm/" = 5.917"/mm, F = 5.36 (DERIVED), 35' with the Megacam corrector, 2 reflections, eps 0.277 (baffle size unknown). F/16 MagAO adaptive secondary (85.1 cm concave ellipsoid, F/16.16 = 1.964"/mm DERIVED) at a Nasmyth station via M3, status unknown. Gaps: f/5 baffle and spider, Clay f/11 ADC/corrector, MagAO Nasmyth side.

Focus notes:

- f/15 infrared Cassegrain (1994 design): f_mm and scale are nominal (15 x 6500 mm), DERIVED

Entrance pupil:

- **f/11 stations (telescope default pupil)**: D = 6.5 m, eps = 0.29 (diameter ratio (obscuration diameter / 6.5 m); set by secondary-mirror baffle (value measured on Clay; no Baade value found)). Spiders: 4 vanes, offset cross: two pairs of vanes at +/-45 deg; each pair starts from a point 0.34 m from the centre on opposite sides, so opposite vanes are parallel and laterally offset by 0.481 m (each vane line 0.240 m off axis, DERIVED). ### Conflicts (shared)

- Central obscuration: 29% diameter ratio (MXP51, M18, L26, HCI; Clay) vs "~5% obscuration" of the area (MM19). 0.29^2 = 8.4% of the area; 5% (eps 0.22) matches the bare ~1.4 m secondary, not the 1.885 m baffle.
- f/11 focal length: 71,526.4 mm (CS98N9 design, f/11.004) vs f/11.02 = 71,630 mm (MXPA1, Clay Zemax) vs 0.347 mm/" = 71,574 mm (P04, rounded) vs 2.88"/mm = 71,620 mm (FSCDR, rounded).
- f/11 secondary: 1.3 m (MXPA1) vs 1.36 m beam footprint (MXP51) vs ~1.41 m (MM19, implied).
- Corrected f/11 field: 30' (CS98N9, S94, D11) with slight vignetting beyond 24' (BD03, LCOT); IMACS sees 0% vignetting to R = 12' and 9% (LCOUM) / ~10% (LCOUM f/2 section) at R = 15'.
- Spider widths: 0.75 in and 1.5 in (MXP51, HCI) = 1.905 and 3.81 cm; C18X rounds the first to 1.9 cm.

### Notes (shared)

- Terminology: the three center-section f/11 ports are called "auxiliary" (LCOT, G16), "folded" (FSCDR, SCH03, FIREM), "auxiliary Nasmyth" (D11), "folded Cassegrain" (LL20) and AUX1-AUX3 / "folded port 2" (CMAGE, MG08). MagAO calls its Nasmyth station the "folded Nasmyth port (NAS)" (MZ14).
- Reflections: 3 to every f/11 station (M1, M2, flat M3); 2 at the Clay f/5 Cassegrain (M15 "two aluminum reflections"); 3 at the MagAO F/16 station (M1, ASM, M3).
- The f/11 Gregorian ratio was chosen to match the IMACS collimator field curvature (param gregorian_ratio_choice); the Gregorian images the pupil in front of the secondary, which the IMACS flat-field screen exploits (param gregorian_pupil_image).
