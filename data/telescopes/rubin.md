## Completeness summary

**Primary (M1)**: the outer annulus of the M1M3 monolith (Ohara E6): 8.36 m clear outside (8.4 m nominal), 5.12 m clear inside; R = 19.835 m, conic -1.215 (BAT), so f1 = 9.9175 m, f/1.19. An observatory-document M1 radius was not found (BAT only).

**M2 and M3**: M2 convex, 3.4 m (3.5 and 3.45 m also quoted), 1.8 m central opening holding the camera, 100 mm ULE meniscus, R = 6.788 m, vertex 6.156 m above M1's vertex (BAT). M3 concave, 5.0 m, R = 8.3445 m, vertex 0.234 m behind M1's vertex (BAT; M2-M3 6.39 m vs ROD 6.4 m), with a central hole for alignment equipment (1.06 m across in the 2006 drawing). Camera: body ~1.6 m x 3.5 m inside the M2 opening; L1 1.55 m; focal plane 4.43 m above M1's vertex (DERIVED from BAT).

**Focus**: the single LSSTCam focus, f/1.233 (f/1.234 quoted), f = 10.310 m (from the pipeline plate scale 20.0059 arcsec/mm), 3.5 deg (9.6 deg^2) on a 64 cm flat focal plane, 3 reflections plus three lenses and a filter. Complete.

**Pupil**: annulus 8.36 / 5.12 m (eps 0.612); eight 50 mm vanes in four parallel pairs that form four bars across the pupil 0.4 m from the center (K06 text and image; imSim geometry); vane area 0.654 m^2 (DERIVED; SB09 ~0.7 m^2); pupil area 33.68 m^2 (DERIVED; SB09 35 m^2 on axis). Pupil orientation relative to the elevation axis NOT FOUND; M2 baffle diameter NOT FOUND.

## Drawing (web/imager.js designs)

The "pb" (Paul-Baker) design in imager.js fixes the mirrors' places and solves for the secondary's and tertiary's focal lengths: the secondary at 0.62 f1, a concave tertiary at the primary's center 0.02 D in front of the vertex, and the focus at 0.85 of the way up to the secondary, in the secondary's shadow, with no intermediate image. With Rubin's F1 = 1.1863 and F = 1.2333, web/imager.js design() gives: secondary 6.149 m above the primary (real 6.156 m, BAT), f2 = -3.184 m (real -3.394 m), tertiary 0.167 m in front of the vertex (real vertex 0.234 m behind), f3 = 4.249 m (real 4.172 m), focus 5.227 m above the primary's vertex (real focal plane 4.429 m). The M1-M2 spacing matches to 0.1%: 6.156 / 9.9175 = 0.621 f1. The tertiary's place is off by 0.40 m and the focus by 0.80 m, and the real light passes three lenses (L1 at 3.40 m above the primary) before the focus; the drawing shows neither the lenses nor the filter.

The camera sits inside the 1.8 m opening of M2, so the camera adds no on-axis obstruction beyond the M1 inner edge. The schematic's focal ratio and plate scale equal Rubin's (F = 1.2333, 20.006 arcsec/mm).

## Conflicts

- M1 diameter: 8.36 m clear (ROD, BAT) vs 8.4 m (RKN, I19, SB09) vs 8417 mm outer edge in the 2006 drawing (K06).
- M1 inner diameter: 5.12 m (ROD; BAT 5.116 m) vs 5.1 m (SB09) vs 5.0 m (I19, T26).
- M2 diameter: 3.4 m (SB09, I19, K06, T26 Sec. 2) vs 3.5 m (ROD) vs 3.45 m (T26 Sec. 1); BAT clear 3.42 m.
- M3 diameter: 5.0 m (ROD, I19, K06) vs 5.02 m (SB09) vs 5066 mm outer edge (K06 drawing) vs 5.016 m clear (BAT).
- L1 diameter: 1.55 m (ROD, SB09 clear aperture) vs 1.57 m (SB09 Fig. 2.8) vs 1.6 m (T26).
- Final focal ratio: f/1.233 (RKN) vs f/1.234 (I19, T26) vs f/1.23 (SB09).
- Spider frame: the K06 pupil image shows the vane pairs vertical and horizontal; imSim places the bars at 45 and 135 deg. Both have bars 0.4 m from the center.
- Collecting area: 35 m^2 on axis (SB09) vs 6.67 m filled aperture = 34.94 m^2 (ROD) vs 33.68 m^2 (DERIVED, annulus less vanes) vs 6.49 m mean over the field = 33.08 m^2 (RKN, with vignetting).

## Notes

- Explorer conventions: F = f / 8.36 m; plate scale = 206264.8 / f; reflections count mirrors only (the camera adds three lenses and a filter).
- BAT coordinates: z along the axis toward the sky with M1's vertex at 0; M2 at +6.1562 m, M3 at -0.2338 m, L1 at +3.3975 m, the detector at +4.4294 m.
- The pupil and the M3 hole: light reaching the central 5.12 m of the glass lands on M3 and never reaches M2 as part of the M1 beam, so the M1 annulus is the pupil.
- File provenance: BAT (yaml) and IMS (py) were read as text and never executed.
