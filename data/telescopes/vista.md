VISTA has one focal station, the Cassegrain, and has carried two instruments there: the near-infrared camera VIRCAM (2009-2023; S15, UM) and the fiber spectrograph 4MOST (first light 2025; ESO's only instrument at VISTA in 2026). The two mirrors serve both; each instrument brings a separate corrector, so the focal length differs (12.072 m with VIRCAM, 12.252 m with 4MOST).

## Completeness summary

**Primary**: a Zerodur meniscus 4.10 m across (3.96 m clear aperture), 17 cm thick, with a 1.20 m central hole; an as-built hyperboloid of vertex radius 8094.2 mm and conic -1.12979, so f = 4.047 m (DERIVED) and f/0.99 on 4.10 m (quoted f/1). ESO publishes no collecting area; the 4MOST exposure-time calculator hard-codes 8.452 m^2.

**Pupil**: the 1.24 m secondary is undersized and is the aperture stop (no cold stop in VIRCAM; the same with 4MOST), so each field point uses a 3.70 m circle on the primary (S15, UM; a real-ray trace of the prescription gives 3.705 m, DERIVED); over the 1.65 deg VIRCAM field the circles fill 3.95 m. Central obstruction: the polished annular baffle around the secondary, 1.63 m across (eps 0.4405 on 3.70 m, DERIVED); 4MOST modified the baffles, and the new size is NOT FOUND. Spider: four legs, each 36 mm wide, slanted and non-radial, in two pairs that join the ~1.4 m central barrel at two opposite points (S15 text; four legs and the attachment on the altitude axis from photographs, FIGURE READ-OFF). The legs' angles are NOT FOUND; the explorer draws +/-60 deg from points 0.70 m off the center on the altitude axis (INFERRED).

**Secondary**: convex hyperboloid of Astrosital, 1241.5 mm (1240.5 mm clear), vertex radius 4018.9 mm, conic -5.5494, 2725.7 mm from the primary's vertex, on a five-axis hexapod.

**Foci**: one Cassegrain station on a rotator behind the M1 cell. 4MOST (current): the Wide Field Corrector with atmospheric dispersion corrector (four lens groups, two of the four being counter-rotating prisms; ADC to 55 deg), 59.4 um/arcsec (12.252 m, f/3.311 on the 3.70 m pupil, DERIVED; the 2012-2014 baseline quoted f/3.32), 2.6 deg nearly unvignetted (4MOST Consortium) or 2.5 deg (ESO), a curved focal surface 535 mm across. VIRCAM (2009-2023): 12.072 m with the VIRCAM three-lens corrector, f/3.25 quoted (f/3.263 on 3.70 m), 17.086 arcsec/mm, a flat 1.65 deg field, the focal plane 1172.6 mm behind the primary's vertex; the telescope is unvignetted to 2 deg. A bare test focus without lenses (commissioning only): 1153.2 mm behind the vertex, ~5 arcmin. Gaps: the 4MOST focus position and focal-surface radius, the 4MOST baffle, the leg angles.

## Drawing (web/imager.js designs)

The drawing supports prime, cass, greg (each with an optional flat Nasmyth tertiary), korsch, pb, schmidt and refr. VISTA is a two-mirror quasi-Ritchey-Chretien Cassegrain with the instrument's corrector lenses in the converging beam, so "cass" represents the three foci. The cass model places the secondary from F, F1 = f1/D and back: s = (m f1 - b)/(m + 1) with m = F/F1. With D = 3.70 m and f1 = 4.0471 m (node run of imager.js `design`, scratch script):

- bare test focus (F 3.2042, back 1.1532 m): s = 2723.7 mm and f2 = -2009.4 mm, the real secondary (2723.6 mm, R2/2 = -2009.45 mm) to 0.1 mm, a check of the prescription and of the 3.70 m pupil;
- VIRCAM (F 3.2627, back 1.1726 m): s = 2736.6 mm and f2 = -1971.5 mm, while the real secondary sits at 2725.7 mm with f2 = -2009.45 mm; the corrector's weak power supplies the rest of the 12.072 m;
- 4MOST (F 3.3114, back 1.1726 m, schematic): s = 2751.0 mm, f2 = -1935.3 mm.

Schematic in the drawing: the correctors (VIRCAM's window and three lenses; 4MOST's four lens groups and ADC prisms, the largest lens ~950 mm) are not drawn, since imager.js draws a corrector only at a prime focus (`P.tel === "prime" && P.corrector`); the focal lengths and plate scales are the telescope's with each corrector. The 4MOST focus sits at VIRCAM's 1.1726 m behind the vertex (NOT FOUND for 4MOST; the 2014 alternative corrector put the focal surface ~1.16 m behind, FIGURE READ-OFF). The focal surfaces are drawn flat (VIRCAM's is flat; 4MOST's is curved, radius NOT FOUND). The pupil is the on-axis 3.70 m circle. The spider legs are drawn in the pupil plane, though the real legs slant toward the sky. A faithful drawing of the correctors needs a Cassegrain branch for `corrector` in imager.js and tel3d.js, like the prime-focus one.

## Not found

- The angles of the four spider legs in the pupil, and the exact radius at which the legs meet the central barrel (S15 gives the barrel as ≈ 1.4 m across and the legs as joining the barrel at two opposite points); the explorer's +/-60 deg from (+/-0.70, 0) m is INFERRED from photographs.
- The 4MOST-era baffle around the secondary (H14: the baffles were modified for 4MOST, and a WFC M1 baffle added); the explorer keeps VIRCAM's 1.63 m.
- The 4MOST focal-surface position (back focal distance) and radius of curvature, the corrector's prescription and the as-built focal ratio; only the 59.4 um/arcsec plate scale, the 535 mm focal diameter and the 2012-2014 baseline f/3.32 are published.
- Whether the M1-M2 spacing changed for 4MOST.
- An ESO collecting area for VISTA (the 4MOST ETC's 8.452 m^2 is the only number found).
- The orientation of the pupil (and the spider) on the sky: the pupil is fixed to the tube of an alt-azimuth telescope, and the Cassegrain rotator turns the instrument.

- Pupil diameter: 3.70 m (S15, UM) vs 3.705 m (DERIVED, real-ray trace) vs 3.80 m (DERIVED paraxial image of M2, too large for the fast primary) vs the 3.95 m envelope (S15) vs the 3.96 m clear aperture (S15 Table 2) vs the 4.10 m mirror (headline).
- Focal length with VIRCAM: 12.072 m (S15 Table 1, UM Sec. 5) vs 12.07 m and 17.0887 arcsec/mm = 12.0702 m (UM Sec. 6.2) vs '13 000 mm' (ESOVP).
- VIRCAM focal ratio: f/3.25 quoted (S15, UM, ESOV, ESOVP) vs f/3.263 on 3.70 m and f/2.944 on 4.10 m (DERIVED).
- 4MOST field: 2.6 deg (UM19, DJ19, WS19) vs 2.5 deg (E4M, ESOI, H14, S15) vs 2.50 deg (DERIVED from the 535 mm focal diameter at 59.4 um/arcsec); fiber hexagon 4.1 deg^2 (ESOI) vs >4.2 deg^2 (UM19, WS19).
- 4MOST plate scale: 59.4 um/arcsec (UM19, EUSUB) vs ~60 um/arcsec (W26) vs ~63 um/arcsec (H14 concept: 12.995 m, f/3.51 on 3.70 m, which does not match H14's own f/3.32 = 59.55 um/arcsec on 3.70 m) vs '~60 mm/arcsec' (WS19 slide 16, a unit slip).
- 4MOST focal ratio: f/3.32 (H14; G14's 2012 baseline) vs f/3.20 (G14's alternative design, not built) vs f/3.311 on 3.70 m and f/2.988 on 4.10 m (DERIVED from 59.4 um/arcsec; round 4 quoted f/2.99 on 4.10 m).
- Secondary: 'f/3 hyperboloid' (UM Sec. 5) vs R2 = 4018.9 mm, f/1.62 on 1.2415 m (DERIVED from S15 Table 2).
- Unvignetted field: 2 deg (UM) vs the 1.65 deg VIRCAM field with cold-baffle vignetting from 0.69 deg to 1% at the corner (S15) vs a top ring sized for 4 deg (S15).
- Site altitude: 2518 m (S15, ESOVP) vs 2500 m (UM Sec. 1) vs 2635.43 m (UM Table 2).
- 4MOST corrector: four lens groups with two counter-rotating prisms (EUSUB, WS19; built) vs the 2012 baseline with two rotating ADC doublets, six elements (G14) vs G14's alternative of four singlets with a laterally displaced ADC element.

## Notes

- One focus, two instruments: VIRCAM was VISTA's only instrument from 2009 to March 2023; 4MOST took the same Cassegrain position (first light 2025), and ESO's 4MOST page (May 2026) calls 4MOST 'the only instrument installed at VISTA'. The mirrors serve both; each instrument brings a separate corrector, so the focal length and plate scale differ.
- VISTA has no cold stop: the undersized secondary is the stop, so the pupil is smaller than the primary and moves across the primary with field angle (up to 0.12 m at 0.825 deg). The explorer draws the on-axis pupil.
- Round 4 (data/round4/4most.md) used D = 4.10 m and derived f/2.99 for the 4MOST focus; on the 3.70 m entrance pupil the same 59.4 um/arcsec gives f/3.31, consistent with the baseline f/3.32. The spectrograph presets are not changed here.
- The pupil frame follows S15 Sec. 5 (x along the altitude axis); with two symmetric pairs of legs the drawn pupil is the same seen from the sky or from the focus.
- The 4MOST ETC's 8.452 m^2 and the explorer pupil (8.491 m^2) differ by 0.46%; with the legs as drawn, 8.452 m^2 corresponds to a 1.65 m obstruction.
