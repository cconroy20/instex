## Completeness summary

**Primary**: 7 circular segments, clear aperture 8.365 m (polished 8.405 m): one on axis, six off axis every 60 deg, tilted 13.522 deg, centred r = 8.71 m from the parent axis on the surface (8.657 m in the pupil, DERIVED), so each projects to an 8.365 x 8.133 m ellipse. Circumscribed 25.448 m (CoDR; 25.447 m DERIVED from OAD numbers; '25.4 m'). Parent conic R = 36 m, k = -0.998286; f = 18.0 m, f/0.71. Gaps between clear apertures 0.408 m (centre-off) and 0.349 m (off-off) DERIVED; physical 359 / 295 mm (CoDR 2006).

**Pupil**: central obscuration = the 3.6 m M2 baffle (OAD; hcipy 3.495 m; CoDR 3.2 m), eps 0.141 of 25.448 m; a three-legged truss shadows only the centre segment (OAD estimate 5.463 m^2; hcipy legs at 30, 150, 270 deg, two 0.119 m diverging struts + one 0.115 m strut each). Collecting area: 368 m^2 headline, 375.558 m^2 geometric, 357.0 m^2 current best estimate (>= 356-358 m^2 required).

**Secondary**: adaptive (ASM; identical fast-steering FSM as backup), 7 concave 1.05 m segments, each conjugate to one M1 segment; conjugate 165 m above M1; exit pupil 2935 mm, 2320 mm below M2. Current M2 prescription NOT FOUND (2006: R2 4.2058 m, K2 -0.71087).

**Foci**: direct Gregorian narrow field f/8.16, 207.589 m, 0.994"/mm, >= 10' (20' usable), 2 reflections, 5.83 m below the M1 vertex (GMACS, ComCam); direct Gregorian wide field with the Corrector-ADC, f/8.34, 20', BFD 6.01 m (MANIFEST); Folded Port via a flat M3 1.93 m ahead of focus, 3', 3 reflections (GMTIFS, GMTNIRS); two Auxiliary Ports on the elevation axis, an Instrument Platform port and the Gravity Invariant Station (G-CLEF) fed by relays/fibres after M3 (reflections NOT FOUND); prime focus for calibration only. Gaps: truss widths from an observatory document, pupil orientation vs the elevation axis, the C-ADC scale.
