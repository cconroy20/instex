## Completeness summary

**Primary**: spherical, R = 26.164 m (pyHETobs; '26 m' MDO), 91 identical 1 m hexagonal Zerodur segments (apothem 0.5 m: 1.0 m flat-to-flat, 1.155 m corner-to-corner DERIVED; 6 spares), 11 mm mean gaps (6.2-15.8 mm, pyHETobs), 5 rings around a centre segment; array 11 x 10 m (9.91 x 11.11 m DERIVED). Fixed elevation 55 deg (zenith angle 35 deg); the structure turns in azimuth only.

**Focus**: one, the prime focus on the tracker with the four-mirror Wide Field Corrector (M2, M3 concave 1 m; M4 convex 0.23 m; M5 concave 0.9 m; light enters through a hole in M3): f/3.65, 36.5 m, 5.65"/mm, 22' field (18' science), concave spherical focal surface R = 984 mm, 5 reflections (DERIVED: M1 + 4). All instruments (VIRUS, LRS2, HPF; HRS2 to come) are fibre-fed there.

**Pupil**: 10.0 m at the centre of the track (9.2 m before the upgrade, set by the old 4-mirror SAC), central obscuration 4.5 m (eps 0.45; K21, pyHETobs; H21 Fig. 6 read-off 0.43-0.45), two 0.29 m support bars at |x| = 1.30-1.59 m (pyHETobs approximation; real strut shadows more complex), fibre bundles, the CCAS tower on East tracks. The pupil moves over the array by R x angle: +/-2.74 m at the 6 deg edge of the 12 deg tracker range (DERIVED). Effective area (DERIVED raster of the pyHETobs geometry): 55.5 m^2 at track centre (94% of the 59.05 m^2 obstructed pupil; 8.4 m equivalent), ~41-42 m^2 at 4 deg, ~33-34 m^2 at 6 deg; K21: up to a factor 2 (8.5 -> 5.5 m equivalent). Gaps: an observatory value for the obstruction and the real strut geometry.

## Conflicts

- Primary radius of curvature: 26.164 m (pyHETobs) vs '26 m' (MDO).
- Primary size: '11 m' (H21; MDOP 'Actual Diameter') vs '11.1-m' (G21) vs '11 by 10 meters' (MDOP) vs 9.91 x 11.11 m (DERIVED from pyHETobs). MDO lists 'Diameter 10.0 m (433")', but 433 in = 11.0 m.
- Tracker range: 12 deg (MDO 'Angular range of tracker on sphere'; G21 '12 deg diameter field-of-view') vs 8.4 deg radius (pyHETobs, for partly illuminated tracks) vs altitudes 48-65 deg (K21), i.e. -7/+10 deg about 55 deg.
- Field gain: '70 times larger area' (H21 Table 4) vs 'an area of sky 120 times larger' (MDOP); (22'/4')^2 = 30 (DERIVED).
- Field: 22' (MDO) vs 18' science + 22' guiding (H21 Table 1).
- Mirror mass/thickness: 13.5 t, 50 mm at the edge (MDO) vs 13 tons, 52 mm (MDOP); telescope weight 153 tons (MDO) vs 80 tons (MDOP).
- Support shadows: two straight bars (pyHETobs, K21 Fig. 2) vs several crossing struts in the pupil-viewer images (H21 Fig. 6).

## Notes

- The HET has one science focus, the prime focus on the tracker; every instrument is fibre-fed from it. The primary is spherical and fixed at 55 deg elevation; the tracker follows the star for 50 min to 2.8 h.
- Pupil drawing: 10 m circle with a 4.5 m central disc and two 0.29 m bars at |x| = 1.30-1.59 m, intersected with the 91-hexagon array (centres in pupil.segments.centres_DERIVED); shift the circle by R x angle (up to +/-2.74 m) to show the moving pupil.
- Reflections at the focal surface: M1 + four WFC mirrors = 5 (DERIVED), the same count as the original 4-mirror SAC.
- f-number on the 10 m pupil: 36.5/10 = 3.65; plate scale 206264.8/36500 = 5.651"/mm.
