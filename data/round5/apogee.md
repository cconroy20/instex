## Round 5 completeness summary (APOGEE)

**APOGEE**: nearly complete, almost all from one instrument paper (W19, Wilson et al. 2019, with the as-built Zemax prescription in its Table 5). Found: Sloan 2.5 m at f/5 (G06, W19); 120 um low-OH fibres, 2.0" on the sky (1.31" on the du Pont at f/7.5); fibre output ~f/3.5 (95% EE), stops at f/3.7 (collimator mask) and f/3.85 (VPH mask); 300 fibres in 10 v-groove blocks of 30 at 0.350 mm pitch; on-axis spherical 'uncorrected Schmidt' collimator mirror, R = 2107.22 mm cold (f_col = 1053.6 mm DERIVED); three-segment mosaic VPH, 1008.6 /mm design (1009.345 /mm as recorded), first order, Littrow at 54 deg (54.061 deg as built) for 1.6042 um; six-element Si/fused-silica camera, 356 mm (measured warm), f/1.3 at f/3.85; three 2048 x 2048 18 um H2RG arrays side by side with 2.9 mm gaps. Validation targets: per-chip coverage, dispersion at three wavelengths, median R per chip and per fibre sample, spectral and spatial image widths, fibre pitch (W19 Table 1, Sec. 4, Tables 11 and 13; M17 Table 2; DR16). With the explorer's ideal f tan theta camera, f_cam = 356 mm, G = 1009.345 /mm, alpha = 54.061 deg and the camera aimed at the mosaic centre (lamC = 1.6158 um, DERIVED) reproduce all six chip edges to within 1 nm and the published A/px at the chip centres to within 1.3% (DERIVED). NOT FOUND: a cold camera focal length, a collimated-beam diameter and collimator focal length stated as such (both DERIVED), the off-axis detector geometry in explorer terms, and APOGEE-S per-chip coverage, dispersion and VPH line density; the VPH paper (Arns et al. 2010) and the 2010/2012 SPIE instrument papers have no open copies.

## Sloan 2.5 m / APOGEE-N (and du Pont 2.5 m / APOGEE-S): cryogenic fibre-fed H-band spectrograph, 300 fibres, uncorrected-Schmidt collimator mirror, three-segment mosaic VPH grating in Littrow, six-element Si/fused-silica camera, three H2RG arrays side by side

**Recommended preset (APOGEE-N):** APOGEE-N on the Sloan 2.5 m: D = 2.5 m; F = 3.85 into the collimator (the spectrograph's aperture stop, the VPH mask, clips beams faster than f/3.85, and the camera is f/1.3 at that beam; the fibres take f/5 from the telescope and emit ~f/3.5 at 95% encircled energy); 120 um core = 2.0" on the sky at f/5, entered as the slit of equal line width (sqrt3/2) x 120 um = 103.9 um = 2.227" at f/3.85 on 2.5 m (DERIVED; the 2.0" sky value belongs to f/5); 300 spectra on the detector (about 230 science + 35 sky + 35 telluric standards); collimator = Mirror (spherical, on-axis 'uncorrected Schmidt'), f_col = 1053.6 mm (R/2 of the 2107.22 mm cold radius, DERIVED), W = 273.7 mm (f_col/3.85, DERIVED; VPH mask 272.8 mm); camera f = 356 mm (measured warm), f/1.3; transmission (VPH) grating, G = 1009.345 /mm (as recorded; design 1008.6 /mm cold), order 1, alpha = 54.061 deg (design 54 deg), Littrow wavelength 1.6043 um (DERIVED); the real camera sits at the Littrow deviation 2 x 54.061 = 108.122 deg with the detector mosaic off-axis, so for the explorer's centred detector use lamC = 1.6158 um (the wavelength at the mosaic centre, DERIVED), beta = 55.21 deg, camera angle alpha + beta = 109.27 deg (DERIVED); detector 3 x Teledyne H2RG 2048 x 2048 x 18 um side by side along the dispersion with 2.9 mm (161 px) gaps: Nx = 6466 px if the gaps are entered as dead pixels (3 x 2048 + 2 x 161.1, DERIVED; reproduces the outer edges) or 6144 px with the gaps not modeled; Ny = 2048; pixel 18 um. Validate: chips 1.514-1.581 / 1.585-1.644 / 1.647-1.696 um (DERIVED model edges with the 2.9 mm gaps: 1.5139-1.5805 / 1.5855-1.6439 / 1.6482-1.6963 um); dispersion 0.326 / 0.282 / 0.235 A/px (the model gives 0.3257 / 0.2858 / 0.2354 A/px at the chip centres 1.548 / 1.616 / 1.674 um); R median 22,800 / 23,700 / 22,000 (blue / green / red; nominal 22,500; model slit-limited R with the slit-equivalent width 26,850-28,940 at 1.54-1.66 um, i.e. 14-32% above the measured values); spectral fibre image ~1.7 px at 1.53 um to ~3.0 px at 1.68 um (model 1.72 / 2.51 px slit-equivalent; 1.99 / 2.90 px full core); fibre pitch 6.6 px (model 6.57 px).

**Variant (APOGEE-S):** APOGEE-S on the du Pont 2.5 m (a near-copy of APOGEE-N): same D = 2.5 m, same fibre (120 um = 1.31" on the sky at f/7.5; FRD expected to speed the f/7.5 input to roughly the APOGEE-N output beam, so keep F = 3.85 and the same slit-equivalent 2.227"), same collimator, camera and detector format; two-panel VPH (as-built line density NOT FOUND; use the APOGEE-N values) with a 29.2" correction wedge on one panel. Validate against the lower APOGEE-S resolution (W19 Table 13: medians 20,100-23,700 blue, 20,900-24,800 green, 19,800-21,000 red). Per-chip coverage and dispersion for APOGEE-S were NOT FOUND.

### Notes

- Explorer mapping (DESI convention): D = 2.5 m with F = 3.85 makes f_col = W x F = 1053.6 mm; the entrance is the slit of equal line width, (sqrt3/2) x 120 um = 103.9 um, i.e. 2.227" at f/3.85. The true sky size is 2.0" (core at f/5); the arcsec number in the explorer is a collimator-side bookkeeping value. R, sampling and coverage do not depend on the F/W split as long as W x F = 1053.6 mm (F = 3.5, W = 301.0 mm gives the same results but a camera f/1.18 instead of the published f/1.3).
- Detector: three 2048-px arrays along the dispersion with 2.9 mm (161 px) gaps. Entering Nx = 6466 (gaps as dead pixels) reproduces the outer edges 1.514-1.696 um; Nx = 6144 (gaps not modeled, as for the HIRES mosaic) gives 1.5194-1.6929 um, trimming 5.5 nm (blue) and 3.4 nm (red) from the ends (DERIVED). Wavelength decreases with pixel number on the real arrays (DR16: negative dispersion; chip a = red, chip c = blue).
- Camera angle: the real camera points along the Littrow direction (deviation 108.122 deg as built) and the arrays sit off-axis (Table 5 notes m, q). The explorer centres the detector on the camera axis, so the preset aims the camera at the mosaic centre (lamC = 1.6158 um, beta = 55.21 deg, deviation 109.27 deg, DERIVED). With that choice the ideal f tan theta model reproduces all six published chip edges to within 1 nm and the published A/px at the chip centres to within 1.3%; the published 1.514-1.696 um span falls on 116.1 mm of the 116.4 mm mosaic, so the edges imply an effective f_cam within ~0.3% of 356 mm (DERIVED; edges rounded to 1 nm and varying by fibre), which supports using the warm-measured 356 mm.
- Resolution: the slit-limited model R (~27,000-29,000 with the slit-equivalent width) exceeds the measured 22,000-23,700 because the delivered LSF (2.0-3.1 px FWHM, non-Gaussian; W19 Sec. 11.2-11.3, N15) includes pixelisation, residual astigmatism and focus errors. Using the full 120 um core gives 23,250-25,065.
- Undersampling: the spectral fibre image is < 2 px in the blue; the survey takes exposure pairs dithered by 0.5 px and interleaves them (W19 Sec. 7.1, 11.6; N15 Sec. 6.5). The explorer's sampling numbers are the native (undithered) values.
- Pseudo-slit: on a sphere of radius 1051.6 mm (half the collimator radius, the Schmidt condition) built as a polygon of 10 flat v-groove blocks, plus a 386 mm lateral curvature that straightens the monochromatic slit image (W19 Sec. 4.1); the slit is an obscuration in the collimated beam (M17). Fold 1 is used twice and bent into a weak cylinder to cancel astigmatism; Fold 2 carries a thermal-blocking dichroic (W19 Sec. 4.3-4.4).
- APOGEE-S (du Pont, LCO): a near-copy with a two-panel VPH plus a 29.2" correction wedge, different cold-plate support and LN2 suspension, and somewhat lower resolution (W19 Sec. 13). On the du Pont the same 120 um core subtends 1.31" (f/7.5). Detector gains differ (1.9 vs 3.0 e-/DN, W19 Sec. 5.4.2).
- Littrow ghost: falls at the Littrow wavelength (1.6042 um) because the fringes are untilted (W19 Sec. 4.5); the model's 1.6043 um checks G and alpha together.
- Quotes: W19 and M17 render the double prime as '00' and the degree sign as U+25E6 in the text layer; these are written here as " and deg / ° and were matched after glyph normalisation. W19 table rows are quoted from the pdftotext -layout rows (superscript note letters such as 'Mirrorg' are part of the text layer).
