## Round 5 completeness summary (UVES)

**UVES**: complete for every explorer input; only the cross-disperser camera-collimator angles come from the pipeline's physical model rather than a manual or paper. The ESO User Manual (Issue 117, Nov 2025) gives the 200 mm beam and white-pupil mirror collimators, both echelles (41.59 gr/mm 76.0 deg; 31.6 gr/mm 75.04 deg; R4, quasi-Littrow), the four cross-disperser gratings (660 and 600 gr/mm for the presets), camera focal lengths (360 / 500 mm), detectors (EEV 2048 x 3000 window; EEV + MIT-LL 4096 x 4096 mosaic, 0.96 mm gap), the standard settings with ranges, deckers and red-gap limits, the R x slit products (41,400 / 38,700), dispersion per pixel and pixel scales, and minimum order separations. Dekker et al. 2000 adds the F/15 -> F/10 pre-slit relay, the 2 m collimator, the 0.8 deg off-plane angle and the camera prescriptions; the VLT interface spec gives D = 8000 mm, f/15.000 at Nasmyth. The ESO ETC (P116) supplies the order-by-order formats of the two presets (orders 142-104 and 128-90, central-column wavelengths, nm/px, spatial scale 0.246 / 0.182 "/px). From the pipeline source only: the cross-disperser camera-collimator angles (46.0 deg blue, 45.336 deg red), which reproduce the ETC spatial scales and order spacings. Gaps: XD blaze angles (wavelengths only), a numeric R-vs-slit table (plots only), the exact collimator focal length (2000 mm DERIVED). Main conflict: Dekker et al. 2000 Table 5 swaps the two echelle groove densities; the manual, ESO pages, Messenger and pipeline agree on blue 41.59 / red 31.6, confirmed by the ETC order numbers.

## VLT UT2 (Kueyen) / UVES (two-arm white-pupil echelle, R4 echelles, grating cross-dispersers; Nasmyth B) -- blue arm 390 nm (CD#2) and red arm 580 nm (CD#3)

**Recommended preset(s):** Two presets = the two halves of the standard DIC1 390+580 setting (UM117 Table 3.1; the manual's own worked example, Sec. 3.6.1, and the twilight-flat monitoring mode, Sec. 4.7.6). Why: BLUE390 and RED580 are the two most-observed standard setups in the ESO archive 2000-2010 (AMBRE Table 1: 11,262 BLUE390 and 27,912 RED580 spectra, REDL+REDU counted separately -- 'the two setups with the largest spectral sample'); 390 is offered only with a dichroic (DIC1 or DIC2), 580 also red-only. Plain 1.0" slit, no image slicer, 1 object. Common: D = 8.0 m (VLT Nasmyth entrance pupil; 8.2 m primary), F = 10 at the slits (pre-slit relay F/15 -> F/10; DERIVED 2.578"/mm, 1" = 0.388 mm), mirror collimator (off-axis parabolas + flats, white pupil, two 200-mm pupils) f_col = 2000 mm (DERIVED 200 mm x F/10; Dekker: 'from 3m to 2m'), W = 200 mm, echelle quasi-Littrow theta = 0 with off-plane gamma = 0.8 deg, reflection-grating cross-dispersers in 1st order. BLUE 390: echelle 41.59 gr/mm, delta = 76.0 deg (R4); XD = CD#2 660 gr/mm, camera-collimator angle 46.0 deg (pipeline physical model); lambda_c = 390 nm; camera f = 360 mm (f/1.8); EEV CCD44-82 window Nx = 3000 (dispersion) x Ny = 2048, 15 um; slit 1.0" x 8" (standard decker). RED 580: echelle 31.6 gr/mm, delta = 75.04 deg; XD = CD#3 600 gr/mm, angle 45.336 deg; lambda_c = 580 nm; camera f = 500 mm (f/2.5); mosaic EEV CCD44-82 (lower, REDL) + MIT-LL CCID-20 (upper, REDU), each 4096 (dispersion) x 2048 x 15 um -> Nx = 4096 x Ny = 4096 with a 0.96 mm (64 px) gap; slit 1.0" x 12". Validate against: R = 41,400 (blue) / 38,700 (red) at 1.0" (published resolving-power x slit products; R ~ 40,000 nominal); dispersion lambda/193,000 (blue) and lambda/250,000 (red) per pixel at order centre (ETC: 0.0020 nm/px in order 120 at 389.04 nm; 0.0023-0.0024 nm/px around 580 nm); 0.215" / 0.155" per pixel along the dispersion and 0.246" / 0.182" per pixel along the slit (ETC); orders 142-104 (325.9-451.9 nm, ETC) and 128-90 with order 105 in the chip gap (472.7-683.5 nm, ETC; manual 326-454 and 476-684 nm, gap 577.9-581.9 nm); minimum order separation 38.9 px = 9.6" (CD#2 at 395 nm) and 77.4 px = 14.1" (CD#3 at 580 nm) (UM117 Table 3.2).

### Notes

- Setting choice: AMBRE Table 1 counts 11,262 BLUE390, 8,907 BLUE437, 5,267 BLUE346 and 27,912 RED580 (REDL + REDU counted separately, i.e. ~13,956 exposures), 13,468 RED860, 11,590 RED564 archive spectra (2000-2010, stellar sample); AMBRE: 'BLUE390 was the second setup tackled after RED580 (the two setups with the largest spectral sample)'. ESO QC: 'a number of standard settings which are used very often'. UM117 uses DIC1 390+580 as its IQ example (Sec. 3.6.1) and for the twilight-flat monitoring (Sec. 4.7.6); the red-only 580 setting is its OB example (Sec. 3.4).
- Explorer mapping: D = 8.0 m (the X-Shooter preset of round 2 uses the 8.2 m primary; UVES numbers require the 8.0 m Nasmyth pupil) and F = 10 give f_col = W F = 2000 mm and 1" = 0.388 mm at the slit; the camera then gives 69.8 / 97.0 um per arcsec and 0.2149 / 0.1547 "/px along the dispersion (published 70 / 97 um/" and 0.215 / 0.155 "/px). The F/15 Nasmyth focus is only an intermediate focus (image slicers sit there).
- Consistency checks (DERIVED): R x s from 2 tan(delta) W / D reproduces 41,400 (blue, 0.1%) and 38,700 (red, 0.3%); lambda/(2 tan(delta) f_cam/px) reproduces lambda/193,000 and lambda/250,000 to 0.3% and 0.2%; the PHYS cross-disperser angles (46.0 / 45.336 deg) reproduce the ETC spatial scales (0.242 vs 0.246; 0.182 vs 0.182) and the 390-setting order spacing (53.6 vs 53 px at 390 nm); the bluest-order spacing of the 580 setting (78.0 px DERIVED at 477-481 nm) matches Table 3.2's 77.4 px and D00's 78 px.
- Cross-disperser sign convention (PHYS): sin(alpha) + sin(beta) = k lambda with alpha - beta = cdbeam; at lambda_c the incidence angle alpha is the larger (31.0 / 33.5 deg) and beta lies on the other side of the normal (-15.0 / -11.8 deg), so the slit image is magnified (cos beta / cos alpha = 1.13 / 1.17) along the slit -- the source of the 0.246 / 0.182 "/px spatial scales.
- Red arm: lower chip EEV ('Sting', REDL, 472.7-580.4 nm) and upper chip MIT-LL ('Zeus' since July 2009, REDU, 581.8-683.5 nm) at 580 nm; order 105 (~578-585 nm) is lost in the gap. D00: instrumental LSF 20 um (red + EEV) vs 27 um (red + MIT) from charge diffusion, hence the 5-10% lower R on the MIT chip for slits < 0.8".
- Image slicers (reported, not the preset): IS#1 0.68" x 7.9" (3 slices, R ~60,000), IS#2 0.44" x 7.9" (4 slices, R ~75,000, blue), IS#3 0.30" x 10" (5 slices, R ~110,000, red); inserted at the F/15 focus before the derotator. Virtual image slicer 0.3" x 6" (VM only).
- The echelle line tilt from gamma = 0.8 deg is removed by rotating the slit by 7 deg (D00); the ETC/pipeline model the format in 2-D without gamma.
- Camera tilt tables shift the echellogram by up to ~200 px (blue) / ~280 px (red) along the dispersion (0.48 deg); standard settings use the nominal tilt.
- PHYS is the pipeline's physical model (used to predict the format before order tracing); its constants are model parameters fitted to the instrument, not design values. Saved file is unmodified; the 108 MB kit tarball was not kept.
