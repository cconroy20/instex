## Round 5 completeness summary (grisms)

**Grism models.** LDSS-3: the three VPH grisms are model (b) *exactly* -- the Carnegie maskgen instrument model (written by the grism designer, M. Gladders) defines each as a VPH layer normal to the axis between two identical prisms with faces at -A/+A (VPH-All: 400 l/mm, 2 x 16.30 deg fused silica; VPH-Blue: 1090 l/mm, 2 x 24.00 deg OHARA S-LAL7; VPH-Red: 665 l/mm in maskgen, 660 nominal, 2 x 24.00 deg S-LAL7). With the maskgen camera (173.08 mm) this reproduces LCO's measured 2007 arc solutions: wavelength at the detector centre to <= 27 A, local dispersion to <= 0.6 %, slits 2 arcmin off-centre to <= 25 A. The retired LDSS-2 BK7 grisms are model (a) exactly. FORS2: the standard grisms (300V, 300I, 600B, 150I, 600V/R/I) are surface-relief grisms whose design table (Fuertig & Seifert 1995) gives glass, lines/mm, prism angle and groove angle, but they differ from model (a): "the entrance surfaces are all tilted by 7.5 deg". ESO's numbers -- the manual's centre wavelengths, dispersions and ranges, and the pipeline's measured distortion tables -- are reproduced (centre <= 20 A, dispersion <= 1 %) only if that tilt lies in the dispersion plane with the grating-face normal at (phi - 7.5 deg) to the axis: m G lambda0 = n sin(phi - eps') - sin(phi - eps), sin eps' = sin(eps)/n; plain model (a) puts lambda0 0.4-0.8 % low and the dispersion 4-11 % too high. The FORS2 VPH grisms are model (b) in kind (ESO: grating between two prisms, central wavelength undeviated), but their apex angles, glasses and line densities are NOT published; measured dispersions and centres are given as targets.

**LDSS-3**: optics from maskgen (collimator 746.8 mm, camera 173.08 mm, Clay focal length 71475.8 mm "measured during the LDSS-3 commissioning run") plus the LCO manual (f/11, 2.88 "/mm, 0.189 "/px, LDSS-3C 2048 x 4096 x 15 um with the 4096 axis along the dispersion, R table for 0.75"). Weak points: no LDSS-3 instrument paper exists; the beam diameter (67.9 mm) and collimator type are not stated (DERIVED / LDSS-2 heritage); slits per mask only from surveys (17 mean, 20-25). **FORS2**: complete for the surface-relief grisms (manual Table 2.2 optics: 1233/280 mm, 528 um/arcsec; 1992 design beam 90 mm; Table 2.4 performance; pipeline-measured dispersion and centre wavelength), D = 8.2 m with f/13.28 DERIVED. Weak points: VPH grism geometry NOT FOUND; tilt orientation DERIVED; telescope f-ratio and beam are design/DERIVED. **Glass**: OHARA S-LAL7 (datasheet Sellmeier) and SCHOTT LF5, N-BAK2, N-K5, SF5, F2HT, SF11 (datasheets); FK5, fused silica and BK7 from round 1; maskgen's own glass power series documented.

## Magellan II (Clay) / LDSS-3 (imaging multi-slit spectrograph; straight-through focal reducer with three VPH grisms between prism pairs)

**Recommended preset:** VPH-All grism (the general-purpose low-resolution mode), 0.75" slit (LCO resolution table) -- 1.0" is the usual MOS width, ~17 slits per mask (DERIVED mean of 35 published masks; 20-25 in another survey), Magellan Clay 6.5 m at f/11 (maskgen focal length 71475.8 mm -> 2.886 "/mm), refractive collimator 746.8 mm (maskgen; 745.935 nominal), DERIVED beam 67.9 mm, camera 173.08 mm (final f/2.5), LDSS-3C Fermilab CCD 2048 x 4096 x 15 um with the 4096-px axis along the dispersion, 1x1 binning. Grism = model (b) exactly: VPH layer normal to the axis between two fused-silica prisms of apex 16.30 deg (outer faces at -16.30/+16.30 deg), 400 l/mm, m = 1 -> DERIVED lambda0 = 6584 A. Validate against: LCO table R = 860 (0.75"), 1.890 A/px, 4250-10000 A, "central" 7100 A; and the measured 2007 arc solution through the centre slit: lambda at detector centre 6588 A, local 2.040 A/px (DERIVED from 36 identified lines), 0.189 "/px. The maskgen geometry reproduces all three grisms' measured arcs (centre wavelength to <= 27 A, dispersion to <= 0.6 %, +/-2 arcmin slits to <= 25 A; Notes). Alternatives: VPH-Blue (1090 l/mm, 2 x 24.00 deg S-LAL7 prisms; lambda0 5166 A; 0.682 A/px, R 1900, 3800-6200 A) and VPH-Red (665 l/mm in maskgen / 660 nominal, 2 x 24.00 deg S-LAL7; lambda0 8294 A; 1.175 A/px, R 1810, 6000-10000 A, with OG590).

### Notes

- Grism model fit: model (b) describes the LDSS-3 VPH grisms exactly. maskgen defines each as EGRISM <lead face -A, glass, grating at 0.00 deg, lines/mm, glass, trailing face +A> = a VPH layer normal to the axis between two identical prisms whose outer faces are tilted by -/+A. No extra tilt, no asymmetric prisms. The three old LDSS-2 grisms are model (a) exactly (entrance face 0.0 deg, BK7, grating face at 9.6/19.5/30.2 deg, exit into air).
- Validation of the geometry against measured data (DERIVED here): fitting x = x0 + f_cam tan(phi(lambda)) to the identified arc lines of the three centre-slit databases with the maskgen grism parameters and only (x0, f_cam) free gives f_cam = 172.36 mm (VPH-All, rms 1.4 px over 36 lines), 172.40 mm (VPH-Blue, rms 2.0 px), 171.97 mm (VPH-Red at 665 l/mm, rms 1.0 px; 172.97 mm and rms 1.4 px at 660 l/mm) -- within 0.6 % of maskgen's 173.076 mm. The predicted wavelength at the detector centre for slits 2 arcmin off-centre matches the measured off-axis databases to <= 25 A (performance table).
- Glass: maskgen stores n^2 as a Laurent series in lambda^-2 (form in the glass section); its S-LAL7 matches the OHARA S-LAL7 Sellmeier to <= 7e-6 in 0.49-0.85 um and 1.7e-4 at 0.365 um, and its Fused_Silica matches Malitson (1965) to <= 6e-6. S-LAL7 is not in the round-1 glass list: OHARA Sellmeier coefficients are given below (SCHOTT N-LAK7 has the same nd 1.65160 but is a different melt).
- Detector history: the pre-2014 LDSS-3 CCD had at least 4064 px along the dispersion (the 2007 arc databases span x = 1..4064; its type is not documented in the saved sources); LDSS-3C (2014-) uses the Fermilab 2k x 4k S3-171 with the 4096-px axis along the dispersion and the 6.4 arcmin spatial trim. Both have 15 um pixels, so the 2007 dispersion solutions apply per pixel.
- The VPH grism wavelength coverage "varies substantially over the detector" (LUM): slits offset along the dispersion by +/-2 arcmin shift the spectrum by about +/-1300 A for VPH-All (measured; reproduced by the model through the field angle).

### Notes

- Grism model fit: the FORS standard grisms are surface-relief (ruled replica) grisms -- model (a) in kind -- but with the entrance face tilted 7.5 deg (FS95). Tested against ESO's numbers (DERIVED, table below), the tilt must lie in the dispersion plane and rotate the whole grism so that the grating-face normal is at (phi - 7.5 deg) from the optical axis. Exact geometry for the explorer: beam along the axis meets the entrance face at 7.5 deg incidence, refracts to eps' = asin(sin 7.5 deg / n) inside, meets the ruled hypotenuse at internal incidence (phi - eps'), diffracts into air at angle beta from the grating normal; undeviated when beta = phi - 7.5 deg, i.e. m G lambda0 = n sin(phi - eps') - sin(phi - 7.5 deg). Equivalently: model (a) with an extra entrance-face tilt parameter eps = 7.5 deg applied with the opposite sense to the apex.
- Measured vs model (DERIVED; measured = pipeline master distortion table at the CCD centre, chip 1): 600B 4629 A / 0.7476 A/px vs tilted 4629 / 0.7490 vs plain-(a) 4604 / 0.6933; 300V 5849 / 1.6549 vs 5858 / 1.6639 vs 5826 / 1.5968; 300I 8574 / 1.6101 vs 8584 / 1.6249 vs 8538 / 1.5366; 150I 7176 / 3.4118 vs 7156 / 3.4370 vs 7117 / 3.3750; 600I 7936 / 0.6594 vs 7940 / 0.6648 vs 7901 / 0.5898. Opposite tilt sense (grating face turned away): same lambda0, dispersion 5-23 % too low. Glasses: N-FK5 for FK5, LF5, N-BAK2 for BaK2, SF5, N-K5 for K5, F2HT for F2 (SCOL).
- The FS95 Table 1 numbers were read from the page image (OCR garbles 17.5, 26.7, 11.5 and the column header "phi prism angle, psi groove angle"). FS95 numbers its grisms std. 1-7 and cross-disperser I-III and does not use ESO names: the identification std. 1 = 600B, cross II (F2) = 600V, std. 2 = 600R, std. 3 = 600I, std. 5 = 300V, std. 6 = 300I, std. 7 = 150I is DERIVED by matching lines/mm, wavelength range and A/mm with FUM117 Table 2.4 (600B also by A98: "600 lines/mm blazed in the blue"), and is confirmed by the tilted model reproducing ESO's centre wavelengths to <= 20 A. Cross-dispersers I-III served the FORS2 echelle mode, later decommissioned.
- Pipeline distortion tables: FPM Sec. 7.6 defines them (x = sum_r c_r (lambda - lambda_o)^r, c_r a bivariate quadratic in CCD position for r = 1..5, in focal-plane position for r = 0). The calibration frames are 2x2 binned (BINX = 2), so c1 is in binned pixels per A; it was evaluated at the reference-wavelength position (x0 = c0, y0 = 119 binned px, the field centre on chip 1 per FUM117 "y-pixel ~238 ... unbinned"). Using the a00 term alone (CCD corner) would give 0.69 A/px for 600B -- that reading is wrong.
- The explorer's slit-image R should include the grism anamorphic factor of the tilted geometry (beam out/in = 0.952-0.991 at lambda0; DERIVED). ESO's R column omits it (R = lambda/(slit px x A/px)).

## Glass dispersion formulas, round 5 (LDSS-3 and FORS2 grism prisms)

### Formula forms

- **Sellmeier_SCHOTT**: `n^2(lambda) - 1 = B1 lambda^2/(lambda^2 - C1) + B2 lambda^2/(lambda^2 - C2) + B3 lambda^2/(lambda^2 - C3)`; um (C in um^2). Source: SCOL, Explanations page: "When calculating the refractive index using the Sellmeier coefficients from the SCHOTT data sheets, the wavelength λ needs to be entered in units of µm."
- **Sellmeier_OHARA**: `n^2 - 1 = A1 lambda^2/(lambda^2 - B1) + A2 lambda^2/(lambda^2 - B2) + A3 lambda^2/(lambda^2 - B3)`; um (B in um^2). Source: OHWEB, Sec. 2.3 Dispersion Formula: "As a practical dispersion formula, we have adopted the use of the Sellmeier formula shown below. ... each wavelength is expressed in µm units."
- **Laurent_maskgen**: `n^2(lambda) = sum_{i=0..N} c_i (lambda^-2)^(i-1) = c0 lambda^2 + c1 + c2 lambda^-2 + c3 lambda^-4 + c4 lambda^-6 + c5 lambda^-8 (the classic SCHOTT power series; file lists N then c0..cN)`; um. Source: MGO, file header and Glass definitions: "#  GLASS <refraction polynomial> ... where y is X*2 or wavl^-2 or temp. ... GLASS Simple_glass 1 0.0 2.175625 ... ! Results in index of 1.475 for all wavelengths..." DERIVED reading, verified: Simple_glass (N = 1, c0 = 0, c1 = 2.175625) gives n = sqrt(2.175625) = 1.475 as the file states; the BK7 entry gives nd = 1.51680, S-LAL7 1.65160, Fused_Silica 1.45846 at 0.5875618 um (= catalog nd).
- **Malitson_1965**: `n^2 - 1 = 0.6961663 lambda^2/(lambda^2 - 0.0684043^2) + 0.4079426 lambda^2/(lambda^2 - 0.1162414^2) + 0.8974794 lambda^2/(lambda^2 - 9.896161^2)`; um; 20 C; 0.21-3.71 um. Source: MAL65 (round 1), publisher abstract (round 1)
