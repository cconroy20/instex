## Round 5 completeness summary

**FIRE**: optics complete from Simcoe et al. 2013 (PASP 125, 270), including the full Zemax prescription (Table 2): f/11 off-axis paraboloid collimator (parent focal length 555 mm, R = 1110.16 mm), 50 mm beam, 54.49 gr/mm R1 echelle blazed at 46 deg in quasi-Littrow, orders 11-32, four prisms (Infrasil 301 P1 single pass and P2 double pass; ZnSe P3 double pass and P4 single pass; six passes; 100 K CHARMS indices), 176.8 mm camera (F/3.5), HAWAII-2RG 2048 x 2048 (18 um pitch from the Teledyne brochure only). Gaps: echelle theta/gamma, prism incidence angles (the prisms are off minimum deviation -- beam compressed 50 -> 30 mm -- so the app's minimum-deviation prisms will under-predict the cross-dispersion ~1.5x), Infrasil apex angles only as tan(A/2) to two decimals (42.6 / 28.1 deg +/-0.5), the central wavelength. Conflicts worth noting: 0.15"/px and 12.5 km/s/px published vs 0.163"/px and 14.74 km/s/px from the optics with 18 um pixels; 21 vs 22 orders; 6" vs 7" slit. **FEROS**: complete from the 1997 Final Design Report (incl. its Zemax system data), Kaufer & Pasquini 1998 (SPIE 3355), Kaufer et al. 1999 (Messenger 95) and the FEROS-II User Manual (2006) for the 2.2-m feed: f/8 telescope, 2.0" fibres (100 um, f/4.6) -> F/11, two-slice slicer (120 x 600 um slits = 1.0" slices), two f = 1500 mm OAP collimators (white pupil), 136 mm beam, 79 gr/mm R2 echelle at 63.4 deg (63 deg 24 arcmin) with off-Littrow gamma = 0.6 deg, one 55 deg LF5 prism at minimum deviation for 462.3 nm (order 49), 410 mm f/3 camera, EEV 2k x 4k 15 um, orders 25-63. LF5 is not on the app list: SCHOTT Sellmeier added. Gaps: published A/px and per-order tables. Conflict worth noting: FUM78 says the dispersion runs along the CCD's shorter side, against FDR, K99 and the order lengths.

## Magellan I (Baade) / FIRE (Folded-port InfraRed Echellette, echellette mode: R1 echelle in quasi-Littrow, four-prism ZnSe + Infrasil 301 cross-disperser with six passes, optics at ~100 K)

**Recommended preset:** Echellette mode, 0.6" x 6" slit (the nominal slit, R = 6000). D = 6.5 m, F = 11 (Magellan f/11 folded port; 2.10 mm per 6" = 0.350 mm/arcsec at the slit, DERIVED), 1 object, mirror collimator (off-axis paraboloid, parent focal length 555 mm, from R = 1110.16 mm) -> W = 50 mm as stated (555/11 = 50.45 DERIVED), camera: refractive (CaF2 / S-FTM-16 / CaF2 + Infrasil 301 flattener), f = 176.8 mm (F/3.5 in the 50 mm direction); echelle 54.49 gr/mm R1, delta = 46 deg, quasi-Littrow (theta and gamma NOT published: 0 here), orders 11-32; cross-disperser = six prism passes in path order: Infrasil 301 P1 A = 42.6 deg (single pass), Infrasil 301 P2 A = 28.1 deg, ZnSe P3 A = 21.81 deg, [echelle], ZnSe P3 again, Infrasil P2 again, ZnSe P4 A = 21.81 deg (single pass) (Infrasil apex angles DERIVED from the printed tan(A/2) = 0.39 and 0.25, +/-0.5 deg); glasses at the 100 K design temperature (CHARMS; new entries Infrasil301-100K / ZnSe-100K below, or the app's 105 K entries, which differ by <= 3e-4 in n); central wavelength NOT published -- 1.20 um (order ~22) puts the order stack symmetric about the detector centre in the app's minimum-deviation model (DERIVED); detector Teledyne HAWAII-2RG 2048 x 2048 x 18 um (pitch from the Teledyne brochure; no FIRE document states it). CAVEAT: FIRE's prisms are deliberately NOT at minimum deviation (they compress the 50 mm beam to 50 x 30 mm), so an app trace with all prisms at minimum deviation spreads orders 11-32 over only ~1320 px (DERIVED) instead of most of the 2048-px height; expect the app to under-predict the cross-dispersion and order separation by ~1.4-1.8x and to give a ~0.163"/px spatial scale instead of the published 0.09-0.12"/px. Validate against: R = 6000 at 0.6" and 8000 at 0.45" (published; geometric DERIVED 5520 / 7370), 12.5 km/s per pixel (published; Littrow DERIVED 14.74 km/s/px with 18 um pixels), 0.15"/px along the dispersion (published; DERIVED 0.163), coverage 0.82-2.51 um in orders 11-32, reddest order (11, FSR 2.29-2.51 um) matched to the detector width (Littrow FSR length 1849 px DERIVED).

### Conflicts between sources

- Spectral pixel scale: 0.15"/px (S13, design text) vs 0.18"/px (FWS, LCOH, FDS, FSS) vs 0.163"/px DERIVED (18 um, 176.8/555.08 mm, 0.3468 mm/arcsec).
- Velocity per pixel: 12.5 km/s (SS12; S11 50 km/s = 4 px; B10C 4-px slit) vs 14.74 km/s DERIVED (Littrow, 18 um, 176.8 mm); the published value fits 0.15"/px + R = 6000, the optics give ~0.163"/px and R ~ 5500 at 0.6".
- Slit length: 6" (S13, LCOFM, LCOFU "The correct length of the echelle slit is 6 arcseconds", SS12) vs 7" (FWS, FSS, FDS).
- Number of orders: 11-32 = 22 orders (S13, FWS) vs "21 orders" (S10, SS12, B10R, B10C) vs 11-28 (FSS, pre-construction).
- Coverage: 0.82-2.51 um (S13, FWS, LCOH) vs 0.8-2.5 (S10) vs 0.85-2.45 (B10C) vs 0.85-2.5 (B10R) vs 0.89-2.51 (FSS).
- Collimator: off-axis paraboloid (S13, S10) vs "tilted field lens and on-axis spherical mirror" (FDS; copied on LCOFE) -- pre-construction design.
- Double-pass prisms: two (S13, S10) vs one (FDS; copied on LCOFE).
- Prism glass: Infrasil 301 (S13, S10) vs "Fused Silica" (LCOFE component list) -- Infrasil is an IR-grade fused quartz, so this is a naming difference.
- Operating temperature: 100 K design indices (S13), bench 90-100 K (S13, S10), 100 K (LCOFE) vs 77 K (FDS; S13 back-up configuration).
- Slit widths: 0.45-1.0" (LCOFM, LCOH, FWS) vs 0.3-2.0" (FSS, pre-construction).
- Resolution at 0.45": R = 8000 (LCOH, FWO) vs DERIVED geometric 7370.

### Notes

- Cross-dispersion caveat (DERIVED): with all six prism passes at minimum deviation (the app model) orders 32-11 span only 23.7 mm = 1319 px, the order spacing is 53-87 px, and the spatial scale stays at the unmagnified 0.163"/px. The real prisms compress the beam to 50 x 30 mm (angular magnification ~1.67), which gives the published 0.09-0.12"/px along the slit and should spread the orders over most of the 2048-px height with "several arcseconds" of dark pixels between 6" slits. Expect the app to under-predict FIRE's cross-dispersion by ~1.4-1.8x; the echelle direction (R, km/s/px, FSR fill) is unaffected.
- Path order through the prisms (S13 Table 2): P1 (Infrasil, single) -> P2 (Infrasil) -> P3 (ZnSe) -> echelle -> P3 -> P2 -> P4 (ZnSe, single) -> camera. S13: "three upstream prism passes (2 Infrasil and one ZnSe)".
- Glasses: S13 traced the design with CHARMS indices at 100 K (Leviton et al. 2005, 2008 = LFK05, LFM08). The app's 105 K entries differ from 100 K by -1.8e-5 (Infrasil) and -2.5e-4 to -3.1e-4 (ZnSe) in n, and by 0.1% in the ZnSe dispersion n(0.82)-n(2.40) (DERIVED): either set works; 100 K coefficients are given below.
- Consistency (DERIVED): the 54.49 gr/mm, 46 deg echelle with f_cam = 176.8 mm puts the reddest order (11) over 1849 of 2048 px in Littrow, matching S13's "detector dimension is matched the free spectral range of its reddest order"; the blaze peaks 26.403/m um reproduce the 0.82-2.51 um coverage (order 11 FSR to 2.509 um; order 32 from 0.812 um).
- FIRE also has a low-dispersion (prism-only) long-slit mode with a flat mirror in place of the grating (R ~ 300-500); only the echellette mode is relevant here.
- Moves: FIRE was installed on Baade on 2010 March 28 (S13); moved AUX2 -> AUX1 in September 2022 (LCOFU).

### Conflicts between sources

- Beam: 136 mm (FDR, KP98, FUM78) vs 135 mm (LSWD); 1500/11 = 136.4 DERIVED.
- Parent paraboloid: 660 mm diameter (FDR) vs 680 mm (KP98); both f/2.2.
- Camera aperture: 155 mm entrance aperture (FDR) vs 178 mm clear aperture (KP98); focal length "410 nm" [sic] in FUM78 Table 1.1.
- Dispersion axis: long (4096-px) side (FDR, K99, FDR App. A image plane, DERIVED FSR lengths) vs "along the shorter dimension of the CCD" (FUM78).
- Telescope beam at the fibre head: f/8 (FUM78, E22) vs "f/15" (FUM77, FUM14 -- left over from the ESO 1.52 m).
- Entrance aperture: 2.0" (FUM78, ESOO) vs 1.6" (2002-2003 ball lenses) vs 2.7" (ESO 1.52 m: K99, FDR, KP98; still in FUM14 Table 1.1).
- Fibre output focal ratio: f/4.6 (FDR, KP98, FUM78) vs f/4.5 (K98S abstract).
- Coverage: 356-920 nm (FUM78) vs ~350-~920 (ESOO) vs 360-920 (K99) vs 370-860 nm / 3700-8600 A (KP98, FDR design).
- Number of orders: 39 (K99, FUM78, ESOO) vs 40 (FDR, KP98 design) vs "35 orders" (LSWF schematic).
- KP98 Fig. 7 caption: "extreme order 32 with lambda_c = 985 nm" -- inconsistent with m lambda = 22.64 um (order 32 -> 707 nm; 985 nm -> order 23).
- Prism glass quality: "PH3 quality" (FDR) vs "homogeneity class H4" (KP98).
- R degradation: < 10% (FUM78; KP98 "at 2/3 of the maximum field") vs < 15% in the extreme corners (K99).
- Fibre length: about 12 m (FDR) vs 14 m (KP98) vs ~15 m (FUM78, fibres replaced October 2003).

### Notes

## Glass dispersion formulas, round 5 (FEROS and FIRE prisms)

### Formula forms

- **Sellmeier_SCHOTT**: `n^2(lambda) - 1 = B1 lambda^2/(lambda^2 - C1) + B2 lambda^2/(lambda^2 - C2) + B3 lambda^2/(lambda^2 - C3)`; um (C in um^2); valid 'from the near UV to 2.3 um'. Source: SCOL, Explanations page ('Constants of the dispersion formula'): "the refractive indices for any wavelength within the range from the near UV to 2.3 µm can be calculated with the help of the constants B1, B2, B3, and C1,C2,C3. ... When calculating the refractive index using the Sellmeier coefficients from the SCHOTT data sheets, the wavelength λ needs to be entered in units of µm."
- **Sellmeier_CHARMS_T**: `n^2(lambda,T) - 1 = sum_{i=1..3} S_i(T) lambda^2/(lambda^2 - lambda_i(T)^2), S_i(T) = sum_{j=0..4} S_ij T^j, lambda_i(T) = sum_{j=0..4} lambda_ij T^j; at fixed T this is the app form with B_i = S_i(T), C_i = lambda_i(T)^2`; um, T in K; absolute index. Source: LFK05, Sec. 3 (worked example): "Si (T) = ∑ Sij ⋅ T j ... λ i (T) = ∑ λ ij ⋅ T j"
- **Malitson_1965**, **Sellmeier_OHARA**: not needed this round (see rounds 1 and 4).
