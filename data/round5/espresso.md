## Round 5 completeness summary

**ESPRESSO**: the optics are well constrained once three SPIE papers (UT Austin copies of Megevand+2014, Riva+2014 APSU, Santana Tschudi+2014 mounts) are combined with Pepe+2014/2021 and the ESO User Manual: F/3.5 fibre feed (140 um = 1.0", 70 um = 0.5", square 280 um for 4-UT), the APSU (3x anamorphic 30 x 10 mm pupil, two half-pupil slices superimposed, image plane F/10 x F/15), the off-axis parabolic main collimator (R 6000 mm -> f 3000 mm DERIVED, double pass, 300 x 200 mm beam per slice), the R4 31.6 gr/mm 76 deg echelle (3 x 1 mosaic, 200 x 1200 mm -- not 2 x 1), the 525 nm dichroic, R 3000 mm transfer mirrors (f 1500 mm DERIVED, 150 x 100 mm pupil at the XD), VPH grisms on fused-silica prisms (22.20 / 25.25 deg, anamorphic 1.5x), the 400 mm f/2.6 blue camera, and two e2v 9232 x 9216 x 10 um CCDs; the ETC order table (orders 161-117 blue, 117-78 red) and the pixel-size table are published. Gaps: VPH line densities and incidence angles, echelle theta/gamma, the red camera focal length, the octagon orientation. Conflicts: the VPH abstract's dispersions (0.88 / 0.47 deg/nm) are 10x too large for the detector format; the design camera (800 mm effective) gives a 6% smaller pixel size than published (755 mm effective); the 'grating without APSU' size is 60 x 240 cm (EP21), 40 cm / 240 x 40 cm (EP13/EP14/EGH18) or 40 cm / 180 x 40 cm (UM); R in all modes moved between the design (134k / 225k / 59k) and the measured values (138-140k / >190k / 70-72.5k).

## VLT / ESPRESSO (fibre-fed, vacuum, white-pupil R4 echelle with an anamorphic pupil slicer; dichroic to blue/red arms with VPH-grism cross-dispersers)

**Recommended presets:** singleHR (1-UT, octagonal 140 um = 1.0" fibres A + B, R = 140,000), ONE PRESET PER ARM; both arms share the front end. Common inputs: D = 8.2 m; equivalent single circular beam W = 600 mm with F = 5.0 (f_col = W x F = 3000 mm = the R 6000 mm main parabola) -- each of the two pupil slices is a 300 mm beam carrying half of the telescope pupil along the dispersion, so the whole pupil is equivalent to 2 x 300 = 600 mm (DERIVED; EP21's 'without the APSU ... 60x240 cm' grating is exactly this beam); entrance width 0.866" (= (sqrt3/2) x 1.0"; 0.858-0.914" for an octagon, orientation NOT FOUND); nObj = 4 (fibres A, B x 2 slices); collimator = Mirror (off-axis parabola, double pass); camera f = 755 mm EFFECTIVE in the echelle-beam frame (DERIVED from the published pixel dispersion; the design value 400 mm x 3000/1500 = 800 mm is the ALT); echelle 31.6 gr/mm, delta = 76 deg (R4), theta = 0 (Littrow, no numeric value published), gamma NOT FOUND (0 here; 4.8-4.9 deg would put the ETC order centres on the blaze peak, DERIVED); detector e2v 9232 (along dispersion) x 9216 x 10 um. BLUE arm: XD = VPH grating on a fused-silica input prism (apex 22.20 deg, anamorphic 1.5x), line density NOT FOUND -- for the app's single-beam frame an equivalent symmetric VPH of ~757 l/mm at 438 nm (DERIVED, see Notes); lambda_c = 438 nm (grism design wavelength); orders 161-117 (380-525 nm). RED arm: XD = VPH on a fused-silica prism (apex 25.25 deg), equivalent ~407 l/mm at 654.8 nm (DERIVED); lambda_c = 654.8 nm; orders 117-78 (525-788 nm). Validate against R = 140,000 (UM Table 1; median 138,000 in EP21), 4.5 px per resolution element, the EP21 Table 2 pixel size (0.00063 nm at 380.04 nm, order 161; 0.00086 nm at 522.97 nm, order 117; 0.00129 nm at 784.45 nm, order 78; = 0.49 km/s/px), the FSR and on-chip length per order (UM Tables 7-8: 5.93 nm in order 161 ... 11.66 nm in order 78), coverage 378.2-788.7 nm. Mode variants on the same optics: singleUHR = 0.433" (70 um, 0.5"; measured R > 190,000 blue / > 220,000 red, geometric 280,000, so the app will over-predict); multiMR (4-UT) = 2.0" square on D = 8.2 m (or 1.0" on the 16.4 m equivalent), measured R = 70,000-72,500, design 59,000, geometric 60,500.

### Conflicts between sources

- R (singleHR): 140,000 measured (UM Table 1; ESO overview 'median') vs 138,000 median (EP21 text; HR11/HR21 in EGH18) vs 130,000 (HR42, EGH18) vs 134,000 design (EP13, EP14, EM14) vs 140,000 2010 design (EP10); requirement 120,000 (EP21).
- R (singleUHR): > 190,000 blue / > 220,000 red measured as lower limits from partly resolved ThAr (EP21) and 'meas. 190 000' (UM) vs 225,000 design (EP13, EM14, EP10) vs ~200,000 (EP14) vs 210,000 (upper end, ESPA12) vs 220,000 'specified' (ELIZ).
- R (multiMR): 72,500 (MR42) / 70,000 (MR84) measured (EP21, EGH18, UM) vs 59,000 design (EP13, EP14, EM14) vs 60,000 (EP10) vs 63,000 (lower end, ESPA12); EP21 also prints '(requirement: R = 30 000)'.
- Grating size without the APSU: '60x240 cm' (EP21; consistent with a 600 mm beam at 76 deg) vs '40 cm in diameter ... 240x40 cm' (EP13, EP14, EGH18) vs '40 cm ... 180 cm x 40 cm' (UM). A 40 cm beam at 76 deg would need a 165 cm grating (DERIVED), so neither 40 cm version is self-consistent.
- Camera scale: design 400 mm (EP14) x 3000/1500 = 800 mm effective (matches ER14's 53 um slice image) vs 749-760 mm implied by every row of EP21 Table 2 (Littrow, 76 deg) and 769 mm implied by EP21's '0.486 km/s' per pixel -- 4-6% discrepancy, unresolved (as-built camera, non-Littrow geometry, or field-lens power).
- VPH grism dispersion: Arns abstract '0.88 degrees/nm' (blue, 438 nm) and '0.47 degrees/nm' (red, 654.8 nm) would spread 375-520 nm over 128 deg; 0.088 and 0.047 deg/nm fit the 92 mm CCD with a ~400 mm camera (DERIVED). The same abstract prints the red aperture as '215 nm x 185 mm'.
- Echelle mosaic: 3 x 1 (EM14 'assembly of three gratings', ELIZ '3 x 1 echelle mosaic'); the brief's 'mosaic of two' is HARPS. Blaze: 76 deg (all ESPRESSO sources) vs nominal R4 75.96 deg.
- Arm split / ranges: dichroic at 525 nm (EM14 text, UM, ESO overview) vs 520 nm (EM14 Table 1) vs grism ranges 375-520 / 535-780 nm (EARNS); total 378.2-788.7 nm (EP21) vs 380-788 (UM) vs 380-790 (EGH18) vs 380-780 (EP13, EP14) vs 380-800 (EP10).
- Spectral sampling: HR 4.5 px (UM, EM14, EP13, EP14, EGH18) vs 3.3 px (EP10, 2010 design); MR 5.5 px binned x2 (UM, EP13) vs 5 (MR42) / 2.5 (MR84) (EGH18) vs 11 px (EP14) vs 4.0 binned (EP10).
- Spatial sampling per slice (HR): 9.0 px (UM, EM14, EGH18 '2x9') vs 11 x 2 (EP14) vs 6.9 (EP10); design image 10.7 px (DERIVED from ER14's 107 um).
- multiMR slices: '1 (merged)' (UM Table 1, ESO overview) vs 'two (adjacent) slices must be extracted simultaneously' (EP21) and '2x20 pix' spatial sampling (EGH18 Table 1).
- CCD size: 92 x 92 mm (UM, EP14, EM14 '~ 92 mm') vs 90 x 90 mm (EP21 Fig. 4 caption, ESO instrument page, EP10); format 9216 x 9232 active (UM) vs '9K x 9K' (EP21).
- Total efficiency (HR): ~11% (UM, EP13) vs 10% (EGH18).
- Fibre-exit pupil in the APSU sketch: 'Pupil 30mm' (ER14 Fig. 2) vs 'Pupil 10mm' (EM14 Fig. 13, same drawing; text layer 'pil 10mm'); ER14 Fig. 2's slicer-stage label '280x840 um' does not follow from its own F/10 x F/15 image plane (200 x 600 um, DERIVED).
- Naming: ER14 calls the 70 um fibre 'HR-SUT' and the 140 um fibre 'LR-SUT' (= singleUHR and singleHR elsewhere).

### Notes

- Beam mapping (why W = 600 mm). For a slit-limited Littrow echelle R = 2 tan(delta) f_col / w = 2 tan(delta) W / (phi D): what matters is the beam width ALONG THE DISPERSION per unit of telescope pupil. The APSU (ER14) collimates the F/3.5 fibre beam into a 30 x 10 mm ellipse (3x longer along the dispersion), cuts it into two 15 x 10 mm half-pupils and superimposes them, then re-images each at F/10 (dispersion) x F/15 (cross). The 3000 mm parabola turns each slice into a 300 x 200 mm beam on the 1200 x 200 mm echelle. Each slice therefore sees the whole 1.0" fibre image (200 um wide at F/10) but only HALF the telescope pupil along the dispersion: 4.1 m of pupil per 300 mm of beam = 8.2 m per 600 mm. The cross-dispersion beam size (200 mm, the 3x compression) does not enter R. Hence app inputs D = 8.2 m, W = 600 mm, F = 5.0 (f_col 3000 mm, slit 0.1988 mm per arcsec) give R = 139,800 at 0.866" (DERIVED). An equivalent mapping with the physical beam is D = 4.1 m, F = 10, W = 300 mm (same slit and R); use it if the app sizes the echelle from W (300/cos 76 deg = 1240 mm vs the real 1200 mm ruled length), but then D no longer means the telescope.
- Effective camera. The camera the app needs is the one that maps echelle angles onto the detector: f_eff = f_cam x f_col/f_transfer = f_cam x 3000/1500. The transfer mirrors halve the beam (300 x 200 -> 150 x 100 mm) before the XD; the XD prism then stretches the cross axis 1.5x to a square 150 x 150 mm pupil (ER14), so each slice image on the CCD is 53 um along the dispersion and 107 um across (ER14 Fig. 2; 2:1 tall). The app's single circular beam cannot show this 2:1 anamorphic image; along the dispersion it is exact.
- XD in the app's frame. The real VPH grisms sit in the 150 mm beam in front of the ~400 mm cameras, so their linear dispersion at the CCD is f_cam(phys) x d(theta)/d(lambda). In the app the XD sits in the W = 600 mm beam in front of f_eff = 755-800 mm, so the app's XD needs HALF the real angular dispersion (ratio f_transfer/f_col = 0.5, independent of the camera). Reading the Arns abstract as 0.088 / 0.047 deg/nm, the app-frame targets are 0.044 deg/nm at 438 nm (symmetric VPH ~757 l/mm, theta_B 9.5 deg) and 0.0235 deg/nm at 654.8 nm (~407 l/mm, theta_B 7.6 deg) (DERIVED). These are stand-ins: the real line densities (~1456 / ~792 l/mm for symmetric VPHs, DERIVED) and incidence angles are NOT published, and the real grisms add a fused-silica input prism (22.20 / 25.25 deg) used anamorphically, not as a straight-through grism.
- Pseudo-slit and nObj: fibres A (object) and B (sky or Fabry-Perot reference) of a mode are stacked across the dispersion, each split into 2 slice images -> 4 traces per order in HR/UHR (EM14, EP21). The HR, UHR and MR fibre pairs sit side by side ALONG the dispersion (only one mode lit at a time), so the three modes are offset in wavelength on the CCD. In multiMR the two slices of the square fibre touch and are extracted as one (EP21, UM).
- Order bookkeeping: the ETC/UM tables use the physical order m (161-78); DRS products number every slice separately starting from 1 at the blue end (HR/UHR orders 1-170), and MR orders 1-85 (UM Appendix A). Order 117 is recorded on both CCDs (blue: 519.13-527.03 nm; red: 519.22-527.16 nm).
- Validation arithmetic (DERIVED): m x lambda_c = 61,187.3 nm in every order (UM Tables 7-8); 9232 px x (centre pixel size) reproduces the on-chip length per order (order 117: 9232 x 0.00086 = 7.94 nm = UM's 7.94 nm), confirming that the 9232-px axis is the dispersion axis; constant 0.495 km/s per pixel in both arms implies equal effective camera focal lengths.
- Mode history: 2010 pre-PDR design (EP10: R 140k/60k/225k, 3.3 px sampling) -> 2013-2014 design (EP13, EP14, EM14: 134k/59k/225k, 4.5 px) -> commissioning from November 2017 (EWEBI), regular operation from October 2018 (EGH18), fibre-link upgrade June 2019, cryostat/NGC intervention May 2022 (orders moved by ~1-2 px, UM Sec. 6.1). The VLT 'requirements' quoted in EP21 are R = 120,000 (HR).
- Detector convention: UM calls the 9216-px cross-dispersion axis X and the 9232-px dispersion axis Y ('The Echellé orders are thus aligned along the CCD columns (Y)'); binning 'NxM' is spatial x dispersion (HR21 = 2 spatial x 1 spectral). Traces are tilted by < 10 deg relative to the pixel grid (ES21).
- UT aperture: the 140 um = 1.0" plate scale at F/3.5 implies 0.140 x 206265/3.5 = 8.25 m (DERIVED), consistent with the 8.2 m primary; ESOUT gives only '8.2-meter'.
