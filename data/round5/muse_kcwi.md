## Round 5 completeness summary

**MUSE (WFM)**: the IFU format, detector and published performance are complete and well sourced (ESO user manual v12.2, ESO pages, Bacon 2010/2017, Weilbacher 2020 pipeline paper): D = 8.2 m, 1.705"/mm focal plane (f/14.75), 24 identical IFUs, 48 slices of 15" x 0.2" per IFU (4 stacks of 12) = 3600 spaxels per CCD, x2 anamorphoser so a 0.2" spaxel covers 1 px (along the slice) x 2 px (along the dispersion), camera f/1.95, refractive collimator, one KOSI VPH grating, e2v 4096 (X) x 4112 (Y) x 15 um with dispersion along Y, 1.25 A/px (1.19-1.31), lab R table 1609-3506 (4650-9350 A), 2.5 px per resolution element. NOT FOUND (no open copy of Kosmalski 2011 / Laurent 2006 / Renault 2010): collimator and camera focal lengths, beam size, VPH line density and angle, and the focal ratio entering the spectrograph. EFFECTIVE values DERIVED: f_cam 258.4 mm and f_col 499.3 mm from the MUSE-derived Potsdam MRS spectrograph (its "up to 118 mm" slit maps onto a 4k x 15 um chip: 118.7 mm), W = 68.5 mm in the dispersion plane (0.2" -> 2 px), G = 458.4 /mm at alpha = beta = 9.23 deg (lamC 7000 A). The explorer is isotropic, so the preset reproduces the dispersion plane (R, A/px, coverage); its along-slit scale reads 0.1"/px instead of 0.2"/px.

**KCWI**: complete for the blue arm from Morrissey et al. 2018 Table 1 (f/13.7, 1.375"/mm, collimator R = 4345 mm, 160 mm compound pupil, 304.4 mm camera, CCD231-84 4096 x 4112 x 15 um) and Table 2 (line densities and typical incidence angles, beta = alpha - 9 deg, A/mm), the Keck configuration page (R by slicer/grating, A/px, coverage) and the official pipeline (line densities of all 12 gratings, angle conventions, 305 mm camera for both channels, 4 deg out-of-plane angle). No fore-optics: the slicer is at the telescope focus, so F = 13.7 and one isotropic magnification apply (D = 10.95 m DERIVED). Recommended: BM + Medium (0.69"), 1900 /mm, alpha 30 deg, lamC 4500 A; alternative BL + Large. KCRM red arm: RL 530 /mm (pipeline), same collimator/beam, 305 mm camera, LBNL 4k x 4k; red incidence angles NOT FOUND (DERIVED with the blue beta = alpha - 9 deg rule, which reproduces the published RL sample 7070-10810 A to <10 A). ## VLT (UT4) / MUSE, Wide Field Mode (24 identical image-slicer IFUs, each with a refractive VPH spectrograph and a 4k x 4k CCD)

**Recommended preset(s):** One preset (WFM, nominal; all 24 IFUs identical, no moving parts). Sourced: D = 8.2 m (VLT UT4; focal-plane scale 1.705"/mm, i.e. f/14.75 on 8.2 m); slice width 0.2" (the "slit"); 48 slices per IFU (4 stacks of 12, each slice 15" x 0.2", ~75 px long on the CCD) = 3600 spaxels per detector; anamorphic fore-optics (x2 across the slices), so one 0.2" spaxel lands on 15 um (1 px) along the slice and 30 um (2 px) along the dispersion; camera f/1.95 (spatial direction); collimator = Lens (fully refractive); one VPH grating (KOSI) in first order; e2v 4096 (X) x 4112 (Y) x 15 um, dispersion along Y (Nx = 4112, Ny = 4096). NOT published: collimator and camera focal lengths, beam size, VPH line density and angle. EFFECTIVE values are DERIVED (not a prescription): f_cam = 258.4 mm and f_col = 499.3 mm from the MUSE-derived Potsdam MRS spectrograph (MRS16), W = 68.5 mm in the dispersion plane (so that the 0.2" slice is 2.0 px wide; spatial-direction pupil 133-137 mm), F = f_col / W = 7.29 (dispersion plane; 3.65-3.77 in the spatial direction), VPH G = 458.4 /mm at the symmetric Bragg setting alpha = beta = 9.23 deg for lamC = 7000 A (only G x f_cam / cos(beta) = 1.2e5 is fixed by the 1.25 A/px dispersion). Validate against: 1.25 A/px (MUM; 1.19-1.31 A/px across the range, W20); R = 1609 / 2737 / 3506 at 4650 / 7000 / 9350 A (MUM lab table); 2.5 px per resolution element (MUM); LSF FWHM 3.0 A at the blue end to 2.4 A at 7500 A (B17); fully sampled range 4750-9350 A nominal, 4600(4650)-9350 A extended (W20, MUM); 0.2"/px along the slices (MUM).

### Notes

- Explorer mapping: the explorer has one isotropic magnification, but MUSE is anamorphic (A = 2). The preset is set up for the DISPERSION plane (slit width 0.2" -> 2.0 px, W = 68.5 mm, F = 7.29), which fixes R, A/px and coverage. Its along-slit pixel scale then reads 0.10"/px; the real along-slice scale is 0.2"/px because the pupil is twice as wide (133-137 mm, camera f/1.89-1.95) in the spatial direction. Equivalently, the spatial direction behaves as F = 3.65-3.77.
- Only G x f_cam / cos(beta) is constrained (1.2e5, from 1.25 A/px with 15 um pixels): any f_cam gives the same A/px, coverage and R if G is scaled as 1/f_cam; only the drawn angles change (alpha ~ 9 deg at f_cam = 258 mm).
- MUSE uses a flat, tilted detector to absorb axial colour (B06); the explorer has a flat untilted focal plane.
- R from the explorer is geometric (slit-limited, 2.0 px); the measured LSF (2.4-3.0 A, ~2.5 px) includes optics and detector blur, so the explorer over-predicts R by ~2-17% (largest in the blue).
- NFM (0.025"/px, 7.42" x 7.43") uses the same spectrographs with x8 extra magnification in the fore-optics (ESOMI); the spectral format is identical (MUM).
- Audit 2026-10-17: MUSE sits at a Nasmyth focus, whose entrance pupil is 8000 mm at f/15 (VLT-SPE-ESO-10000-2723 Table 3, saved under rawdata/presets/uves/); the preset now uses D = 8.0 m with W = 66.82 mm (the 0.2" slice still 2.0 px), which leaves R, A/px and the scales unchanged. The 1.25 A/px fixed the effective VPH line density, so it is not a validation target; the 1.19-1.31 A/px of W20 is the window the arc-line matcher assumes, not a measured spread. The x2 anamorphoser magnifies along the dispersion (across the slices).

### Notes

- KCWI has no anamorphic fore-optics: the reflective slicer sits at the f/13.7 telescope focus and the collimator (f = 2172.5 mm) is filled by the 160 mm compound pupil (f/13.6), so one isotropic explorer magnification is correct (both the slice width and the along-slice scale come out right with D = 10.95 m, F = 13.7, W = 160 mm, f_cam = 304.4 mm).
- The explorer slit for an IFU = one slice; "number of spectra" = 24 slices (or 3330 spatial pixels at 1x1, ~1665 at 2x2 binning).
- Grating mode: transmission VPH with a fixed incidence (tAlpha) and the camera following beta; the camera articulation angle = alpha + beta. Blue typical alpha: BL 15, BM 30, BH 50-55 deg (M18 Table 2), with beta ~ alpha - 9 deg at the design central wavelength because of the ~2 deg fringe tilt.
- The virtual slit is curved in 3-D ("C"-shaped) and the pipeline uses a 4 deg mean out-of-plane diffraction angle; the explorer works in plane (0.24% in lambda).
- KCRM shares the slicer, collimator and FM1 with the blue arm; a 560 nm dichroic takes the place of the FMD fold mirror (KSPEC, KPRIM, M18 Fig. 2 caption).
- Audit 2026-10-17: the KCRM camera (305 mm) is NOT a sourced KCRM value: it is the single FCAM of the pipeline's kcwi.cfg, listed under 'parameters that should be the same for both channels', so the 0.92 A/px comparison tests that assumption. The pipeline's resolution() (kcwi_file_primitives.py) assumes R = 800 for RL with the Large slicer and halves the FWHM for the Medium slicer (R = 1600), against the model's 2129; the same function gives BM 2500 / 5000 (Large / Medium), the M18 Table 3 values.
