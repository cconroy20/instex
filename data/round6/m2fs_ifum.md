## Round 6 completeness summary

**M2FS**: the spectrograph prescription is nearly complete from Mateo et al. 2012 (SPIE 8446 preprint; M12): f/11 Nasmyth East of Clay; 150 um pupil-fed fibers (f/3.7 in, f/3.4 out) behind 1.2" apertures; 128 fibers per spectrograph in eight 16-fiber tetrises on a 300 um pitch; six post-fiber slits (180-45 um); a quasi-Littrow lens group used as collimator and camera (500 mm, f/3.4, 147 mm beam, 1:1); HiRes: R2.6 (69 deg) 200 x 420 mm echelle with a 23.6 deg Schott F2 prism in double pass and plane-parallel order-isolating filters; LoRes: swappable plane gratings (600 l/mm in use); two e2v 4096 x 4112 x 15 um CCDs (T17, W23). Gap that matters: the echelle groove density (NOT FOUND). EFFECTIVE G = 51.74 /mm at theta = 66.99 deg comes from the arc-line identifications of a published MgWide setup (Ji pipeline, AJPID: m x lambda = 355,789 A, 156,944 per px); with the sourced F2 prism the same echelle reproduces the measured order spacing to 1.5% (DERIVED). Recommended: (A) HiRes single order 69 at Mg b, 128 fibers per detector, the most published setup (W23, P23, W15, W16); (B) HiRes four-order MgWide (28 fibers, orders 69-66) or the six-order Bulge_GC1 (24 fibers, orders 58-53, J15); (C, optional) LoRes 600 l/mm. Published HiRes R at the 125 and 95 um slits exceeds the geometric values by 19-49% (Conflicts).

**IFUM**: three IFUs (HR 864 x 75 um, 0.54"; STD 552 x 150 um, 1.1"; LSB 360 x 260 um, 1.9") with Barlow lenses and pupil-imaging lenslet arrays (f/3.5 in, f/3.3 out) at the same Nasmyth East port, feeding the M2FS spectrographs (M22, LCOMI, TAC26, C25). Recommended: LSB with the "blue" 600 l/mm grating and the 300 um slit, 180 fibers per detector, 4800-6700 A, R ~ 1000 (C25, the only refereed IFUM science paper). Gaps: Barlow magnifications, lenslet pitch, IFUM pseudo-slit pitch, blaze of the 600 l/mm grating, R measured with STD and HR.

Fit with the explorer model: (1) both instruments use pupil-fed fibers (a ball lens or lenslet images the primary onto each core), so the on-sky aperture (1.2", 1.9") and the virtual core angle (1.40", 2.43") differ; the virtual angle is the one that maps onto the collimator. (2) The quasi-Littrow opening angle is out of the dispersion plane (INFERRED), so psi = 0 in grating mode. (3) The post-fiber slits truncate the round fiber image to a near top-hat; enter a slit narrower than ~0.8 core with the slit width. (4) The pseudo-slit follows a curved arc of 8 faceted tetrises (M2FS); the explorer draws a straight slit. (5) The order-isolating filters are not modeled: in echelle mode the explorer places all orders that fit on the chip, while the filter passes 1, 4 or 6. No anamorphic optics and no white pupil.

## Magellan II (Clay) / M2FS (Michigan/Magellan Fiber System: 256 fibers feeding the twin MSpec spectrographs, f/11 Nasmyth East; HiRes = R2.6 echelle + F2 prism with order-isolating filters, LoRes = plane reflection gratings)

**Recommended preset(s):** Two M2FS presets plus one optional LoRes preset (table below); IFUM in the next section. Sourced: D = 6.5 m, f/11 Nasmyth East of Clay (M12, W23, LCOM2) with the M2FS wide-field corrector; 150 um fused-silica fibers (NA 0.22) behind 1.2" apertures and ball lenses that image the telescope pupil onto the fiber at f/3.7; f/3.4 +/- 0.1 out of the fibers (M12); 128 fibers per spectrograph in 8 "tetrises" of 16 on a 300 um pitch (M12); six post-fiber slits from 180 to 45 um (B12, J15); two identical quasi-Littrow spectrographs whose singlet/triplet lens group is both collimator and camera: f = 500 mm, f/3.4, 147 mm beam, 1:1 scale (M12 Table 4); HiRes: 200 x 420 mm R2.6 (69 deg) Richardson echelle and a 23.6 deg Schott F2 cross-dispersing prism in double pass, with plane-parallel order-isolating filters after the fiber ends (M12); LoRes: swappable 165 x 220 mm plane reflection gratings (600 l/mm used in HU24, B19, C25); one e2v CCD per spectrograph, 4096 (dispersion, x) x 4112 x 15 um (T17, W23). Entrance (explorer convention "virtual"): F = 3.4 (fiber output = collimator ratio; 500/147 = 3.401 DERIVED), so a 150 um core is 1.400" and a fiber-limited line is 0.866 x 1.400 = 1.212"; a slit narrower than the core enters with the slit width, w/(3.4 x 6500 mm) (95 um = 0.887", 45 um = 0.420"). NOT published: the echelle groove density and the LoRes blaze angles. EFFECTIVE (DERIVED from the arc-line identifications of the MgWide setup, AJPID): at the chip center the four orders 66-69 give m x lambda = 355,789 A (+/-2 A) and lambda/(dlambda/dx) = 156,940 per 15 um pixel, so with f_cam = 500 mm the Littrow angle is theta = 66.99 deg (tan theta = 2.354) and G = 51.74 /mm; the published 69 deg (R2.6, M12) would give 0.0297 A/px instead of the measured 0.0329 A/px at 5156 A, and B16 calls the echelle R2.0 (63.4 deg). Usage: W23 reports 92 M2FS fields for one program, 74 with both spectrographs in the HiRes Mg b setup; configuration statistics across all M2FS programs NOT FOUND.

### Notes

- M2FS fibers are pupil-fed: a ball lens behind each 1.2" aperture images the primary mirror onto the 150 um core at f/3.7, and the fiber delivers f/3.4 to the f/3.4 spectrograph (M12). The explorer's "virtual" convention (core/(F_out x D) = 1.400") therefore maps the core correctly onto the collimator, while the on-sky aperture is 1.2".
- The slit mechanism makes M2FS unusual among fiber spectrographs: each tetris carries six slits just after the fiber ends (B12), so the resolving power is set by min(slit, 0.87 x core) and the light loss grows as the slit narrows (B16: "Because the slits are positioned after the fibers we sacrifice roughly 60% of the incident light" with the 45 um slit).
- Model check (DERIVED, EFFECTIVE echelle, F2 prism at minimum deviation, f_cam = f_col = 500 mm): order spacing 28.4 / 27.8 / 27.3 px vs measured 28.0 / 27.7 / 27.0 px (AJPID); R (fiber-limited) 18,120 vs ~18,000 (JI23); 0.0329 A/px at 5156 A (definition of the EFFECTIVE angle). The four MgWide orders of one fiber span 3 x 27.6 = 83 px plus a ~10 px trace, which fits within 5 fiber pitches (100 px); the pipeline uses fibers 1, 6, 11, 16 of tetrises 1-4 and fibers 2, 9, 16 of tetrises 5-8 (AJP rows 12-39), 28 in all.
- At 1:1 a fiber-limited line is 8.7 px wide, so M2FS is oversampled in all modes; 2x2 binning, the common choice, leaves 4.3 binned px per line.
- Explorer check (web/physics.js required read-only from a scratchpad script, with SCHOTT F2 added in memory; no project file changed): preset A gives 0.03273 A/px at 5160 A, Rs 18,199 and Reff 18,077 (alpha = beta = 67.08 deg) and 5088-5222 A on the chip; with the 95 um slit Reff 24,577, with 45 um Reff 49,808. Preset B gives order spacings 28.39 / 27.82 / 27.27 px (orders 69-68 / 68-67 / 67-66) and 0.03285 / 0.03334 / 0.03383 / 0.03435 A/px at the four chip-center wavelengths (measured 28.0 / 27.7 / 27.0 px; 0.03292 / 0.03328 / 0.03378 / 0.03439 A/px). Preset B' places orders 57 / 56 / 55 / 54 at 6155-6317 / 6265-6430 / 6379-6547 / 6497-6668 A (J15 Table 1: 6152-6315 / 6262-6426 / 6376-6543 / 6494-6664 A). Preset C covers 3684-5707 A (C18: 3700-5700 A) with Reff 1089 (C18: 2000).
- Two spectrographs can run different modes at once (W16: HiRes in one channel, MedRes in the other; W23: 17 of 92 fields).
- Quotes from the M12 preprint: "Updated 1 March 2012", written before first light (August 2013, T17); the as-built CCD format (4096 x 4112) differs from the design table.
- Usage evidence: W23 lists 92 M2FS fields for one dwarf-galaxy program (74 with both spectrographs in the HiRes single-order Mg b setup, 1 MedRes, 17 mixed); P23, W15, W16 use the same Mg b setup; NSF17 counts "over 30 distinct science projects proposed by 18 different investigators" by 2017. The HiRes Mg b setup is the best-documented and most-published configuration; a full usage census is NOT FOUND.

### Conflicts between sources

- LSB field: 34" x 35" (LCOMI, TAC26) vs 32.7" x 31.4" (C25); spaxel 1.9" vs 1.90" (C25, effective diameter of the hexagons).
- LSB array: "18×20 array" (C25 text) vs "20 × 18" (C25 Table 2, LSB Array Size spaxels).
- CCD format: 4096 x 4120 (C25 first mention) vs 4096 x 4112 (C25 later; T17, W23).
- R with the 600 l/mm grating: ~1000 (300 um) and ~3600 (80 um) (C25) vs 778 and 2190 geometric at 1:1 (DERIVED). The coverage (4800-6700 A, C25) fixes the dispersion near 0.49 A/px, so the gap is in the line width, not the dispersion.
- Availability: general observing "in the 2025B semester" (LCOMI) vs "in the 2026B semester" (TAC26).
- Fiber output: f/3.3 (M22) vs the f/3.4 the MSpec optics accept (M12): the IFUM fibers slightly overfill the 147 mm beam (500/3.3 = 151.5 mm, DERIVED).

### Notes

- Like the M2FS fibers, the IFUM fibers are pupil-fed: each lenslet images the primary mirror onto one fiber (M22, C25). The explorer's virtual core angle (2.427" for LSB at f/3.4) is therefore larger than the 1.90" spaxel; the spectrograph sees the 260 um core.
- IFUM shares MSpec with M2FS, so the M2FS dispersers (HiRes echelle with filters, MedRes echellette, LoRes gratings) can follow any of the three IFUs (TAC26: "generally also available"); the fiber core sets the slit: 75 um (HR) gives R = 36,200 with the echelle, 260 um (LSB) gives R ~ 800-1000 with 600 l/mm (DERIVED, C25).
- nObj in the explorer's IFU convention (spaxels per detector): LSB 180 (C25), STD 276 and HR 432 (DERIVED). With 4112 px across the dispersion, 180 LSB fibers of 17.3 px each (260 um) need at least 3120 px, so the LSB pseudo-slit fills ~76% of the detector height (DERIVED, contiguous fibers).
- Explorer check (web/physics.js from a scratchpad script; no project file changed): preset D covers 4738-6751 A (C25: 4800-6700 A) with Rs 777 and Reff 776 (C25: ~1000); preset D' gives Reff 2150 (C25: ~3600); 180 spectra spread over 4112 px are 22.8 px apart.
- The IFU shoe also carries an 80 um slit for STD and LSB and a 180 um slit for LSB (IFUSHOE), so IFUM, like M2FS, trades light for resolution at the pseudo-slit.
