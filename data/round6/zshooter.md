## Superseded by the audit of 2026-11-16 (data/audit/2026-11-16_zshooter.md)

- H orders: the website gives 41–51, 11 orders; 42–51 is the June 2026 Zemax archive via the simulator.
- The 0.6° out-of-plane angle: the website says only that "A 0.6° out-of-plane tilt separates the returning dispersed
  beam from the entrance slit"; neither the sign nor whether 0.6° is the grating's tilt or the beams' separation is
  published. The preset's γ = +0.6° is one choice; the choice moves R by 0.7%.
- f/10 vs f/10.5: f/10 gives the 530.6 µm/″ slit scale on the explorer's 10.95 m pupil; the website's f/10.5 gives
  the same scale on a 10.42 m pupil. Not an inconsistency in the website.
- "Off-axis paraboloid": the website says "parabolic collimator".
- B: the passband starts at 3100 Å; 3050 Å is the simulator's trace edge on the chip.
- NIR pixels: 15 µm is the website's target; the Zemax image aperture (36.864 mm) fits 2048 × 18 µm.
- Validation: the slit image (4.46 px), the pixel scale (0.157″/px) and one order's span are computed from the same
  inputs that set the effective camera, so they are reference lines; the simulator's detector placements were set by
  hand. Bailey et al. 2026 was never read (Crossref metadata only) and is no longer cited.

## Round 6 completeness summary

**ZShooter (design stage; six presets)**: the project website and the project's public simulator repositories give a near-complete first-order prescription for the post-CoDR design built from the June 2026 Zemax archive. Keck I Right Nasmyth; f/14.5 relayed to f/10.5 with 530.6 um/arcsec at the slit; 10" slits (0.30-1.25" in the simulator; final list open); per-arm ADC and K-mirror; a VIS/NIR dichroic near 950 nm. Each spectrometer: 1500 mm off-axis-parabola collimator (150 mm beam), R2 echelle at 65.6 deg (50.15 /mm VIS, 24 /mm NIR; gamma = 0.6 deg), Mangin mirror at the intermediate slit, 1250 mm second collimator (125 mm white pupil), two dichroics, first-order VPH cross-dispersers (B 2000, G 1100, R 625, YJ 350, H 300, K 270 /mm), 225 mm f/1.8 cameras, 2048 x 4096 qCCDs (VIS) or 2048 x 2048 LmAPDs (NIR) of 15 um with the dispersion along the 2048-px axis. Published performance: median R ~ 18,000 at 0.70" (4.46 px) and ~ 38,000 at 0.33" (2.10 px), 0.157"/px, the order range of each channel, per-channel dispersion ranges, and the simulator's order positions and per-order detector coverage. EFFECTIVE values for the white pupil: camera 270 mm (225 x 1500/1250, the simulator's own 'dispersion_focal_length') and VPH line densities x 225/270; lamC DERIVED from the simulator's detector placement. With these, the explorer gives Rs = 17,677-17,734 (-1.5 to -1.8% from 18,000), 4.48-4.49 px, 0.1568"/px, the published dispersion ranges, the simulator's minimum order spacings (<1%) and middle-order coverage (0.4-2.4%). Gaps: the SPIE 2026 paper itself (paywalled, no arXiv), echelle theta, VPH design angles, camera prescriptions, VIS dichroic crossovers, the final slit list, any measured R. Main conflicts: f/10.5 (published) vs f/10.0 (1500 mm / 150 mm and the 530.6 um/arcsec scale) at the slit; 50 vs 50.15 /mm; H orders 41-51 vs 42-51; YJ order 57 dropped in the simulator; the published 15,700-21,100 R range holds the slit image at 4.46 px.

### Conflicts between sources

- Focal ratio at the slit: f/10.5 (ZSPEC, from f/14.5 x 500/690) vs f/10.0 = 1500 mm / 150 mm (collimator and pupil, ZSPEC) and f/9.995 from the published 530.6 um/arcsec on the 10.95 m perimeter (DERIVED). Keck's focal ratio is f/13.7 on the 11 m perimeter (O95), and the 530.6 um/arcsec scale implies f/13.79 at RNAS (DERIVED). f/14.5 fits a 10.42 m pupil, close to the perimeter-masked cold-stop pupil (47.8 mm at 690 mm = f/14.44, DERIVED). Preset uses F = 10.0 (keeps the published slit scale and the 150 mm beam).
- VIS echelle ruling: 50 grooves/mm (ZSPEC text; ZSFIG layout label '50 gr./mm') vs 50.15 /mm (Zemax archive 20260610B via ZSXD, ZSXDD, IRDBT; newest, adopted) vs 50.00 /mm (older simulator 'main', ZSXD). Blaze m x lambda: 36.318 um (50.15) vs 36.427 um (50.00), 0.3%.
- R range across the format: 15,700-21,100 at 0.70" and 33,200-44,700 at 0.33" (ZSPEC, ZSPERF; ZSNB per-channel 'R k' columns) hold the slit image at 4.46 px; ZSRES (rerun 2026-10-21) shows the geometric R varies only by ~1/m (1-3%) across an FSR while the slit image ranges 3.99-5.22 px. The explorer agrees with ZSRES (Rs 17,455-17,921; slit image 4.01-5.15 px at on-chip FSR points).
- R definition: geometric slit-limited R ~ 18,000 (0.70") vs simulated point-source FWHM R 20,000-24,000 in natural seeing (ZSRES); different widths.
- Requirement vs model at the narrow slit: L1/L2 R >= 40,000 with a 0.3" slit (ZSL1, ZSL2) vs median R ~ 38,000 at the 0.33" slit in the performance model (ZSPEC). At 0.30" the closed form gives 41,526 (DERIVED).
- Slit inventory: 'the paper's 0.30-0.70" table, its wider prose range, and the presentation's 0.30-1.30" range' (ZSPEC) vs the simulator wheel 0.30-1.25" (IRDB).
- VIS/NIR dichroic: 'transition near 950 nm' (ZSPEC) vs 969.6 nm at 50% transmission in the simulator curve (DERIVED from IRDB TER_dichroic_bgr-yjhk.dat); the R passband ends at 980 nm and YJ starts at 960 nm (ZSOD, ZSPEC).
- NIR pixel size: 15 um LmAPD target (ZSPEC, ZSDET, IRDB baseline) vs 18 um H2RG alternative (IRDB README); the Zemax NIR image aperture is 36.864 mm = 2048 x 18 um (DERIVED from ZSXDD zemax_channels.csv), while the VIS aperture is 30.72 mm = 2048 x 15 um.

- Design version: the post-CoDR two-spectrometer, six-channel design (ZSTECH: 'a move from three to two spectrometers') in the June 2026 Zemax archive 20260610B_ZS_6CH_Complete_Spectrograph.zar, as summarized on the project website (last modified 2026-10-16), in the SPIE 2026 paper (published 2026-08-21; not obtained) and in the simulator package ZShooter_v2 'version: pdr0.1' (IRDB commit 7cc3e9a, 2026-11-06). Where sources differ, the Zemax-derived simulator values (newest) are preferred; the website's rounded values are listed as alternatives.
- White pupil (does not fit the explorer directly): slit -> 1500 mm off-axis parabola (150 mm pupil) -> R2 echelle -> back through the same collimator to a Mangin mirror at the intermediate white-light slit -> 1250 mm second collimator (125 mm white pupil) -> two dichroics -> three VPH cross-dispersers -> three 225 mm f/1.8 cameras. The explorer has one collimator, one beam and one camera, so the preset keeps the echelle beam (W = 150 mm, f_col = 1500 mm) and replaces the 225 mm camera by the EFFECTIVE 270 mm (225 x 1500/1250), which reproduces the slit image (4.46 px), the echelle dispersion and the along-slit scale (0.157"/px). The VPH line density is scaled by 225/270 so that the 270 mm camera gives the real order spacing (the G-CLEF convention, web/presets.js). With these inputs the explorer reproduces the simulator's minimum order separations to within 0.8% and the middle-order detector coverage to 0.4-2.4% (per-arm tables).
- Not modeled by the explorer: the per-arm ADC and K-mirror, the relay (entered through F), the NIR cold stop, the six internal and front dichroics (orders beyond the passbands appear partly on the chip in B, G, R, YJ and H), the detector rotations (5-9.5 deg) and fixed detector offsets, the tapered AR coatings, and the 3200 A atmospheric cutoff in the explorer (B's orders ~114-118, 3050-3200 A, are cut).
- lamC (DERIVED): the blaze wavelength 2 sin(65.6 deg)/(G m) of the order whose full-slit footprint center lies nearest the detector's y center in the simulator's selected geometry (ZSXDD selected_orders.csv): B order 99 (center y = 2002 of 4096), G 67 (2086), R 44 (2128), YJ 67 (1039 of 2048), H 46 (1039), K 34 (1048). The explorer centers the blaze of lamC's order on the chip; the real camera origins sit 0.4-2.9 mm (x) and 3.2-5.4 mm (y) from the detector centers (IRDBT detector_offset_x/y), which the explorer cannot reproduce.
- Explorer runs (web/physics.js design(), not edited): all six presets give a valid design; per-arm results are in each arm's explorer_model_DERIVED entry.
- nObj = 1: one 10" long slit per spectrometer, nodded by up to +/-6".
